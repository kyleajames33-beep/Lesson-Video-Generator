## Exact conventions and limits

EM fields translate in phase. E is vertical; B uses page-normal dot/cross symbols, with E cross B rightward. Symbol radius encodes amplitude. No microscopic emission or photon orbit is implied.

Double-slit fronts spread rightward. Geometry is illustrative and not scale-matched to the angular intensity strip. Equal coherent illumination gives cosine-squared fringes under a sinc-squared finite-slit envelope. At progress 0.76 a labelled comparison cut removes coherence. A broad envelope remains, rather than exactly two bands. This is not a detector mechanism simulation. Intensity uses alpha from -2 to 2 and d/a=4. Bright/dark path formulas describe the coherent equal-slit state.

Diffraction graphs reveal single-slit and two/six-slit array factors. Horizontal coordinate is a sin(theta)/lambda, with zero at midpoint. Patterns are independently peak-normalised. Six slits illustrate narrowing; commercial gratings use many more. No measured screen-position scale is claimed.

Polarisation rotates an ideal analyser through 90 degrees. Input is already plane-polarised; the first-filter half-intensity rule is stated separately. The ellipse is an apparatus convention rather than a quantitative perspective projection of E.

Spectra use rounded visible Balmer wavelengths of hydrogen in nm. Display is wavelength-linear and colours schematic. Redshift scales all lines by 1+z. No arbitrary-speed approximation z=v/c or cosmological distance is inferred. Source processes remain unimplemented.

Light clock uses beta=0.6, gamma=1.25 and mirror separation 180 display units. Lab round trip is 450 units versus 360 at rest. Panels show equal fractions of a tick, which have different coordinate durations. One round trip defines one tick. Dot means ideal light pulse, not orbit. Gravity and acceleration are outside this asset.

Length is an endpoint coordinate measurement at one lab time. Perpendicular height stays fixed. This is not a photograph or squeezing. Progress compares selected inertial-frame speeds rather than accelerating a real rod.

Simultaneity transforms events initially at x=-1,+1 and ct=0. S-prime axes have equal length scales. S-prime moves right, so positive-x event B is earlier there. The connecting line joins simultaneous-S events, not a trajectory. Progress compares inertial frames, not acceleration.

Energy plots K/(mc squared) against beta for nonzero invariant rest mass. Curve stops at beta=0.979 rather than plotting infinity at c. Dashed classical curve is a comparison extrapolation. Photons require E=pc rather than the massive-particle gamma expression. Nuclear mechanisms are absent.

Photoelectric apparatus idealises charge collection with an ammeter and has no variable stopping-voltage supply. Frequency rises from 4 to 10 times 10^14 Hz; work function is a fictional 2.20 eV. Below threshold no emitted dots or graph point appear. Above threshold the endpoint and readout use the same frequency. This is a prediction, not Millikan data. Dot speeds do not encode energy. Electrons may have a range of energies up to Kmax.
