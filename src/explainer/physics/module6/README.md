## Mechanisms and decisions

- `plates`: positive upper and negative lower plates produce a downward field. An entering electron curves upward; velocity and force arrows remain attached. The path is schematic, not a solved apparatus calculation.
- `potential-energy`: a stable potential scale locates the electron while paired bars show equal and opposite energy changes. The blue bar is magnitude of the negative change in U, labelled explicitly.
- `magnetic-orbit`: a positive charge moves counterclockwise in an into-page field. Green velocity remains tangent and orange force points to the centre. Speed arrow length stays constant.
- `selector-analyser`: positive right-moving ions have downward electric force and upward magnetic force. Balanced forces select E/B. Two semicircles then compare mass-to-charge ratio at that speed. The magnetic region is a schematic plan view.
- `parallel-wires`: dot symbols mark currents out of the page. Circular fields are counterclockwise, with local directions marked between wires. Equal inward forces remain attached as separation decreases.
- `coil-torque`: end-on coil sides carry opposite currents. With B right, the out-of-page side feels upward force and the into-page side feels downward force. The out-of-page side moves from the left toward the top in a physical alignment quarter-turn. The plane-to-field angle increases from 0 to 90 degrees as the force lever arm and torque shrink. This differs from the normal-to-field angle used for flux.
- `generator-flux`: the coil is edge-on when its area normal is parallel to B. The normal rotates with the coil, and the normalised emf trace grows as sin(theta). The equation states the complementary cosine flux linkage. The graph deliberately does not overlay differently dimensioned curves.
- `sliding-rod`: rightward rod motion increases into-page flux. Counterclockwise current is upward in the rod, producing leftward magnetic force. A supplied push is needed for constant speed and Fv equals I squared R.
- `transformer-grid`: visible coils link a closed core. Flux emphasis follows the magnitude of an AC cycle; this does not portray a travelling flux particle. A loaded ideal step-up doubles voltage and halves current. At fixed transmitted power, line heating becomes one quarter.
- `motor-back-emf`: a rotating armature accompanies a current-versus-speed trace for V=12 V, R=2 ohm, k=1 V s/rad. Progress prescribes speed; it is not a motor acceleration simulation.
- `induction-slip`: the resultant stator field rotates through one turn while the rotor turns 0.8 turns. Numbers are illustrative speed ratios, not a time scale. The bars show rotor conductors schematically; shorting end rings and individual induced currents require a later apparatus visual.
- `eddy-brake`: a conducting plate enters a local into-page field. Counterclockwise circulation supplies an out-of-page induced field to oppose the increasing flux. The current loop straddles the field boundary and grows only as enough conductor enters the field. Its upward current segment lies inside the field and experiences leftward force. The drawn local circulation is schematic rather than a solved current-density map. Keep this asset within the entry interval.

## Science sources

These sources support specific mechanisms, not claims that the assets provide full syllabus coverage.

- [OpenStax, applications of magnetic forces and fields](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/11-7-applications-of-magnetic-forces-and-fields): balanced selector forces followed by magnetic analysis.
- [OpenStax, generators and back emf](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-6-electric-generators-and-back-emf): rotating-coil induction and opposing motor emf.
- [OpenStax, motors, generators and transformers](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/20-2-motors-generators-and-transformers): shared flux, transformer winding ratio and power transfer.
- [OpenStax, Lenz law](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-2-lenzs-law): induced response opposes flux change.
- [OpenStax, eddy currents](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-5-eddy-currents): counterclockwise circulation on entry into an into-page field, magnetic damping and slotted-conductor comparison.
- [Nidec, induction motor characteristics](https://www.nidec.com/en/products/small-precision-motors/contents/technology/motor/basic/00027/): synchronous speed and slip.
- [NXP, induction motor tutorial](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/supporting-information/MOTORBLACTUT.html): rotating stator field induces rotor-bar voltage; deliberately exaggerated visual slip is a schematic convention.
