# Project working rules

- Start a takeover on another computer with HANDOFF.md. Restore its ignored
  lesson state and media before reviewing selected narration or artwork.

- Do not use em dashes (Unicode U+2014) in new or revised copy, narration,
  captions, lesson titles, documentation, review pages or responses. Use a full
  stop, comma, colon or parentheses as appropriate. Check existing copy in the
  selected lesson before generating speech or publishing it. Do not change
  recorded narration text without rebuilding the affected audio and alignment.
- Preserve existing usable layouts, artwork and animations. The approved
  direction allows a mix of editorial, hand-drawn and painted treatments,
  selected by teaching purpose. Do not restyle the catalogue wholesale.
- Follow docs/visual-design-handbook.md and docs/animation-planning.md for
  scene selection and review. Motion must explain, direct attention or give
  useful context. Deliberate reading and thinking holds are valid.
- Before preparing a selected video, read the existing research in
  docs/research/hsc-video-production-standard-2026-10-02.md and its
  implementation plan, plus docs/production/teaching-templates.md. Apply
  docs/production/teaching-visual-brief-template.md to that exact lesson.
  Explain causal relationships in conversational narration; choose a useful
  question, contrast or surprise where appropriate. Record per-scene visual
  reuse/adjust decisions and purposeful narration cues. Do not impose feature
  quotas or use unrelated artwork.
- The accepted conversational, purposeful-visual revisions set the minimum
  for new selected videos. For calculations, separate the short task,
  equation and grouped givens. Associate supplied values with their units
  and reference constants. Show one reasoning stage at a time, retain useful
  established results and align later result lines to their spoken cues.
  Do not pack a full problem and all constants into one oversized heading.
- Use docs/production/course-progression-plan-2026-10-09.md and its ledger
  and content checklist before choosing the next video. New production
  briefs require entry knowledge, start/stop boundaries and the next
  handoff. For the current Year 12 Module 5 run, also use
  docs/production/module5-video-route-2026-10-10.json for the focused
  video boundaries and candidate syllabus actions. The opening lesson is
  not the whole module. Keep playlist order separate from upload chronology. Catalogue
  presence does not establish reviewed dotpoint or practical coverage.
- Use docs/production/preview-first-review.md. Before paid narration, check
  the production brief at recording stage. Before a full export, play the
  exact voiced revision in Remotion (or a short recorded pilot), check the
  brief at export stage, resolve findings, then freeze inputs. The default
  scripts/render-release.mjs full-render command requires teachingBriefPath
  in its config and enforces this check before bundling. Short frameRange
  pilots remain possible with pending review. Keep source,
  still/UI observations and human listening separate. Pending listening is
  not a pass; public release needs the existing full-package review gate and
  its current teachingBriefPath. Preserve old releases and their evidence.

- The user explicitly authorised coordinated parallel agents for this video
  production project on 10 October 2026. Root acts as project manager, assigns
  bounded file ownership and selects available models by task. The latest
  user preference is Sol 6.1 for design, authoring, implementation and
  independent review. Use a lighter model for bounded mechanical inventory.
  Astra is no longer a default assignment; reconsider only if a concrete
  unresolved task warrants it and the user agrees.
  These are routing judgements, not claims of guaranteed quality or savings.
  Keep authoring and independent review separate. Root resolves findings and
  owns selected source integration, paid narration, exports and publication.
  Respect the session concurrency limit; queue roles in waves. Do not let
  parallel writers modify the same files or frozen release inputs. Maintain
  docs/production/parallel-production-board-2026-10-10.json with actual task
  state, ownership, evidence and unresolved gates. Delegation never supplies
  missing human listening or release approval.
