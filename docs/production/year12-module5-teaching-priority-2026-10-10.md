## Source findings that affect the first drafts

1. Chem L1 concept-two-states treats completed irreversible magnesium burning as nothing happening at either scale; concept-conditions says reversible plus closed, both no exceptions. Its quick-check infers equal rates just from stable measurements and species presence. Review the static/dynamic terminology in the declared course scope, distinguish constant observation from evidence for dynamic exchange and avoid claiming any sealed, unchanged mixture must be at equilibrium. A simplified fixed-condition school model needs an explicit scope.
2. Chem L3 and L6 repeatedly claim catalysts accelerate both directions by exactly the same factor and only temperature/pressure can raise Haber yield. Preserve the core fact that a catalyst does not alter equilibrium composition/K at fixed temperature, but qualify ideal energy-diagram/model claims. Distinguish equilibrium yield from rate or amount produced within a finite time; concentration/removal conditions can matter too. Remove unsupported most-tested/marks assertions.
3. Chem L5 quick-check heats cobalt indicator paper in an oven and drives water away, while the general narration calls the model closed. The selected example must distinguish temperature change from water removal and avoid using an open drying process as proof of a temperature-only disturbance. A reviewed supplied-data example is preferable to implying a student should perform the heating procedure from the video.
4. Bio L1 quick-check says uniformity removes any chance of a resistant survivor and infection of one clone means essentially all are infected. Those outcomes are not guaranteed. Explain shared susceptibility and reduced genetic diversity under the stated case, with environmental/infection and mutation limits; sexual reproduction can supply varied combinations, not guarantee resistance. Preserve the lineage/population diagrams and crop context if directly supported.
5. Bio L6 says no other base combinations physically fit and every daughter sequence is a faithful copy. Preserve canonical complementary pairing and polymerase/proofreading reasoning, but qualify mismatches and residual errors. Its semi-conservative narration should clearly say two daughter molecules each contain an original strand, rather than implying daughter molecules are not newly formed. Retain labelled original/new strands and check 5-prime/3-prime orientation throughout the fork, not just the final still.
6. Bio L8 says every gamete is unique, all meiosis directly produces gametes and only meiosis provides variation. Specify the model organism/life-cycle and distinguish increased variation from guaranteed uniqueness. Bio L14's autosomal section says traits affect sexes equally and dominance appears every generation; use the stated simple model/penetrance assumptions and do not infer those guarantees for every real trait. Keep per-child probability language and existing useful Punnett/cross diagrams.

These are bounded source findings, not full scientific audits of all 37 lessons. Every selected script still needs an independent science/notation/data check.
