## Variant A: Descriptive worked explanation

179 spoken words.

**introduction:**

> Molar mass is mass per mole. For carbon, use twelve point zero one grams per mole. We will find the mass of two point zero zero moles of carbon, then find an amount in moles from a mass.

**worked-explanation:**

> To find mass, use m equals n times M. Substitute two point zero zero for n and twelve point zero one for M. Multiply the values to get twenty-four point zero two grams. Report twenty-four point zero grams to three significant figures. To find an amount in moles from a mass, use n equals m divided by M. Substitute the values, divide, then report your result.

**prompt:**

> A sample contains thirty-six point zero three grams of carbon. Would you multiply or divide by twelve point zero one to find the amount in moles? Explain why. Pause if you need longer.

**Assembly instruction:** six seconds of exact silence after prompt audio finishes. Keep only the question and supplied values. No answer cue or explanatory arithmetic.

**feedback:**

> Divide. You are finding how many one-mole masses fit into the sample. Thirty-six point zero three divided by twelve point zero one gives three point zero zero moles. Multiplication finds a mass from an amount; division finds an amount from a mass.
