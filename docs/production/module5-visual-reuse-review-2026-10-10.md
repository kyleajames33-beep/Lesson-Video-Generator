### Specific Chemistry implementation choices

1. `StaticDynamicDiagram` is hardcoded to `2Mg + O2 -> 2MgO`, the no-activity caption and its accessible description. Props only change timing. A revised source cannot repair these labels through props. Do not swap the speech while leaving this diagram selected. Also disclose token/plinth models as schematic rather than a literal molecular trajectory or separate physical compartment.
2. `ExchangeDiagram` is the better existing bounded reuse candidate. Start with `kind: chem12m5Exchange`, `equation: A ⇌ B`, `left0: 4`, `right0: 6`, `startAtEq: true`, `graph: none`, `meter: true`, no disturbances. `countLabels`, `meterLabels` and `captions` allow concise wording. Its equal starting rates provide a useful contrast with unequal amounts. Keep A/B schematic labels; do not relabel this 1:1 first-order model as an arbitrary balanced reaction. Review the in-flight tokens as well as the stable counters.
3. If later using the exchange graph, its equality marker is triggered when rates are within 4%, and its displayed particle counts come from rounded smooth expected counts. That is a visual approximation, not exact experimental evidence. Use `startAtEq` for this introductory balance beat; defer approach/perturbation graphs to the intended next lesson. Graph labels are 15 to 16 SVG units and need enlargement or a separate graph view.
4. `BottleDiagram` cap opacity uses `openAt` for both bottles, even though cap displacement is conditional on the opened bottle. Gate the entire cap fade by the opened flag. Its schedule then disables all gas-to-liquid moves in the open bottle and exhausts all dissolved tokens. If retained, change the model to net loss while exchange can continue, and revise `escapes only`, zero endpoint and no-equilibrium verdict. The book/source comparison avoids needing this repair merely to draft the lesson.
5. Final cues are scene-local frame plus the diagram's `delay` (default 62), not just each named `at` value. Rebind to the selected recording; old long-lesson cues cannot be copied into a shorter script.

### Specific Biology implementation choices

1. `LineageDiagram` hardcodes the viability pill. The script owner reported a reversible draft suppression using `at.viable: 1000000`; this hides the misleading pill without shared edits. Verify the selected duration remains shorter and that no other scene needs the pill. A later opt-in label/visibility prop is cleaner if promoting this reuse. The remaining diagram uses one selected line; narrate its limited scope. The ribbon represents transmitted information, not one physical DNA molecule persisting unaltered across generations.
2. Births occur at `parade`, `parade + 70` and `parade + 140`; each previous organism dims 40 frames after the next birth. With original props, all these events finish long before the later offspring/transfer labels. Retiming `parade` alone cannot independently align all handovers. Inspect the sequence at actual narration speed; keep the compact schematic sweep if the speech names its overall meaning, or add opt-in event cues later.
3. Population clone mode hardcodes `susceptible: () => true`, 16 identical tokens, preserved exactly and every clone susceptible. `labels` only exposes a small subset, so copy-only overrides cannot correct it. Varied mode maps six colours to fixed susceptibility and always retains a subset. The animation can represent an explicitly stipulated example, but it cannot substantiate the current general claims. A tiny no-guarantee footer is insufficient to counter the deterministic picture.
4. If a later selected revision repairs the population model, use an explicit scenario/outcome prop, scoped labels and genotype identifiers/patterns as well as colour. Keep all original genotypes visible before exposure. Reveal only the stipulated affected subset; make clear that exposure/environment and genetic susceptibility are distinct. Do not add random death solely to make the picture appear uncertain. In the sexual process, show one fertilisation producing one zygote before discussing a set of offspring from repeated reproductive events.

## Shared layout, cue and review priorities

- `HookSlide` inserts `AtomGlyph` and a one-atom annotation when image, diagram and comparison are all absent. Simply deleting a missing image is not a valid intentional text hook. Existing comparison cards (`comparisonIsPrompt` when appropriate) or a concept-type opening avoid that fallback. A future no-visual option should be explicit.
- `ConceptSlide` has `conceptVisualLayout: diagramFocus` for a larger visual area and shorter left column. The old two-column scenes inspected here have clear hierarchy but devote much of their area to long copy. Shorten heading/secondary text before enlarging the diagram. Essential model labels are currently much smaller than body teaching copy; a 15 to 22-unit SVG label in a roughly 744px panel is not adequate evidence of phone readability.
- `TableReveal` preserves subject colours and stable text, supports variable columns and fits content down to about 17px. Use short cells and only the dimensions needed in the current beat. Enlarging the concept stage alone does not enlarge its 740px maximum table width. A compact two-column comparison is safer than adding paragraphs to a three-column matrix. `FlowDiagram` supports short directed chains, but its moving beads must represent a meaningful direction, not imply literal DNA or matter flow.
- `QuickCheckSlide` uses `answerTiming`. `responseHold.endFrame` or `revealDelays.answerVisibleStart` establishes the first-exposure boundary. Legacy `answerStart` is a fade midpoint and starts disclosure 24 frames earlier. Separate prompt and feedback recordings, then assemble and verify the measured silence; provisional silent-draft frames are not recorded timing. Keep answers out of teaching captions and any diagram throughout the attempt.
- Highest-priority silent review: corrected static/dynamic comparison, unequal amounts with equal rates, matter-boundary representation, lineage transfer, reproduction comparison and response hold. Check the precise selected revision at early reveal, active change, settled state and answer boundary. Test at a 390px player width with captions; these desktop PNGs do not establish an actual-device pass.
- Then prepare one difficult voiced pilot per selected lesson: Chemistry exchange and its evidence question; Biology lineage/variation reasoning and the conditional crop answer. Check actual cue alignment, reading holds and caption clearance. Hold labels stable during reasoning. Preserve distinct records for source review, still observations, UI/encoded playback and human listening.
