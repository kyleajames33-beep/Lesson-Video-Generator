# Selected Biology learner follow-up

Original formative drafts, 4 October 2026, for the selected Year 11 Biology
11–12 (2025) content. Use the relevant student sheet before the separate
teacher key. No learner responses or practical-completion evidence exist yet.

| Lesson | Student material | Intended evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enzyme practical | [Defined assay and controls](enzyme-practical.md) | Process repeats, distinguish average/initial rate and evaluate a matched comparison. Extend into a teacher-reviewed investigation of all three named factors. |
| Enzyme graphs | [Three defined datasets](enzyme-graphs.md) | Plot labelled data, separate observation from mechanism and choose a discriminating follow-up. |
| DNA replication | [Build, operate and assess a fork model](dna-model.md) | Record antiparallel templates, 5′ to 3′ extension, enzyme roles, primer processing, nick sealing and justified model limits. |

The [teacher key](teacher-key.md) contains checked calculations and reasoning
rubrics. Suggested times and local point totals are planning choices, not
validated instruments or official NESA marking rules. Collect initial answers
before feedback and record corrections separately.

Use synthetic data only as scaffolded reasoning practice. The enzyme laboratory
follow-up needs a teacher-approved procedure and risk assessment. The DNA
modelling investigation needs actual learner construction, operation and
assessment records. Viewing the video or reading these sheets does not satisfy
the verbs “conduct” or “assess” by itself.
