# Mandatory content and action checklist

Prepared 9 October 2026. This maps all **27** cached official points in new Chemistry Year 11 Quantitative chemistry, plus **four** Biology enzyme role/model/practical/graph points. It expands into **63 actions**, including each specified concentration measure and both planning and conducting for the three enzyme factors. **None is marked approved.**

Use the [JSON](course-content-checklist-2026-10-09.json) for exact official text, URLs, page/content hashes, candidate source hashes and per-action gaps. Use the [CSV](course-content-checklist-2026-10-09.csv) to filter action type and unresolved scope. Run `node scripts/build-course-content-checklist.mjs`, then `node scripts/check-course-content-checklist.mjs`. These commands use the saved 8 October official extraction; they do not browse or certify teaching.

The source pages are [NESA Quantitative chemistry](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/chemistry-11-12-2025/content/year-11/faf0876f2f) and [NESA Cells as the basis of life](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/biology-11-12-2025/content/year-11/fa0edb304c), cached on 8 October. Their full page hashes are respectively `705dc49e5b964ded3eb3c51db5f4816c73eea925e601196a37b41c1364e3f65d` and `d1dde6525e7e3d5d5de7824aa541ecca5047b77beec3cc7c492a5e71f00ba97b`. Mathematics in the extraction is flattened; inspect raw source before recording equations. Examples are retained as context and do not become additional compulsory points.

A candidate video can explain an action, support a practical plan or model data analysis. It does not establish that a learner carried out a laboratory investigation. Practical conduct remains **not evidenced**. Current/public/revised status is separate in the [course ledger](course-progression-plan-2026-10-09.md). Every remaining focus area, and the non-enzyme portion of Cells as the basis of life, still needs its point-level checklist.

## Decisions before recording

- The next empirical lesson needs both forward mass percentage and inferred simplest atom ratio (cibccbf29b), not just the older percentage-to-formula procedure. Its molecular extension does not replace either action.
- Molar mass from a formula and solving MM from supplied mass/amount are different tasks. Check the public selected revision for the latter before claiming ci18f59d1c complete.
- Existing concentration material does not yet establish uses and calculations for all eight measures. Keep % w/w, % w/v, % v/v and ppm/ppb definitions/assumptions distinct; record units one by one.
- Gas molar volume is not the complete gas-law/data route. PV=nRT and the three controlled P/V/T data relationships remain named actions, followed by a real integrated reacting-mass/solution/gas application.
- L17 currently develops a temperature method and discusses all three factors. That does not establish complete pH/substrate methods or conduct of any investigation. Expand the planned practical support while retaining actual conduct as a separate route.
- L18's temperature/pH/substrate scenes are useful candidates. Shape matching alone does not pass analysing a graph and explaining the supplied evidence under its conditions.
- Required First Nations cooking and plant extraction contexts need direct sourcing/cultural review. Do not replace them with a generic open-system diagram or treat an optional clay example as a compulsory new video.

For every action, eventually attach exact reviewed scenes, the learner task/feedback and the type of evidence. Keep missing actions visible, even when a polished video already exists. The [teaching brief](teaching-visual-brief-template.md) and preview-first release workflow remain required.
