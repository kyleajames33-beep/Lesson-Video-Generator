## Botanical and causal teaching assessment

The opening reuses B1's fusion criterion with a useful contrast: one plant can supply a vegetative offspring or both gametes in a sexual event. Self-compatibility is supplied, avoiding the claim that every flowering plant can self-fertilise. Parent count is not substituted for mechanism.

The structure explanation correctly places pollen production at the anther, reception at the stigma, the connecting style above the ovary, ovules within the ovary and an egg within an embryo sac inside an ovule. It makes the locations useful by explaining why arrival at a stigma is not arrival of sperm at an egg. Pollen is not called a sperm cell. The compatible-model account then connects pollen germination, tube growth, sperm delivery and fusion causally rather than merely listing them. Primary research describes the pollen grain's tube-producing cell and its distinct nonmotile sperm cargo, supporting this separation and the transport constraint in B3-02. [Oh et al., Analysis of sticky generative cell mutants](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tpj.15162), summary and introduction inspected.

The corrected development account separates the zygote's embryo from the surrounding ovule's seed and the ovary's usual fruit development. It also distinguishes seed formation from establishment. The narrative is restricted to its sexual pathway, while the notes explicitly exclude apomixis from the check's model. Primary Arabidopsis research distinguishes embryo, endosperm and maternal tissues contributing to seed development; this supports the nesting/identity requirement rather than a whole-ovule-to-zygote transformation. [Robert et al., Maternal auxin supply contributes to early embryo patterning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41477-018-0204-z), indexed abstract inspected; full publisher retrieval was unavailable. No hormone mechanism from this paper is proposed for the lesson.

The strawberry runner is correctly identified as a stem, with a new rooted shoot at a node and eventual independence. Vegetative growth without gamete fusion is correctly classified as asexual. The mutation qualification avoids making genetic identity absolute. The model also retains resource requirements rather than promising successful establishment. Original strawberry research describes a stolon supporting a rooted daughter ramet until it can be independent on its own roots, consistent with the candidate and its warning against immediate detachment. [Savini et al., Strawberry plant relationship through the stolon](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18533001/), abstract inspected. This is a factual check, not a proposed propagation experiment.

Self-pollination and cross-pollination are defined by pollen source/destination. Confirmed compatible sperm-egg fusion, not a transfer arrow, supplies the classification in the worked cases. The statement that self-fertilisation does not guarantee a genetic copy is appropriately limited; it does not promise that every selfed offspring differs. The candidate avoids a universal variation or survival ranking. Later meiosis/allele explanations are correctly left to their focused lessons.

The check is logically well defined. Pollen reception establishes pollination; all tubes stopping before ovules removes the stated sperm delivery route; unfertilised eggs, no alternative route and an explicit fusion-dependent embryo model exclude other explanations within the task. The inferred result is no new embryo through that pathway, not a universal claim about all seeds, an exact survival count or a reported experiment. The feedback repairs transfer/fusion confusion and retains the model limitation.

Connected conversational narration explains why each distinction matters. It avoids a catalogue tour, unsupported examination stakes and repeated viewing commands. The conditions comparison links pollen limitation to the runner pathway while retaining growing conditions, giving a contextual comparison rather than a winner badge. Repeated brief reminders of fusion serve classification; they do not require a feature quota or major editorial rewrite.
