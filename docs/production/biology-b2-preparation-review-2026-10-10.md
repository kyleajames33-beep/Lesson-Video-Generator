## Science and causal explanation

The chromosome-set account is correct for the stated diploid animal model: haploid sperm and egg supply one set each, and fertilisation restores two sets. The speech explicitly limits the drawing's count and molecular detail. Keep the drawing at the level of genetic set combination rather than treating stylised merging nuclei as a detailed account of every animal fertilisation mechanism. Detailed meiosis remains outside this video's boundary.

Hydra budding supplies a named asexual animal mechanism, with growth, separation, no gamete fusion and a mutation qualification. Classifying the reproductive event rather than permanently labelling an animal sexual or asexual is a useful correction. Original research on green hydra directly investigates both asexual budding and sexual reproduction; it supports this bounded example, without importing a universal environmental trigger. [Kaliszewicz, Interference of asexual and sexual reproduction in the green hydra](https://esj-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1007/s11284-010-0771-6), abstract inspected.

The external/internal distinction is location of fusion, with development and parental investment handled as separate dimensions. The bird contrast is sound when fertilisation is stipulated. Primary chicken research identifies fertilisation in the hen's infundibulum, supporting an internal meeting before a fertilised egg is laid. It does not establish that every laid egg is fertilised. [Transcriptome analysis and identification of genes associated with individual fertilisation rate differences in hen infundibulum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36416611/), abstract inspected.

The draft does not rank all internal fertilisation above all external fertilisation, infer a fixed offspring number or parental care, or promise survival. Moisture and proximity/timing are causally connected to exposed gametes and encounter opportunities. Salmon are a suitable external-fertilisation example; an official culture method handles eggs and sperm outside the fish and activates fertilisation in water. This is a factual classification check, not a proposed student procedure or evidence for natural success rates. [USGS, Atlantic salmon culture manual](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/tm2A21/full).

The altered-current question is valid as a supplied model: stronger current is explicitly stipulated to carry sperm away while initial numbers and viable lifetime remain equal. Its expected conclusion concerns encounter opportunities, not an exact fertilisation or survival count. Preserve the author's warning that this is not a universal water-flow law. Primary laboratory/field research in two broadcast-spawning mussels found that flow and turbulence effects varied with velocity and recirculation, supporting the need for that boundary. [Quinn and Ackerman, near-bed turbulence and broadcast-spawning bivalves](https://aslopubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1215/21573698-1504517), abstract and introduction inspected. The lesson's prediction is an inference from the supplied separation condition, not a transfer of those species' measured results.

The author's two referenced OpenStax pages were also opened as a provenance check. They were not used to inherit broad success, offspring-count or animal-group generalisations into this review. No reference artwork or copied speech is proposed.
