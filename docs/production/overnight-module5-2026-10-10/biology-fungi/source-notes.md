Scientific verification, accessed 10 October 2026:

- [Saccharomyces Genome Database: What are yeast?](https://wiki.yeastgenome.org/index.php/What_are_yeast%3F), Yeast Life and Cell Cycles, lines 48 to 57. Supports named baker's yeast budding, nuclear division and daughter separation. Use for the budding scene, not a claim that all yeasts bud.
- [Andrew Murray, iBiology: Yeast Life Cycle and Sex](https://www.ibiology.org/cell-biology/yeast-life-cycle/), researcher lecture and transcript, 06:02 to 06:46 in the second transcript. Supports the simplified compatible mating-type cell fusion, nuclear fusion and meiotic spore cycle. No claim that every strain undergoes the complete cycle, or that diploid budding itself is sexual. Nutrient limitation is conditional, not sufficient for every strain.
- [OpenStax Microbiology 5.3 Fungi](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/5-3-fungi), publisher text and Figure 5.28. Supports Rhizopus stolonifer sporangia, mitotic asexual spores, hyphae and germination. [Biology 2e 24.1](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/24-1-characteristics-of-fungi), Reproduction, supports both sexual and asexual fungal spores. The script does not call all spores asexual or promise survival in all harsh conditions.
- [Sensory input attenuation allows predictive sexual response in yeast](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms12590), original research, Introduction and Figure 1. Supports compatible mating responses. No experimental quantitative claims are imported into student copy.
