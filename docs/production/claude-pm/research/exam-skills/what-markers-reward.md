# What recent HSC science marking guidelines reward

Evidence: NESA Chemistry and Biology HSC marking guidelines from 2021 to 2025, the same ten subject-year papers counted in [verb-frequency.md](verb-frequency.md). Each example below names a question in the official marking guidelines. The differences between adjacent mark levels are specific to that question. They are useful teaching patterns, not a fixed mark allocation for every use of a verb.

NESA explains that its sample answers guide the expected nature and scope of a response; they are not complete or exemplary answers. See the [2025 Biology exam pack](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2025).

## Command verbs: what raises the mark

| Skill | Difference shown by adjacent criteria | Question-level evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explain** | Name the relevant feature, then show how it causes the stated outcome. In one three-mark question, outlining a campaign's features reached two marks; linking the campaign to changed behaviour and lower disease incidence reached three. In a 2025 Chemistry question, two marks could come from describing both reaction features without explaining their effects, while three required explanations of both. | [Biology 2024, Q24(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2025, Q30(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |
| **Assess** | Make a judgement that is supported by the stated evidence. For the vaccination schedule, the five-mark level needed a comprehensive explanation using the graph and other information plus a judgement about the schedule's value. The four-mark level accepted a sound explanation with only some reference to the evidence and a relevant judgement. | [Biology 2023, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology-mg.pdf), checked against the [question paper](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology_20240326.pdf) |
| **Evaluate** | Test the thing named in the question against the evidence and reach a supported judgement. For cervical cancer campaigns, six marks required detailed use of the stimulus and a well-supported judgement; five marks required an analysis and an informed judgement. In a 2025 seven-mark genetics task, the highest level required extensive understanding of societal impacts and a relevant judgement; the next band required thorough understanding and a judgement. | [Biology 2022, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2022-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2025, Q30(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-biology-mg.pdf) |
| **Justify** | Give the choice or conclusion and connect it to reasons drawn from the case. Naming a safety precaution alone was insufficient for the two marks; its protection against infection had to be explained. For a practical improvement in 2025, three marks required two changes each justified by improved accuracy; two marks accepted only one justified change or two identified changes. | [Biology 2024, Q22(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2025, Q23](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |
| **Compare** | Deal with the same feature on both sides, then cover the features requested. In the reproduction table, all features compared correctly earned three marks; most features compared correctly earned two. In a 2025 task, a complete comparison of artificial insemination and pollination reached three marks; an incomplete comparison reached two. | [Biology 2024, Q21(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2025, Q23](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-biology-mg.pdf) |
| **Analyse** | Break down the evidence or process and show relationships to the question's claim or outcome. In a vaccine data question, the top level required comprehensive interpretation linked to the given statement and arguments on both sides; the next level accepted a sound interpretation and arguments on both sides. In a 2025 mutation question, four marks required a thorough account of how separate mutations caused the cell differences; three marks accepted a sound but less complete account. | [Biology 2021, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2021-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2025, Q33(c)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-biology-mg.pdf) |

The verbs do not replace the rest of the question. The Biology 2023 assess question explicitly required the provided data. The Chemistry 2022 analyse question specified a particular organic synthesis, an equation and design factors. These demands are part of the assessed response, not optional examples. See [Biology 2023, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology_20240326.pdf) and [Chemistry 2022, Q33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry.pdf).

## Calculations

- **Working can earn marks when the final answer is wrong or missing.** Chemistry 2025 Q33 distinguishes complete calculations and a balanced equation with states for seven marks, substantially correct calculations and an equation for six, and the main calculation steps with an equation for four to five. Chemistry 2024 Q32 awards four marks for the correct cadmium concentration to three significant figures, three for most relevant calculation steps, and two for some relevant steps. Biology 2024 Q28(a) distinguishes the complete genetic setup and probability from partial working. [Chemistry 2025, Q33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q28(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- **Units and significant figures matter where the criteria state them.** Chemistry 2022 Q29(a) includes the estimate's units and negative sign in its three-mark criterion. Chemistry 2025 Q25 explicitly gives two rather than three marks for otherwise correct calculations with incorrect significant figures. Chemistry 2022 Q28(b) and Chemistry 2024 Q32 also name significant figures in their top criteria. [Chemistry 2022, Q29(a) and Q28(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2025, Q25](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
- **Consequential marks are not stated as a general rule in these public guidelines.** Across the ten papers reviewed, calculation bands reward correct or relevant steps, but do not publish a universal rule that a later answer receives full credit after an earlier numerical error. Teach students to show the equation, substitution and later reasoning so a marker can see the steps available for credit. Do not promise an automatic carried-forward mark. The published step bands are visible in [Chemistry 2021, Q31](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2021-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf), [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) and [Chemistry 2025, Q33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).

## Graphs and data

- For a drawn graph, NESA may assess axes and units, suitable scales, plotted points and the right line of best fit together. Chemistry 2025 Q26(a) gives three marks for a column graph with accurate scale, axis labels and column heights, two for a substantially correct graph, and one for a relevant feature. Biology 2024 Q29(a) gives two marks for all points correctly plotted with an appropriate best-fit line, and one for some correct points or an attempted line. [Chemistry 2025, Q26(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q29(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- Use the graph for the requested inference. Biology 2024 Q29(b) combines extrapolating the best-fit line, reading the percentage from the axis and calculating a population number. Chemistry 2021 Q33(b) requires deductions at all three named temperatures with support from the graph for four marks; deductions at two temperatures with graph reference earn three. Chemistry 2023 Q31 awards seven marks for an equilibrium constant supported by a correct line graph; a substantially correct graph and calculation earn six. [Biology 2024, Q29(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2021, Q33(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2021-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2023, Q31](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2023-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
- In an explanation of plotted trends, identify the relevant trends, use numbers or graph features, and connect them to the science. Biology 2024 Q27 separates thorough treatment of both the trends and Pasteur's work from partial treatment of either. [Biology 2024, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).

## Practical and investigation questions

- Name a change **and explain what it fixes**. Chemistry 2025 Q23 awards three marks for two procedural changes each justified by improved accuracy, two for one justified change or two identified changes. Biology 2024 Q22(b) awards two for explaining how repetitions make an investigation reliable, rather than only naming a relevant action. [Chemistry 2025, Q23](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q22(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- Match the diagnosis to the design. Biology 2023 Q27 asks students to evaluate an epidemiological method. The highest band needs a comprehensive analysis of validity and an informed judgement; simply identifying study features sits at two marks. Biology 2025 Q32 asks for a study design that could produce the graphed results; its top band requires justifying its features with reference to the given information. [Biology 2023, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2025, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-11/2025-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- When asked whether a method is suitable, make the decision and support it with properties of that method. Chemistry 2022 Q31(a) awards three marks for evaluating suitability, two for showing sound understanding of suitability, and one for relevant information. [Chemistry 2022, Q31(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
