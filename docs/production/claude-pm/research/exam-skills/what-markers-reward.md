# What recent HSC science marking guidelines reward

Evidence: NESA Chemistry and Biology HSC marking guidelines from 2020 to 2024. Each example below names a question in the official marking guidelines. The differences between adjacent mark levels are specific to that question. They are useful teaching patterns, not a fixed mark allocation for every use of a verb.

NESA explains that its sample answers guide the expected nature and scope of a response; they are not complete or exemplary answers. See the [2024 Biology exam pack](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2024).

## Command verbs: what raises the mark

| Skill | Difference shown by adjacent criteria | Question-level evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explain** | Name the relevant feature, then show how it causes the stated outcome. In one three-mark question, describing a campaign's features reached two marks; linking the campaign to changed behaviour and lower disease incidence reached three. In a four-mark graph question, the highest level also needed two balanced equations rather than one. | [Biology 2024, Q24(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q34](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |
| **Assess** | Make a judgement that is supported by the stated evidence. For the vaccination schedule, the five-mark level needed a comprehensive explanation using the graph and other information plus a judgement about the schedule's value. The four-mark level accepted a sound explanation with only some reference to the evidence and a relevant judgement. | [Biology 2023, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology-mg.pdf), checked against the [question paper](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology_20240326.pdf) |
| **Evaluate** | Test the thing named in the question against the evidence and reach a supported judgement. For cervical cancer campaigns, six marks required detailed use of the stimulus and a well-supported judgement; five marks required an analysis and an informed judgement. For an epidemiological method, seven marks required a comprehensive analysis of study validity and an informed judgement, while five to six marks required a sounder but less complete analysis and a suitable judgement. | [Biology 2022, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2022-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2023, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology-mg.pdf) |
| **Justify** | Give the choice or conclusion and connect it to reasons drawn from the case. Naming a safety precaution alone was insufficient for the two marks; its protection against infection had to be explained. In a seven-mark structure question, the top level required correct structures, extensive interpretation of titration and reaction data, and extensive reference to the given information. Six marks required the same structures with a less complete interpretation and reference. | [Biology 2024, Q22(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q35](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |
| **Compare** | Deal with the same feature on both sides, then cover the features requested. In the reproduction table, all features compared correctly earned three marks; most features compared correctly earned two. In a chemistry process choice, the three-mark response both compared the processes and justified a preferred method; comparison of one aspect earned two. | [Biology 2024, Q21(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q31](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |
| **Analyse** | Break down the evidence or process and show relationships to the question's claim or outcome. In a vaccine data question, the top level required comprehensive interpretation linked to the given statement and arguments on both sides; the next level accepted a sound interpretation and arguments on both sides. In a synthesis design question, the top level needed connected treatment of reagents, conditions, hazards, safety, yield and purity, a correct equation, and a logical explanation; the next level showed thorough knowledge but less complete coverage. | [Biology 2021, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2021-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2022, Q33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) |

The verbs do not replace the rest of the question. The Biology 2023 assess question explicitly required the provided data. The Chemistry 2022 analyse question specified a particular organic synthesis, an equation and design factors. These demands are part of the assessed response, not optional examples. See [Biology 2023, Q30](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology_20240326.pdf) and [Chemistry 2022, Q33](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry.pdf).

## Calculations

- **Working can earn marks when the final answer is wrong or missing.** Chemistry 2024 Q32 awards four marks for the correct cadmium concentration to three significant figures, three for most relevant calculation steps, and two for some relevant steps. Chemistry 2021 Q31 likewise separates the correct result from the main steps and from some relevant steps. Biology 2024 Q28(a) awards two marks for a correct probability with some working, or for correct parental genotypes with suitable working, while all three marks require both the genetic setup and the probability. [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2021, Q31](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2021-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q28(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- **Units and significant figures matter where the criteria state them.** Chemistry 2020 Q25 includes units on graph axes and on the calculated concentration in its seven-mark criterion. Chemistry 2022 Q29(a) includes the estimate's units and negative sign in its three-mark criterion. Chemistry 2022 Q28(b) and Chemistry 2024 Q32 explicitly include significant figures in their top criteria. [Chemistry 2020, Q25](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2020-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2022, Q29(a) and Q28(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
- **Consequential marks are not stated as a general rule in these public guidelines.** Across the ten papers reviewed, calculation bands reward correct or relevant steps, but do not publish a universal rule that a later answer receives full credit after an earlier numerical error. Teach students to show the equation, substitution and later reasoning so a marker can see the steps available for credit. Do not promise an automatic carried-forward mark. The published step bands are visible in [Chemistry 2020, Q31 and Q36](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2020-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf), [Chemistry 2022, Q28(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf) and [Chemistry 2024, Q32](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2024-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).

## Graphs and data

- For a drawn graph, NESA may assess axes and units, suitable scales, plotted points and the right line of best fit together. Chemistry 2020 Q25 makes each visible in its highest criterion. Biology 2024 Q29(a) gives two marks for all points correctly plotted with an appropriate best-fit line, and one for some correct points or an attempted line. [Chemistry 2020, Q25](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2020-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q29(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- Use the graph for the requested inference. Biology 2024 Q29(b) combines extrapolating the best-fit line, reading the percentage from the axis and calculating a population number. Chemistry 2021 Q33(b) requires deductions at all three named temperatures with support from the graph for four marks; deductions at two temperatures with graph reference earn three. Chemistry 2023 Q31 awards seven marks for an equilibrium constant supported by a correct line graph; a substantially correct graph and calculation earn six. [Biology 2024, Q29(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2021, Q33(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2021-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Chemistry 2023, Q31](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2023-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
- In an explanation of plotted trends, identify the relevant trends, use numbers or graph features, and connect them to the science. Biology 2024 Q27 separates thorough treatment of both the trends and Pasteur's work from partial treatment of either. [Biology 2024, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).

## Practical and investigation questions

- Name a change **and explain what it fixes**. Biology 2020 Q25(b) awards one mark for a suitable improvement and two for justifying it. Biology 2024 Q22(b) awards two for explaining how repetitions make an investigation reliable, rather than only naming a relevant action. [Biology 2020, Q25(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2020-hsc-biology-mg.pdf); [Biology 2024, Q22(b)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2024-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- Match the diagnosis to the design. Chemistry 2020 Q28 requires separate explanations of why a titration method gives inaccurate and unreliable results for three marks. Biology 2023 Q27 asks students to evaluate an epidemiological method. The highest band needs a comprehensive analysis of validity and an informed judgement; simply identifying study features sits at two marks. [Chemistry 2020, Q28](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2020-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf); [Biology 2023, Q27](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-02/2023-hsc-biology-mg.pdf).
- When asked whether a method is suitable, make the decision and support it with properties of that method. Chemistry 2022 Q31(a) awards three marks for evaluating suitability, two for showing sound understanding of suitability, and one for relevant information. [Chemistry 2022, Q31(a)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-05/2022-hsc-chemistry-mg.pdf).
