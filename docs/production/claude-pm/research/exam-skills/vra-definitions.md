# Validity, reliability and accuracy in NESA science syllabuses

## Current published science glossary wording

These are NESA's exact definitions in the published 2025 Biology and Chemistry syllabuses and the shared Science 7–10 glossary. Each quoted definition is linked to an official NESA page.

| Term | NESA definition, word for word | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | “The extent to which measurements are considered to be close to the true value.” | [Biology 11–12 Syllabus (2025), glossary](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/biology-11-12-2025/glossary) |
| Reliability | “An extent to which repeated observations and/or measurements taken under identical circumstances will yield similar results.” | [Chemistry 11–12 Syllabus (2025), glossary](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/chemistry-11-12-2025/glossary) |
| Validity | “The extent to which the processes and resultant data measure what was intended.” | [Science 7–10 Syllabus (2023), shared glossary](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/science-7-10-2023/glossary) |

The 2025 Biology and Chemistry glossary pages both display all three terms with the same definitions and identify them as shared science terms. Checked 19 October 2026.

## Which version applies to the current HSC papers

The 2017 Biology and Chemistry syllabuses remain the courses for the 2026 HSC. NESA's [Biology course page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/science/biology-stage-6-2017) says the first HSC examination for the 2025 replacement syllabus is in 2028. Its [Chemistry course page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/science/chemistry-stage-6-2017) gives 2029 for Chemistry.

The active [2017 Biology syllabus](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/biology-stage-6-syllabus-2017.docx) defines reliability as: “An extent to which repeated observations and/or measurements taken under identical circumstances will yield similar results.” The [2017 Chemistry syllabus](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-word.docx) defines validity as: “An extent to which tests measure what was intended or to which data, inferences and actions produced from tests and other processes are accurate.” These are exact quotations from the glossary tables in the official DOCX files.

The 2017 Biology validity entry uses a semicolon and repeats “an extent to which” where Chemistry uses “or to which”. Neither 2017 document has a standalone glossary entry for *accuracy*, although both use the term in working scientifically outcomes. The 2025 validity sentence above is therefore not a word-for-word quotation from the current HSC syllabuses.
