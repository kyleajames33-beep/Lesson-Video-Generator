# NESA HSC glossary of key words

Source: [NESA, Glossary of key words](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/hsc/student-guide/glossary), undated page, accessed 19 October 2026. The source has 35 headwords, including two forms of *account* and two forms of *critically analyse/evaluate*. The descriptions below are paraphrases. For the exact official wording, use the linked NESA page. A full word-for-word copy of the glossary is not included here.

NESA says these words have similar uses across subjects but must be read in the context of the particular question. Its page also notes that questions can use ordinary prompts such as *how*, *why* and *to what extent*. A glossary label alone does not set a fixed answer structure.

## Syllabus check

The Yes/No columns report an exact, case-insensitive whole-word match for the headword in the complete official 2017 syllabus DOCX, including front matter and glossary. They do not count inflected forms, synonyms, exam questions or the combined headword's component words. For *account for / give an account of*, the check used `account`; for *critically analyse / critically evaluate*, it used `critically`. This is a text-presence check, not a claim that a verb is assessed in a syllabus outcome.
