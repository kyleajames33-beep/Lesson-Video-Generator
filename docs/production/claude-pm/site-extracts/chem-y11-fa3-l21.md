## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What Molar Enthalpy Means: Raw heat depends on how much you burned, so it can't describe the reaction on its own.
2. The Two-Step Calculation: Molar enthalpy is really three small steps chained together: get the heat into the water, get the moles of fuel, then divide.
3. Sign and Thermochemical Equations: The sign of $\Delta H$ is not decoration, it tells you the direction of energy flow at a glance.

## 4. Key terms

- Molar enthalpy ($\Delta H$): The enthalpy change per mole of a substance reacting or combusting, in kJ mol -1.
- Heat released ($q$): The thermal energy transferred to the surroundings, found from $q = mc\Delta T$ for the water being heated.
- Exothermic: A reaction that releases heat to the surroundings, giving a negative $\Delta H$.
- Thermochemical equation: A balanced equation written with state symbols and the $\Delta H$ value for the moles shown.
