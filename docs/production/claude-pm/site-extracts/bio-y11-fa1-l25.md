## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Replication and Mitosis: Growth, Repair and Continuity: DNA replication and mitosis matter because together they let an organism grow, repair itself and reproduce asexually without losing genetic information.
2. Meiosis and Cytokinesis: Variation and Completing Division: Meiosis and cytokinesis matter because one generates the variation life depends on, and the other physically completes the production of new cells.
3. Reaching a Judgement: A good evaluation does not just list importance, it recognises how the processes depend on one another and concludes accordingly.
4. From a grazed knee to a whole new generation: These four processes are not abstract, they are the difference between life continuing and life stopping.

## 4. Key terms

- Evaluate: To weigh up the importance or significance of something and reach a justified conclusion, using evidence.
- Growth and repair: Increasing cell number and replacing damaged cells, achieved by mitosis and cytokinesis.
- Genetic continuity: Passing an accurate, complete copy of the genetic information from one cell or generation to the next.
- Genetic variation: Differences in the genetic make-up of offspring, generated by meiosis (and fertilisation).
- Asexual reproduction: Producing offspring from one parent by mitosis, giving genetically identical offspring.
