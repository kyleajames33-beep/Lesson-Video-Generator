## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The Big Idea: Structure Determines Properties: Everything in this focus area hangs off a single idea: the properties you can measure are a direct consequence of how the particles are bonded and arranged.
2. Predicting Properties From Bonding: Each bonding type produces a recognisable set of properties, and in every case the reason traces back to what has to be broken or moved.
3. Intermolecular Forces and Periodic Trends: Within the covalent molecular group, boiling point is set not by the covalent bonds but by how strongly the separate molecules attract one another.

## 4. Key terms

- Structure: The type of particles in a substance and the way they are bonded and arranged.
- Property: A measurable bulk behaviour such as melting point, electrical conductivity, hardness or solubility.
- Delocalised electrons: Mobile electrons shared across a metallic lattice that allow metals to conduct and be malleable.
- Intermolecular force: A weak attraction between separate molecules (dispersion, dipole-dipole or hydrogen bonding) that must be overcome to melt or boil a molecular substance.
