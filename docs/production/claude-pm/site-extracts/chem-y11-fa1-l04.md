## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What Isotopes Are: Isotopes are atoms of the same element that have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons, so they have the same atomic number but different mass numbers.
2. Why Isotopes Share Chemical Properties: A substance's chemistry depends on its electrons, and all isotopes of an element have the same number and arrangement of electrons.
3. Why Isotopes Differ in Density: Adding neutrons adds mass to the atom without meaningfully changing its size, so a heavier isotope is denser.

## 4. Key terms

- Isotopes: Atoms of the same element with the same number of protons (same Z) but different numbers of neutrons (different A).
- Mass number (A): Protons + neutrons; isotopes of an element have different mass numbers because they have different neutron counts.
- Chemical properties: How a substance reacts and bonds, determined by its electrons, which are identical across isotopes of an element.
- Density: Mass per unit volume; a heavier isotope packs more mass into the same volume, giving a higher density.
