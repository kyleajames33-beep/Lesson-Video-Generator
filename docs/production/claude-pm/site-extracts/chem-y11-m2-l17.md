## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The titration back calculation: A back calculation works in the opposite direction to a forward stoichiometry problem.
2. Concordant titres: Multiple titrations are performed to ensure reliability.
3. Primary standard back calculation: A known mass of a pure primary standard is dissolved to a known volume, giving a precisely known concentration.
4. The gravimetric back calculation: We just saw the titration back-calculation, starting from a known standard to find an unknown concentration.
5. Spot the error in a back calculation: Spot the slip-up a student finds c(NaOH) from a titration with 0.1000 mol L⁻¹ HCl.
