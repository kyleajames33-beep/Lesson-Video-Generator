## 3. Key ideas in order

1. OIL RIG, Redox as Electron Transfer: At its core, a redox reaction is simply electrons moving from one species to another, one species hands electrons over, and another grabs them.
2. Oxidation States and the Rules: An oxidation state (or oxidation number) is a bookkeeping device: it is the charge an atom would have if every bond were treated as fully ionic, letting us track electron control without needing real ions.
3. Identifying Redox by Change in Oxidation State: Track each element's oxidation state from reactants to products: if the number increases the species is oxidised; if it decreases the species is reduced.

## 4. Key terms

- Oxidation: The loss of electrons by a species. Its oxidation state increases.
- Reduction: The gain of electrons by a species. Its oxidation state decreases.
- Oxidant (oxidising agent): The species that takes electrons from another, and is itself reduced.
- Reductant (reducing agent): The species that gives electrons to another, and is itself oxidised.
- Oxidation state (number): A signed number tracking the electrons an atom appears to control, used to spot redox changes.
