## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The Hypothalamus, Brain Meets Hormones: The hypothalamus is the place where the brain's electrical signals are turned into hormonal commands, making it the bridge between the nervous and endocrine systems.
2. The Pituitary, The Master Gland: The pituitary takes the hypothalamus's instructions and relays them to glands all over the body by secreting its own hormones into the blood.
3. Feedback Regulates the Whole Chain: The hypothalamus and pituitary do not just switch hormone production on, they are switched off again by the very hormones they cause to be released, through negative feedback.
4. Why doctors test pituitary hormones to diagnose thyroid disease: Because the pituitary and target glands are linked by feedback, doctors can read the whole chain from a blood test.

## 4. Key terms

- Endocrine system: The system of glands that secrete hormones directly into the blood to regulate body functions.
- Hormone: A chemical messenger secreted by a gland into the blood that acts on specific target cells.
- Hypothalamus: A region at the base of the brain that links the nervous and endocrine systems and controls the pituitary gland.
- Pituitary gland: A small gland below the hypothalamus that secretes hormones controlling many other endocrine glands, the "master gland".
- Tropic hormone: A pituitary hormone whose target is another endocrine gland, controlling that gland's hormone secretion (e.g. TSH, ACTH).
