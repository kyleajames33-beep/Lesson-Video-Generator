## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What concentration actually measures: A solution is a homogeneous mixture of a solute (the substance being dissolved) in a solvent (the dissolving medium, usually water).
2. The critical unit conversion: The volume in $c = n \div V$ must be in litres.
3. Concentration in g L⁻¹ vs mol L⁻¹: Sometimes concentration is given or required in grams per litre (g L⁻¹).
4. Concentration Explorer: Adjust moles and volume to see how concentration changes in real time.
