## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Materials for Energy: Glucose, Oxygen and Carbon Dioxide: A cell's most urgent requirement is energy, and three of the six materials are tied directly to releasing it.
2. Materials for Building and Structure: Amino Acids and Ions: Beyond energy, a cell must build proteins and maintain the chemical conditions for its reactions, and two materials do this job.
3. Water, the Material Behind Every Reaction: Water is the most abundant requirement of all, because every other material has to dissolve and react within it.
4. Why sports drinks contain electrolytes: When you sweat heavily, you lose water and dissolved ions (electrolytes) such as sodium and potassium.

## 4. Key terms

- Raw material: A substance a cell takes in (or produces) and uses to build structures, carry out reactions or release energy.
- Glucose: A simple sugar, the cell's main fuel; broken down in respiration to release energy.
- Amino acid: The building block of proteins; cells join amino acids together to make enzymes and structural proteins.
- Ion: A charged mineral particle (e.g. Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Cl⁻) needed for nerve signals, enzyme function and structure.
- Reactant / product: A reactant is consumed in a reaction; a product is made, the same molecule can be either depending on the cell.
