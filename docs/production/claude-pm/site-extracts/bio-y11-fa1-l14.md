## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What Photosynthesis Does: Photosynthesis is the process that captures light energy from the Sun and uses it to build energy-rich glucose from simple raw materials.
2. The Summary Equation: A summary equation captures the whole process in one line: it lists what goes in (reactants), what comes out (products), and the energy input that drives it.
3. Photosynthesis and Respiration: A Cycle: The products of photosynthesis are the reactants of respiration, and vice versa, together they cycle carbon, oxygen and energy through living things.
4. Photosynthesis and the climate: Because photosynthesis pulls carbon dioxide out of the air and locks carbon into plant tissue, the world's forests, grasslands and ocean phytoplankton act as enormous carbon "sinks".

## 4. Key terms

- Photosynthesis: The process that uses light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.
- Chloroplast: The organelle in plant and algal cells where photosynthesis takes place; it contains chlorophyll.
- Chlorophyll: The green pigment that absorbs light energy used to drive photosynthesis.
- Reactant: A substance present at the start of a reaction and used up by it (here: CO₂ and water).
- Product: A substance made by a reaction (here: glucose and oxygen).
