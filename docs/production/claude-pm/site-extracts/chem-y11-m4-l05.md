## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Where this lesson fits: Lesson question: how can lowering just one number on an energy diagram, the activation energy (E a ), turn a reaction that barely runs into a fast one, without changing how much energy the reaction releases?
2. Know what matters most: A catalytic converter on a modern car contains a thin honeycomb of platinum and palladium.
3. What you'll master, and the words for it: A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy, increasing the proportion of collisions that have enough energy to react, without being consumed in the process.
