## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Dissolving an ionic solid is a competition between breaking the lattice (endothermic) and hydrating the ions (exothermic).
2. The sign of ΔH dissolution depends on whether |HE| or |LE| is larger: exothermic dissolution warms the solution, endothermic dissolution cools it.
3. Endothermic dissolution can still be spontaneous, because the entropy increase (TΔS) can outweigh ΔH.
4. A saturated solution is a dynamic equilibrium, with dissolution and recrystallisation at equal rates.
5. A radioactive KBr tracer shows ions exchanging in a saturated solution.
6. Temperature changes Ksp, so most salts become more soluble as temperature rises.
7. Cycasin is water-soluble, so running water removes it from seeds by dissolution equilibrium and LCP.

## 4. Key terms

- Dissolution: "The process of a solute dispersing uniformly into a solvent to form a homogeneous solution."
- Enthalpy of dissolution (ΔHsoln): "The heat change when one mole of a substance dissolves; can be endothermic or exothermic."
- Entropy of dissolution: "Dissolution typically increases entropy as particles become more dispersed in solution."
- Solubility equilibrium: "The dynamic equilibrium established when dissolved ions and undissolved solid are in balance: AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq)."

## 5. Worked examples

**Worked Example 1 (Band 4-5).** MgCl₂ dissolves and the solution becomes significantly warmer. (a) Write the dissolution equation. (b) Explain in terms of lattice and hydration energy why it is exothermic. (c) Evaluate the claim that adding water to a saturated solution causes precipitation.
Answer: (a) MgCl₂(s) ⇌ Mg²⁺(aq) + 2Cl⁻(aq). (b) Hydration energy exceeds lattice energy in magnitude. (c) Wrong: adding water dilutes the ions and shifts the equilibrium right.

**Worked Example 2 (Band 5-6).** Crushed cycad seeds are submerged in a running stream for two weeks. (a) Identify the relevant property of cycasin. (b) Explain why running water is more effective than still water. (c) Explain why crushing is significant.
Answer: (a) Cycasin is water-soluble. (b) Running water keeps [cycasin] near zero, so LCP shifts the equilibrium right; still water saturates and slows. (c) Crushing increases surface area and so the dissolution rate.
