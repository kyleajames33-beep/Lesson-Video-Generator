## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Open vs Closed Systems: A "system" is just the part of the universe we choose to study, the chemicals in a flask.
2. Why Mass Seems to Disappear: When a reaction makes a gas in an open container, that gas escapes into the surroundings.
3. Measuring Mass Loss and Gas Volume: A gas-producing reaction can be followed two ways at once: weigh the open flask over time to record the mass it loses, and collect the gas to record the volume it occupies.

## 4. Key terms

- Open system: A system that can exchange both matter and energy with its surroundings, gas, for example, can escape.
- Closed system: A system that can exchange energy but not matter with its surroundings, it is sealed, so no substance enters or leaves.
- Surroundings: Everything outside the system, such as the air, the bench and the rest of the room.
- Gas syringe: A graduated, gas-tight syringe used to collect and measure the volume of gas produced by a reaction.
