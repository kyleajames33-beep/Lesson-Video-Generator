## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Reaction Types: Predict and Balance: Once you recognise which family a reaction belongs to, you already know the shape of the products, classifying first is what makes predicting and balancing reliable.
2. Rate: Collision Theory and the Four Factors: A reaction only happens when particles collide with enough energy (at least the activation energy) and the correct orientation, so anything that makes successful collisions more frequent speeds the reaction up.
3. Energy: Enthalpy and the Calculation Chain: Breaking bonds always absorbs energy and making bonds always releases it, so the overall sign of $\Delta H$ just compares the two, and because energy is conserved, the heat released into the surroundings is the heat the reaction loses.

## 4. Key terms

- Reaction type: The category a reaction falls into, synthesis, decomposition, combustion, precipitation, neutralisation, redox or displacement, which lets you predict products.
- Collision theory: The idea that particles must collide with enough energy and the right orientation to react; it explains every factor that changes rate.
- Activation energy ($E_a$): The minimum energy a collision must have for reactant bonds to break and a reaction to proceed.
- Enthalpy change ($\Delta H$): The heat absorbed or released at constant pressure: negative for exothermic reactions, positive for endothermic ones.
