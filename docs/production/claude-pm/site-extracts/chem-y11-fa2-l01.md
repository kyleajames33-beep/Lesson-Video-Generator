## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The Law of Conservation of Mass: A chemical reaction does not make matter appear or disappear.
2. Why Equations Must Balance: If no atom can be created or destroyed, then a correct equation must show exactly the same number of each kind of atom on both sides.
3. From Balancing to Stoichiometry: Once an equation is balanced, the coefficients are not just bookkeeping, they tell you the fixed ratio in which the substances combine.

## 4. Key terms

- Law of conservation of mass: In a chemical reaction, the total mass of the reactants equals the total mass of the products, matter is neither created nor destroyed.
- Balanced equation: A chemical equation with the same number of atoms of each element on both sides, achieved using coefficients.
- Coefficient: The whole number written in front of a formula that tells you how many of that species take part in the reaction.
- Stoichiometry: The study of the fixed quantitative (mole and mass) relationships between reactants and products in a balanced equation.
