## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Compression favours fewer gas moles; expansion favours more; equal gas totals give no shift.
2. Compression raises all gas concentrations, causing unequal changes in opposing reaction rates.
3. Inert gas at constant volume leaves reacting partial pressures unchanged; the constant-pressure comparison is extension material.
4. Compressing 2NO₂(g) ⇌ N₂O₄(g), ΔH = −57 kJ/mol, darkens the gas immediately, then it becomes paler than that immediate colour as NO₂ is consumed.
5. Heating this exothermic forward reaction shifts left and darkens the gas; cooling shifts right and pales it.
6. Catalysts lower both activation barriers and speed equilibrium attainment without changing position, Keq or ΔH.
7. Only temperature changes Keq.

## 4. Key terms

- Pressure disturbance: Increasing pressure at constant temperature shifts equilibrium towards the side with fewer moles of gas.
- Volume disturbance: Decreasing volume increases pressure, shifting equilibrium towards fewer gas moles.
- Inert gas addition: Adding an inert gas at constant volume does not change partial pressures of reactants/products, no shift.
- Mole ratio of gases: The stoichiometric coefficients of gaseous species determine how pressure changes affect the equilibrium.

## 5. Worked examples

- Halve container volume: (a) N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g), 4 → 2 gas moles, shift right; (b) H₂(g) + Cl₂(g) ⇌ 2HCl(g), 2 → 2, no shift; (c) Fe₃O₄(s) + 4H₂(g) ⇌ 3Fe(s) + 4H₂O(g), 4 → 4, no shift, solids excluded. Keq unchanged.
- Haber equilibrium, ΔH = −92 kJ/mol, 450°C/200 atm → 550°C/300 atm: temperature alone shifts left and decreases Keq; pressure alone shifts right with Keq unchanged. Page answer: effects oppose; “temperature likely dominates → net slight left shift; yield decreases, rate improves.”
- Simulator: 350 K, initial 0.100 mol L⁻¹ N₂O₄; final [NO₂] = 0.09638 mol L⁻¹ in both runs. Uncatalysed approximately 12.1 s; catalysed approximately 1.5 s, about 7.9 times sooner.

## 6. Misconceptions the page addresses

Pressure always shifts right: count gas moles first. Count solids: exclude them. Compression only darkens NO₂: distinguish immediate colour from re-equilibration. Catalyst increases yield or Keq: it speeds both directions without changing the final composition. Pressure changes Keq: it does not.
