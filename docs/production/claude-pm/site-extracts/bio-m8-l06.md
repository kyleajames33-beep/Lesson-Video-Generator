## 6. Misconceptions the page addresses

- The dividing criterion is transmissibility, not severity, treatability or genetics. Cystic fibrosis and Huntington's disease are genetic and still non-infectious, because no pathogen passes them between people. Writing that "non-infectious means not genetic" is one of the most common errors in this module.
