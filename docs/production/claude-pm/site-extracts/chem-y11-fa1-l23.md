## 4. Key terms

- Intramolecular bond: A strong bond within a substance's particles: ionic, covalent or metallic.
- Covalent molecular substance: Discrete molecules held to each other only by weak intermolecular forces (e.g. CO 2, water, wax).
- Covalent network solid: A giant lattice in which every atom is joined by strong covalent bonds throughout (e.g. diamond, silicon dioxide).
- Mobile charge carriers: Free-moving charged particles (delocalised electrons in metals, or ions in molten/aqueous ionic compounds) needed to conduct electricity.
