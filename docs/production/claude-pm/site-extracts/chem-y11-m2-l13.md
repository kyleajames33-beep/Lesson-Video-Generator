## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The five-step method, and the words for it: ⚠️ Never identify the LR by comparing masses or moles alone, you must divide each by its coefficient first.
2. Limiting and excess reagents, and theoretical yield: When two reactants are mixed in non-stoichiometric amounts, one will be completely consumed before the other.
3. Theoretical Yield: The theoretical yield is the maximum mass of product that can form, calculated from the moles of the limiting reagent.
4. Put the method in order: Sort the steps given the masses of two reactants, find the theoretical yield of the product.
