## 3. Key ideas in order

1. From First Lenses to the Light Microscope: The first microscopes revealed that living things are made of cells at all, a discovery impossible without the technology.
2. The Electron Microscope Changes Everything: Using electrons instead of light gave roughly a thousand-fold jump in resolution, exposing organelle detail no one had seen before.
3. Weighing It Up, Strengths and Limitations: Each technology has trade-offs, so a fair assessment weighs benefits against limitations rather than crowning one as best.
4. Seeing a virus for the first time: When a new virus emerges, one of the first things scientists do is image it with an electron microscope, because viruses (~20–300 nm) are far too small for any light microscope to resolve.

## 4. Key terms

- Magnification: How many times larger an image appears than the real object.
- Resolution: The smallest distance between two points a microscope can still show as separate, the real limit on detail.
- Electron microscope: Uses a beam of electrons (not light), giving resolution ~0.2 nm, about 1000× finer than a light microscope.
- TEM / SEM: Transmission EM (internal detail) and Scanning EM (3D surfaces), two types of electron microscope.
- Assess: Make a judgement of value, weighing up strengths and limitations to reach a conclusion.
