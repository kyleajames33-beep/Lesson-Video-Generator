## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What the Activity Series Is: The activity series is simply a list of metals lined up from the one that reacts most eagerly down to the one that barely reacts at all.
2. Constructing the Series from Practical Data: To build the series you compare reactions.
3. Comparing with Secondary Sources and Reliability: A constructed series is only as trustworthy as the data behind it, so the final step is to compare your practical order with a standard published activity series and account for any differences.

## 4. Key terms

- Activity series: An ordered list of metals from most to least reactive, used to predict reactions with water, acid and other metal salts.
- Reactivity: How readily a metal loses electrons to form a positive ion; the more reactive a metal, the more easily it is oxidised.
- Displacement: A reaction in which a more reactive metal pushes a less reactive metal out of its compound.
- Secondary source: Data you did not collect yourself, such as a published activity series in a textbook or data sheet.
