## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What a Catalyst Actually Does: A catalyst does not push the reaction harder; it opens a different door.
2. The Energy Profile With a Catalyst: On an energy profile, the reaction climbs from the reactant level over a barrier (the activation energy) and down to the product level.
3. Catalysts in Industry, the Economics: In a factory, a faster reaction is a cheaper reaction.

## 4. Key terms

- Catalyst: A substance that increases the rate of a reaction by providing an alternative pathway with lower activation energy, and is recovered unchanged at the end.
- Activation energy ($E_a$): The minimum energy colliding particles need to react, the height of the energy barrier between reactants and products.
- Reaction pathway: The route a reaction takes from reactants to products; a catalyst offers a different route with a lower barrier.
- Enthalpy change ($\Delta H$): The overall heat released ($\Delta H < 0$, exothermic) or absorbed ($\Delta H > 0$, endothermic); a catalyst does not change its value.
