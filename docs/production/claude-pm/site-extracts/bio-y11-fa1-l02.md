## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Boundary, Control Centre and Powerhouse: Before the specialised departments, a cell needs a boundary that controls what enters, a control centre that holds the instructions, and a power supply.
2. The Protein Production Line: Making and exporting a protein is a sequence: build it, fold and modify it, then package and ship it, and a different organelle owns each step.
3. Digestion, Storage and the Solar Panel: A cell also needs to break down waste, store materials, and (in plants) capture light energy, three more membrane-bound organelles take care of these.
4. When organelles fail: Tay–Sachs disease: Tay–Sachs disease is caused by a faulty lysosomal enzyme.

## 4. Key terms

- Organelle: A specialised structure within a cell that carries out a particular function.
- Endoplasmic reticulum: A network of membranes for synthesis: rough ER (with ribosomes) makes proteins; smooth ER makes lipids.
- Golgi body: Stacked flattened membranes that modify, package and dispatch proteins and lipids in vesicles.
- Lysosome: A membrane-bound sac of digestive enzymes that breaks down waste and worn-out organelles.
- Cristae: The folds of the inner mitochondrial membrane that increase surface area for aerobic respiration.
