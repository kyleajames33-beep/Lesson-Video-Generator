## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Demonstrating Synthesis and Combustion Safely: When you hold a strip of magnesium ribbon in a Bunsen flame it ignites and burns with a brilliant white light, leaving a fine white powder.
2. Demonstrating Decomposition: In a decomposition one compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances, usually when energy is supplied.
3. Demonstrating Neutralisation and Designing a Valid Method: In a neutralisation an acid reacts with a base to produce a salt and water.

## 4. Key terms

- Qualitative observation: A description of what is seen, heard, felt or smelt (colour, light, gas, temperature change) without a measured number.
- Risk assessment: Identifying the hazards of a practical and the controls used to reduce the risk to a safe level.
- Limewater test: A confirmatory test for carbon dioxide: the gas turns clear limewater (calcium hydroxide solution) milky.
- Valid test: A method that actually measures what it claims to, because the relevant variables are controlled or accounted for.
