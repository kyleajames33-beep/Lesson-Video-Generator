## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Very negative ΔG° favours products; smaller values allow equilibrium mixtures.
2. Reversibility is tested by restoring a changed system.
3. Equilibrium has ΔG = 0 and minimum free energy.
4. Methane combustion has a negligible reverse reaction.
5. Endothermic reactions are spontaneous when positive ΔS makes TΔS exceed ΔH.
6. Haber production recycles unconverted gases.

## 4. Key terms

- Reversible reaction: A reaction that proceeds in both forward and reverse directions, reaching dynamic equilibrium.
- Irreversible reaction: A reaction that goes effectively to completion with no significant reverse reaction.
- Entropy (S): A measure of the disorder or dispersal of energy in a system.
- Gibbs free energy (ΔG°): A thermodynamic quantity combining enthalpy and entropy; ΔG° = ΔH° − TΔS°.
- Non-equilibrium system: A reaction mixture where Q ≠ Keq and net reaction is still proceeding.
- Spontaneous reaction: A reaction that proceeds without continuous energy input; has ΔG < 0.

## 5. Worked examples

- Example 1: 2H₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2H₂O(g), ΔG = −457 kJ/mol per mol H₂O: irreversible; reverse +457. CH₃COOH(aq) ⇌ CH₃COO⁻(aq) + H⁺(aq), ΔG° = +27 kJ/mol: reversible, reactants favoured. N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g), ΔG° = −33 kJ/mol: reversible, products moderately favoured.
- Example 2: C₆H₁₂O₆(s) + 6O₂(g) → 6CO₂(g) + 6H₂O(l), ΔH = −2803 kJ/mol. Page answer: ΔS > 0; both terms make ΔG negative; reverse requires ΔG = +2803 kJ/mol. Photosynthesis ΔG = +2870 kJ/mol requires continuous solar energy and an open system.
- Q6: minimum free energy means zero net driving force.
- Q7: H₂ + F₂ → 2HF, ΔG° = −543 kJ/mol: irreversible; 2NO₂ ⇌ N₂O₄, −4.7 kJ/mol: reversible, constant colour/concentrations with ongoing equal rates.
- Q8: Haber ΔG° = −33, ΔH = −92 kJ/mol; 4 mol gas becomes 2, ΔS < 0; heating makes ΔG less negative and reduces NH₃ yield at 400–500°C.

## 6. Misconceptions the page addresses

⇌ does not mean 50/50 concentrations. Heat release alone does not establish irreversibility. Endothermic does not mean non-spontaneous. System entropy need not increase. ΔG, rather than generally non-zero ΔG°, is zero at equilibrium. One colour change does not demonstrate reversal.
