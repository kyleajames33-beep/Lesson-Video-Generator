## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Models for Covalent Bonding: For covalent molecules we use three complementary models, and each one trades detail in one area for clarity in another.
2. Models for Ionic and Metallic Bonding: For giant structures we model a repeating pattern, so the strengths and limitations centre on what a small drawn portion can and cannot capture about an effectively infinite lattice.
3. Evaluating: Matching the Model to the Job: Evaluating means reaching a judgement: no model is best for everything, so the "best" model is always relative to the characteristic you are trying to illustrate.

## 4. Key terms

- Model: A simplified representation of something that cannot be seen directly, designed to make certain features clear.
- Electron-dot (Lewis) diagram: A model showing valence electrons as dots/crosses and how they are shared or transferred in bonding.
- Sea-of-electrons model: A model of metallic bonding: a lattice of cations surrounded by delocalised, mobile valence electrons.
- Evaluate: To judge something by weighing its strengths against its limitations and reaching a justified conclusion.
