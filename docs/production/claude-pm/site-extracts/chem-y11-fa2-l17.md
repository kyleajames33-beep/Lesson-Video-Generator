## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Boyle's Law and the Particle Picture: For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, pressure and volume are inversely proportional, push the volume down and the pressure goes up by the same factor.
2. Using $p_1V_1 = p_2V_2$: If you know one pressure-volume pair and either the new pressure or the new volume, the constant product lets you solve for the missing quantity.
3. Analysing $p$ vs $V$ Data: If a gas obeys Boyle's law, then for every measured pressure-volume pair the product $pV$ should come out to the same value within experimental error.

## 4. Key terms

- Boyle's law: For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, pressure and volume are inversely proportional: $pV$ stays constant.
- Inversely proportional: As one quantity increases, the other decreases by the same factor, so their product stays the same: $p \propto \dfrac{1}{V}$.
- Closed system: A container that traps a fixed amount of gas so that no particles can enter or leave.
- Pressure (kPa): The force exerted per unit area by gas particles colliding with the container walls, measured here in kilopascals.
