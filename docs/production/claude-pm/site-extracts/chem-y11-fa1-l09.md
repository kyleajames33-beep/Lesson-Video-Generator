## 3. Key ideas in order

1. From Indivisible Spheres to Electrons: The story begins with the atom as a solid, unbreakable ball, and the first crack in that idea was the discovery that something smaller could come out of it.
2. The Nucleus and Energy Levels: A surprising experiment showed the positive charge was not spread out at all, but packed into a tiny central core, and that core needed a new model.
3. The Neutron and the Quantum Model: Two final pieces completed today's picture: a neutral particle in the nucleus, and a more accurate description of where electrons actually are.

## 4. Key terms

- Atomic model: A simplified picture of atomic structure used to explain and predict observations.
- Plum-pudding model: Thomson's idea of electrons embedded in a sphere of positive charge.
- Nuclear model: Rutherford's model with a tiny, dense, positive nucleus and mostly empty space around it.
- Energy level (shell): Bohr's idea that electrons occupy fixed, quantised energy levels around the nucleus.
