## 3. Key ideas in order

1. The Levels of Organisation: Living things are organised in a hierarchy where each level is constructed from the level beneath it, building up from tiny organelles to a whole organism.
2. Why Each Level Exists: Grouping units at each level creates abilities that the individual units could not achieve alone, which is why complex bodies need every rung of the ladder.
3. The Hierarchy in Plants: The hierarchical pattern is universal to multicellular life, so plants are organised on exactly the same ladder, just with their own organelles, tissues and organs.
4. Why surgeons think in levels: When doctors describe disease, they pinpoint exactly which level of organisation has failed, and that decides the treatment.

## 4. Key terms

- Organelle: A specialised structure within a cell that carries out a particular function (e.g. mitochondrion).
- Tissue: A group of similar cells working together to perform a shared function (e.g. muscle tissue).
- Organ: A structure made of several tissue types working together to carry out a major function (e.g. the heart).
- System: A group of organs working together to perform a broad life function (e.g. the circulatory system).
- Hierarchical organisation: An arrangement in levels where each level is built from, and depends on, the level below it.
