## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Levels, Sublevels and Orbitals: Electrons live in a nested structure: each principal energy level splits into sublevels, and each sublevel is made of one or more orbitals, and energy increases at every step.
2. Writing Configurations of Atoms: To write any atom's configuration, place its electrons one sublevel at a time in order of increasing energy until you run out.
3. Configurations of Ions: An ion is just an atom with electrons added or removed, so its configuration is the atom's configuration adjusted for the change in electron count.

## 4. Key terms

- Energy level (shell): A principal energy level labelled by n = 1, 2, 3, 4 …; higher n means higher average energy.
- Sublevel (subshell): A division of an energy level into s, p, d or f, each with a slightly different energy.
- Orbital: A region where there is a high probability of finding an electron; each orbital holds a maximum of 2 electrons.
- Aufbau principle: Electrons fill the lowest-energy sublevel available before moving to a higher one.
- spdf notation: Shorthand listing each occupied sublevel with the number of electrons as a superscript, e.g. 1s²2s²2p⁶.
