## 4. Key terms

- Secondary source: Information you gather from others rather than from your own experiment, such as articles, textbooks, reports or databases.
- Reliability: Whether the information is consistent and supported by other trustworthy sources, so it could be repeated or confirmed.
- Validity: Whether the source actually addresses the question being asked and the evidence genuinely supports its claims.
- Bias: A slant that favours one viewpoint, often when the author has a commercial, political or personal interest in the conclusion.
- Currency: How up to date a source is, which matters because scientific understanding and data change over time.
