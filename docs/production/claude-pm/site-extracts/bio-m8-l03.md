## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Two loops. One variable.
2. Know what matters.
3. After a meal, insulin helps reduce the rise in blood glucose.
4. During fasting or exercise, glucagon helps increase blood glucose by signalling the liver to release glucose from stored glycogen.
5. Diabetes mellitus is the collective name for conditions in which blood glucose homeostasis fails.
6. These five errors appear in HSC scripts every year.
