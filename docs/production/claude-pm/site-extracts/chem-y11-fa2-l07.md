## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Percentage Composition by Mass: Imagine you have exactly one mole of a compound.
2. Finding the Empirical Formula: The empirical formula is the simplest whole-number ratio of atoms.
3. From Empirical to Molecular Formula: The molecular formula is always a whole-number multiple of the empirical formula.

## 4. Key terms

- Percentage composition: The proportion of each element in a compound, expressed as a percentage of the total mass.
- % w/w: Percentage by weight (mass), the mass of one component divided by the total mass, times 100.
- Empirical formula: The simplest whole-number ratio of atoms (or moles of atoms) of each element in a compound.
- Molecular formula: The actual number of atoms of each element in one molecule, a whole-number multiple of the empirical formula.
