## 3. Key ideas in order

1. What ppm and ppb Mean: When a solute is present in vanishingly small amounts, expressing it as a fraction of the whole and then scaling that fraction up by a million or a billion gives a clean, readable number.
2. Why mg L⁻¹ ≈ ppm for Dilute Water: Because dilute water has a density of about 1 g mL⁻¹, one litre of solution has a mass of about 1 kg, which is exactly 1 000 000 mg.
3. Converting Between ppm, ppb and Percentages: Percentage, ppm and ppb are all the same kind of mass ratio scaled by different multipliers, so converting between them is just multiplying or dividing by factors of ten.

## 4. Key terms

- Parts per million (ppm): The mass of solute per million units of total mass of solution: mass solute ÷ mass solution × 10 6.
- Parts per billion (ppb): The mass of solute per billion units of total mass of solution: mass solute ÷ mass solution × 10 9. 1 ppm = 1000 ppb.
- mg L⁻¹: Milligrams of solute per litre of solution. For dilute water (density ≈ 1 g mL⁻¹) it is numerically equal to ppm.
- % (w/w): Mass of solute per 100 units of total mass, as a percentage. 1% (w/w) = 10 000 ppm.
