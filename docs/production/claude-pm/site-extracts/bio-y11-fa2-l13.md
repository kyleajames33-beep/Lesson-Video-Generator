## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Why Animals Need Organ Systems: Because animals are large and use energy quickly, materials cannot simply diffuse far enough or fast enough, so specialised organ systems take over the jobs of supply and removal.
2. The Four Management Systems: Each of the four needs is managed mainly by one organ system, built from a team of organs suited to that job.
3. The Circulatory System Ties It Together: No system reaches the body's cells directly, they all hand their materials to the blood, which is the shared transport network linking supply, exchange and removal.
4. Why a failing kidney affects the whole body: Because the organ systems are interdependent, a problem in one ripples through the rest.

## 4. Key terms

- Organ system: A group of organs that work together to perform a major body function (e.g. digestion, circulation).
- Digestive system: The organs (mouth, stomach, intestines, etc.) that break food down and absorb nutrients and water.
- Respiratory system: The organs (lungs, airways) that exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide between air and blood.
- Circulatory system: The heart, blood vessels and blood that transport materials between all the other systems and the cells.
- Excretory system: The organs (kidneys, etc.) that remove metabolic wastes and regulate water and salts.
