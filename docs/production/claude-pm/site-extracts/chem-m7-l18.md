## 3. Key ideas in order

1. pKa is not just a number, it is a ranking of how willing a molecule is to give up a proton.
2. The pKa difference between carboxylic acid, phenol, and alcohol is not arbitrary, it is a direct consequence of how stable the conjugate base is after the proton is lost.
3. The reactions of NaOH, Na₂CO₃, and NaHCO₃ with organic acid classes are thermodynamic probes, each tells you whether the organic acid is stronger than the reference acid produced in the reaction.
4. The pKb scale ranks organic bases by the same principle as pKa ranks acids, the more available the lone pair for proton acceptance, the stronger the base.
5. (a) Ethanoic acid + Na₂CO₃ (b) Phenol + NaHCO₃ (c) Propan-1-amine + HCl (d) Ethanamide + HCl.
