## 1. Title and main question

Biodiversity Change Caused by Genetic Techniques. How can biotechnology increase, reduce or have mixed effects on biodiversity?

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year12/module6/lesson11.sa.html`.

## 2. Syllabus content covered

“evaluate the effect on biodiversity of using biotechnology in agriculture”

## 3. Key ideas in order

1. Judge biotechnology at genetic, species and ecosystem levels separately.
2. Reliance on a few successful crop varieties can narrow genetic variation and increase vulnerability to disease or environmental change.
3. Conservation genetics can identify at-risk populations, guide breeding and support threatened species.
4. A sound judgement names the level affected and weighs benefits against risks and context.

## 4. Key terms

- Biodiversity: “Variety of life at genetic, species and ecosystem levels.”
- Genetic diversity: “Variation in alleles within a population or species.”
- Species diversity: “Variety of species present in a habitat or ecosystem.”
- Ecosystem diversity: “Variety of ecosystems, communities and ecological interactions.”
- Monoculture: “Large-scale growth of genetically similar or identical crop types.”
- Conservation genetics: “Use of genetic information or technologies to help manage and conserve populations.”

## 5. Worked examples

- The seven-mark evaluation compares crop uniformity, herbicide-tolerant and insect-resistant crops, land sparing and conservation gene banks, then makes a judgement that depends on the farming baseline.
- The six-mark analysis traces two routes by which a modified crop could reduce genetic diversity in a compatible wild relative: introgression with selection at linked loci, and repeated crossing that swamps a small wild population with crop alleles.

## 6. Misconceptions the page addresses

- Biodiversity means only species count. The page uses genetic, species and ecosystem levels.
- Improved crop productivity must increase biodiversity. Production and diversity can move in different directions.
- Biotechnology always helps or always harms biodiversity. Effects depend on the technique and context.
