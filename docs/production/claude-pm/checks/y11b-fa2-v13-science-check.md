Direct experiments in [Wind speed affects the rate and kinetics of stomatal conductance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/tpj.17066) and [Low wind, high impact](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13529328/) support retaining the response rather than treating wind as an unconditional arrow. The [Bureau's reference evapotranspiration documentation](https://www.bom.gov.au/watl/eto/about.shtml) uses radiation, wind, temperature and humidity; it supports the weather connection but does not establish that evening watering is always best for every crop. The source says often, which is appropriate.
