1. **Should fix, 11a repeats v09's 12a (double practice, partly double teaching).** v09's 12a was "500 posters distributed" with feedback "count change, not activity"; v13 taught "visibility isn't impact"; v14 scene 7 teaches "doing things isn't impact" again and 11a is "60 sessions and 5,000 fact sheets", with the same answer. Recall v09 in one line at the start of scene 7 ("You met this with the poster campaign: counting what you did isn't counting what changed. Here's the full staircase.") and change 11a to a claim that sits higher on the staircase, so the new step is tested: "An organisation reports that visits to its youth service rose by a third after its campaign. Explain why this is stronger evidence than a count of sessions, and outline what it still cannot show." Feedback: it is a behaviour change, two steps above activity and output; it cannot show the health outcome or attribution (no comparison group; other changes at the same time); crossed mistake "service visits up, so health improved". Students then use the top of the staircase and the attribution idea, which are new in this video.
