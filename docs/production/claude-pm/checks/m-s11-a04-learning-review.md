## Should fix

**SF1. Scene 6 packs three new ideas into one scene (1,065 characters, the longest in the strand) and its rounding sentence will confuse.** It introduces significant figures, rounds a large number, reads the calculator's E display, rounds a small number and states the leading-zeros rule. Two specific gaps:
- "To three significant figures, keep the one, two and one, and round. That's one point two two": a student hears "keep 1, 2, 1" and then sees 1.22. The visual lights the 9 as "round up", but the narration never says why the 1 became 2. Say: "Keep the first three digits, one, two, one. The next digit is a nine, so the last one rounds up to two."
- The slip example 0.000 304 6 has a zero between digits that does count, but the rule is only stated as "counted from the first digit that isn't zero". A student will ask why that zero counts. Add one sentence: "Zeros before the first digit just hold the place, so they don't count. A zero between digits does."
To make room, move the E-display beat ("A calculator shows three point one two five E minus ten") to its own short stage after the rounding, or use the reserve trim "Nobody needs all eight digits." The video has 25 s spare.
