Distance/time is a valid within-capillary uptake-rate proxy when bore stays constant. Volume rate=pi*r^2*distance/time; do not compare raw mm/min between different bores or plant sizes. ## Findings

### 1. blocker: 3 to 6 method

Text: "filled with clear water"

A sealed, water-filled setup needs a controlled introduction of the measuring bubble at the capillary, not an air break in the stem. This is absent from the instructions.

Repair: Add and show the bubble-introduction step in learning finding 1, with the capillary returned to water.

### 2. should fix: 10 screen and narration

Text: "a peel can't tell"

An epidermal imprint can be used to inspect stomatal morphology and sometimes aperture. Drying or solvent effects may alter it; that does not justify saying no imprint can show opening. A stomatal count alone is insufficient to infer flux.

Repair: Say "a count alone does not measure water-loss rate" and remove the false open/closed prohibition.

### 3. should fix: 10 counts

Text: "lower about 20, upper about 3"

Counts compare surfaces only for equal sampled area; one field each lacks replication.

Repair: Specify same magnification and field area, multiple random non-overlapping fields and multiple leaves. Keep 20/3 as example counts.

### 4. should fix: 11

Text: "Only the air movement changed"

Fixed treatment order and uncontrolled drift are not eliminated by using one shoot in one room.

Repair: Use the alternating-order/monitoring repair; describe validity as supported by controls, not proved.

Counts: **1 blocker; 3 should fix; 0 optional**. Findings repeated between the two reviews describe the same issues and must not be added as separate defects.
