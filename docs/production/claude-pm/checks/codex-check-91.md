## chem-m6-c13

1. **Scene 11 Visual, strong acid with strong base:** The methyl orange band is shown wholly inside the labelled one-drop jump of about pH 4 to 10. Its stated transition starts at pH 3.1, outside that bracket. In this 0.100 mol/L HCl and NaOH system, pH 3.1 occurs at about 24.61 mL and pH 4.4 at about 24.98 mL, with equivalence at 25.00 mL. **Correct visual text:** "Methyl orange starts changing before the one-drop bracket and finishes near equivalence. Bromothymol blue and phenolphthalein change within the bracket." The spoken conclusion that all three *finish* within about a drop can remain. [University of Wisconsin indicator ranges](https://www2.chem.wisc.edu/deptfiles/genchem/lab/labdocs/modules/titrate/titrqind.htm); calculation from the design's 25.00 mL of 0.100 mol/L HCl and 0.100 mol/L NaOH: `[H3O+] = 0.100(25.00 - V)/(25.00 + V)` with volumes in mL.

## bio-m6-b12

1. **Scenes 7 and 8, rhino species-diversity arrow and model sentence:** The northern white rhino is a *subspecies* of the white rhino, and the lesson defines species diversity as the variety of species in an area. Saving that subspecies preserves a distinct lineage but does not, by itself, add a species. Scene 7's species-level up arrow and scene 8's generic "Up at the species level" sentence teach the wrong taxonomic level for this case. **Correct narration and visual text:** "This could preserve the northern white rhino subspecies and some of its genetic diversity. It would add a species to a particular area only if white rhinos are absent from that baseline area." Keep the genetic bottleneck and ecosystem effects as separate judgements. [Ol Pejeta Conservancy, northern and southern white rhino classification](https://www.olpejetaconservancy.org/what-we-do/conservation/wildlife-conservation/rhinos/).

2. **Scene 4 Visual, QCAV-4 genetic row:** `+1 gene` implies that the approved banana carries only one inserted gene. The licence lists multiple copies of the resistance gene `MamRGA2` and an inserted `nptII` selectable-marker gene. **Correct visual text:** "one added resistance trait; crop still one clone". The narration's "one useful gene" can remain if it refers only to the resistance trait. [Australian Gene Technology Regulator, DIR 199 licence conditions](https://www.ogtr.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-02/dir199_licence_conditions.pdf).

## skills-s03

1. **Scenes 6 and 7, worked Biology model answer; Questions used and model answers, point 2:** The answer says both heterozygotes differ from their parents, "so neither allele is dominant." The observations show that neither allele *completely masks* the other. Incomplete dominance can still involve partial dominance. **Correct model sentence:** "Both heterozygotes look unlike either parent, so neither allele completely masks the other." Use the same wording in narration, answer row and the written model. [OpenStax Biology 2e, incomplete dominance and codominance](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/12-2-characteristics-and-traits).

## skills-s04

1. **Scene 15 decision rule, "Every time: verdict, criteria, evidence"; any matching on-screen chip:** This is presented for all four verbs, including *analyse*. NESA defines analyse as identifying components and relationships and drawing out implications. It does not require a verdict or criteria. The three-part structure also cannot be a universal marking rule for *justify*, whose demand is to support an argument or conclusion. **Correct narration and visual text:** "For assess or evaluate, make a judgement and support it with relevant criteria and evidence. For justify, support the argument or conclusion with reasons from the case. For analyse, show the parts, their relationships and what follows." Keep the mnemonic on judgement answers only. [NESA glossary of key words](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/hsc/student-guide/glossary).
