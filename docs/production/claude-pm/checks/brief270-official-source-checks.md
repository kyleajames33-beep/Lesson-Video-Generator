## Drugs in sport: m4-v22

Confirmed against WADA's *2026 Prohibited List*, effective 1 January 2026, read directly on 25 November 2026. This is the edition in force on the task date.

[Official WADA 2026 List](https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).

- S7, printed page 17: tramadol is among the named narcotics prohibited in competition. This does not mean every painkiller is prohibited.
- S9, printed page 19: all glucocorticoids are prohibited in competition when given by injection, orally or rectally. Cortisone is explicitly listed. The design's injectable cortisone example and exemption condition agree.

No source-check hold on v22. An injection outside competition still needs the relevant washout and exemption assessment. The release check must use the edition then in force.
