## Design correction 9 is not verified as a false statement

The design proposes correcting L19's sentence: "Transgenes have already introgressed into wild populations, with herbicide-tolerance genes found in feral canola in Canada and the United States."

Its reason says feral canola is "not a wild population". That is too categorical. Feral populations are escaped crop descendants living outside cultivation. [Schafer et al., 2011 original roadside-canola study](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0025736) found transgenic feral canola, including combined traits, and [the 2024 repeat survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11111013/) examined persistence over a decade. Finding a transgene in feral canola alone does not prove introgression into a taxonomically wild relative, but the HTML did not explicitly say "wild relative". Distinguish the meaning before logging a false claim.

An optional precision edit is "Transgenes have persisted and mixed in feral, escaped crop populations, including herbicide-tolerant canola along roadsides in North America." Do not add the proposed "some weedy relatives" clause without naming and citing its own actual hybrid/introgression study. This is a clarification proposal, excluded from the six confirmed rows. Root should remove or qualify the design's claim that the existing sentence was scientifically disproved.
