## Findings

1. **Should fix, 8a is scene 3's coffee group plus scene 5's early-stopping norm.** Scene 3: "A group that loves its coffee after training but shares no standard has fun, and doesn't improve"; scene 5: "if most people stop early, stopping early becomes normal". 8a's happy team that finishes early is that pair. Scene 6 (who a group leaves out) has no practice at all, so use 8a for it: "A free Saturday community walk has run for two years with a loyal group of retirees. Local teenagers never join. Explain this using belonging and group norms, and propose one change. (3 marks)" Model: belonging draws a boundary, and a group that all looks alike reads as "not for us"; its norms (pace, time, talk) suit the members it already has; a change such as a teenager-led start group or a second time, planned with teenagers. Crossed mistake: "teenagers just aren't motivated". Alternatively keep 8a and replace scene 3's coffee example with one that does not preview it.

2. **Note, the main question's "they don't enjoy" is never answered directly.** Scene 4 explains turning up without enjoyment, but no sentence says so. Add one clause in scene 4: "You don't have to enjoy a 6 am alarm when someone's waiting for you."

3. **Note, plates.** Both hook plates are not yet painted; the supply board's two columns carry the comparison if they are late.
