## Should fix

**T02-BORROW, scene 3, "If you do subtract, borrow an hour as sixty minutes, and eighty-three minus forty-seven gives the same thirty-six."** A student will ask where 83 came from. The narration never says that 1423 becomes 13 hours and 83 minutes (23 + 60). The grey row "borrow 60: 83 − 47 = 36; 13 − 10 = 3" shows the result, not the step. Fix: say "fourteen twenty-three is the same as thirteen hours and eighty-three minutes, because one hour is sixty minutes; eighty-three minus forty-seven is thirty-six, and thirteen minus ten is three", and add the row "1423 = 13 h 83 min" before the grey row. Otherwise cut the borrowing sentence and leave borrowing to the site; counting up is already the taught method.
