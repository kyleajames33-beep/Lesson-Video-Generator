## Verification and recalculation

The 2022 ABS National Health Survey figure 1.3 million (5.3%) refers to diabetes overall, not exclusively type 2. It is a self-reported survey estimate, distinct from linked registry counts. The design correctly avoids repeating it. Baker's AusDiab page confirms baseline testing of 11,247 adults aged 25+ in 1999-to-2000. AIHW's report says about half the diabetes in that baseline was undiagnosed. The historical date is shown in the design; it must not imply half of present cases remain undiagnosed. A precise 7.4 versus 7.5% baseline figure is unnecessary here and is not independently settled by the current page summary.

Compensatory hyperinsulinaemia can preserve glucose before beta-cell dysfunction and insulin resistance cause sustained hyperglycaemia. A fixed eight/fifteen-year drawing is illustrative, not an individual prognosis. Microvascular and cardiovascular complications are suitable high-level consequences, but nerve damage is not solely a tiny-vessel phenomenon. No treatment belongs here. Diet, adiposity, activity, age, genes, some medicines and food access affect risk; the design appropriately avoids blame and a genetic population stereotype.

NIH supports iron's role in haemoglobin, deficiency-related impaired oxygen carriage, vitamin C-dependent collagen synthesis/scurvy and vitamin D-dependent intestinal mineral absorption/rickets. Vitamin D enhances rather than creates all calcium absorption, and calcitriol is its active metabolite. Iodine deficiency can reduce thyroid hormone, raise TSH and promote goitre; not every goitre is iodine-deficiency disease. FSANZ confirms mandatory iodised salt for bread in 2009, with exceptions including organic bread and home bread mixes. The draft says bread broadly, not "every loaf", so the exceptions can remain an author note.

All numerical teaching rates are illustrative: 3, 7, 15, 30 cases per 1,000 adults per year. 30/1000=3% per year, leaving about 97% without an incident diagnosis in that year. Family-history rate ratios 7/3=2.33 and 30/15=2; obesity ratios 15/3=5 and 30/7=4.29. None identifies dietary intake or an individual's eventual lifetime outcome. The first attempt correctly traces deficient vitamin D, reduced calcium absorption and poor mineralisation in growing bone. Its correct explanatory scope does not make "drink more milk" a generally wrong clinical remedy; the crossed chip should mean an answer that fails to explain the asked mechanism.

The site's fixed Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander genetic-baseline claim is not repeated in any of B06/B07/B08 learner copy. Proposed log entries require owner cultural review and do not infer genetic causation from prevalence ratios.

## Findings

### 1. blocker: 6

Text: "Muscle, liver and fat cells ... fewer transporters move"

The reduced-GLUT4-translocation account is suitable for muscle/adipose tissue but not the liver, which relies on different glucose transport and regulation of glucose production.

Repair: Use the tissue-specific repair in learning finding 1. Show reduced muscle uptake and failure to suppress hepatic output as two contributions to high glucose.

### 2. should fix: 7 screen

Text: "diagnosis: fasting glucose 7.0 mmol/L or more"

The numerical threshold is right, but the screen presents a single result as a diagnosis without a clinical/repeat-testing condition. It is not needed to teach pathogenesis.

Repair: Remove this incidental diagnostic tag, or say "one diagnostic criterion: fasting plasma glucose >=7.0 mmol/L, normally confirmed by repeat testing". Prefer removing the tag and its competing reading load.

### 3. should fix: 9d

Text: "Excess intake raises the chance"

The supplied chart has no dietary-exposure measure; the stated conclusion exceeds its evidence.

Repair: Explicitly separate the obesity/family-history associations from an unmeasured intake claim, using learning finding 3.

### 4. should fix: 4 and memory line

Text: "Rickets needs daylight"

Vitamin-D deficiency rickets is not a requirement for daylight exposure; vitamin D from food or supplementation can prevent it. Calcium-deficiency rickets also exists.

Repair: Use a vitamin D anchor and keep this example explicitly about vitamin D deficiency. Do not turn it into UV-exposure advice.

Counts: **1 blocker; 3 should fix; 0 optional**. Findings repeated between the two reviews describe the same issues and must not be added as separate defects.
