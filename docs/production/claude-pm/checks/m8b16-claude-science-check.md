## Findings

### 1. Should fix: scene 9

The closing cause claim is wrong for cataract surgery: it removes the cloudy natural lens. Use learning finding 1 replacement. Cataract replacement can remain the only treatment here that removes the opacity itself.

[Primary/reference source](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts).

### 2. Should fix: scene 5 and technology comparison

"No lens in front of the eye can fix that" should distinguish removing opacity from improving early cataract vision. New glasses can improve vision in early cataract even though they do not remove it. Replace with "Glasses cannot clear a cloudy lens." This is shorter and makes the intended mechanism exact.

[Primary/reference source](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts).
