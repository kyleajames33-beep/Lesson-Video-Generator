## Confirmed
- **"Close to half" (scene 2, flagged).** Rhodes and de Bruijn (*Br J Health Psychol* 2013, 18:296 to 309; 10 studies, N = 3,899) report a physical activity intention-behaviour gap of 46 per cent of intenders. "In exercise studies, close to half ... don't follow through" is correct, and consistent with hms-y11-m1-v12's resolved wording.
- Outcome, performance and process goals and their control; SMART properties (3). Correct.
- Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer and Sheeran, *Adv Exp Soc Psychol* 2006, 38:69 to 119, medium to large effect); attaching a plan to an existing routine (4). Correct.
- Self-report over-estimates physical activity against records (Prince and others, *Int J Behav Nutr Phys Act* 2008); self-monitoring reactivity (5). Correct.
- Attributions (specific and temporary against personal and permanent) and persistence (5). Correct.
- Lapse and relapse planning; if-then coping plans (6). Correct.
- 9a/9b and 9c/9d: correct.
- Memory aids sound. No people; plates named correctly.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (facts section; affects the pre-voicing check only).** The facts section gives "about 48%". The paper's figure is 46 per cent. Change to "46% of intenders did not act". Optional narration precision: "close to half of the people who intend to exercise don't follow through" ("intend to start" describes the samples less exactly).
2. **Note (title and main question).** "Almost everyone intends to exercise more, so why do so few change" overstates against the video's own figure (roughly half of intenders do act). Optional: "so why do so many fail to change".

## Verdict
Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 1 should fix, 1 note. The flagged figure is confirmed.
