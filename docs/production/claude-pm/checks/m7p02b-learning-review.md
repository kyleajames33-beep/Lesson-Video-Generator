Main question traced: *Is the tank water safe, and is the creek full of disease? How do you design and run a safe, valid test that counts the microbes in a water or food sample, and what can the colonies tell you?* Useful outcome: the student can plan the test (variables, controls, three plates per dilution), explain each handling step by its reason, pick the countable plate, calculate CFU per mL from new data, word a conclusion that claims only culturable microbes, and write the key risk controls.

## Strengths to keep

- **The hook puzzle is the route's stop.** Three plates (3, 214, 0) and two tempting wrong claims, crossed on "Neither", then answered honestly in scene 10. The video makes students want the method.
- **Every handling step is given its cause** before the memory aid: label the base (lids get swapped), tape with two strips (shut but aerobic), invert (condensation), 25 to 30 °C (body temperature favours microbes adapted to us).
- **The plate row is a strong visual comparison.** Four aligned sealed plates from film to 14 colonies make the 30 to 300 window visible, with a reason at each end.
- **The calculation follows the house worked-example rule:** short task, grouped givens with units, equation on its own line, one stage at a time, result kept.
- **The two controls get distinct jobs**, and the crossfade from the clear plate to a contaminated control shows what a failed negative control means.
- **Safety content is real and specific** (risk group 1, AS/NZS 2243.3:2022, taped plates never opened, autoclave), in the format the owner accepted for bio-m6-p01.

## Plates: are all eleven new plates needed?

Most are. A plate count lesson needs painted plates, and coded colonies are not allowed. Assessed one by one:

- **Three sample bottles** (scenes 1, 3, 4, 14): yes. The violet IV labels need three matching bottles. `m8-river-sampling-bottle-v1.png` (inspected: a pole, cup and one bottle) is kept for the sampling step.
- **Dilution tube rack** (scene 5): yes. `m5x-sample-tube-rack-v1.png` is blood tubes, and the ladder needs a bottle plus four tubes.
- **Incubator** (scene 6): optional. The causes in scene 6 (condensation, temperature) are carried by the coded flip, condensation drops and dial. It earns its place mainly as context and for reuse in later microbiology practicals (p05). Cut second if needed, as the design says.
- **The aligned sealed-plate set (eight):**
  - `clear`, `few`, `creek`: yes (hook, scenes 7, 10, 12).
  - `lawn`, `crowded`, `countable`, `sparse`: yes (scene 8's window is the explanation; lawn also in scenes 5 and 13d).
  - `control-growth`: **cut.** Use `m7-plate-few-v1.png` rotated 180° (3 colonies) for the contaminated negative control. Scene 7 never shows the hook plates at the same time, and any growth makes the point. This is also the design's own first cut.

Result: ten new plates, nine if the incubator is also cut. One brief note for Codex: a painter will not reliably produce exactly 156 or 214 colonies. The chips carry the numbers and the tally overlay marks only a few colonies, so ask for "about 150 to 160" and "about 200 to 220" with no merged colonies, instead of "exactly 156 if possible".
