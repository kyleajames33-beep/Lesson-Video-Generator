## Findings

1. **Blocker, scene 2 and key note 1 teach an "anaerobic threshold" at 85 to 90 per cent beside Year 11's lactate threshold curve at a different number.** Root's Module 2 ruling 3 says "lactate threshold" only, never "anaerobic threshold", with numbers given as a share of maximum aerobic capacity; hms-y11-m2-v14 now teaches the lactate threshold at about 50 to 60 per cent of maximum aerobic capacity untrained and 70 to 80 per cent trained. Scene 2 draws "anaerobic threshold ~85 to 90%" (of maximum heart rate) and aligns Year 11's curve's bend with that line. A student who did Year 11 sees the same bend labelled 75 per cent last year and 85 to 90 per cent now, under a new name, and will write that there are two thresholds or that one of the numbers is wrong. Key note 1 makes the copy permanent. The site uses "anaerobic threshold", so students will meet the word. Fix: name it "**lactate threshold**" throughout (scene 2, scene 3, scene 5, 8b and the key note), with one silent grey chip "also called the anaerobic threshold" on its line. Say the unit every time: "around eighty-five to ninety per cent of maximum heart rate in trained athletes. That's the same point as Year 11's seventy to eighty per cent of maximum aerobic capacity, just measured by heart rate." Label the band's axis "% of maximum heart rate" and put a small second scale "% of maximum aerobic capacity" beside the threshold line (75). Key note 1: "Aerobic zone: ~70% to ~85 to 90% of max HR (the lactate threshold)." Students should know that one threshold is being described in two units. v06 and v08 inherit the term (see their reviews).

2. **Should fix, both 8a halves and 8c are answered by scene 5.** Scene 5's rows give "a two-thousand-metre rower ... aerobic intervals near it, limited by their cost" ("1 to 2 a week") and "a soccer midfielder ... fartlek, plus intervals ... hard to measure across a squad". 8a asks for a 2000 m rower and a soccer squad, and its feedback gives aerobic intervals with "one or two a week" and fartlek with "the load is hard to record": the same methods and the same trade-offs. 8c (the forward's aerobic base) is answered by scene 5's last four sentences and its PCr rows. Fix: replace the body's rower and midfielder rows with a 1500 m swimmer (aerobic intervals) and a hockey midfielder (fartlek), or keep the body and set new 8a cases, for example "A: an AFL umpire who runs about 12 km a game at changing speeds and tires in the last quarter; B: a 50-year-old whose 30-minute easy jog has felt the same for a year". For 8c, keep WE b2's question but label it "from the lesson" (root's ruling 7 for site questions the body teaches), or turn it into a prediction: "A coach runs SIT for forwards with a poor aerobic base, 6 s sprints and only 30 s rest. Predict what happens to sprint power across the set, and explain." Students should apply the routine to a case they have not watched.

3. **Should fix, aerobic intervals and v03's HIIT overlap with no line between them.** Scene 3 defines aerobic intervals as "five four-minute efforts near the threshold" with 2 to 3 min recovery; v03 taught HIIT as 30 s to 4 min efforts at 1:1 to 1:2. A student cannot tell them apart. Fix: one clause in scene 3, "Unlike HIIT, the efforts stay just below the threshold, so they're longer and the rests are shorter than the work", and a thin orange line on the band showing HIIT above the threshold. With v03's 8a repaired (see the v03 review), the two methods have distinct ratios and intensities.

4. **Should fix, the aerobic floor disagrees with Year 11.** Scene 2 sets the aerobic training threshold at about 70 per cent of maximum heart rate, and v06 8c calls 60 per cent "below the aerobic threshold". hms-y11-m2-v12 (Revision 2) says "the aerobic training zone, sixty to eighty-five per cent", while v13 uses 70 to 85. Root should pick one floor for the course; 70 to 85 per cent matches Year 11 v13, this video and the site. The fix is in hms-y11-m2-v12 (scene 4 and 9b), not here; this video already says "rules of thumb".

5. **Should fix, narration speaks an internal video number.** Scene 2 "That's v-three's anaerobic work" and scene 5 "Use v-three's routine" name a file id that students never see. The course brief says "earlier", never module numbers. Say "That's the anaerobic work from last time" and "Use the routine from last time".

6. **Note, the hook asks for a prediction, two reasons and a change with a one-second hold.** Add 2 s with the slots lit.
