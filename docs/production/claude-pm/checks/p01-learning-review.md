## Designer's open questions (decisions)

1. **Temperature system swap (cobalt solution instead of the heated iron tube 6): accepted.** It is the syllabus example in its usual school form, its colour change is large in both directions (the iron thiocyanate change on heating is small and easily confused with dilution or evaporation), C3 already taught its direction so nothing is re-taught, and the return to purple doubles as a second reversibility check. The iron tube 6 stays as an unspoken screen alternative. Recorded in the design's Open decisions.
2. **Iron reversal step (site step 5): not added.** Agreed with the designer; the faint Fe(OH)3 makes "red returns" a poor comparison, and two clean reversibility checks already exist.
3. **Cobalt chloride school category:** unconfirmed (science check); learner copy is true whatever the category. Owner action, not a recording blocker.
