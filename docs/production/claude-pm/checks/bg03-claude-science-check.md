## Summary

- Kuchenbaecker et al. 2017 figures are confirmed: 9,856 female carriers (6,036 BRCA1, 3,820 BRCA2); cumulative breast cancer risk to age 80 of 72% (95% CI 65 to 79) for BRCA1 and 69% (61 to 77) for BRCA2. But the 72% is an estimate from a prospective analysis of 3,886 women unaffected at baseline (2,276 BRCA1), not an observed count, so "had developed" needs changing.
- "About 5 in 100" breast cancers carrying a BRCA variant is too high for population-based data (about 2 in 100).
- Hardy-Weinberg arithmetic is correct for both examples.
- The Iceland ruling sentence is accurate; no deCODE numbers appear in learner copy (correct, given site error B2).
- Scene 6 has a logic error (deviation measured against a prediction made from the same data) and an impossible simulation setting.
