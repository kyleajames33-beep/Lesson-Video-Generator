## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 6 re-teaches Year 11's participation idea without recalling it, and the promised Year 11 recall is missing from the narration.** The design says localisation and the Ottawa action areas are "recalled in one sentence in 2", but scene 2's narration never mentions Year 11. Scene 6 then teaches consultation against community-led with a new two-card contrast, and 8c's classic mistake is "a survey = community-led". Year 11 taught this with the participation ladder (informed, consulted, partner, lead; reused in m1-v14, v16 and v18), and m1-v18's checklist line was "If young people were surveyed but had no vote, that's consultation." Fix: in scene 2 add the recall the design promises: "In Year 11 you localised the goals: start from a local need, and act across the Ottawa action areas. Here's a community that did it for decades." (about 5 s). In scene 6, replace the two-card contrast with Year 11's participation ladder, small, grey recall tag "earlier", with "consulted" and "lead" lit, and say "Remember the ladder: being consulted isn't leading." (about 3 s; take it from the five mechanism cards by trimming "It reaches households an advertisement can't."). Students who did Year 11 see the ladder they know, and 8c's mistake then points at a rung.

2. **Note, a fourth method list in three videos.** Students now meet v20's five-step method, v21's seven features, v21's five transfer steps and v21's five community-led mechanisms, plus Year 11's six-step localisation method and strategy checklist. Each is reasoned, but students may not see that the seven features and the method overlap (community sets the priority, across sectors, measure by group). In scene 3, tag the three features that repeat v20's method steps with a thin link and a grey "same as the method" tag, so the seven features read as the method plus four new checks. See the module summary.

3. **Note (science check), the syllabus example's current status.** The design flags this. If the organisation has closed, say so in one clause and use it: "and when its long-term funding ended, so did much of the work", which is feature 6 and the funding barrier in action. That is honest and makes the scene 3 point concrete.

4. **Note, 8b at about 30 s.** At the limit. Trim "and volunteers to supervise" to "and volunteers".
