## Findings

1. **Should fix, 8a demand and marking disagree.** The card asks for "at least TWO of direction, shape, distribution and cause" for 3 marks, but the marking gives one mark each to direction or shape, distribution and cause and says "any two of the three earn 2". A student who follows the card exactly and uses two words well can lose a mark the card never asked for. Change the demand to "using direction or shape, distribution, and cause" (card and narration), or change the marking so two well-linked words can earn 3. Students should be able to see what full marks need.

2. **Should fix, scene 4 carries two ideas and about ten new items.** One scene defines life expectancy, places two dated figures and two brackets, writes three "what it hides" tags, introduces the `HealthyYearsBar` (the module's recurring model) and then lays five plum tags for the sex gap (773 characters, the longest scene). Split at "And the sex gap?" into 4a and 4b: in 4b the three hide tags and the bar go grey and small, the gap brackets enlarge, and the five tags write around them. No narration change is needed. Students then meet the recurring model with its own moment, and the sex-gap argument has the screen to itself.

3. **Note (science check), "Many of those extra years are lived with illness."** As far as this reviewer recalls, AIHW health-adjusted life expectancy reporting puts most recent gains in full health, with a modest rise in years lived with illness, so "many" may overstate; the science check should confirm against the current burden of disease release. Safer: "Some of those extra years are lived with illness or disability." The bar's lengthening grey part should stay small to match.

4. **Note, "the population sits in between."** The syllabus asks for the general population, and key note 4 gives only the female lead. This is fine, since ABS publishes by sex; one grey tag on the ink line, "general population: between the two", would make the third figure explicit in the notes.

5. **Note, 8b cause names policies the body never taught.** "Taxes, plain packaging and smoke-free laws" are a fair example, but a student who wrote "norms and stress" (scene 5's causes) should hear that it also earns the mark. Add "or norms and stress in some groups" (about 2 s) or keep as is if timing is tight.
