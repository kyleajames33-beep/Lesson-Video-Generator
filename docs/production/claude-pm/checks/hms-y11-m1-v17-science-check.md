# hms-y11-m1-v17 science check (the Sustainable Development Goals)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- SDG facts (scene 2, key note 1): adopted 25 September 2015 by all 193 UN member states; 17 goals, 169 targets, for 2030; replaced the 8 Millennium Development Goals (2000 to 2015); universal; "leave no one behind". Correct (UN, *Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development*, A/RES/70/1).
- SDG 3 title and targets (scene 3): 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4 (one third reduction in premature NCD mortality; promote mental health), 3.6 (halve road traffic deaths; original date 2020, carried into the Second Decade of Action for Road Safety 2021 to 2030, UN A/RES/74/299), 3.8 (UHC). No date spoken; correct.
- Diarrhoea still kills young children (scene 4): diarrhoeal disease remains a leading cause of death in children under five (WHO fact sheet, *Diarrhoeal disease*). Correct.
- Girls' education mechanism (scene 5): Gakidou and others 2010 (*The Lancet* 376:959 to 974) attributed about half of the fall in child mortality from 1970 to 2009 to increased education of women of reproductive age. The mechanism steps are supported.
- WHO uses (scene 6): custodian of many SDG 3 indicators, *World Health Statistics* annually; UHC (target 3.8); health in all policies (Adelaide Statement 2010, Helsinki Statement 2013); not legally binding. Correct.
- Australia (scene 7): first Voluntary National Review presented July 2018 (DFAT, https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/sdg-voluntary-national-review.pdf); a national SDG data reporting platform exists; aid framed by the goals; Closing the Gap targets align with several goals (unnamed). Correct. No second Australian VNR could be confirmed (HLPF 2026 searched); the narration does not count reviews, so it stays true.
- Practice 10a/10b (goal 11) and 10c/10d: correct; the model accepts any developed mechanism.
- Cultural content: general (a national gap, unnamed agreement). Compliant.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 2 narration and key note 2).** "They added the environment and inequality." The MDGs already included environmental sustainability (MDG 7). Inequality (SDG 10) was new; the environment was greatly expanded (goals 6, 12, 13, 14, 15). Corrected narration: "They gave the environment far more weight, and added inequality." Key note 2: "Changes from the MDGs: universal, integrated, more environment, inequality, no one behind."
2. **Should fix (scene 4).** "Clean water: diarrhoea still kills young children, and it's fixed by water, not clinics." Clinics also prevent and treat it (oral rehydration, zinc, rotavirus vaccine). Corrected: "Clean water: diarrhoea still kills young children, and much of it is prevented by safe water and sanitation, not by clinics alone."
3. **Should fix (scene 5).** "together they tend to save more children than the same money spent on clinics alone." No cost-comparison study supports this general claim; the evidence is about education's large share of the historical decline. Corrected: "Each step is modest, but worldwide, more education for women helps explain about half the fall in child deaths since 1970. So spending across sectors can save more children than clinics alone." If root prefers no figure: "together they can save more children than the same money spent on clinics alone."
4. **Should fix, pre-voicing gate (scene 8).** "Independent reviews have criticised Australia on inequality, climate and Indigenous health outcomes." Confirmed sources: Monash Sustainable Development Institute, *Transforming Australia: SDG Progress Report, 2020 Update* (on track for 12 of 56 indicators; most off track on climate action; rising wealth inequality; https://www.monash.edu/msdi/about/transforming-australia-2020) and the SDSN *Sustainable Development Report* dashboards (Australia among the lowest-scoring countries on SDG 13, for example the 2024 edition). Neither was confirmed to assess Indigenous health outcomes. The Productivity Commission's Closing the Gap annual data compilation reports several targets not on track, which supports that clause but is a statutory government body, not an independent review. Corrected: "Independent reviews have found Australia off track on climate and inequality, and national reports show several health targets for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people are not on track." Name the Monash and SDSN reports in the design's facts section.
5. **Note (scene 2).** "all one hundred and ninety-three United Nations member states adopted": correct.

## Verdict
Pass with 4 should fix (finding 4 must be resolved before voicing, as the design already requires). 0 blockers, 4 should fix, 1 note.
