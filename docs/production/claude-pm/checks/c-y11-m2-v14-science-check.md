The equation Na2CO3+2HCl -> 2NaCl+H2O+CO2 balances. A 20.00 mL aliquot contains 0.002006 mol carbonate and reacts with 0.004012 mol HCl. The three titres average 18.80 mL and give 0.2134 mol/L; a 1:1 slip gives 0.1067. The source spread of 0.10 mL meets its inclusive concordance rule. The rough titre is a guide and is excluded for that reason, not because it is universally the largest. NaOH/H2SO4 practice gives 0.08000 mol/L acid, not 0.1600. Water in the pipette gives a smaller actual aliquot and an overestimate of original acid concentration; water in the burette does the opposite. The methyl-orange endpoint refers to the complete carbonate neutralisation, so 1:2 is appropriate.
