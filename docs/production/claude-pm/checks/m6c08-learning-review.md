## Main question and the useful outcome

Main question (as stated): "A pH meter reads 2.85 in a propanoic acid solution, but treating it like hydrochloric acid predicts 0.82. Why does that method fail for a weak acid, and how do Ka and an ICE table give the right hydronium concentration and pH, or the hydroxide and pH for a weak base?"

One main question: yes. The hook's mismatch carries scenes 1 to 5, and its "fewer than one molecule in a hundred" result closes the loop. The weak base (7, 8) is the same method applied to a second case the dotpoint names ("for a range of solutions"), kept to two scenes as the project manager decided. Useful outcome: a student can set up an ICE table for a weak acid or base, write Ka = x²/(c − x), drop x when it is small, find x, show the 5% check, give all three equilibrium concentrations, and convert x to pH, through pOH for a base, choosing the weak method only after checking strength.

## Findings

### S1. Should fix: give the decimal-places reason in terms of x, matching C07's rule

- Exact text (scene 5): "The pH is **two point eight five**, two decimal places because K a has two significant figures."
- Student question: "C07 said significant figures in the concentration. 0.150 has three. Why not 2.855?"
- Replacement: "The pH is **two point eight five**: x has only two significant figures, because K a does, so two decimal places. That matches the **meter**." With C07's Revision 2 rule card reading "significant figures in [H₃O⁺]", the two videos now state one rule. Screen: a small grey tag "x: 2 s.f. (from Ka) → 2 d.p." on the answer chip; key note 5 adds "(2 d.p., because x has 2 s.f.)".
- Understanding enabled: students apply one precision rule to strong and weak cases.

### S2. Should fix: strong-method values must follow the strict rule (project manager decision 2)

- Exact text: scene 1 "so the pH is **zero point eight two**" and "pH = −log(0.150) = 0.82"; main question "predicts 0.82"; scene 5 crossed "0.82" chip; feedback 1 "gives zero point seven zero" and chip "pH 0.70"; feedback 2 chip "pH 13.60".
- Problem: 0.150, 0.200 and 0.400 mol/L have three significant figures, so the strong-method values are 0.824, 0.699 and 13.602.
- Replacement: "zero point eight two four" (hook), 0.824 on screen, in the main question, the cause chain and the YouTube description; feedback 1 "Treating the acid as **strong** gives zero point six nine nine." with chip "pH 0.699"; feedback 2 chip "pH 13.602".

### S3. Should fix: state the A1 shortcut tolerance in feedback (project manager decision 6)

- Exact text (scene 12): "The pH is **two point two two**. Treating it as strong gives zero point seven zero, as if every molecule ionised."
- Student question: "I solved the full equation and got 2.23. Am I wrong?"
- Decision: keep 0.200 mol/L. Switching to 0.400 mol/L does not remove the problem: the shortcut gives 2.07 and the full equation 2.08 (science check S2). Replacement: "The pH is **two point two two**. The full equation gives two point two three, only **point zero one** away. Treating the acid as **strong** gives zero point six nine nine." Screen tag "full equation: 2.23 (shortcut within 0.01)". Pay for it by opening "Methanoic acid is weak, so drop the x: x squared is ..." and cutting "as if every molecule ionised". No marking claim.
- Understanding enabled: a student who solved exactly sees the same answer within the data's precision; feedback stays under 30 s.

### S4. Should fix: more than about three bright text items in scenes 4, 5 and 7

- Scene 4: dim the givens card and the strip on "Step 1 of 2" (the table's column heads keep the species); remove the unspoken "Ka has 2 s.f." tag (scene 5 now says it).
- Scene 5: replace the returned meter plate with a small amber "2.85 ✓" chip on "meter"; the E row dims to its x cells when the pH line writes; the pH line dims on "one molecule in a hundred". Bright at the end: the answer chip, the lens thumbnail and the crossed chip.
- Scene 7: the plate and the givens card dim once the table is filled.
- Understanding enabled: during each step the student sees the current line, the needed table cells and one result.

### M1. Minor: show the Kb expression before substituting (scene 7)

- Replacement (screen only): "Kb = [NH₄⁺][OH⁻] / [NH₃] = x² / (0.200 − x)".

### M2. Minor: state symbols in key notes 1 and 4

- Replacement: "HA(aq) + H₂O(l) ⇌ H₃O⁺(aq) + A⁻(aq)" and "B(aq) + H₂O(l) ⇌ BH⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq)".

### M3. Minor: scene 6 HF card

- Repair: the HF card dims to grey once the ruler crosses 5%.
