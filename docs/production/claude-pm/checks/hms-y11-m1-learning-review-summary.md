## The blocker

**v09 12a (the poster proposal).** The demand asks "which of the five tests this proposal fails" and the feedback accepts only evaluation, but by the video's own tests the proposal also fails "a cause, not a symptom" and probably "an empty level" and participation. A student who applies the tests correctly is told they were wrong. Fix: "Identify the test that its measure of success fails", plus one feedback clause acknowledging the missing cause.

## Module-level findings

1. **"Unseen" practice items that repeat the taught example (should fix, seven designs).** The designs claim unseen items, but attempt 1 reuses the worked case almost verbatim in v05 (the vegetables campaign), v06 (peer approval), v07 (interviewers wording questions differently), v11 (scrolling as avoidance), v12 (sleep, "no discipline", the charger) and v17 (SDG 8, named with its mechanism in scene 4); v14's 11a repeats v09's 12a (activity counted as success). Students can recite instead of apply. Each review gives a replacement case that tests the same idea.

2. **Judging a strategy is taught four times with four different lists (should fix, v09, v14, v18).** v09: three evaluation questions and five tests; v14: five questions plus the impact staircase; v18: six steps, five questions and a sixth. "Activity is not impact" is taught in v09 (12b), v13 ("visibility isn't impact"), v14 (scene 7) and v18. Each later list should be shown as the earlier one plus what is new (grey tag "from the sleep proposal" on the recalled lines, amber on the new ones, one spoken sentence), and v14 should recall v09 in one line before the staircase. Students should leave the module with one checklist that grew.

3. **Recurring models are mostly consistent; four drifts to fix.**
   - **Bathtub** (v03): correct and exact in v03; v06 relabels its tap "diagnoses", which conflates incidence with recorded diagnoses. Use v03's "presents, diagnosed, recorded" strip there (should fix in v06).
   - **Continuum** (v02): v11 redraws it as "optimal" to "poor"; keep v02's labels (should fix in v11).
   - **Participation ladder** (v13, v16, v18; v04's participation and v14's community control feed it): the top rung is "lead" in v13, "control" in v14's and v16's narration and "lead (control)" on v16's screen. Use "lead (control)" from v13 onward (notes in v13 and v16).
   - **Impact staircase** (v14): v18 calls hours of use "an outcome", the staircase's top step; say "a change, not an activity" (should fix in v18).
   - Consistent as designed: **access gates** (v04, small in v14 and v16), **determinants chain** (fills v04's empty boxes in v05; reused in v08, v13, v17, where the SDGs are laid against it), **research cycle** (v07, next-question station in v09), **risk and protective balance** (v08, its trusted-adult weight reused in v10 and v11), **Ottawa grid** (v16, reused in v18 and tied back to v15's bands), **SDG ring** (v16 handoff, v17, v18), the **campaign model** (v05; reused in v08, v09, v10, v14, v15, where scene 5 names it as the module's repeated pattern).

4. **Terms that change meaning or appear before they are taught (should fix).** "Biomedical" is a meaning of health in v01 and an approach in v15 with no bridge; "dynamic" is defined only in v02's feedback; "semi-structured interviews" appears only in v07's feedback; "leverage" in v08 is unglossed but becomes a v09 ground. Each review gives a one-sentence fix.

5. **Contradictions between videos (should fix).** v13 scene 2 says "every other strategy so far works on what a person does", which v05 and v09's condition-level strategies contradict. v04 scene 5 says the financial barrier is "handled" while its own visual reopens lost wages.

6. **Feedback length.** Most feedback is 24 to 30 s by the authors' counts. Over 30 s: v06 10d (34), v04 10d (33), v01 11d (32), v05 10d (32), v07 10d (32), and seven at about 31 s (v02 11d, v03 11b, v11 9d, v12 10d, v13 10d, v17 10d, v18 9d). Should fix for the 32 to 34 s items; trims for the rest are in each review. Exact audio decides.

7. **Hooks that ask students to write something.** v09, v12 and v15 return to the student's card well; v03 ($100 million), v08 (three threats), v10 (the kept change, visual only) and v14 (organisations used) never answer their own question. v03's is should fix (the measures are the answer to its decision); the others are notes. v09 and v12's callbacks need a few seconds of quiet to be usable.

8. **Does the sequence build?** Yes. The order runs from meanings (v01) to change (v02), to population measures (v03), to fairness (v04), causes (v05), one group (v06 to v08), method (v07, v09), personal skills (v10 to v12), changing conditions (v13, v14), health promotion (v15, v16) and global goals applied locally (v17, v18). Each opening reproduces the previous handoff frame exactly (all seventeen joins match; v18 to hms-y11-m2-v01 matches the m2 design's recorded opening). Recalls are one line each and earlier models return small rather than being re-taught, with the exceptions in findings 2 and 3.

9. **Double teaching.** Apart from finding 2, the main overlap is Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander causes (v05 scene 7, v08 scene 8) and community-controlled services (v04, v08, v13, v14, v16). Each instance serves its own dotpoint and stays general per the owner's cultural ruling; v08 and v14 could say "as we saw earlier" before restating causes. Note only.

## Checks across all eighteen

- **Causes before reliance:** met; every scene names what acts and why the change follows, with the term-level exceptions in finding 4.
- **Attention and the visual medium:** every video has a moving model that carries its main relationship (ladder and trade line, loop and continuum, bathtub, gates on a clinic, chain, task spine, cycle and target, burden bar and balance, issue frame and sleep window, sockets, coping curves, agency columns, ask light and ladder, interlocking sectors and staircase, nested bands, Ottawa grid, SDG ring, council map). No static-slideshow risk in the written plans; motion still needs judging at playback speed.
- **Response opportunity:** all 36 cards keep the full demand on screen through the 10 s countdown and after; all end "Pause here to answer."; no picture slot shows an answer.
- **Feedback shape:** every feedback gives the answer, one or two reasons and exactly one crossed classic mistake; none re-teaches the lesson.
- **Reading load:** at most three bright items per frame by design; densest scenes flagged in v03 (3), v06 (6), v07 (5), v11, v15 (8), v18 (2).
- **People, culture, statistics:** nobody is drawn; cases use objects, places, documents and quote cards. Cultural content is general throughout. Dated figures (v05, v08, v09, v15) carry release re-check flags; v17's "independent reviews" claim needs a source.
- **Style and length:** 0 em dashes in all eighteen files (en dashes appear only in v09 and v15 source notes and v15's dated figure). Narration 5,601 to 6,193 characters by this reviewer's count; the authors' totals with holds run 520 to 569 s, so v01 (569) and v09 (566) have no headroom and any added speech needs a reserve cut.
