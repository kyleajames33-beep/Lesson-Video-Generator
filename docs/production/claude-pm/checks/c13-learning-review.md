## Main question and useful outcome

*When you mix two clear solutions of ionic compounds, how can you tell whether a solid will form, what it is, and what's left dissolved?* Afterwards a student should be able to swap partners and check both products, name the precipitate (and its colour for the three syllabus pairs), write molecular, full ionic and net ionic equations with spectators struck and charges checked, say "no precipitate" with no net equation, and list what is in a filtrate, including an excess ion.

## Trace from entry knowledge

- **Why a solid can appear (1 to 3).** C11's hydrated ions are recalled in one clause; "four kinds of ions, only new partners" is the right causal step; spectators and the composition card answer the hook's second question. Followable.
- **Rules (4, 5).** Rules come from experiment (a forward pointer to P03), then NAGSAG one row per cue. Heavy but well paced (see N2).
- **Method and three pairs (6 to 11).** One equation line per scene; the solid is never split; the PbI2 charge reason is given where it is needed; BaSO4 gives a real consequence (barium meal). Good worked-example practice.
- **No precipitate and the filtrate (12, 13).** Both are taught before attempt 2 applies them.
- **Attempts.** A1 (CuSO4 + NaOH) needs the hydroxide rule and the 2+ charge; A2 (excess chloride) reverses the scene 13 case. All demands and the rules card stay visible; nothing answer-bearing.

## Designer's open question, decided

- **Attempt 3 with two accepted answers:** both answers are correct (sodium sulfate: white BaSO4 with barium only; sodium hydroxide: white Mg(OH)2 with magnesium only, Ba(OH)2 soluble; science check confirms both at school concentrations), and a two-answer feedback is fine in principle. Decision: cut from this video for length (L1). Telling ions apart by an added test is also Module 8 territory (sequential ion tests). The item and its feedback are kept in the design as a reserve for P03 or a later practice set.
- **Rules card reading load:** keep, preview check (N2).
- **P03 dependency:** keep the forward pointer.

## Retained successful moves

The merged particle lens where only the insoluble pair locks and sinks; the token flight with the solid dropping unsplit; spectators struck in grey on both sides; the charge tally under every net equation; "no precipitate", not "no reaction"; the filtrate lens with leftover ions tagged "excess".

## Verdict

Pass for recording after Revision 2, provided the owner accepts about 10 minutes at the measured rate (reserve cut named). Repeat on the exact voiced preview, with special attention to the scene 5 card and the BaSO4 interim plate.
