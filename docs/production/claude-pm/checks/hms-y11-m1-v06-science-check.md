Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- Developmental tasks (scene 2) and emerging adulthood (Arnett, 2000): correct.
- "The brain's reward system tends to mature earlier than its impulse control" (dual-systems or maturational-imbalance model; Steinberg 2008; Casey and others 2008): fair with "tends to". The model is debated in detail, but the wording does not overclaim.
- Injury as a leading cause of harm for young people (scene 3): AIHW reports injury (including transport and intentional self-harm) as the leading cause of death for 15 to 24 year olds. Correct.
- 10b trend directions: daily smoking among young Australians has fallen (AIHW, National Drug Strategy Household Survey 2022 to 2023); drink driving among young people has fallen in NDSHS trends. Online risks are new. Correct, no figures spoken.
- Smoking less normal and mental health more discussed (scene 6): correct.
- Scene 7 measurement caution: correct.
- Practice 10a/10b and 10c/10d: correct; no reasonable alternative crossed.
