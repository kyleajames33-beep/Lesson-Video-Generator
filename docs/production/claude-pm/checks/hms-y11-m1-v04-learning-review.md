## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 5 (the financial gate is "handled", then reopened).** The narration says "It's free, so the financial barrier is handled", but the visual then turns the "lost wages" sub-chip amber again on "open while most people are at work", and the readout says "5 of 6 untouched". Students see the financial gate both ticked and reopened and will ask which it is. Say "Free handles the fee, but not the lost wages of coming during work hours." and draw the financial gate half green, half amber; keep the count as "barriers left: 5 of 6, and the financial one only half fixed". Students should see that scene 4's point (financial is more than the fee) applies to this very clinic.

2. **Should fix, scene 1 asks "Who won't use it?" and the three amber cards are never filled.** Scene 5 names barriers but never the people they stop. Fill the three hook cards in scene 5 as each gate closes (visual only, 30 px): "shift and office workers" (hours), "people from outlying towns" (geographic), "people who don't read English" (language). Students should be able to answer the hook's first question in terms of groups, which is what 10a and the handoff to v05 need.

3. **Should fix, 10d feedback length (about 33 s).** Cut "Use a case, like the two schools, where the same grant widens the gap." (step 3 can write silently with its picture). About 5 s shorter, under the owner's 30 s.

4. **Note, scene 6 claim.** "That's why community-controlled health services ... work so well" is a strong causal claim; leave it to the science check, and keep it general per the cultural ruling.
