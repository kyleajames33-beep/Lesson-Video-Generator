## Findings

**F1 (should fix). Scene 8 narration.**
Quoted: "The forward reaction takes in heat, so each collision needs more energy for it to happen: it has the higher **activation energy**."
Problem: "each collision needs more energy" is ambiguous. It can be heard as "every collision now needs more energy", or as "the heat taken in is what the collision needs". The comparison that matters is forward against reverse, and that comparison is not stated. C4B's ridge picture is available to anchor it.
Replacement: "The forward reaction takes in heat, so a forward collision must climb a higher ridge than a reverse one: it has the higher **activation energy**."

**F2 (minor). Scene 8 narration.**
Quoted: "Heating increases those high-energy collisions the most, so the forward rate rises **more**."
Problem: the point is about proportion. C4B used "factor" language, and "the most" invites "the most of what?".
Replacement: "Heating multiplies those higher-energy collisions by a bigger factor, so the forward rate rises **more**."

**F3 (should fix). Scene 10 attribution.**
Quoted: "a chip 'two balanced equations' with the grey tag 'e.g. a NESA Chemistry 2024 guideline'"
Problem: this is not in `what-markers-reward.md`, so it is an unsupported NESA attribution (see learning review S4).
Replacement: "a chip 'a balanced equation with states' with the grey tag 'e.g. a NESA Chemistry 2025 guideline'"
