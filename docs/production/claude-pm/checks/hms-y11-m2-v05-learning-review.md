## Findings

1. **Should fix, 12b models four of 12a's five marks.** The design says the fifth mark (live high, train low, or the aerobic-only qualification) is "in scene 5", but a student marking her own answer hears four points for a 5-mark question. Add "That's why athletes live high and train low." after the EPO line (about 3 s).

2. **Should fix, 12c repeats scene 9.** Scene 9 says "Dehydration shrinks the plasma, so stroke volume falls ... Heat sends blood to the skin", and 12c's hot netballer is answered by that sentence. Use a case the video has not worked, on a different link: "A runner gives blood. A week later, at her usual pace, her heart rate is about ten beats higher. Explain why, using the oxygen delivery chain. (3 marks)" Model: link two, carrying (fewer red cells, so less haemoglobin in each litre; the plasma refills in days but red cells take weeks); each litre carries less, so the heart must move more litres; heart rate rises to hold delivery. Crossed mistake: "she's lost fitness". Also relieves the module-level repetition of the hot-day mechanism (see the summary). Science check to confirm the recovery times.

3. **Should fix, scene 9 puts blood volume on the wrong link.** "Endurance training strengthens link three, and link two through more blood volume." In the video's own model link two is oxygen per litre of blood; more plasma does not raise that (it can dilute it slightly). A student placing "more blood volume" on link two would contradict scene 3. Say "Endurance training strengthens link three: a bigger ventricle and more blood volume raise stroke volume." Science check to confirm.

4. **Note, "two unseen items".** 12a is the site's WE b1 verbatim and scenes 4 and 5 teach its whole answer. Keeping the site's question is reasonable; label it honestly in the design ("from the lesson").

5. **Note, units.** "From about a hundred and sixty to about a hundred and eighteen" is spoken without units; show "mmHg" on the air columns.
