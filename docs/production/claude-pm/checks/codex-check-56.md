# Brief 56: fact check of P02, C10, B6b and B6c

Checked the four designs against the narration text in each `measured/lesson.json`. The chemistry calculations were recomputed independently. The syllabus wording was compared with the official 2017 NSW syllabus files.

## P02: colourimetry

**Error:** In the `standards` narration, "the red ion's concentration equals the thiocyanate concentration in that standard" states an approximation as an equality. The design's own recipe gives 0.160 mol/L Fe³⁺, and its K of about 208 implies only **97.1%** of the added SCN⁻ is complexed. For the largest nominal standard, 2.00 × 10⁻⁴ mol/L SCN⁻ gives approximately **1.94 × 10⁻⁴ mol/L FeSCN²⁺** at equilibrium. Say the concentrations are *approximately* equal. The [Alameda College experiment](https://alameda.edu/hubfs/2025-website-alameda/Chem%201B/Laboratory/Exp7Rev_Kc_determination.pdf) likewise calls this an assumption and says the amount formed nearly equals the starting SCN⁻.

No error found in the dilution, fitted calibration line, ICE table, worked K (207.61), attempt K (208.33), or the bottle-concentration mistake (27.70). No error found in 447 nm ([Alameda College experiment](https://alameda.edu/hubfs/2025-website-alameda/Chem%201B/Laboratory/Exp7Rev_Kc_determination.pdf)) or the approximate ε of 4.7 × 10³ L mol⁻¹ cm⁻¹ ([University of Central Arkansas experiment](https://faculty.uca.edu/kdooley/equilibrium_21714.pdf)). The excess-Fe³⁺ method and the cited [2017 Chemistry syllabus point](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-word.docx) check out.

## C10: Ka and Kb

**Error:** The design's syllabus citation stops at "dissociation of acids and bases". The full final subpoint in the [official 2017 Chemistry syllabus](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-word.docx) is **"dissociation of acids and bases (ACSCH098, ACSCH099)"**. Include the codes when quoting this dotpoint word for word. The parent point and "dissociation of ionic solutions" subpoint match.

No error found in the stated Ka values for acetic acid, HF, methanoic acid and HCN, or Kb for ammonia. The values fall within published 25 °C table values ([Wisconsin chemistry text](https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Interactive_Chemistry_%28Moore_Zhou_and_Garand%29/06%3A_Appendix/6.12%3A_Acid-Base_Ionization_Constant), [Purdue chemistry text](https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Purdue/Purdue_Chem_26100%3A_Organic_Chemistry_I_%28Wenthold%29/Chapter_01%3A_Introduction_and_Review/1.8_Acids_and_Bases/Overview_of_Acids_and_Bases)). No error found in the 1.33% acetic acid and 7.91% HF ionisation at 0.10 mol/L, the two Ka ratios, the strong-acid explanation, or the comparisons qualified by "at the same concentration".

## B6b: transcription

No error found in the worked or attempt DNA and mRNA sequences, their 3′ and 5′ ends, or the choice of the top strand as the attempt's template. The template 3′-TGC AAT CGG-5′ gives mRNA 5′-ACG UUA GCC-3′.

No error found in the mushroom-toxin question: α-amanitin inhibits mammalian RNA polymerase II during mRNA transcription ([primary structure study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29550768/)). No error found in the cited [2017 Biology syllabus wording](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/biology-stage-6-syllabus-2017.docx).

## B6c: translation

No error found in the stated codons against the [standard genetic code](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK2327/bin/stdcode.pdf), including the worked Met-Glu-Ala-stop message and the attempt's Met-Lys-Pro-stop answer. No error found in the anticodons or their ends: 5′-GAA-3′ pairs with 3′-CUU-5′, and the attempt's 5′-AAG-3′ pairs with 3′-UUC-5′.

No error found in the initiation order: the small subunit with initiator Met-tRNA binds near the 5′ end, finds AUG, then the large subunit joins ([Molecular Biology of the Cell](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26829/)). No error found in the streptomycin account: bacterial codon misreading and binding to the human mitochondrial small subunit are supported by [structural studies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10497419/) and [mitochondrial ribosome work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9731571/). No error found in the cited [2017 Biology syllabus wording](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/biology-stage-6-syllabus-2017.docx).
