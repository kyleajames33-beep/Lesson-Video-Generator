| m1-v17: independent SDG reviews criticised inequality, climate and Indigenous health outcomes | A named assessment supporting all three topics was found: Monash's Transforming Australia SDG Progress Report 2020 Update. Its institutional account says Australia was failing to address economic inequality and emissions, and that Indigenous life expectancy remained below the national average. This supports a dated reference to that assessment. The broader plural wording is only partly confirmed: the current SDSN profile is a second independent assessment, but its fact sheet alone does not establish criticism of all three topics, especially Indigenous health. Root should name the 2020 assessment or inspect the relevant newer indicators before asserting a current combined judgement. | [Monash report page, 2020](https://www.monash.edu/business/stl/sustainable-development-goals/transforming-australia/transforming-australia-2020), [Monash's account of the findings](https://www.monash.edu/news/articles/australia-lags-on-un-targets-for-a-more-prosperous%2C-greener%2C-fairer-nation%2C-monash-report-shows), and [SDSN Sustainable Development Report 2026, Australia](https://dashboards.sdgindex.org/profiles/australia/fact-sheet/). Retrieved 24 November 2026. Monash's indexed primary text was available; repeated direct page fetches timed out and its summary PDF returned 403. No claim of reading that PDF. |
| m1-v06: youth smoking has fallen | Confirmed as a historical trend through the 2022-23 NDSHS. AIHW reports the lowest recorded daily smoking among young people in that survey, including fewer 18-24 year olds than in 2019. This supports tobacco smoking, not a claim that all nicotine use fell. No new figure is proposed for the design. | [AIHW, young people's alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarette and other drug use](https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/illicit-use-of-drugs/national-drug-strategy-household-survey-2022-2023/contents/detailed-findings/young-people-alcohol-smoking-drugs), 2022-23 data, report updated 27 May 2025. Retrieved 24 November 2026. The page flags a newer release; this finding is explicitly about the dated historical series. |
| m1-v06: youth drink driving has fallen | Not confirmed from a comparable youth-specific time series in this check. Risky drinking, support for penalties, police detections and alcohol-related road fatalities are different measures. The NDSHS youth page does not establish this drink-driving trend. Root needs an age band, period and matching measure before the claim is cleared. | Same [AIHW youth page](https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/illicit-use-of-drugs/national-drug-strategy-household-survey-2022-2023/contents/detailed-findings/young-people-alcohol-smoking-drugs), checked 24 November 2026; targeted AIHW and Transport for NSW searches did not yield the required series. |
| m1-v09: later starts lengthen school-night sleep in overseas studies | Confirmed for the cited studies. Dunster et al. found median sleep duration rose 34 minutes after Seattle's 55-minute start-time delay in a pre/post study. Minges and Redeker reviewed six experimental studies and found increases in weeknight sleep. These findings support the design's cautious general statement; they do not guarantee the same result in every Australian school. | [Dunster et al., Science Advances, 12 December 2018, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aau6200](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6291308/). [Minges and Redeker, Sleep Medicine Reviews 28, August 2016, pages 86-95, DOI 10.1016/j.smrv.2015.06.002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26545246/). Retrieved 24 November 2026. |
| m2-v05: about 92% saturation at 2,500 m | Confirmed as an explicitly illustrative input in this design, not a measured universal value. The arithmetic is correct: 200 x 92/98 = 187.755 mL/L; 980/187.755 = 5.2196 L/min; divided by 0.070 L/beat gives 74.566 beats/min, rounded to about 75. No primary dataset was found here that authenticates 92% as a general resting value at exactly 2,500 m. Keep this fact's status illustrative; the calculation cannot validate its physiological input. | Design `hms-y11-m2-v05.md`, facts and numbers section, read 24 November 2026. No empirical source confirmed for the exact 92% value. The brief's v05 refers to Module 2, not Module 1's determinants lesson. |
