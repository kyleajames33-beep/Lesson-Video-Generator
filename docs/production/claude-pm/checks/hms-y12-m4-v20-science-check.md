# hms-y12-m4-v20 science check (injury classification and TOTAPS)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: script, on-screen text in the visual lines, practice, key notes, memory aids and facts section; no build or playback; plates checked by name and description). Independent check; the design was not edited. Line numbers refer to `lessons/hms-y12-m4-v20.md` as of this check.

## Confirmed
- **Overuse as cause or onset (flagged, line 53; marking line 128).** The syllabus phrase "direct and indirect, soft and hard tissue or overuse" supports both readings, and NSW PDHPE texts commonly teach direct, indirect and overuse as three causes. The video's three axes with "Some books list overuse as a third cause instead. Either way, say it built up over time." is correct, and the marking accepting "overuse" as the cause is right.
- Sprain (ligament), strain (muscle or tendon), fracture, dislocation (do not relocate), contusion. Correct (SMA fact sheets; Australian Resuscitation Council guidelines).
- TOTAPS order, what each step tests, stop at any failed step, not a diagnosis, not for head, neck or spinal injury or an unconscious player (5). Correct; consistent with hms-y11-m2-v08's first aid.
- **Concussion (flagged, line 73).** "any suspected concussion ... no return that day, and medical clearance before playing again" is consistent with the Australian Concussion Guidelines for Youth and Community Sport (AIS, with SMA, the APA and ACSEP, February 2024) and the AIS Concussion and Brain Health Position Statement 2024, "If in doubt, sit them out". "When in doubt, sit them out" is a near paraphrase (see finding 2).
- Prevention (6): load spikes, surface and footwear changes and fast returns precede overuse injuries. Correct. 8a: fast bowlers' lumbar bone stress injuries follow workload spikes (Orchard and others, *Br J Sports Med* 2015). 8c: lateral ligament sprain, fails the skill step. Correct.
- Memory aids sound. No people (the dislocation is a coded outline on a bone plate).

## Findings
1. **Should fix (line 73, scene 5).** Current: "any suspected **concussion**, a brain injury from a knock: no return that day". The national guidance defines concussion as a brain injury caused by a knock to the head, or to the body that jolts the head; students commonly believe it needs a hit to the head, which this wording reinforces. Corrected: "any suspected concussion, a brain injury from a knock to the head or a jolt through the body: no return that day, and medical clearance before playing again." Gloss on screen likewise: "concussion: brain injury from a knock to the head or body".
2. **Note (line 73).** The guideline's own phrase is "If in doubt, sit them out"; using it exactly would match what students meet in clubs.

## Verdict
Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 1 should fix, 1 note. Both flags (overuse marking, concussion wording) resolved.
