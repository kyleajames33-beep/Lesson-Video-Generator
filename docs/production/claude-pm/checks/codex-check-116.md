## bio-m8-b04

1. **Scene 10b equates not sweating with losing no water.** Narration says "The absorbed salt raises blood osmolarity, even though no water was lost." The Visual paragraph underlines "has not been sweating" as its evidence. A person still loses water through respiration, skin diffusion, urine and other routes. Correct: "The absorbed salt raises blood osmolarity, even without sweating." Use the same wording in the cue explanation. [National Academies, Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, chapter4](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10925/chapter/6).

The earlier blocker is otherwise fixed: the answer now says conservation limits further loss and drinking supplies water, rather than claiming ADH creates replacement water. Hypothalamic synthesis and posterior-pituitary release, apical aquaporins and the aldosterone water-channel condition remain correct.
