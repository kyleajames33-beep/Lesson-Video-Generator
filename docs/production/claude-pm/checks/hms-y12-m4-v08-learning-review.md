## Findings

1. **Should fix, the main question and its worked answer were taught in Year 11.** hms-y11-m2-v05 opens on the same puzzle (a trained runner at 42 beats against 76, "How can both hearts be enough?"), works Q = HR × SV for both ("Same output, fewer beats"), and concludes "the low heart rate is a sign of fitness". It also teaches the contrast "In the runner, the chamber grew ... With high blood pressure, the wall grew." v08 recalls only the equation, then re-teaches the same hook, mechanism and calculation as new, and its overlay "thickens the plate's left ventricle wall" first, which a Year 11 student has learned to associate with high blood pressure. A returning student hears scene 2 as a repeat and may be confused by the wall. Fix: recall in scene 1 or 2: "In Year 11 you saw why a trained heart beats less at rest: same output, bigger beats. Now we'll see what actually changed, and what that buys at exercise and at maximum." Keep the 72 against 48 strip (it anchors scene 3) but present it as recall with a grey "earlier" tag and one stage instead of two. Reword the mechanism to Year 11's contrast: "Endurance training enlarges the left ventricle's chamber, and its wall strengthens to match", and draw the overlay as a widening chamber first (the red ventricle at the viewer's right). Spend the saved 20 to 25 s on scene 3 (maximum cardiac output and oxygen uptake), which is new. Consider sharpening the heading to the new part: "What does training change inside the body?" Students who did Year 11 should build; students who did not still get one clear sentence.

2. **Should fix, scene 5 re-teaches fibre types from Year 11.** hms-y11-m2-v02 taught slow and fast twitch traits, the capillaries, mitochondria and myoglobin cluster, that the proportion is "largely inherited" and that "training doesn't turn slow fibres into fast ones". v08 introduces "Muscles also contain two main fibre types" as new and crosses the same chip. Fix: "You met the two fibre types in Year 11, and that their mix is mostly inherited. What training changes is inside each type." Keep the glosses as grey recall tags, and spend the time on what is new to this dotpoint: hypertrophy as bigger fibres, the neural first weeks, and which training changes which type.

3. **Should fix, two intensity percentages do not name their maximum, and one sits inside Year 11's zone.** 8a chain B says heart rate "stays under sixty-five per cent of maximum" and the card says "65 per cent of maximum"; scene 6 says race pace "sits at a lower share of maximum". Root's Module 2 ruling 4 requires the maximum every time. Also, hms-y11-m2-v12 still teaches the aerobic zone as 60 to 85 per cent of maximum heart rate, so a Year 11 student reads 65 per cent as inside the zone and cannot reach mark 4 ("below threshold"). Fix: 8a "under sixty per cent of maximum heart rate" (or 55, if root keeps Year 11 v12's 60 floor; see the v04 review), card likewise; scene 6 "at a lower share of maximum aerobic capacity".

4. **Note, 8d is about 30 s.** Trim "and sleep fell" from the third sentence (the line on screen keeps it); about 1 s.

5. **Note, do not build scene 5 on the v1 fibre plate.** The root note switches to v2's mosaic. If v2 is late, wait or use a coded mosaic; the v1 plate's two zones would teach that fibre types sit in separate regions, which is the problem Module 2's review found.

6. **Note, scene 3 is dense (about 850 characters) with maximum cardiac output, VO₂ max, delivery, extraction and the lungs.** Finding 1's saved time can pay for a 1 s settle between "maximum cardiac output is much higher" and "maximal oxygen uptake".
