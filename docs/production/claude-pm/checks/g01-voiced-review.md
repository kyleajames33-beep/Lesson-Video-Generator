### SHOULD FIX

1. **S1 non-equilibrium, f1500 to f1620 (narration "A system where the net change is still going, with no steady balance, is called a non-equilibrium system").** The screen shows ash, a zero burning-rate bar and "nothing runs back" circled, so the picture says the fire is finished at the moment the definition says "net change still going". Students could read "non-equilibrium" as "finished". Fix: keep the fire burning with a visibly non-zero burning-rate bar until the definition lands (about f1620), then show the ash only on "until the fuel runs out" in the right order, or move ash after the definition.
2. **S1, f1560 and f1500: stray white rounded bar under the ash pit with a clipped fragment of text ("a...") behind the stones.** Looks like a label clipped or hidden behind the pit image. Fix: remove it or raise it above the image and size it so the text is readable.
3. **S6 heading-not-speed, f7477 to f7650.** The scene opens on a blank page with only the title for about 5 s of narration ("While the fuel is still burning, the system is a non-equilibrium system"). At f7600 a single red square labelled "burning rate" and a "non-equilibrium" chip are present, but no fire or running process is shown. Fix: show the fire (or a running burning-rate bar) on the first clause, then the static "both rates zero" state on "Once the fuel is gone".
4. **S6, f7750 and f8000: on-screen text "the C1 case: finished, nothing running".** "C1" is an internal lesson code. Narration says "the static case from the first video". Fix: change to "the first video's static case: finished, nothing running" (or "static case: nothing running").
5. **S13 key-notes, f17350 to f18078 (hold about 21 s after the narration finishes at f17453).** Six bullets plus the memory aid are about 120 words in small body text, including a long bullet that mixes the glucose result with "Work out both delta H and T delta S, because entropy can fall (hydrogen burning) and the heat term can still win". This is not copyable in 21 s even with the "pause if you need more time" prompt, and it introduces the hydrogen reasoning that was an attempt item. Fix: lengthen the hold to about 40 to 45 s, or cut to the essentials (delta H and S meaning, delta G equation and sign meaning, non-equilibrium definition, delta G is direction not speed, memory aid) and split the combustion/photosynthesis bullets into shorter lines.
6. **S11 feedback, f15500 to f16200 (end state).** By the last wrong-answer cue the screen holds eight items at once (formula result, slider, bar diagram, two tube diagrams with clock and calendar, misconception pill and its explanation). Text such as "dynamic equilibrium needs both rates equal. This tube: neither." and "spreading lost" is about 14 px at half scale (about 28 px at 1080p) and tight against the bar chart. Fix: fade the diagram parts the current explanation no longer uses when each misconception pill arrives (for example drop the bar diagram before the "-670" pill) so one stage is current.

## Student learning trace (entry knowledge: C1 and C2, Year 11 enthalpy, entropy, delta G)

- Causal chain is taught in an order a learner can follow: why fire goes one way (heat out and spreading, then delta G), what big or small delta G means, direction versus speed, then the reverse (photosynthesis) with light as the energy input.
- Terms get plain-language meanings before use (non-equilibrium, enthalpy, entropy, spontaneous). The "heat vs spreading in the mixture" separation is stated aloud and drawn.
- Calculation presentation meets the project standard: task, equation, grouped givens, one stage at a time, results kept.
- Attempt demands stay visible through the hold; feedback addresses the three likely errors (-670 sign slip, entropy-opposes-so-mixture, "no change means equilibrium").
- Application and memory: the "heat out and spread out" memory aid and the three-questions summary are readable and match the voice.
