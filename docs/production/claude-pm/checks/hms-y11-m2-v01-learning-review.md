## Findings

1. **Should fix, 11a repeats scene 9's last sentence.** Scene 9 ends "On the way up, everything reverses: the hip and knee extend, and the ankle plantar flexes." A vertical jump take-off is that up phase, so 11a can be answered by recall. Use a movement where the joints do not all move the same way, for example: "A soccer player kicks a ball. Describe the joint actions at the hip, knee and ankle of the kicking leg as it swings forward to the ball. (3 marks)" Model: hip, ball-and-socket, flexion; knee, hinge, extension; ankle, hinge, plantar flexion (held pointed). Crossed mistake: "everything flexes together", or everyday language ("the leg swings"). Students should show they can read each joint's angle, not reverse a remembered sequence.

2. **Should fix, scene 7 names the wrist twice for two different shapes.** "Gliding joints, between the small bones of the wrist and ankle" and then "A condyloid joint, like the wrist". A student will ask whether the wrist is gliding or condyloid. Say "between the small carpal bones inside the wrist and ankle" and "a condyloid joint, like the wrist joint itself, where the radius meets the carpals", and put both leader labels on the arm plate's wrist end. Students should leave knowing that one region holds two joint types.

3. **Note, 11d is about 32 s.** Trim "a hinge elbow against a ball-and-socket hip" from the spoken feedback (the shapes table on screen carries it).

4. **Note, scene 8 pace.** Six action pairs in about 45 s, plus circumduction. The rows grow one per cue and the linkage demonstrates each, so it should hold, but judge it at playback speed and keep the pronation and supination demonstration short.

5. **Note, plates match their uses with two cautions.** The knee cutaway is an oblique front view (cruciate ligaments, menisci, patella, translucent capsule and blue bursae are all visible), not a side view as written; leader lines must follow the painted parts. On the sockets plate the glenoid and acetabulum depths look closer than the narration implies at small size, so keep the coded depth gauge in scene 5; it is doing the teaching.
