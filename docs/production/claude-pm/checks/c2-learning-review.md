## Notes (no change required to record)

- **Hook:** the puzzle ("only reactant, nothing to run backwards") is a real question, and the colourless-to-yellow-green payoff is concrete evidence. Add a clause for why the flask is hot: "hot enough that everything stays a gas".
- **Pronunciation load:** "P C L five" and "P C L three" are spoken more than 25 times. Say the names once ("phosphorus pentachloride", "phosphorus trichloride") before the letters, and check in the voiced preview that the letter-by-letter reading is clear.
- **Prediction beat (optional):** a short silent hold after the start stage ("what will the forward rate do as the flask empties of P C L five?") would be a cheap thinking moment before the cause is given. Not required.
- **Repetition:** the five-step story appears in scene 6 (aid), scene 9 and the notes. Acceptable as retrieval, but trim scene 9 to the decision rule if the voiced preview feels long.
- **Product-start visuals:** scene 8 says the rate bars are "mirror-imaged". With the same rate constant the reverse rate at a 0.20 and 0.20 start would be far higher than 50. Show it as "high", not the same 50, or the ghost comparison will contradict the numbers.
- **Memory aid assumptions:** closed system, steady temperature, simple one-step reaction. Once reworded (finding 2) the aid holds for either start. "Equal rates, not equal amounts" is correct here: 0.12 versus 0.08.
