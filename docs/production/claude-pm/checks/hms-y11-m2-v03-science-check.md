## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 4 narration and gloss).** "Your centre of gravity is the point where your mass is evenly spread." Mass is not evenly spread; the centre of gravity is where the weight is balanced. Corrected narration: "Your centre of gravity is the point where your weight is balanced in every direction." Gloss: "centre of gravity: the balance point of your weight".
2. **Should fix (scene 10 narration).** "the deltoid and triceps push harder each stroke, fewer strokes are needed". Wheelchair guidance (Paralyzed Veterans of America, *Preservation of Upper Limb Function Following Spinal Cord Injury*, 2005) recommends an axle position that gives long, smooth strokes at lower frequency and lower peak force; a good set-up does not make each push harder. Corrected: "Set well, the shoulder works in its middle range, each stroke is longer and smoother, fewer strokes are needed, and the shoulder takes less repeated strain." On screen: "longer strokes, fewer of them".
3. **Note (scene 7).** "the biggest part is form drag" holds for most swimming and running speeds; wave drag grows large near elite race pace. "usually the biggest part" is safer.
4. **Note (scene 7, now a silent tag).** Buoyancy acts at the chest, so on its own it lets the legs sink; it keeps the body high, not level. The design already cut the spoken clause; no action unless the line returns.

## Verdict
Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 2 should fix, 2 notes.
