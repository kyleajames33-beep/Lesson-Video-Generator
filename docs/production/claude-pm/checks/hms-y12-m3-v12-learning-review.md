## Findings

1. **Should fix, the grid says the cell decides the risk, then 8b gives an "instead of" case a low lamp.** Scene 6 and the memory line teach "Alongside or instead? That decides the risk", and the design notes that "the cell decides the lamp". In 8b, person 2 (a herbal tea instead of an over-the-counter medicine) sits in an "instead of" cell, yet the feedback calls the risk low and turns the lamp "green-amber". The real third factor, how serious the condition is, appears in scene 4 ("a serious, treatable illness") but is not on the grid. A student who applies the grid as taught calls person 2 risky and is told otherwise. Also the stem never says whether person 2 told anyone, so the row is undefined. Fix: in scene 6, after "nobody knows to step in", add "And all of this assumes the condition needs treatment. For a mild problem that clears up on its own, instead-of matters much less." (about 4 s), with a small dimmer on the lamps labelled "how serious?". Change the memory line to "Alongside or instead, for how serious a problem? That decides the risk", or keep it and add the dimmer to the memory card. In 8a, add "and has not mentioned it to anyone" to person 2, or remove the told question for person 2 in the marking. Students should be able to predict person 2's low risk from the model, not learn it as an exception in feedback.

2. **Note, scene 5 is long (about 880 characters).** It carries the chemotherapy and back pain examples, why it helps, non-disclosure, interactions with three tags, the memory line and the loop. Split at "And it loops" into 5a and 5b, with the ring dimming to grey in 5b. No narration change is needed.

3. **Note, "approved" is described but not shown in Australian terms.** The narration says "an approved product has been checked for safety and that it contains what it says, not that it works", which is correct for listed medicines. A small grey tag "listed medicine: AUST L" on the stamp would help students who see the code on real labels; optional.

4. **Note, 8b at about 29 s.** Finding 1 adds no time to 8b if the dimmer is taught in scene 6; if the clause goes into 8b instead, trim "Just watch for symptoms that persist or worsen" to "Review it if it persists."
