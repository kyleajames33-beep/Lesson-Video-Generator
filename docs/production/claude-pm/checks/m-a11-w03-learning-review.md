## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 1 (a rule without a cause).** Narration: "That's a square root on the bottom of a fraction, and maths prefers it on top." Students hear a preference, not a reason, and the whole lesson rests on it. Fix: "That's a square root on the bottom of a fraction. Dividing by a never-ending decimal is hard to picture or compare, so we rewrite the fraction with a whole number underneath." The student should know why rationalising is worth doing.
