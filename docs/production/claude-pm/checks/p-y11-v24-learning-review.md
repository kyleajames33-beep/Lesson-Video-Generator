# p-y11-v24 learning review

Revision 1, 3 November 2026. Design SHA256 `835a30426758bc5a306c0a4b41917f477d4c648b811816733dc5c4e9b1960099`. Reviewed v23's prediction handoff, the two-gate method, simulated data, risk controls and FA0 proposal; source only, no playback.

The prediction is labelled, the two graphs contrast angle with sin θ, and the timing example links velocity change to acceleration. The repeat table explicitly identifies its numbers as illustrative. The three risk rows connect each hazard to controls. A real practical still needs to be conducted for the syllabus action.

1. **Blocker:** Half the range of three repeats is presented as the full uncertainty, while angle, flag length, timing, calibration, model and systematic effects are omitted. No gradient uncertainty is calculated, yet scene 16 says the gradient matches g within it. Label the bar as repeat scatter; include appropriate angle and instrument limits and an actual gradient comparison, or make the conclusion conditional. Replace the repeated mnemonic and redundant graph reading to fund the repair.
2. **Blocker:** Scenes 8, 9 and A1/A2 feedback identify rolling resistance as the established cause of an intercept or three low readings. These data alone do not identify the cause: calibration, timing, angle bias and rotational inertia can also affect the fit. Present resistance as a possible assumed model, compare independent controls and qualify “systematic” as a hypothesis. Replace deterministic wording; no new scene.
3. **Blocker:** A2's line is 9.6 sin θ - 0.05 while the model is 9.8 sin θ. Their gap is 0.05 + 0.2 sin θ, not a constant 0.05; at sin θ = 0.5 it is 0.15. Correct the feedback and picture or change the given gradient to 9.8 if a constant-offset demonstration is intended. Replace existing feedback.
4. **Should fix:** “Everything else is controlled, so any change ... comes from the angle” overclaims validity. Say controls reduce alternative explanations and check resistance and calibration; the same trolley does not guarantee identical rolling resistance at every angle.
5. **Should fix:** The height-and-length improvement claims ±0.001 in sin θ from “heights measured to ±1 mm” without specifying whether that is the uncertainty in the difference or in each of two readings and neglects length uncertainty. State the uncertainty of the height difference and length, then propagate them; do not promise ±0.001 from unspecified measurements. Replace the display text.
6. **Should fix:** A fitted nonzero intercept means acceleration is approximately linear in sin θ, not exactly proportional through the origin. Scope the scene 16 and notes conclusion accordingly; retain the illustrative tag. The 2025 Working Scientifically outcome PY-11WS-03 exists, so the design's proposal to remove WS03 as an obsolete 2017 label is not established; use the full code if clarification is desired.
