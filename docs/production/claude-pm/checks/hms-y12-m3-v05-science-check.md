## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 8, line 81).** "Cardiovascular disease is largely a prevention story: fewer people now develop it." Modelling studies split the fall in coronary deaths between risk factor change and treatment: in the United States about 44 per cent risk factors and 47 per cent treatments (Ford and others 2007, *NEJM* 356:2388); studies in other high-income countries attribute about half to three-quarters to risk factors. "Largely" overstates the even split. Corrected: "Cardiovascular disease is, in large part, a prevention story: about half or more of the fall came from fewer people developing it, because policy moves a few big risk factors." The memory line ("fewer cases and more survivors", line 86) is already correct.
2. **Note (scene 6).** A National Lung Cancer Screening Program for eligible people at high risk began on 1 July 2025. "Screening ... for bowel, breast and cervical cancer" stays true as examples; optional tag "and, since 2025, lung screening for people at high risk".
