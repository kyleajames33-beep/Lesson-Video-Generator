## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 3 (the phone's slow-motion film strip is never narrated).** The Visual puts the reused phone above the table "with a coded slow-motion film strip on its screen", its frames stepping closer with each row, but no sentence in scenes 3 or 4 mentions filming or frames. This is the video's densest spoken scene (815 characters, about 61 s of decimals). An unexplained second moving object competes with the table, and students will ask why a phone is there; the analogy only helps if it is named. Fix: name it in one sentence after the 2.1 row: "It's like filming the car in slow motion: the closer together the frames, the truer the speed you measure." (about 6 s; scene 2's reserve trim pays for it). If the take cannot afford it, remove the phone and strip from scene 3 instead; the table and the pivoting secant already carry the idea.

2. **Optional, open decision 2 (A2's picture).** Keep the drawn tangent. The card already states both tangent points, so the picture gives nothing away, and showing the tangent beside the curve points (0, 0) and (5, 25) is exactly the choice the crossed slip tests.

3. **Optional, feedback 1 (about 29.6 s by estimate).** Within the limit but the closest in the video; measure on the voiced take. If it runs over 30 s, drop "At one second, it's travelled three metres." and keep the row "d(1) = 3, d(1.1) = 3.63" on screen.
