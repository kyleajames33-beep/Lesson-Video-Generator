## i12 to i23

Appended by the second author, 18 November 2026. Same sources, rules and shared frame as the i01 to i11 section above (names checked against `src/explainer/math/studio/finance-parts.tsx`, `money.ts`, `series-parts.tsx`, `plot-core.tsx` and `index.ts` on 18 November 2026). These are requirements for later build work, not implemented capability or a pilot pass. No source file was changed.

Standard methods differ from Advanced here: no repayment formula, no logarithms, no geometric series. Repayments come from a table of repayments per $1 000 or a loan calculator; terms and balances come from a spreadsheet's rows or a supplied graph; annuity values come from future value and present value interest factor tables. `money.ts` already produces the numbers the parts display (`loanSchedule`, `loanBalance`, `loanRepayment`, `loanPresentValue`, `loanPayoff`, `savingsSchedule`, `annuityFV`, `accountFV`, `depositForGoal`, `ceilCents`); its closed forms may run behind the scenes but are not shown to Standard students.

### New parts for i12 to i23 in one list

1. **InterestFactorTable** (i15, i20, i21, i23): the main new part. A lookup table with row and column highlights and a lifted cell, in three titled versions (repayment per $1 000, FV factors, PV factors). Course-neutral; any table-reading video (tax tables in Year 11 e08, the normal table if ever needed) can reuse the lookup behaviour.
2. **ReducingBalanceTable** (i13 to i17, i19): a row-by-row period table with carry-down arrows, a Standard 1 row limit chip, and contribution, withdrawal and new-purchases modes.
3. **CreditCardStatement** (i17; optionally i16 and i18): a coded statement with field highlights and the minimum repayment warning.
4. **CostSplitBars** (i12, i14, i15, i18, i22): total-paid bars split into price and extra.
5. **RateLadder** (i16, i18): annual rates with one month's interest beside each and a source line.
6. SpreadsheetGrid options (on top of the a-strand and i01 to i11 lists): rows-to-zero counter, row jump, input panel with results log, and the =E2 link arrow.
7. Small options: **PaymentPairing** on `PeriodTimeline` (i14), a zone band on `PeriodTimeline` (i18), a draining tank from `GoalMeter` (i19), **PayslipLine** (i23), a desktop calculator frame for `ScreenApp` (i22).

No simulation engine is required. Every number shown comes from `money.ts` or the SymPy recalculation files in `work/brief-standard-i2/` (`recalc-output.txt`, `practice-output.txt`, `factor-tables-output.txt`).
