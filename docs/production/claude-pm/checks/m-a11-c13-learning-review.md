## Findings

1. **Should fix, scenes 12 and 7 (claims about the rest of the year).** Scene 12: "That's every rule for differentiating this year". Scene 7: "Next year you'll meet new functions, and this same test still works on them." But e02, in this same Year 11 course, teaches that eˣ is its own derivative, and Year 12 d01 relies on it. Students are told the toolkit is complete and that new functions wait for Year 12, then meet a new derivative a few videos later. Fix:
   - Scene 12: "That's every rule for combining functions, and how to choose between them."
   - Scene 7: "Later you'll meet new functions, like e to the x, and this same test still works on them."

2. **Optional, key note 1 (too long for one line).** "First, simplify if you can: x² · x³ = x⁵; (x³ + 1)/x = x² + x⁻¹; 4/(x² + 1)³ = 4(x² + 1)⁻³." is about 75 characters. That will not fit one line at 38 px in 58 per cent of the width. Use "Simplify first: x² · x³ = x⁵; 4/(x² + 1)³ = 4(x² + 1)⁻³."

3. **Optional, feedback 1 (the common-bottom step is not shown).** "Over a common bottom, that's one minus three x" jumps from √(1 − 2x) − x/√(1 − 2x). Add an unspoken row "= ((1 − 2x) − x)/√(1 − 2x)". The feedback is about 29.7 s, so leave the narration unchanged.
