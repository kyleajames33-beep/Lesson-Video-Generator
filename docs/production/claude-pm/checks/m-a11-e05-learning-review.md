## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 1 ("underneath it is six").** Narration: "Find three on the middle scale, and underneath it is six." The asset brief and sidecar put the body's scale band "just above the groove", and the visual says the slide's 3 "meets the body's 6" with a hairline. If the build follows the brief, the 6 is above the 3, and students looking underneath find nothing. Fix: "Find three on the sliding scale, and the cursor lines it up with six on the fixed scale." (or move the body scale below the groove in the brief, and keep "underneath"). Students should be able to point to both numbers.

2. **Should fix, scene 3 (one letter, two meanings, both on screen).** Narration: "Now raise m to a power, n. m to the n is a to the p, all to the n." The linking strip "let m = aᵖ, n = a^q" stays at the top of the stage while "mⁿ = (aᵖ)ⁿ = a^(pn)" writes beneath it, so the screen says n = a^q and uses n as an exponent in the same frame. A student will substitute a^q. The magenta colour alone does not undo a definition still on screen. This answers open decision 1. Fix: on "Now raise m to a power", fade "n = a^q" from the linking strip (keep "m = aᵖ, p = logₐ m"), and say "Now raise m to any power. The syllabus calls this power n. It's a new number here, not the n from before." Keep the syllabus letter so the key note matches exam wording.

3. **Optional, scene 1 ("a distance from the one that's equal to its log").** A length cannot equal 0.301 without a unit. Add "measuring the whole scale, from one to ten, as one unit". Small, but it stops a student asking "0.301 of what?".
