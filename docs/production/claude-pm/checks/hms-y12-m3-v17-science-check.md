## Confirmed
- My Health Record (3): opt-out since early 2019, so most Australians have one; holds discharge summaries, medicines, pathology and imaging reports, immunisations; individuals set access controls and can see an access history. Correct.
- Sharing by default (3, 6; currency item, site correction 30): the Health Legislation Amendment (Modernising My Health Record, Sharing by Default) Act 2025 passed in February 2025; the Share by Default rules were made in December 2025; the requirement for providers of pathology and diagnostic imaging services (reports written by a pathologist or radiologist) to share to My Health Record took effect from 1 July 2026, with a transition period (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing; Australian Digital Health Agency). "New rules are starting to make some test results upload by default" is true in November 2026; scene 6's "every key document, not just some test results" is true (other document types remain voluntary).
- Telehealth permanent with conditions since 2022; electronic prescriptions since 2020; Australian Immunisation Register and National Cancer Screening Register. Correct.
- 8b and 8d: correct.
