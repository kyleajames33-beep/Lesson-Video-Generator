## Should fix

**T04-SEASON, scene 3, "Real clocks in Perth are two hours behind Sydney, not two hours twenty."** This is true only on standard time. From the first Sunday in October to the first Sunday in April, Sydney is on daylight saving and Perth is three hours behind; that is the case on this review's date (24 November). t05 then teaches the 3 h summer gap, so an unqualified "two hours" here becomes something t05 has to undo. Fix: "On standard time, in winter, real clocks in Perth are two hours behind Sydney" and label the two clock faces "standard time". Scene 4 already says "before any daylight saving", so the qualifier matches. The student should hear one consistent fact across t04 and t05.
