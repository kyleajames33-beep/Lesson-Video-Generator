Source traceability:

- Finding 1: [Peters et al., Follicular growth: the basic event in the mouse and human ovary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/128630/) distinguishes continuing follicle growth from cyclic ovulation. [Westergaard et al., Epidermal growth factor in small antral ovarian follicles of pregnant women](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2250159/) studied small follicles in women at term and identified growth regulation up to 6 mm.
- Finding 2: [WHO and UNICEF, Baby-friendly Hospital Initiative, Section 3](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/10665/43593/5/9789241594981_eng.pdf), session 3.2.6, explains prenatal colostrum production and the larger milk quantities after birth.
- FSH, LH, corpus luteum and the next cycle: [Physiology, Ovulation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441996/). Pregnancy suppression and hormone origins: [Physiology, Pituitary Issues During Pregnancy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK551724/). These support the lesson's hormone roles and the stated high-dose progesterone-action attempt, without treating its fictional tablet as a claim about a real contraceptive product.
- Late-pregnancy oxytocin responsiveness: [Fuchs et al., Oxytocin receptors in the human uterus during pregnancy and parturition](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6093538/) measured the increase in human myometrial receptor concentration. Progesterone's uterine role: [Physiology, Progesterone](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK558960/).
- Cervical stretch, oxytocin release and the reinforcing labour loop: [The physiology and pharmacology of oxytocin in labor and in the peripartum period](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38462255/). Suckling, prolactin production and oxytocin ejection: [WHO, The physiological basis of breastfeeding](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK148970/). The two hormone jobs support attempt item 2 and the memory aid. The loop mechanisms support attempt item 3.
- Syllabus: [NESA, Biology Stage 6 Syllabus (2017)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/science/biology-stage-6-2017) and [NSW Department of Education, Biology HSC study day slides](https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/key-learning-areas/science/media/documents/biology-s6-aurora-hsc-study-day-powerpoint-slides.pdf), Outcome 12.1b. The lesson addresses hormonal control within the fertilisation, implantation, pregnancy and birth content point; it does not claim this part alone covers the entire point.
