- Windows (3): carbohydrate-rich, low fat and fibre meal before (fat and fibre slow gastric emptying); carbohydrate during events longer than about an hour; carbohydrate, protein, fluid and sodium after; loading for long continuous events (over about 90 min). - Fluid (4): sweat-chain recall consistent with hms-y11-m2-v05; exercise-associated hyponatraemia from drinking well beyond losses, commonest in slower long-event participants, can be fatal (Hew-Butler and others, *Clin J Sport Med* 2015). Correct.
