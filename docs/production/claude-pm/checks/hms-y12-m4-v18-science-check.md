## Confirmed
- Readiness against adaptation (2). Correct framing.
- Cool-down and active recovery recall (3), consistent with v07.
- Cold-water immersion (3): reduces perceived soreness and fatigue over about 24 to 96 h (Machado and others, *Sports Med* 2016; Leeder and others, *Br J Sports Med* 2012); regular use after resistance training blunts gains in muscle mass and strength (Roberts and others, *J Physiol* 2015). "appears to" is the right register. Contrast therapy evidence weaker; heat avoided on a fresh injury (SMA "No HARM"). Correct.
- Psychological recovery (4): relaxation, mindfulness, psychological detachment (Balk and others, *Psychol Sport Exerc* 2017). Correct in general terms.
- Hierarchy (6): sleep as the foundation (Halson, *Sports Med* 2014). Correct as consensus.
- 8a/8b: correct. Memory aids sound. No people.
