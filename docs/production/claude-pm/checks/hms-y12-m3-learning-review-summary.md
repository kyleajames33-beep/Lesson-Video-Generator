## The blocker

**v06 9b (dementia on the condition map).** v01 taught that dementia is now the leading cause of death in Australia. 9b places dementia "low on the map", "mostly disability", and accepts only "towards morbidity", while its own feedback says deaths are rising. A student who applies v01 and places dementia towards "mostly kills" is marked wrong, and "mostly disables" is itself doubtful on burden data. Fix: feedback "Dementia sits high on both: it's now the leading cause of death, and it brings years of disability and care before that. Either height scores if you justify it", token at mid-height near the centre, marking widened; the science check confirms dementia's fatal and non-fatal burden split.

## Module-level findings

1. **Too many checklists, and they do not visibly grow (should fix in v13, v16, v20, v21, v22).** Students meet: v08's four moves; v13's four tests, six questions, eight patterns, three service checks and five-gate sixty-second check; v15's four propositions; v16's three questions; v20's five-step method; v21's seven features, five transfer steps and five community-led mechanisms; v22's six tests and six failure modes. Many items repeat in new words ("who benefits?" twice in v13; "who first?" in v15 and v16; reach, sustainability, measurement by group in Year 11's strategy checklist, v20, v21 and twice in v22). Year 11's review asked for "one checklist that grew"; Module 3 repeats the problem at larger scale. Each review gives the fix: link the later list's items to their sources with grey "earlier" tags and one spoken sentence, and spend the time on what is new. Target outcome: one claim-and-technology checker (v13 grown by v15 and v16) and one strategy checklist (Year 11 m1-v18 grown by v20, v21 and v22).

2. **Year 11 double teaching in the SDG run (should fix in v19, v20, v21, v22).** v01 to v18 recall Year 11 well (one plain sentence, grey "earlier" tag). The SDG videos do not: v19 re-teaches Year 11's features, SDG 3 targets, the girls' education mechanism and an almost identical memory line ("can't make anyone act" against Year 11's "can't compel anyone"); v20's method restates m1-v18's "start from a local need, not from the goals" and "measure by group"; v21 promises a Year 11 recall in its decisions that is missing from the narration and teaches consultation against community-led without Year 11's participation ladder; v22's tests 4 to 6 are m1-v18's strategy checklist. The route's "Builds-on" lines need to cite hms-y11-m1-v17, v18 and v13 for these videos.

3. **Practice demands narrower than the marking or the answer (should fix in v01, v02, v08, v09, v12, v14; the v06 blocker is the extreme case).** v01 8a asks for "at least TWO" reading words but needs three for full marks; v02 9c's marking rewards a mechanism and a design condition the card never asks for; v08 8a case 2 has a defensible second gate; v09 8a asks whether a payer and provider split "creates a seam" when scene 4 defines seams as crossings; v12 8b gives an "instead of" case a low risk the grid cannot predict; v14 8a asks for a binary and answers "both". Root should require that every mark appears on the card and that feedback accepts any answer the video's own model supports.

4. **Contradictions between or within videos (should fix).** v05 answers its main question two ways (hook slot and memory line say treatment; scene 8 says "mostly prevention"); v14 files chronic disease as an outside pressure that "can't be reversed", against v05, v06 and v10's prevention message; v06 says new type 2 diabetes cases have nearly halved, then that it is "appearing in younger adults", with no bridge; v02 draws a life expectancy trend beside a source that the next scene says cannot be compared over time; v10 attaches "opportunity cost" to "cost deferred isn't cost avoided"; v15's overdiagnosis pile sits outside the 59 positives it belongs to. Each review gives a one-sentence or one-picture fix.

5. **"Unseen" items that reuse the body's example (should fix in v17 and v19; notes in v02, v10, v13).** v17 8a case 1 is scene 2's repeated blood test; v19 8c's expected action is scene 5's own pin (school hearing checks). Replacement cases are in each review.

6. **Scene density.** Scenes over about 850 characters with four or five new glossed terms: v01 scene 4, v12 scene 5, v13 scene 5, v15 scene 4, v16 scene 4 (should fix: five terms), v18 scene 5 (should fix: four terms and eight measures), v14 scene 4. In every case the fix is a split with a settle and earlier items fading to grey, with no narration change.

7. **Hooks.** Every hook is a genuine puzzle and almost all are answered on screen with ticks or filled slots, which is a module strength. Several ask students to think and give no time (v02, v03, v04, v06, v16: add 2 to 3 s); a few leave a slot unfilled (v07 green slots, v15 "who pays?", v19 "evidence in ten years").

8. **Feedback length.** Most feedback is 22 to 29 s by the authors' counts. At about 30 s: v04 8d, v10 9d, v14 8b, v16 8b, v18 8b, v21 8b, v22 8d. Trims are in each review; exact audio decides. Every feedback gives the answer, one or two reasons and one crossed classic mistake, with no re-teaching.

9. **Currency for a 2026 student (for the science check).** The designs handle dated facts well: no retired aged care program names (site correction 28), no bulk-billing trend (site corrections 29, 30 avoided or taught), dated figures with release re-check flags. Items to confirm: v01 "many of those extra years are lived with illness" (health-adjusted life expectancy); v04 8d existing child dental coverage (a means-tested Commonwealth child dental benefit and state school dental services); v06 dementia's burden split and young-adult type 2 diabetes trend; v08 "cost rarely blocks a GP"; v09 Support at Home client contributions; v11 rebates can rise as well as lag; v17 sharing-by-default commencement; v21 the syllabus example's current status (if it closed when funding ended, use that as feature 6 in action).

## Module coherence

- **Order and joins.** All twenty-three joins match: hms-y11-m2-v22 to v01, each video's handoff frame to the next opening, and v22 to hms-y12-m4-v01's recorded opening. The order follows the syllabus's three inquiry questions: how healthy (v01 to v07, closed by v07's recap), the healthcare system (v08 to v14, closed by v14's recap strip), technology and data (v15 to v18, closed by v18's recap), then the SDGs and community strategy (v19 to v22, closed by v22's focus-area close). Each video keeps one main question.
- **Recurring models.** Mostly consistent and well reused: the bathtub (v01, v05, v06), the determinants chain (v01, v02), `GapLines` (v02, v03, v16, v20), gradient steps (v03, v07, v10, v13, v20), `HealthyYearsBar` grown into `CompressionBar` (v01, v07), `ConditionBoard` folded into `ConditionProfile` (v05, v06), `TransferTest` (v04, reused in v21), the seam glow and carry test (v09 to v18), the spending bar (v10, v11, v18), v15's "reaches the best-off first" base band (v15 to v22). One deliberate change: Year 11's six access gates are regrouped into five dimensions in v08 by sliding each old gate into its new frame (good practice); v03 still uses six, and later videos should use five only.
- **Repetition across the module.** Apart from findings 1 and 2, overlap is mostly deliberate recall. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander content appears in v01, v02, v03, v05, v06, v08, v16, v18, v19 and v20, each time general and serving its own dotpoint; v02 is the only video that teaches causes in depth, and later videos recall it, which is right.
- **Syllabus coverage.** Every dotpoint and sub-point in the focus area is owned and taught, with the syllabus examples (colonisation, privatisation, function of hospitals, measuring, monitoring, early diagnosis and precision surgery, apps, AI and assistive technology, Healthy Cities Illawarra). The "one other group" is rural and remote, with one mechanism line for each other group (v03 finding 1 asks for the culturally and linguistically diverse line to be spoken); the "one other condition" is type 2 diabetes. No gaps found.

## Checks across all twenty-two

- **Causes before reliance:** met throughout; the module consistently says "because" and crosses lists without mechanisms. Exceptions are the term-level items in finding 4 and v03's silent CALD mechanism.
- **Visual medium:** every video has a moving model that carries its main relationship, and many make a visual argument (v05's pathway, v10's cost chain, v14's surge slider, v15's threshold slider and funnel, v17's completeness gauge, v18's re-identification probe, v20's interlock). Two plate issues found on inspection: v02's housing plate shows one person's insecure room, not crowding (should fix), and v02's "left valve" instruction is ambiguous on the painted heart (the mitral valve is on the viewer's right). The roster and puffer crops in v14 and v18 work. Motion still needs judging at playback speed.
- **Response opportunity:** all 44 cards keep the full demand on screen through the 10 s countdown and after; all end "Pause here to answer."; no picture slot shows an answer.
- **People and culture:** nobody is drawn; people are tokens, dots and rows. Cultural content is general throughout, with no named communities, organisations, policy titles or symbols.
- **Reading load:** at most three bright items per frame by design, with the densest scenes flagged in finding 6.
- **Style and length:** 0 em dashes in all twenty-two files. Authors' narration totals run 5,735 to 6,215 characters and about 529 to 569 s, so v02 (569 s), v05 (569 s), v03 (568 s) and v10 (568 s) have no headroom; any added speech needs a reserve cut.
