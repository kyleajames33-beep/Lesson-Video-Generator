## Findings

### 1. blocker: 6 and 7

Text: "Muscle, liver and fat cells ... fewer transporters move"

The sequence names three tissues, then gives only the muscle/fat GLUT4 mechanism for all of them. Students may infer that liver glucose control works by insertion of those same transporters.

Repair: Scope transporter failure to muscle and fat. Add "The liver also keeps releasing glucose when insulin should slow it down." Replace, rather than append to, the existing liver/fat exposition. Keep the visual current case as muscle, with one small liver glucose-output arrow.

### 2. should fix: 3

Text: "Take iron. Iron sits at the centre of haemoglobin"

B06 already gives the same full chain; this duplicates the prerequisite rather than deepening or transferring it. B08 is only 13 characters above the floor, so deleting it wholesale is not an acceptable repair.

Repair: Use a one-sentence iron recall, then use the saved words for the distinction between low intake, poor absorption and blood loss, or for the liver-versus-muscle insulin mechanism. Recount the integrated revision to keep at least 5,500 characters without padding.

### 3. should fix: 9c and 9d

Text: "Excess intake raises the chance"

The chart only measures body-weight category, family history and incidence. It cannot identify energy intake, let alone prove dietary causation. The feedback must answer the question about what these data show.

Repair: Replace feedback conclusion with "These data associate obesity and family history with higher incidence; they did not measure food intake, so they cannot establish that eating too much caused these cases." Keep the non-certainty point and crossed mistake. Fund any extra words by shortening scene 8's repeated definition.

### 4. should fix: 1 and 4 memory aid

Text: "Rickets needs daylight"

It risks making sunlight the needed nutrient or a treatment instruction, while adequate vitamin D can also come from diet or supplements and deliberate UV exposure has risks taught in b07.

Repair: Use "Rickets: think vitamin D" as the anchor, then keep "skin can make vitamin D in sunlight" as an explanation of one source. Replace the hook's broad "too much" claim with "long-term imbalance and other risk factors" if revising it. No extra scene.
