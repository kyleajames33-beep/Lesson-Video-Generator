## Findings

1. **Should fix, "fewer babies are born... That's two successes" is not explained.** Longer life is obviously a success; fewer births is not, to a seventeen-year-old. A student asks: "Why is a falling birth rate a success?" The memory card then repeats "two successes". The design's own memory aid check gives the reason (choice, education, access to contraception) but the narration does not. Add: "and fewer babies are born, because families can now choose how many children to have." (about 3 s). Students should be able to justify the memory line in an answer, not just repeat it.

2. **Note, the hook's two green slots ("two ways that's true") are never filled.** The amber slot fills in scene 4 and the magnifier in scene 5, but the green slots stay empty. Fill them silently in scene 4 as the challenges write: "rising demand" and "workforce squeeze". The hook then closes fully.

3. **Note, scene 6 is a seven-row list in one breath.** Each consideration has its because ("training takes years", "falls between them"), which is good, but seven rows plus a separate non-government card is the heaviest list in the video. Fade rows 1 to 4 to grey as rows 5 to 7 write, so only the current row and the title are bright.

4. **Note (science check), 8b "it usually costs less".** Home care costing less per person than residential care is broadly true for lower-need clients but less clear for high-need care. "usually costs less for people with lower care needs" is safer, or keep and confirm.
