## Should fix

**D08-2HRS, scene 5, "measured every two hours, from a hundred per cent at seven in the morning".** The ten readings are at 7, 9 and 11 am, then 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 11 pm. Readings at 3, 4 and 5 pm are one hour apart, and the narration itself says the charge happened "between four and five". A student who checks the axis finds the stated interval false, and a reader who trusts it may misread the 4 to 5 pm rise as a two-hour change. Fix: say "measured ten times through the day" (no interval), or make the readings truly two-hourly and move the charge to "between three and five".
