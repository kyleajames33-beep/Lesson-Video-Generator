## Findings

1. **Learning blocker, 12a (the poster proposal) has several correct answers under the video's own tests.** The demand asks "which of the five tests this proposal fails", singular, and 12b accepts only evaluation. But by scene 8's tests the proposal also fails test 1 ("raise awareness" names no cause, only a symptom) and probably test 2 (it sits on education, the level scene 6 called crowded) and test 4 (nobody affected helped design it). A student who applies the taught tests correctly and answers "cause" or "empty level" is told, in effect, they were wrong. Fix the demand: "Identify the test that its measure of success fails, explain why, and repair it." (card and narration), and add one clause to 12b: "It also names no cause, but the clearest failure is its measure." About 3 s. Students should be rewarded for applying the tests, not for guessing the intended one.

2. **Should fix, scenes 7 and 8 stack three checklists on one decision.** Students meet the five table questions plus two (scene 3), three evaluation questions (scene 7) and five tests (scene 8) for nearly the same judgement; "who does it reach" and "how would we know" appear in both lists. Show the overlap instead of a new list: in scene 8, as tests 3 and 5 write, their boxes take a thin amber link to scene 7's questions 1 and 3 with a grey tag "same question, now for your own idea". Narration: "Two of these you've just used: who it reaches, and how you'd know." (about 3 s). Students then hold one evaluating habit applied twice, which v14 and v18 can recall instead of adding further lists (see the summary).

3. **Note, scene 3 "five questions plus two more" next to a seven-question frame.** Two different sevens on screen at once. Label the data card "table questions" and keep the frame titled "the issue: seven questions".

4. **Note, scene 13 callback has no time to do it.** "Now run your sentence from the start through the five tests" is followed straight by the handoff. Add a 5 s quiet hold with the checklist's boxes empty, or say "Pause here if you want to test it."

5. **Note, length.** About 566 s by the author's count; findings 1 and 2 add about 6 s, still under 570 s only if a reserve cut is used.
