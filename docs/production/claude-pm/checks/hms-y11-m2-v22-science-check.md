## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 3 narration and key note 3).** "Task cohesion ... it's linked more closely to performance. Social cohesion ... it's linked more closely to turning up." Carron, Colman, Wheeler and Stevens (*J Sport Exerc Psychol* 2002, 24:168 to 188) found a moderate to large cohesion-performance relationship in sport; the separate task and social effect sizes could not be confirmed from the full text here, and my recollection is that both were related to performance. The adherence link for social cohesion is well supported. Safer narration: "Task cohesion is unity around a shared goal. Social cohesion is liking each other and valuing the time together, and it's strongly linked to turning up. Both are linked to performance." Key note 3: "Norms; task cohesion and social cohesion both help; social keeps people coming." The two failure examples that follow still work.
2. **Note (scene 2).** "exercising alone ... the one people tend to drop": group settings show better adherence in Carron, Hausenblas and Mack (*J Sport Exerc Psychol* 1996), though some trials found home-based programs with support adhered well. "the one many people drop" is safer.

## Verdict
Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 1 should fix, 1 note.
