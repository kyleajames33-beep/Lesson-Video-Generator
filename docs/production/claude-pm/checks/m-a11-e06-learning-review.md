## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 5 (why taking logs of both sides is allowed).** Narration: "Take the natural log of both sides." This is the first time students do it, and a student starting Year 11 will ask whether that is a legal move, as with squaring both sides. The memory aid check notes the condition ("both sides positive before taking logs") but the learner never hears it. Fix: "Both sides are the same positive number, so their natural logs are equal too. Take the natural log of both sides." Add the grey chip "both sides > 0". Students should know why the step is valid and when it is not.

2. **Should fix, scenes 5, 6 and feedback 2 (brackets that the ear cannot hear).** The lesson's own key slip is a missing bracket, yet the narration reads several bracketed expressions without one:
   - Scene 5: "t times ln zero point seven minus ln zero point eight, equals ln eight hundred minus ln twelve hundred" sounds like t ln 0.7 − ln 0.8.
   - Scene 6: "ln three equals x times two ln five, minus ln three" and "x is ln three over two ln five minus ln three".
   - Feedback 2: "x equals three ln two, over ln three minus ln two".

   Fix: say the grouping. Scene 5: "t times the bracket, ln zero point seven minus ln zero point eight, equals ln eight hundred minus ln twelve hundred." Scene 6: "ln three equals x times the bracket, two ln five minus ln three. So x is ln three, divided by that whole bracket." Feedback 2: "x equals three ln two, divided by the bracket, ln three minus ln two." The rows on screen already show the brackets; the narration should match them.
