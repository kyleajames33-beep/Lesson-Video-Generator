## Verification and recalculation

One pack/day x 20 years=20 pack-years; pack-years is a cumulative exposure shorthand, not proof equal schedules give equal risk. Tobacco carcinogens can form DNA adducts; unrepaired lesions can become mutations, but not every smoke chemical directly binds DNA. COPD combines chronic airflow obstruction mechanisms; inflammation/protease injury can destroy alveolar walls, reduce surface area and impair clearance. CO binds haemoglobin strongly and reduces oxygen carriage; nicotine increases sympathetic demand, while endothelial injury/inflammation and thrombosis contribute to vascular events. The simplified three pathways are defensible, but do not teach that narrowed lumen alone explains all heart attacks.

Cancer Australia's current mesothelioma guidance gives a common 20-to-50-year latency. Other Australian agencies use 20-to-60; these are typical ranges, not mutually exclusive hard cutoffs. The 31 December 2003 national asbestos ban did not remove asbestos in existing buildings. New exposures can still occur. The hook's phrase "after exposure stopped" must be attached to a former worker, not a claim that all exposure stopped nationally in 2003. Mesothelioma is a mesothelial cancer, usually pleural, not ordinary lung carcinoma.

NCI supports a more-than-additive smoking/asbestos interaction for lung cancer, and does not support applying it to mesothelioma. "50 to 90 times" is not a universal multiplier; an old cohort estimate needs its population and comparator. The design wisely omits the number. BRAF prevalence varies by subtype and study. NCBI's medical genetics chapter gives approximately 50 to 60%, so the site's "about 60%" cannot be logged as a clear falsehood merely because other summaries give about half. Prefer a dated subtype-specific estimate if a number is needed; none is spoken here.

AIHW's current melanoma commentary estimates 17,400 cases in 2025, with 2022-to-2025 projected, and calls Australia one of the highest-incidence countries. This differs from older 20,100 projections; do not mix vintages. The design gives no melanoma case count or UV percentage. UV-induced pyrimidine lesions and failed repair support the attempt; melanoma originates in pigment cells, which should stay named in the answer visual. SunSmart's five actions are correctly recalled.

No independent proof was found for the site's exact IARC-2020/22-cancer attribution. This is an unresolved attribution, not proof that tobacco causes fewer cancers. No new unsupported Australian death count is used in the design.

## Findings

### 1. should fix: 9

Text: "around one adult in ten"

The latest primary Australian release dated 17 July 2026 reports daily smoking in 2025 as 5.8% of adults, and 5.6% of people aged 14 and over. The draft's undated current rate is outdated.

Repair: Use "In 2025, about six in a hundred Australian adults smoked daily" and cite the release on screen/author note. Preserve adult versus 14+ denominators.

### 2. should fix: 10a and feedback 10b

Text: "teaching data, shaped on Australian trends"

The table supplies unsupported specific historical Australian measurements. The smoking series and lung-cancer series lack sampling definition and age standardisation.

Repair: Use a plainly fictional teaching cohort with illustrative trends, or rebuild from cited Australian series. Do not describe invented 1945/1982 values as Australian rates.

### 3. should fix: 10b

Text: "quit or never started"

The stated latency mechanism cannot assign past smoking mutations to never-smokers.

Repair: Use the replacement in the learning review.

### 4. should fix: 6

Text: "Most tickets lose, because the damage is repaired."

The mechanism is presented as explaining risk for all three pathways. COPD and atherosclerosis also involve non-mutational inflammatory and tissue injury.

Repair: Scope the lottery to cancer. Keep separate causal explanations for other diseases.
