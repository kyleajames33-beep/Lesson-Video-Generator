## Confirmed
- **8c/8d constraint (flagged).** Netball's rules give a player 3 s to pass or shoot after catching (World Netball, *Rules of Netball*, current edition) and contain no time limit for moving the ball into the circle; there is no shot clock in standard netball. "a ten-second clock to get the ball into the circle, in a smaller space" is therefore a genuine training constraint stricter than the game. The marking's refusal of a 3 s hold limit alone is correct.
- Skill early, conditioning last by default, maximal-speed exception (2, 3). Consistent with the coaching literature and the site; attention is limited (Year 11).
- Practice-type recall (2): blocked practice looks better in session and retains and transfers worse (contextual interference; Shea and Morgan 1979; smaller effects in sport, Barreiros and others 2007). Consistent with hms-y11-m2-v18 and v19.
- Strategy and tactic (4), constraints-led tactical training, reflection and evaluation (5). Correct.
- 8a/8b: correct. Memory aids sound. No people.
