2. **should fix, scene 7.** Date the under-40 outcome statement and identify invasive melanoma. The primary Queensland study covers 1995 to 2014 and finds declining age-specific invasive melanoma incidence below 40, particularly later birth cohorts. It does not establish the precise national onset date from that window. [Primary registry study](https://doi.org/10.1002/ijc.31141). The national AIHW commentary describes a late-1990s decrease; use that date for the national statement. [AIHW](https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/cancer/cancer-data-in-australia/contents/cancer-data-commentaries/melanoma-of-the-skin-in-australia).
SunSmart's 1988 start and the 1987 to 2017 Melbourne repeated surveys are supported by the [primary PLOS study](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002932). The TV-advertising association is supported by the [2008 original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18201638/). These are observational associations, consistent with the lesson's package-level conclusion. Do not relabel them as randomised causal estimates.
3. **should fix, attempt 2 feedback.** Screening changes ascertainment of diabetes as well as health. State comparable testing and adequate follow-up before treating new diagnoses as an outcome measure.
