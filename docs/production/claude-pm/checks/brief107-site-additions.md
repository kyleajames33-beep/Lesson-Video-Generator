### P01 safety check before pink artwork

The current design already makes the teacher add the indicator. Public NSW CSIS documents support risk assessment, minimum quantities, PPE and teacher controls. They also describe phenolphthalein in school demonstrations. The current chemical-specific Appendix D entry redirects to NSW Education staff authentication and was not accessible. Student handling permission, any additional current chemical-specific restriction and the school's own risk assessment therefore remain unverified. An inaccessible entry is not evidence that the teacher-added method is prohibited. The nonprocedural pink plates depict the result of the teacher-added method and do not depend on student permission to handle indicator. They can be commissioned with this check recorded; this is not permission to run the practical in a school.

Sources: [NSW CSIS Volume 2, Appendix D instructions and handling rules](https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/asset-management/chemical-safety/5._Volume_2_Appendices.pdf), [protected live Appendix D](https://education.nsw.gov.au/asset-management/chemicals/appendices-to-volume-2/appendix-d-generic-assessment-advice-for-chemicals-used-in-secondary-schools), [NSW science safety page](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/science/science-safety-and-compliance).
