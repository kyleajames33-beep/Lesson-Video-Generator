## bio-m7-g03

- **Question 3 card and narration:** `Rabies vaccine and rabies antibodies also protect against this virus` promises protection in an individual case. Current Australian guidance recommends both for a non-immune person after a bat scratch, but NSW Health documented a fatal breakthrough infection despite treatment given according to the national guidance. Correct the card and spoken line to say that rabies vaccine and human rabies immunoglobulin are used to prevent Australian bat lyssavirus infection and are highly effective in almost all cases. Sources: [Australian Immunisation Handbook](https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/contents/vaccine-preventable-diseases/rabies-and-other-lyssaviruses), [NSW Health expert panel](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/Infectious/factsheets/Documents/ablv-expert-panel.pdf).

## bio-m7-b10

- **Scene 7 narration, screen tag and key notes:** `Contacts who aren't immune go into quarantine` and the 18-day screen tag apply exclusion to all susceptible contacts and to health settings as a single rule. NSW Health calls for advice and assessment of contacts. The 18-day exclusion rule applies to susceptible contacts in childcare and primary schools after exposure outside the facility; adults in ordinary work or tertiary education do not always require exclusion, and healthcare facilities make a local management plan with the public health unit. Correct the narration to `Some susceptible contacts need quarantine, depending on their exposure and setting.` Limit the 18-day screen tag and notes to the stated NSW childcare and primary school rule. Source: [NSW Health measles control guideline, contact management and isolation](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/Infectious/controlguideline/Pages/measles.aspx).
- **Attempt 2 and feedback:** The question gives the threshold as `80% ... immune`, but the town data are `92% ... vaccinated` and `70% ... vaccinated`. The feedback treats vaccinated and immune as identical. A vaccine may fail for some recipients, so the given vaccination percentages do not establish the immune percentages without a vaccine effectiveness assumption. Correct the stem to give the proportions **immune** in each town, or supply enough vaccine effectiveness data to calculate them. Then match the feedback to that condition. Source: [CDC measles vaccine effectiveness, which illustrates that vaccination and immunity are different measures](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/hcp/vaccine-considerations/).
