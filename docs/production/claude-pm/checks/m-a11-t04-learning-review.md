## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 2 (the copy formulas are announced, and t03's 60° is not named).** "In the second quadrant you get a hundred and eighty minus A ... In the third, a hundred and eighty plus A ... In the fourth, three sixty minus A." The reflection shows where each copy lands, but nothing says why the label is 180° − A rather than, say, 90° + A. A student will ask how to get the formula for a new quadrant. Also, root ruling 17 says t04 names t03's "sixty degrees with the negative x-axis" as the related angle; scene 2 defines the term from 150° only. Fix. Narration, after the definition: "Last time, a hundred and twenty degrees made sixty degrees with the negative x-axis. That sixty degrees is its related angle." Then: "In the second quadrant the copy stops A short of a half turn, so it's a hundred and eighty minus A, a hundred and fifty degrees. In the third it goes A past a half turn, a hundred and eighty plus A, two hundred and ten degrees. In the fourth it stops A short of a full turn, three sixty minus A, three hundred and thirty degrees." Visual: on each copy a short blue bracket of size A from the 180° (or 360°) mark to the copy's ray, beside the existing label. Students should be able to rebuild each form from "how far from the x-axis".

2. **Should fix, scene 7 and the memory card (root ruling 18).** The ruling says to spell out "All Stations To Central" once and then use the letters. Scene 3 says it once, as ruled, but scene 7's narration says it again ("All Stations To Central tells you the positive ratios") and the card's main line carries it. Fix. Scene 7: "A, S, T, C tells you the positive ratios, quadrant by quadrant." Card: "Size from the related angle, sign from the quadrant: A S T C." Root may decide that the framed card can keep the phrase as the memory aid's written form; if so, record it as an exception to ruling 18.

3. **Should fix, feedbacks 1 and 2 (two spoken lines that are hard to hear).** Feedback 1: "Tangent of minus one thirty-five degrees is minus tangent one thirty-five, which is minus minus one, so plus one." By ear, "minus minus one" blurs, and tan 135° = −1 is given no reason, in the very term whose slip is crossed. Feedback 2: "the root of two eighty-nine minus sixty-four" can be heard as "two point eight nine". Fix, at about the same length. Feedback 1: "Tangent a hundred and thirty-five degrees is minus one, from the second quadrant. Tangent of minus a hundred and thirty-five degrees has the opposite sign, so it's plus one." Feedback 2: "With x as minus eight and r as seventeen, Pythagoras gives fifteen for the third side, so y is minus fifteen." Both feedbacks sit at 28 to 29 s, so keep the replacements no longer than the lines they replace. Students should hear each sign decided by a reason.
