## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 5 (the derivation skips its hardest step in speech).** Narration: "Divide by a, and move c over a across. Half of b over a is b over two a. Add its square to both sides, and the left side becomes x plus b over two a, all squared. Put the right side over four a squared, and it's b squared minus four a c, over four a squared." The right side before the common denominator (b²/4a² − c/a) is shown on row 3 but never spoken, and rows 1 and 2 have no cue words, so they appear before the student hears them. This is a first meeting with the derivation for most Year 11 students. Fix (about 60 more characters): "Start with a x squared plus b x plus c equals zero. Divide every term by a, and move c over a **across**. Half of b over a is b over two a, so add its square to both sides. The left side becomes x plus b over two a, all **squared**. The right side is b squared over four a squared, minus c over a. Over a common denominator of four a squared, that's b squared minus four a c, over four a **squared**." Rows 1 and 2 land on "across". The student should be able to follow each line and see where b² − 4ac comes from, which w05 relies on.

2. **Should fix, scene 8 (memory aid drops a condition).** Narration: "Factorise if you can, complete the square if b is even, and the formula always works." Scene 7 said a = 1 and b even. A student will complete the square on 3x² + 6x − 1 and struggle with fractions. Fix: "Factorise if you can, complete the square if a is one and b is even, and the formula always works."
