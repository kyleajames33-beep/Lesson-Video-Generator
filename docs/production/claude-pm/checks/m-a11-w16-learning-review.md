## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 6 (the logical link is missing).** Narration: "But a quadratic or a line that isn't zero everywhere has at most two zeros. So every coefficient of the difference is zero, which means the matching coefficients are equal." The step that this difference has a zero at every x is left for the student to supply, so "so" does not follow from what was said. Fix: "But a quadratic or a line that isn't zero everywhere has at most two **zeros**. This difference is zero at every x, so it must be zero everywhere, and every coefficient in it is zero. That means the matching coefficients are equal." The visual already shows the flat line "zero everywhere"; let it land on that phrase. The student should see why equating coefficients is allowed, not just that it is.
