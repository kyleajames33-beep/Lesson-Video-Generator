## Findings

1. **Note (currency), scene 4 teaches a lagging rebate as the default.** The mechanism is correct and stated as a condition ("If it rises more slowly..."), but a 2026 student will hear that bulk billing has risen since the incentives were raised in 2023 and extended to all patients in November 2025. Add one clause after "with no law ever announcing a fee": "And it can run the other way, when government lifts rebates or pays doctors to bulk bill." (about 3 s). Students then see the boundary can be defended as well as eroded, which scene 7 says.

2. **Note, 9a's card says "schedule fee" unglossed.** The narration says "the set fee" and the body never shows "schedule fee". Write "schedule fee (the set fee Medicare bases its rebate on)" on the card, or use "set fee" on both.

3. **Note, scene 5 has three government supports named by what they do.** "Premium rebate", "levy surcharge" (on screen) and "later = dearer" arrive in about 6 s, and "levy surcharge" is a term the narration avoids. Keep the screen wording the same as the speech: "tax penalty for high earners without cover".

4. **Note (science check), "community rating means insurers must charge everyone the same, whatever their health".** True for health status; premiums still vary by product, state and the late-joiner loading the scene has just named. "the same premium for the same policy, whatever their health" avoids an apparent contradiction with "higher premiums for late joiners".
