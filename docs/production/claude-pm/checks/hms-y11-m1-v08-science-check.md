# hms-y11-m1-v08 science check (health status of young people)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- Paradox (scenes 1, 2): young people have the lowest mortality and little chronic disease; mental and substance use disorders and injuries are the leading contributors to burden at 15 to 24 (AIHW, Australian Burden of Disease Study 2024). Road trauma and self-harm among the leading causes of death (AIHW). Correct.
- Trends both ways (scene 3, key note 2): daily smoking among young Australians down (AIHW NDSHS 2022 to 2023); young driver road deaths down over recent decades (BITRE road deaths data); reported psychological distress and mental disorders up (ABS National Study of Mental Health and Wellbeing 2020 to 2022 against 2007; 16 to 24 year olds with a 12-month mental disorder rose from about 26 per cent to about 39 per cent). The help-seeking caution is fair. No figures spoken; correct.
- Risk and protective factors (scenes 4, 5): "one trusted adult is a consistent protective factor" (softened by root) is supported.
- Practice 10a/10b and 10c/10d: correct; no reasonable alternative crossed.
- Cultural content (scene 8): causes and strengths stated generally ("forced removal of children" is a general historical cause, not a named event or people). Compliant.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 8).** "they cluster, so their effects multiply": same issue as v05. Corrected: "and they cluster, so their effects compound."
2. **Should fix (scene 8).** "community-controlled health services work because communities run them": unqualified effectiveness claim (see v04 finding 1). Corrected: "and community-controlled health services are trusted because communities run them."
3. **Note (scene 8).** "Connection to culture, community and Country protects social and emotional wellbeing" is supported (Gee and others 2014 SEWB model; national survey evidence linking cultural connection to wellbeing). No change.

## Verdict
Pass with 2 should fix. 0 blockers, 2 should fix, 1 note.
