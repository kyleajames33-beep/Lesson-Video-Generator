**Verdict: should-fix** (0 blockers, 2 should fix, 2 notes).

Framing each key feature as a fix for an earlier failing ("fixes: only poor countries") gives students a reason for each feature rather than a list. "Outcomes here, actions elsewhere" (SDG 3's rungs sending arrows out to other goals) is a sharp idea that sets up v20. `TwoWayLoop`, with a green token for the virtuous direction, a grey token on the same circle for the trap, and an amber pin that turns the loop from any node, explains bidirectional causation in a way students can redraw. The six mechanisms from education to health include "not because educated people are better" with the crossed chip, which removes a common and unkind misreading. The rule against official SDG colours is kept, and the school wall plate was inspected and rejected for that reason.

## Findings

1. **Should fix, scenes 2, 4 and 6 re-teach Year 11 content as if new.** hms-y11-m1-v17 taught: the goals were adopted in 2015 with targets to 2030 and replaced the earlier goals; they apply to every country including Australia; they are integrated (indivisible); they add environment and inequality; "leave no one behind" means reporting by group; SDG 3's targets (maternal and child deaths, epidemics, chronic disease and mental health, road deaths, universal health coverage); the girls' education mechanism for child survival; and "the goals aren't law... they coordinate and make failure visible", with the memory line "the goals can't compel anyone". v19 recalls only "the ring with health in the middle" and then teaches the same features, the same targets, the mother's-education mechanism and a near-identical memory line ("they can't make anyone act") as new. This breaks the no-double-teaching rule and costs about two minutes a returning student does not need. Fix: in scene 2, recall the Year 11 changes in one sentence with grey "earlier" tags on universal, indivisible, comprehensive and leave no one behind, and spend the time on what is new here (targets and indicators as checkable progress; time-bound but not binding as the design trade-off; Australia's own gaps). In scene 3, mark SDG 3's clusters as recall ("you met these targets in Year 11; here's where Australia stands") and keep "outcomes, not actions". In scene 4, say "In Year 11 you traced how keeping girls at school saves children's lives. Here are the six routes underneath that." In scene 6, recall "the goals can't compel" in one clause and teach the limits that are new (scattered effort, selective reporting, small-group measurement gaps). Replace memory line 2 with something new to Year 12, for example "Outcomes in goal 3; actions in the other goals." Students who did Year 11 then build instead of repeating, and students who did not still get each feature in plain words.

2. **Should fix, 8c's expected answer is the body's own example.** Scene 5 says "you can intervene anywhere and turn the loop, like with vision and hearing checks or meals at school", and drops pins labelled with those actions. 8c (remote children with repeated ear infections and low attendance) is answered with "regular ear and hearing checks at school". The case is new, but the action is given. Use a case where the action is not one of the pins, for example: "In a town, many teenagers miss school to care for a parent with a chronic illness, and adult diabetes rates are high. Using the relationship between SDG 3 and SDG 4, explain how ONE action could improve both health and education." Feedback keeps its shape: an action on the parent's care (community nursing, carer support) frees the teenager to attend (SDG 4) and controls the parent's disease (SDG 3); over time, completion turns the loop. Students should show they can find the intervention point themselves.

3. **Note, the hook mirrors Year 11's.** hms-y11-m1-v17 opened on a fixed budget split between clinics and keeping girls at school; v19 opens on a clinic, a school program or housing. Acknowledge it: "You've argued this once, in Year 11. This time, three options." The hook's "what would you need to see in ten years?" box is never filled; fill it in scene 6 with "outcomes by group, checked against the targets".

4. **Note, scene 2 is long (about 900 characters) with five glossed terms.** Finding 1's recall shortens it.
