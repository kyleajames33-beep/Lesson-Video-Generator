# Root rulings: Year 11 Maths Advanced t, g and s packs (17 November 2026)

Root read both pack reports (trigonometry t01 to t16, route inputs 4; transformations g01 to g06 and probability s01 to s11, route inputs 5). These rulings bind the designs.

## Trigonometry
1. **Order.** Keep degrees through t07 and introduce radians in t08, as the packs propose. The site's radian-first order is not followed.
2. **Possible splits (t12, t14, t15).** The designer splits only if the video cannot be taught well under 570 s, and records the new boundaries and joins. Suffixes a and b (for example t15a, t15b).
3. **Exact values with surds.** The existing ExactFraction part takes whole-number fractions only. A small surd formatter is a builder kit need, not a design constraint.

## Transformations
4. **Notation.** Use the syllabus and Year 12 form y = k f(a(x + b)) + c throughout g01 to g05. The site's af(b(x − h)) + k is not used on screen. Where a site source uses it, say nothing about it in narration; the site correction covers it.
5. **Order of transformations (g04).** Teach the correct order and never show the site's translate-then-dilate reading (S11-G03). The "order matters" task uses two moves that really do give different graphs (S11-G04).
6. **Splits.** g05 may split (for example by function family) only under the ruling 2 test. g06 builds on w24's circle without re-deriving it.
7. **Join.** g01 opens on t16's final frame (written by the t09 to t16 designer). g01 relies only on w15, w21, w31 and e01 from earlier strands.

## Probability and data
8. **No gambling.** No lotteries, betting, pokies or casino games as anchors or questions. Dice, coins, spinners and cards may appear only as neutral sample-space objects, never with money at stake. Leave out the site's Oz Lotto copy.
9. **Notation.** Use the syllabus notation as in the crosswalk: |A| for the number of elements (n(A) may be mentioned once as an alternative), U for the universal set, A̅ or A′ exactly as the crosswalk writes the complement. Speak set symbols in words.
10. **s02 and s03 stay separate.** No merge: the union counting rule earns its own video.
11. **Possible splits (s07, s08).** Ruling 2 test applies.
12. **s09.** Teach the correct reason that P(X = x) = 0 for a continuous variable (probability comes from area over an interval, and a single value has no width), never the site's "infinitely many values" reason (S11-P02).
13. **s11.** Use the corrected quartiles (S11-P03 to S11-P05).
14. **Year 12.** The packs' Year 12 delivery notes (g01 to g05, s04, s05, s06, s09 to s11) are requirements.
