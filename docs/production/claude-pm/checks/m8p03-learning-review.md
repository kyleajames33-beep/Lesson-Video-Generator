## Findings

### 1. blocker: Decisions, scenes 4 and 10, risk table and metadata

Text: "students use them by the drop"

The design explicitly permits lead unknowns and knowns in student wells. This contradicts the settled no-student-lead decision. A teacher filling the bottle does not remove student exposure.

Repair: Keep lead test observations as data or a teacher demonstration. Replace any lead student unknown with a teacher-approved non-lead cation from c03a. Replace scene 10 lead handling with: "Lead results are provided as data; students do not handle lead compounds." Change scene 4 to "less silver and barium". This is shorter; no added time.

### 2. should fix: 9

Text: "Each test should detect only the ion it's meant to."

This defines validity as perfect reagent selectivity even though the lesson uses non-specific hydroxide, sulfate and silver tests. A student could judge every taught test invalid.

Repair: Replace with: "Valid? The sequence should identify the ion it is meant to, while controlling interference." Screen: "Validity: does this sequence identify the intended ion?" Keep the existing interference controls. Length-neutral.

### 3. should fix: 5 and 8

Text: "the colourless flame rules out calcium"

A negative flame is made decisive before the later detection-limit warning. The known magnesium control proves the hydroxide result, not the sensitivity of the calcium flame test.

Repair: Include a calcium positive flame control tested with the same loop and burner before interpreting the absence. Replace "rules out calcium" with "does not support calcium at the tested concentration". Recover time by dropping scene 7's last sentence, already carried by its column headings. Retain the declared single-ion list condition.
