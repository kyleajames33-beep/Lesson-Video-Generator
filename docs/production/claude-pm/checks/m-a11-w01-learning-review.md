## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 4 (counts become shares without warning).** Narration: "One divided by three is a third, so three to the minus one is one third. Here's what that means on the cube." Scenes 1 and 3 called one layer 3² (nine blocks). The visual first shows a third of a single block, then a layer as one third of the cube. A student will ask: "Is 3⁻¹ a third of a block or a whole layer, and how can the layer be 3² and 3⁻¹ at once?" The unit has changed from blocks to the whole cube, and nobody says so. Fix: after "so three to the minus one is one third", add: "So far we've counted in blocks. Now measure each piece as a share of the whole cube instead." In the visual, label the dashed sliced block "1/3 of a block" before the share board replaces it, and head the share board "share of the cube". The student should understand that the number a piece represents depends on the unit, while 3⁻¹ always means one third.
