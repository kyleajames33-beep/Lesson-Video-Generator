## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 2 introduces six glossed terms plus the hook's three classifications in one scene (about 1,000 characters).** Direct, indirect, soft tissue, hard tissue, acute and overuse arrive in about 45 s, then the three hook tokens are classified on all three axes. A student is still placing "indirect" when the shin is called "indirect and overuse". Fix: split at "Now the hook" into 2a (the three axes, with a 1 s settle on the three sliders and their "tells you" tags) and 2b (the three tokens set the sliders and the hook's slots fill). No narration change. Students should hold the three axes before applying them.

2. **Should fix, narration and screen name internal video numbers.** Scene 6 says "as in v-seventeen" and "that spike on v-nineteen's load chart"; the board shows grey tags "v09" (scene 5), "v07, v09", "v17" and "v07" (scene 6). Students never see file ids; the brief says "earlier". Say "as you saw earlier" and "the spike on the load chart from last time", with "earlier" tags.

3. **Note, the dislocation overlay sits on a plate whose ball is painted in its socket.** Scene 3 shows the shoulder ball "displaced from its socket by a coded offset outline", but the painted humeral head stays seated, so the frame shows two heads, one in place. Grey the painted head (full-ink grey, as the brief requires), tag it "normal position", and slide the outline down and forward out of the socket on "forces a bone out of its joint". That matches root's ruling that an annotation on a painted plate is acceptable.

4. **Note, "overuse" sits on its own axis here, but the syllabus lists it alongside direct and indirect.** The syllabus text is "direct and indirect, soft and hard tissue or overuse", which some school texts teach as three causes (direct, indirect, overuse). The video's three-axis model (from the site) calls the shin "indirect and overuse" and 8b calls the bowler "indirect, overuse". Both readings can score; for the science check, confirm the marking accepts "overuse" given as the cause without "indirect", and consider one spoken clause, "Some books list overuse as a third cause; either way, say it built up over time."

5. **Note, the hook asks for three first actions and a judgement with a one-second hold.** Add 2 s with the slots lit.
