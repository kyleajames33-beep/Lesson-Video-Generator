## Should fix

**SF1. Buyer two's lay-by timing is slightly wrong and the reason is not shown.** Scene 5 says buyer two "has two hundred dollars, and can put aside two hundred a fortnight ... collects the TV in about four months", and the author note uses 1600 ÷ 200 = 8 fortnights. If his $200 today is the first lay-by payment, he needs 7 more fortnights, 14 weeks, about three and a quarter months. A student who checks will be confused. Fix: say "collects the TV in about three months" and show "200 now + 7 × 200 = $1600, 14 weeks" on the token card, or make his $200 a separate buffer and keep "8 fortnights, about four months" with that stated. Either is fine; the numbers must match what is said.

## Notes

- N1. Scene 3 says "The seller doesn't call it interest. It's built into the repayments." In Australia most terms contracts do state an interest rate. The point the lesson needs is that the repayments hide the extra, whatever it is called. Suggested wording: "Whatever the seller calls it, the extra is built into the repayments, so the only way to see it is to add everything up."
