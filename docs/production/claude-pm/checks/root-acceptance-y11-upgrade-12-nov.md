## Rules for every Y11 Physics and Biology build (they override the design text)
R1. **One frame and mistake style.** Answers sit in dark-ink frames with ink ticks. Mistakes get a slate scribble and a slate hand tag. "Not present", "not possible" and "not recorded" get a slate cross. Use this everywhere in Y11 Physics and Biology, whatever a design says about green frames or red crossings. Green and red then stay free for their subject meanings (charge, oxygen, chlorophyll, blood, dyes). Builders apply it; no design edits are needed.

R2. **Handoff and final frames are drawn solid.** Wherever a design says "faint", "ghost", "pale", "dim", "glow", "greyed" or "wash" for a final or opening frame, builders draw it solid in full ink. Where a painted plate exists for the object, builders use the plate (Physics v26's coils use the wide and spread coil plates, as v27 does). The two frames of every join must match.

R3. **Oxygen colour.** In atom and molecule drawings, oxygen is red (the usual molecular-model colour). In particle-box models, oxygen may take a role colour, which the colour key states. This settles Biology FA1 U7.

R4. **Narration added to reach 5,500 characters is accepted** (Physics FA3 U7; Biology FA1 U5, FA2 U3 and FA3 U3). Each changed design still needs Codex's error check before voicing, as usual. Two spoken claims need a source in that check, or the clause goes:
