## Strengths to keep

- The hook (sealed soda water) is concrete and the question "has everything stopped?" is answered visibly in scene 2 with two live counters.
- "Equal rates, not equal amounts" is a real causal anchor, and returning to the molecule view with more dissolved than gas reinforces it.
- Static equilibrium is taught from the site's finished-reaction meaning, with the explicit "not slow dynamic" cross.
- The cap-off explanation gives a cause ("the room is huge, so very little comes back") and shows it with diverging counters.
- The attempt is on unseen cases with all conditions visible, and the salt-jar feedback names the "still means stopped" trap.
- Narration is natural for speech: varied sentence length, no stacked one-word sentences, no production jargon. Key notes are short and accurate.
