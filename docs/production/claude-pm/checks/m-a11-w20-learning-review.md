## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 4 (an undefined term, and a form the student has not met).** Narration: "The leading coefficient is one, times minus two, times minus two, which is plus four, so it finishes at the top right." "Leading coefficient" is used for the first time without meaning, and the tray x(30 − 2x)(20 − 2x) is not in the k(x − a)(x − b)(x − c) form just taught, so students cannot see why k is not 1. Fix: "This isn't quite in our form, because of the minus twos. Multiply the numbers in front of x in each bracket: one, times minus two, times minus two, which is plus four. That's the number in front of x cubed once it's all multiplied out, so it acts like k, and the curve finishes at the top **right**."

2. **Should fix, feedback 2 (about 32 s).** The card asks for k and the equation, so factored form is enough. Delete "or two x cubed minus ten x squared plus four x plus **sixteen**" and move the cue to the factored line: "So y equals two, times x plus one, times x minus two, times x minus four." Keep the expanded form on screen in grey. That brings the feedback to about 28 s.
