## Findings

1. **Should fix:** The summary “without organelles” contradicts the lesson’s own ribosomes and the v02 category. Use “without membrane-bound organelles” for this bacterium and remove the claim that small size means compartments are unnecessary in all bacteria. Some prokaryotes do have specialised internal membrane systems. Keep the short-distance explanation rather than a universal absence claim. Shared learning finding; use the named summary cut.

2. **Should fix:** Scene 11 and feedback animate a closed plasmid ring travelling along a pilus. That makes the transferred object a complete double-stranded ring; conjugative transfer commonly passes a single DNA strand and each cell synthesises its complement. The donor-keeps-copy idea is correct. Replace the travelling closed ring with a thin DNA strand; show complete plasmid rings in donor and recipient after copying. No new narration or time is needed.

3. **Should fix:** Attempt 13c asks how B became resistant, but the evidence only shows survival after mixed culture. Transfer through a pilus is a plausible explanation, not uniquely established: the stem does not state that A carries a transferable plasmid or that spontaneous change/other DNA transfer has been excluded. Ask “Explain how plasmid transfer could account for this result” and retain the expected pathway, or add the specific experimental evidence. The short could-explain replacement is length-neutral.

## Confirmed checks and sources

The nine required structures are present, with a structure and function for each. Capsule is optional and usually polysaccharide; wall peptidoglycan differs from cellulose; membrane respiratory enzymes and cytosolic reactions support energy conversion. The wall-damage sequence is a dilute-medium example: keep water entry visible and label that condition, rather than implying lysis in every medium. Plasmids are distinct from the chromosome, replicate separately and can carry resistance. Some pili mediate DNA transfer; others attach. The flagellum rotates rather than lashes, and optional structures are labelled. “Some bacteria, ideal conditions, about twenty minutes” is suitably scoped; it is not a universal doubling time. The 3+4+2 mnemonic is a tour of the syllabus list, not a claim that all bacteria have every feature. With 1.5 mm DNA and 2 µm cell length, the ratio is 750, so the approximate thousandfold tag is an order-of-magnitude illustration, not a measured value for an unidentified species; “hundreds of times” is a safer unnumbered alternative without extra time.
