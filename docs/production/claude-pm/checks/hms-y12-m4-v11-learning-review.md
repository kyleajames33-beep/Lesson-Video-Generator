This is one of the strongest designs in the module. The fitness and fatigue picture is the right model for a concept students usually memorise: fatigue hides fitness, fatigue clears faster, so the green performance line rises into the gap and peaks on the day, then falls when the axis extends ("too long"). Cutting intensity shrinks the peak ("rested but slow"), so both taper errors are visible consequences of one run. Two lines across 52 weeks (volume moving far more than intensity) give "the year is mostly a volume decision" in one picture. The sub-phase ladder that loses its bottom rungs and wobbles is a good answer to the "jump straight to game speed" school error. The comparison grows from one card ("calendar: chosen or given"), which models a compare answer with a cause rather than a list.

The marking's reason ("without missing competition") shows the logic; keep it, and make sure "finally" stays on the card.
