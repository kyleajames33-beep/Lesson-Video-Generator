## Findings

### S1. Should fix: hydration does not use "plenty of water"

- Exact text (scene 3): "To add water to ethene, we used steam, so plenty of water, at high pressure."
- Problem: the c04a and c06 process uses excess ethene, with steam kept below a 1 to 1 ratio because excess steam dilutes the phosphoric acid catalyst and can wash it off its support (Chemguide; c06 author note a). The narration contradicts the module's own earlier video.
- Replacement: as learning S1 ("To add water to ethene, we used steam, at high pressure. ... the alkene escapes from the hot mixture. ... adding water pushes it towards the alcohol ..."). The Le Chatelier statement stays, as a principle.

### S2. Should fix: "just one product" for alkene addition

- Exact text (scene 6, screen and key note 2): "An alkene plus H B r gives just one product."; "1 product"; "(one product)".
- Problem: propene and HBr give two possible products (c04a). The intended contrast is "no second product such as water".
- Replacement: learning S2 ("An alkene and H B r join into one molecule, with nothing left over.").

### M1. Minor: say "dilute" on the oxidation chip

- Exact text (scene 7 arrow chip): "K₂Cr₂O₇ / H⁺".
- Replacement: "K₂Cr₂O₇ / dilute H₂SO₄" (scene 7 only; later chips can stay "K₂Cr₂O₇ / H⁺"). Key note 3: "acidified (dilute H₂SO₄) K₂Cr₂O₇". This separates oxidation's dilute acid from dehydration's concentrated acid, the confusion in site correction 2.

### M2. Minor: permanganate in the key notes

- Exact text: the scene 7 silent chip only.
- Add to key note 3: "Acidified KMnO₄ also works (purple → colourless)." HSC items use either oxidant; a silent chip in a busy scene is not enough.

### M3. Minor: the aldehyde method uses limited oxidant

- Add to author note g: "To stop at the aldehyde, the oxidant is the limiting reagent (excess alcohol) and the aldehyde is distilled off as it forms (Chemguide)." Optional screen chip under the distillation plate: "excess alcohol". No narration change.

### M4. Minor: author note on the HX mechanism

- Add to author note h: "In acid the OH is protonated and leaves as water; the narration's 'a halogen pushes out the O H' is the HSC-level picture, consistent with 'the O H joins the H from H B r'."

### M5. Minor: complete combustion

- Exact text (scene 1): "Burning an alcohol breaks it right down, to carbon dioxide and water."
- Replacement: "Burning an alcohol completely breaks it right down, to carbon dioxide and water." (+11 characters.)
