Keep the question's judgement tied to the evidence. Source: [Kimchi-Sarfaty et al., Science 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17185560/), a primary study of a synonymous change affecting protein function; [BRCA1 synonymous substitution and splicing study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3353946/).
