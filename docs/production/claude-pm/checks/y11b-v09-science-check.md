## Confirmed science and calculations

Random thermal movement, a concentration difference, net versus individual movement and dynamic equilibrium are correctly distinguished. The 40/6 population conserves 46 particles, and 23/23 is the equal-population expectation for equal chamber volumes, not a demand for exact equality in every small random sample. Genuine random walks should fluctuate; do not overwrite their counts to force a scripted result. The crossing-rate correction makes the explanation consistent with that mechanism.

O₂/CO₂ crossing the lipid core, oxygen use maintaining an inward concentration difference, and CO₂ production supporting outward diffusion are suitable for the stated respiring-cell example. Root decision 6 accepts the introductory water-concentration account of osmosis; it is retained as an equal-pressure, sugar-blocking example rather than reopened as a thermodynamics lesson. Net water movement continues through water channels as well as the lipid route. [Original CHIP28 reconstitution research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021925819369534) directly confirms protein water channels. No active energy supply is required for that downhill water transport.

Carrier-mediated glucose transport is reversible and saturable. The stylised uptake curve should represent matched initial-rate conditions, keeping cell amount, internal loading and temperature fixed; it is an original question graph, not a reproduced clinical data set. The author-only Km range of about 1.5–3 mmol/L is plausible under particular uptake conditions, not a universal GLUT1 constant: [primary reconstitution work](https://febs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1046/j.1432-1033.2000.01788.x) reports different influx/efflux and reconstituted values. A Michaelis comparison gives at 10 mmol/L about 77–87 percent of maximum for Km3–1.5, and at 20 mmol/L about 87–93 percent. Draw a gradually flattening curve, not a mathematical hard cutoff at 10 mmol/L, and do not derive a measured Km from this sketch. The learner demand appropriately says “suggests” carrier mediation. Its fixed-condition simple-diffusion comparison has no carrier-saturation plateau; it is not an unlimited-rate claim under arbitrary conditions. Scope notes about the rate levelling off to the illustrated carriers, not every channel’s kinetics.

Both original tasks can be answered from their givens. A is simple diffusion, B osmosis for the described dilute-soil/more-concentrated-cell condition, and C facilitated passage through a channel. The current card already states the observed chloride direction, so finding 3 does not invalidate that classification. The protein route alone does not establish active transport.
