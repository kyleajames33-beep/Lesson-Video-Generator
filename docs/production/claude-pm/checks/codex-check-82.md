# Brief 82: Revision 2 error-only fact check

## chem-m6-c01.md

1. **Scene 7, gas tube: “there's no water anywhere” is false for the apparatus shown.** The design's cotton wool is wetted with concentrated aqueous ammonia and hydrochloric acid. The reaction is between diffusing NH₃(g) and HCl(g), and it does not need water as the reaction medium. Replace the narration sentence with “The gases react directly, without water as the reaction medium, so a definition limited to reactions in aqueous solution cannot describe it.” Replace the screen tag “no water” with “gas reaction: no aqueous reaction medium”. [Royal Society of Chemistry demonstration](https://edu.rsc.org/experiments/diffusion-of-gases-ammonia-and-hydrogen-chloride/682.article).

2. **Scenes 4 and 14, key note 2: methane does not disprove that acids contain hydrogen.** Methane shows that containing hydrogen is insufficient to identify an acid; the historical hydrogen theory was later refined by Liebig to require replaceable hydrogen. “Broken by: methane” and “each model fixed a failure of the one before” turn a necessary-condition claim into a claim that every hydrogen compound is acidic. Replace with “Hydrogen alone did not explain acidity: methane contains hydrogen but is not acidic; the idea also did not define bases.” Change the scene 4 stamp and key note to “limit: hydrogen alone does not identify an acid; no base definition.” [History of the hydrogen theory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10831364/), [2017 Chemistry syllabus, model limitations](https://kiama-h.schools.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/doe/sws/schools/k/kiama-h/content/learning-at-our-school/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-pdf.pdf).

## chem-m6-c02.md

1. **Scene 2 and key note 1: “Acids come in two families” is too broad.** This lesson divides the common inorganic acids it names into binary acids and oxoacids; the syllabus also includes organic acids, such as carboxylic acids. ## bio-m6-b02.md

1. **Scenes 3, 9 and 12, key notes and metadata: copying before repair is incorrectly stated as the only route to mutation.** The sequence described in scene 3 is a useful route for a damaged base to become a fixed base substitution. It does not cover the same lesson's other mechanisms: wrong joining of double-strand breaks can create a deletion or translocation, and viral DNA integration changes the host genome without first being copied. Replace the universal rule “damage only becomes a mutation if it's copied before it's repaired” with “Some base damage becomes a mutation when DNA is copied before repair; a wrongly repaired break or an inserted viral sequence can also make a lasting DNA change.” Revise the summary bar, memory, decision rule, notes and metadata to match. [Experimental double-strand break repair and rearrangements](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7959522/), [HBV integration and insertional mutagenesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4033461/).

2. **Scene 5 Visual: “UV reaches only the outer skin” is false.** Solar UVB is mostly absorbed in the epidermis, but UVA reaches the dermis. Replace the tag and the depth-bar label with “UV mainly affects skin; UVA reaches the dermis.” Keep the narration's “mainly damages skin cells.” [IARC, solar and ultraviolet radiation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK304366/), [measured UVA penetration through skin](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1809712/).

3. **Scene 11b: the crossed “classic mistake” says UV cannot break DNA strands.** UV chiefly produces pyrimidine photoproducts, but UVA can also cause DNA strand breaks indirectly through oxidative damage. Replace the crossed chip “UV breaks strands, like X-rays” and the narration's absolute “Saying UV breaks strands like X-rays do is the classic mistake” with a narrower error: “UV and X-rays damage DNA in exactly the same way.” Keep the main comparison of typical dimer formation versus ionisation. [Experimental UVA-induced double-strand breaks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3488256/), [experimental UV-induced single-strand breaks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1986263/).

4. **Draft YouTube description: the required lesson link is missing.** Insert this first line before the sunlight opening: “Full lesson, notes and practice questions: https://hscscience.com.au/subjects/biology/year12/module6/lesson02”.
