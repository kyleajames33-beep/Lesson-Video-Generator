## Points for the owner's cultural reviewer (wording only; not decided here)

Q3 is integrated as a real strategy evaluation, with the protocols as a criterion equal to the evidence, not as a curiosity or an add-on. The community decides, consents and is credited, and no real community, plant or knowledge is named. These wording points are for the reviewer:

1. **"Aboriginal community organisation"** (spoken) and **"Aboriginal community-controlled organisation"** (card). Choose one term. Community-controlled is the sector's own term.
2. **"which community members have used for generations to treat skin sores"**: confirm the phrasing, and that no wording implies a health deficit in the community.
3. **"Community knowledge holders will be named as co-authors"**: some communities prefer collective attribution, or not naming individuals. A possible wording is "credited as the community chooses (for example, as co-authors)".
4. **"an agreed share of any income"**: b15 taught that money, jobs or services can count and the community chooses. The reviewer may prefer "agreed benefits, chosen by the community".
5. **Finding 5's sentence** ("That's a test of one lab extract, not of the community's knowledge.") and the card's "grown in a laboratory dish": confirm they remove any implication that the community's use is harmful.
6. **The crossed mistake** ("the extract killed bacteria, so it's ready to treat skin sores") concerns the lab extract only. Confirm it does not read as doubting the knowledge, with finding 5 applied.
7. **The verdict "a strong partnership"**: a student judging a community's partnership. The reviewer may prefer "a partnership that follows the protocols".
8. **The question strip's "who pays?" for Q3**: the decision board lists "Q3 the community" under "who pays? who decides?". Confirm it cannot read as the community carrying costs; "who decides" alone may fit Q3 better.
9. **Finding 6's collaboration line** ("help plan the laboratory tests and review the results before anything is published"): confirm it is accurate to the protocols.
10. **Finding 11's leaf plate**: whether any plant image is acceptable beside a constructed case.
11. **The review notes' fallback** (replace Q3 with a non-Indigenous case): if used, b15 alone carries B7-IQ4-07, and the route needs a note.
