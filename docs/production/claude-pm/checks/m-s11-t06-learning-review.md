## Blocker

**T06-NY, scene 3, the season toggle set to "July" with "New York −5" and "1400 − 5 h = 0900 Wednesday".** In July New York is on daylight saving, UTC−4, so 1400 UTC is 1000, not 0900. More broadly, Sydney on +10 and New York on −5 never happen at the same moment: Sydney's standard time (April to October) falls inside New York's daylight saving (March to November). Scene 3 says "At one moment, three places", so it presents an impossible moment as real, in the very lesson whose memory aid says "check each city's daylight saving". A student who checks the July date will find the lesson wrong. Fix (either): (a) keep the arithmetic and set the toggle to "January" with Sydney +11 (1400 + 11 = 0100 Thursday) and New York −5 (0900 Wednesday); or (b) keep July and Sydney +10 and change New York to −4 (1400 − 4 = 1000 Wednesday), with the ladder tag "New York −4 (summer)". Option (a) also keeps "two different days". The student should see that the lesson obeys its own season rule.
