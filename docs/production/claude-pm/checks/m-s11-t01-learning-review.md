## Should fix

**T01-333, main question line, Decisions "Anchor" heading and the YouTube description.** They say "A 200-second song is not 3.33 minutes" and "isn't 3.33 minutes, because time steps in sixties". That is false: 200 s is 3.33 minutes as a decimal. Scene 4 then teaches that 3.75 h is a correct way to write a time, so a careful student meets a contradiction ("so is 3.33 min wrong or right?"). The narration itself is correct ("So is the song three minutes and thirty-three seconds? No."). Fix: reword those three places to "A 200-second song is 3.33 minutes, but not 3 minutes 33 seconds" (or "the player says 3:20, not 3:33"). The student should leave knowing that 3.33 min and 3 min 20 s are the same length written two ways.
