## Top findings

1. **r04 scene 4: the crossed slip is not a mistake.** 3.5 km ÷ 25 000 = 0.00014 km, which is exactly 14 cm, and scene 2 has just said a scale "works in whatever unit you measure with". Keep "convert first" as advice (ruler-ready centimetres), and cross only "0.00014 cm".
2. **r06b scene 5: "which paint is better value?" is left open.** Give the decision: one tin of B paints 60.25 m² only if it covers at least 60.25 ÷ 4 ≈ 15.06 m² a litre.
3. **r07 scene 2: "average width times length" is not joined to h/2 × (d_f + d_l).** Say (12 + 16) ÷ 2 = 14, 14 × 8 = 112, and that this is the same as 8/2 × (12 + 16). Take one named trim to hold the 12 s margin.
4. **r06a scene 3: why "scale the area once" fails.** "The area grows a hundred times in each direction" is muddled; say each side grows × 100, so the area grows × 100 × 100 = × 10 000, and show the two stretches.
5. **r03 scene 3: why the net's fraction matches the lake's.** Add: even mixing gives every part of the lake the same share of tagged fish, so a net full of fish is a small copy of the lake.
6. **r01 scene 4 and A2 (b): a/b is always crossed, then asked for.** Add one positive sentence ("One quarter does answer a different question: the cordial is one quarter as much as the water").
7. **r05 scenes 3 and 8: why 1 : 50 and not 1 : 40.** Say that 1 : 40 also fits but 1 : 50 is easier and leaves a margin; the worked example should not imply 40 is unfriendly.
8. **r06b scenes 4 and 6: screen rows a student cannot reproduce.** 30.13 × 2 = 60.26 (shown 60.25) and 23.53 × 2 = 47.06 (shown 47.05); nothing downstream changes.
9. **r06a: ruling 51's "example price" labels** on the benchtop, both carpets and the vinyl box, which the design (written before the ruling) shows in one chip only.
10. **r02 key note 3: "its terms" should be "its number of parts".**

Notable optional items: r02's stakes of $100 and $60 instead of $50 and $30 (a sixfold return on drinks is unrealistic; every share, $300 and $180, is unchanged; r01 scene 12 changes with it); r01's "topped up with one litre" heard as "filled to one litre"; r04's unexplained "now 1 : 12 500" tag; r06a's carpet "laid" against vinyl supply only; r06b's ten-number spoken task (the named trim also pays for finding 2).
