## Confirmed content and numerical checks

Hooke 1665 to clear cristae 1952 gives 287 years, so nearly 300/about 290 is sound. [Palade 1953](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1.4.188) reports cristae and their relation to the internal membrane. The 1930s electron-microscope introduction is accurate; [Max Planck's instrument archive](https://www.nobel.mpg.de/en/ernst-ruska) distinguishes 1931 prototype and 1939 commercial model. The scene 2 cell-theory summary is appropriate for the established sequence, not a claim that Hooke personally formulated both principles.

200 nm ÷ 0.2 nm = 1000. One mm = 10⁶ nm, so one nanometre is a millionth of a millimetre.50 µm cheek width/0.2 µm=250;1 µm mitochondrial width/0.2 µm=5;25 nm ribosome/200 nm=.125;7 nm membrane/200 nm=.035. The size contrast and feedback reasoning are consistent. Visible 400–700 nm and about 200 nm ordinary light resolution are reasonable introductory values; numerical aperture also matters, so the half-wavelength explanation is an approximation, not an exact universal equation. [Nikon's technical resolution account](https://www.microscopyu.com/microscopy-basics/resolution) supplies the distinction. Conventional light microscopy may detect a labelled tiny object without resolving its internal dimensions; the design's standard unlabelled-ribosome example concerns resolved detail, not all possible detection methods.

The best-instrument 0.2 nm figure is distinguished on screen from usual cellular 1–2 nm detail, not a promise that all cell images reach atomic resolution. TEM internal/SEM surface contrast is appropriate. SEM 3D refers to surface appearance/topography here, not an asserted single-image volumetric reconstruction. Mitochondrial membrane folding increases area for respiratory machinery; microscopy supplies structural evidence while the explanation uses previously learned function, not a claim that an image alone measured respiration.

Mesosomes are a valid preparation-artefact example. [Ebersold, Cordier, Lüthy 1981 primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6796029/) found the invaginations absent following cryofixation/freeze-substitution. The paired plates must communicate preparation changes rather than a structure vanishing during live observation. Millions of ribosomes is reasonable for a typical growing animal cell, not a universal cell count; [primary mammalian ribosome-inventory work](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2446-y) supports the order of magnitude. Digital photo enlargement ×4000/×400 = 10 changes apparent scale, not captured information. 5 µm chloroplast/0.2 µm=25;7 nm internal membrane is far below ordinary optical resolution, supporting the living-light/internal-TEM choices.
