## Trace from entry knowledge

- **Main question earned?** The hook names the two types that find amounts and says they are the hardest, so a student who has just done G03a knows what changed: now the unknown is an amount, not K or a direction. The bracket from card 3 to the dim card 4 ("same givens as type four, different demand") ties the new type to the one they just met. Yes.
- **Tiny-K question (re-worded).** The softened line, "You can use the quadratic formula if you like, but HSC questions are written so you shouldn't need it. Look for a perfect square or a tiny K.", gives the same instruction without an unprovable rule. Both demands stay on the task line through the countdown (from G03 Revision 5). Nothing answer-bearing.
- **Huge K and the amount added (reused).** Unchanged teaching. The amount-added worked example keeps its try-it (4c), its answer in moles and two named slips.
- **Decision board and key notes.** Two types only. The notes keep the conditions a student needs (the 5 per cent rule of thumb, reactants in the equation's ratio for huge K, K unchanged at the same temperature) and drop the G03a material.
- **Handoff.** "Next: practice questions, each with a trap in it." Matches G03c's hook ("a small trap early on").
