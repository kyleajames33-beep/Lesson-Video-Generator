## Trace from entry knowledge

- **Main question earned?** The hook names the two types that find amounts and says they are the hardest, so a student who has just done G03a knows what changed: now the unknown is an amount, not K or a direction. The bracket from card 3 to the dim card 4 ("same givens as type four, different demand") ties the new type to the one they just met. Yes.
- **Tiny-K question (re-worded).** The softened line, "You can use the quadratic formula if you like, but HSC questions are written so you shouldn't need it. Look for a perfect square or a tiny K.", gives the same instruction without an unprovable rule. Both demands stay on the task line through the countdown (from G03 Revision 5). Nothing answer-bearing.
- **Huge K and the amount added (reused).** Unchanged teaching. The amount-added worked example keeps its try-it (4c), its answer in moles and two named slips.
- **Decision board and key notes.** Two types only. The notes keep the conditions a student needs (the 5 per cent rule of thumb, reactants in the equation's ratio for huge K, K unchanged at the same temperature) and drop the G03a material.
- **Handoff.** "Next: practice questions, each with a trap in it." Matches G03c's hook ("a small trap early on").

**Verdict: pass for recording.** 0 blockers, 1 should-fix (applied), 3 notes. Design: `../lessons/chem-m5-g03b.md` Revision 2 (owner decision: G03a dropped, five-type map moved in, tiny K cut). Scope: the new hook-board, type-three-recall and decision-board, the new key notes, and what the cut changes; the seven reused take02 scenes keep the G03 Revision 5 independent review. Read as a student who has watched C1 to C8 (including C6a, C6b and C8) but not G03a. Not learner evidence; no stills, preview or audio. Independent review of the exact voiced preview and human listening remain pending. U+2014 search of new copy: none.

- **Main question earned?** The hook starts from what the student has (three types in the C6 videos), adds two, and names each once by what it gives and what it finds, with where it was taught. The student knows which parts are recall and which are new (huge K, the amount added). Yes.
- **Huge K without the tiny-K scenes.** The recorded 3a opens "A huge K is the mirror image." With the tiny-K scenes cut and the hook moving on through types 4 and 5, "mirror image" of what? The hook alone names tiny K but not what it does. Repair (should-fix S1, applied): new scene 2, type-three-recall (168 characters), recalls the C6b drop and test in two sentences, with the C6b iodine thumbnail and a mirror line on screen, immediately before 3a. The recall works because C6b is entry knowledge.
- **Type 4 and the "Q video".** The student has C8; type 4 is named, shown on the board with Q below K forward and Q above K reverse, and not practised here. G03c and G03d practise the other types; no learner-facing gap.
- **Decision board.** All five rows use "You see / You do" with typical question wording, which is the transfer skill the main question asks for. One slip per row keeps the reading load low; the board is spoken row by row, so the student is not reading a full table cold.
- **Key notes.** Five short lines, one per type, plus the two memory aids. The 5 per cent condition stays in learner copy because dropping it would teach an untested shortcut.
