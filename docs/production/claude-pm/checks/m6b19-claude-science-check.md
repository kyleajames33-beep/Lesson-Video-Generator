## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **He Jiankui.** He announced the births in November 2018 at the Second International Summit in Hong Kong and was sentenced on 30 December 2019 to three years in prison and fined. "He was sent to prison" and "Scientists everywhere condemned it" are correct (MIT Technology Review, as cited in the design). His team targeted the CCR5-delta-32 region but did not reproduce the 32-base-pair deletion: the reported edits were different, untested small deletions and insertions, one twin had only one copy edited, and both twins appear to be mosaic. "The edits didn't copy the natural protective version of the gene, and their effects were untested" is correct. "The twins carried all the risk, with no say" is correct (infants cannot consent; the parents' consent process was also criticised). (Searched: https://www.sciencealert.com/china-s-failed-experiment-proves-we-re-not-ready-for-human-gene-editing ; https://wikipedia.com/wiki/He_Jiankui ; excerpts of the unpublished manuscript in MIT Technology Review, 3 Dec 2019, for Codex to open: https://www.technologyreview.com/2019/12/03/131752/chinas-crispr-babies-read-exclusive-excerpts-he-jiankui-paper/.)
- **Sperm washing.** The fathers were HIV-positive and the mothers negative; sperm washing was used during IVF. Confirmed (finding 1 adds the missing half).
- **Papaya mechanism and "no ordinary variety resisted it".** PRSV destroyed nearly all papaya acreage in Puna, which grows about 95 per cent of Hawaii's papayas; Rainbow (an F1 of transgenic SunUp and non-transgenic Kapoho) and SunUp were released in 1998; no commercial Hawaiian variety resisted PRSV. "The plant's cells use it to recognise the virus's genetic material and destroy it" is a correct plain account of RNA silencing. "Killing papaya trees" is acceptable: infected trees become stunted and unproductive and orchards were abandoned. (Searched: Plant Disease 2002 86:101; ARS 2004.)
- **Pig and cattle insulin.** Porcine insulin differs from human at one position (B30); bovine at three (A8, A10, B30). Correct.
- **Cochrane review.** Richter and Neises 2005 (CD003816): randomised trials, mostly of purified porcine insulin, "did not show clinically relevant differences" in metabolic control or adverse reactions. "Purified pig insulin controlled blood sugar about as well for most patients" is correct and hedged. (Searched: https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003816_human-insulin-versus-animal-insulin-people-diabetes-mellitus ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/15674916.)
- **Insulin supply and shortages.** No narration line claims a shortage occurred. "Insulin no longer depends on pancreases from abattoirs" is correct. Site correction 9 (forecasts in the 1970s, not an actual shortage) is supported by the b18 check. "Many people with diabetes around the world still can't reliably get it" is correct as hedged (WHO 2021, as cited).
- **Growth hormone.** Cadaveric pituitary hGH was linked to CJD in 1985; US distribution stopped in 1985 and was replaced by recombinant hormone; the UK and Australian programmes also stopped in 1985. Genentech's Protropin (somatrem) was FDA-approved in 1985 (month commonly given as October; not spoken). "Until 1985 ... That year it was withdrawn, and hormone made by bacteria replaced it" and "some batches carried a fatal brain disease" are correct. Author note: Protropin carried one extra methionine; Lilly's somatropin with the exact human sequence followed in 1987. Not needed on screen. (Searched: CIDRAP 2024 summary; CDC stacks 23596.)
- **Chymosin identity and fungal coagulants.** FPC is the same enzyme as calf chymosin, so "the cheese turns out the same" is correct. Rhizomucor coagulants are more proteolytic and "can taste bitter in aged cheese" is correct as hedged.
- **Laundry item.** Most of a washing machine's energy goes to heating water (about 75 to 90 per cent in US Department of Energy and ENERGY STAR guidance, as cited); the card says "most". The scenario is constructed and labelled HSC-style. Model answer correct.
- **Hepatitis B item.** The plasma-derived vaccine was licensed in the US in late 1981; Recombivax HB, made in baker's yeast carrying the HBsAg gene, was licensed in July 1986 and was comparable in immunogenicity. Fears about blood-borne infection (including HIV) deterred uptake of the plasma vaccine, which supports "eased fears of infection from blood products". Australia added universal infant hepatitis B vaccination to the National Immunisation Program in May 2000, which supports the screen tag "since 2000". The model answer's note that plasma vaccine purification inactivated viruses is correct. (Searched: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK236297/ ; https://cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-10-hepatitis-b.html ; https://ojs.cdi.cdc.gov.au/index.php/cdi/article/view/2720/4199.)
- **Site corrections 8 to 16.** All supported; correction 15 (CCR5) is confirmed above; correction 10 (insulin infection risk) by the Cochrane review.
- **Absolute words.** "No ordinary variety resisted it" is true for Hawaii's commercial varieties. "Insulin no longer depends on pancreases" is true. "It fails all three" is a supported judgement. "The same enzyme" is true. "Only the purified product leaves" is covered by finding 8. "Both protected well" is from the card. None others are problems.
- **Memory aids.** "Better than what? Shown how? Who gets it?" is a correct test of a benefit claim, with the trade-off and verdict limits stated in scene 11. "A gain in harvest isn't a gain in biodiversity" is correct and does not claim farm biotechnology always lowers biodiversity.
- **Exam style.** Both items are HSC Section II style, use NESA verbs (assess, evaluate) with mark values and carry the "HSC-style question, written for HSCScience" tag. Biology 2023 Q34 (a table of biotechnologies and cattle biodiversity) and 2025 Q30(b) (judging the impacts of genetic technologies on society) do not overlap with a laundry enzyme or a two-vaccine comparison. Neither item closely paraphrases NESA wording. (Fetched: https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2023 ; https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2025.)
- **YouTube metadata.** The dotpoint is verbatim. "Find out why the clearest benefit of human insulin is supply" is correct. The description names no researcher, which is appropriate.
- **Em dashes:** none.
