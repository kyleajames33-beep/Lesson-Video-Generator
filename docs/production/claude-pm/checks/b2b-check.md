| 1 | science | Scene 2: "Each tiny pollen grain carries sperm cells"; "the sperm are up on the anthers"; Visual: "two tiny sperm dots inside" | Most flowering plants release pollen containing a generative cell that forms the two sperm later, so sperm already present in every grain on the anther is false. [Strawberry tissue study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3723606/), [strawberry seed anatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9454143/) | Outline both cream cotyledons and the radicle as the embryo, point food storage to the cotyledons, and point seed coat to the thin inner layer, or use a seed-only cutaway. |
