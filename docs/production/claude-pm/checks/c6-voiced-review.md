## Student learning trace (entry knowledge: C1 to C5)

- Main questions: from one measured concentration, how do I get the other two and K; given K and a start, how do I get the settled concentrations; what happens to K if I reverse or scale the equation? The hook states the first, the "Now the other way round" scene introduces the second, and the reverse scene the third.
- The ICE table is introduced from its three letters, filled one row at a time, and every row is derived from the previous one (concentrations from moles and litres, change by coefficients, equilibrium as initial plus change). The cause of the ratio in the change row (one PCl5 per Cl2) is spoken and drawn.
- The K-given path is one stage per scene: define x, write the change row, equilibrium row, substitute, take the square root, justify the sign, rearrange, hold, solve, check, justify rejecting the other root. Earlier stages are cleared when the next begins.
- The solve hold and the attempt each give the student a bounded task with its conditions visible and a 10 s countdown. Feedback names three likely numerical errors (initial used, change not halved, power left off) with the recomputed value for each, though the second error's arrow is misplaced (SHOULD FIX 1).
- Memory aid ("Only the change row follows the coefficients"; "Flip the equation, flip K. Multiply the equation, power K.") is placed after the worked example, carries its condition on screen, and is applied in the attempt (item 2). Teaching it after the check scene works because the student has just seen H2, I2 and HI end up at 0.10, 0.10 and 0.70, which break the ratio.
- Honest scope: the "perfect squares" caveat and "other starts: later" name what has not been taught (SHOULD FIX 2 improves the wording).
