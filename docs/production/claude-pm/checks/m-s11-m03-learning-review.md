## Should fix

**SF1. The memory aid contradicts the lesson's own worked example.** The card says "Free only while every payment is on time", but scene 3's example plan costs $492 with every payment on time, because of the $6 monthly account fee. A student who copies the card will believe "pay on time = free", which is exactly what scene 3 disproves, and will get A1 (b) wrong ($732, not $720). The design's argument ("free only while" is a necessary condition) is logically sound but not how a Year 11 student reads it. Fix: keep the main line, because Year 12 i12 recalls it, and add the condition to what is said and shown. Scene 7 narration: "Buy now, pay later can only be free if every payment is on time and the plan has no account fee." Key line: "Total = price + account fees + late fees. Account fees cost even when you pay on time." Change key note 5 or add to note 3: "Account fee: per month owed, even if on time."
