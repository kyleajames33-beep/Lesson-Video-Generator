# hms-y11-m1-v01 science check (meanings of health)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- WHO definition (scene 2, card, key note 1, 11c, 11d): wording matches the Constitution preamble ("complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity"). The site's spelling "wellbeing" with the grey source tag is acceptable. Constitution signed 22 July 1946, in force 7 April 1948, definition unamended: "came into force in 1948, and the wording hasn't changed since" is correct (site correction 1 stands).
- Criticism of "complete" (scene 4): the standard criticism (for example Huber and others, BMJ 2011, "How should we define health?"). "An aim for health systems, not a test" is a fair defence at this level.
- Biomedical, functional, WHO and holistic meanings (scenes 5 to 7) and the relevance-precision trade: sound as a teaching model; the memory-aid assumption is stated correctly in the design.
- Practice 11a/11b and 11c/11d: marked answers correct. The model already accepts "holistic" for the officer. No reasonable alternative is crossed.
- Cultural content (scene 6): general description, no community, nation or person named. Compliant with the 18 Nov rule.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 6 narration and ring labels).** The social and emotional wellbeing model in Australian frameworks (Gee, Dudgeon and others 2014, used in the National Strategic Framework for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples' Mental Health and Social and Emotional Wellbeing 2017 to 2023) has seven domains: body; mind and emotions; family and kinship; community; culture; Country; spirituality and ancestors. The design merges "body, mind and emotions" into one label (six labels) and drops "ancestors", so a teacher comparing the ring to the framework would flag the count. Corrected narration: "It covers connection to body, to mind and emotions, to family and kinship, community, culture, Country, and spirituality and ancestors." Ring: seven labels, splitting "body" and "mind and emotions" and using "spirituality and ancestors". Adds about 25 characters.
2. **Note (scene 3).** "Older official ideas worked the other way round" is the site's claim; there was no single "official" definition before 1946, but medical practice did treat health as the absence of diagnosed disease. Optional wording: "Older medical ideas worked the other way round".
3. **Note (scene 3).** "That's why governments quote it" is a fair generalisation; no change needed.

## Verdict
Pass with 1 should fix. 0 blockers, 1 should fix, 2 notes.
