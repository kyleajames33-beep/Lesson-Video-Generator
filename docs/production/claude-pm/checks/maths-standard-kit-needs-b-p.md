### New parts in one list

1. **ScatterBoard** (b01 to b07): the main new part, course-neutral and reusable for Advanced Year 12 or Year 11 data work. Driven by one table of pairs: rows lift from a board into points; axes rescale to fit (with a break mark); form, direction and strength cues (guide line or curve, arrow, strength band); a draggable line by eye with an above-and-below counter and gap sticks; the mean point; a trendline from exact least squares; a live r readout; an x-range band with solid and dashed line segments and an interpolation or extrapolation readout. Exact maths from a small helper (mean point, least-squares a and b, r) beside `stat-math.ts`.
2. **StudioToolPanel STAT mode** (b05, b06): an unbranded calculator flow with a paired data table and a results screen (A, B, r).
3. **SpreadsheetGrid** additions (b04, b06): a scatter chart with a linear trendline and equation label; SLOPE, INTERCEPT and CORREL in the formula bar; the argument-order swap with a red check cell. SpreadsheetGrid itself is already a listed need (a-strand, i-strand).
4. **ChanceLine** (p01, p02, p08) and **ClaimCard** (p08).
5. Small options: a strength band (b02), DriverArrows as a tag pattern (b03), squared-gap squares (b06, optional), lettered tokens (p03), bag-changes animation (p04), a running-proportion check on RunningMeanTrace (p05), an expected band on TrackerFill (p06), a Venn-to-table slide (p07).

No new simulation engine is required: the probability sims and trees exist, and the scatter maths is a few exact formulas.
