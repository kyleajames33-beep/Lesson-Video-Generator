## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 4 narration and key note 2).** "Because they cluster, their effects multiply rather than add." Clustered disadvantage does compound (evidence of more-than-additive harm exists for some combinations), but "multiply" states a mathematical relationship the evidence does not establish in general, and the key note repeats it. Corrected narration: "Because they cluster, their effects compound, so together they do more harm than each would alone." Key note 2: "They interact: chains, clustering (effects compound) and feedback." The scene's grey tag "they compound" already fits.
2. **Note (scene 7).** "historical and structural, ... not cultural" is accurate (culture is not the cause), and v08 adds that culture is protective. No change.
