## Should fix

**T05-MOST, scene 2, "For most of the year, there are three zones."** Daylight saving runs from early October to early April, about 26 weeks, so standard time is about half the year, not most of it. Also, Australia always has three zones; it is the number of different clock times that changes. Fix: "Australia has three time zones. On standard time, from April to October, each zone has one time." The student should not carry a wrong picture of how long daylight saving lasts into A1 and A2, which both depend on the month.
