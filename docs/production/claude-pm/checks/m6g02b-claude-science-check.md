## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Q1 (illustrative data).** Labelled "data are illustrative". Each row's direction is consistent with published patterns: Bt maize cuts insecticide applications against target caterpillars, and non-target invertebrates are more abundant in Bt fields than in insecticide-treated conventional fields (Marvier et al. 2007, Science 316:1475, for Codex to open); a few varieties replacing many narrows crop genetic diversity; field-evolved resistance to Bt maize has been documented where refuge compliance was low (for example Busseola fusca in South Africa and fall armyworm in Puerto Rico; Tabashnik and Carrière 2017). "Fewer insects that don't eat maize were killed" is a correct causal reading. Not judging the ecosystem level is correct.
- **Q2 (constructed example).** Interspecies nuclear transfer for a wild sheep is real: Loi et al. 2001 cloned a mouflon from granulosa cells of animals found dead, using enucleated domestic sheep oocytes and domestic ewes as surrogates. Of 23 reconstructed oocytes, 7 reached the blastocyst stage and were transferred, giving 2 pregnancies and 1 apparently normal lamb. The clone's nuclear DNA was mouflon and its mtDNA domestic sheep. This supports the card's method, the mitochondria line and "about 2 in 100" as a conservative constructed rate (mammalian SCNT live-birth efficiency is typically low single digits per embryo transferred, higher in cattle). (Searched: https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt1001-962 ; https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/gb-2001-3-1-reports0006 ; https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/scientists-successfully-c.) "Ten clones of one sheep add no more alleles than one" is correct (barring new mutations). Clones of distinct donors bred into the herd do return their alleles, as in the black-footed ferret (b16 check) and Przewalski's horse.
- **Q3 edits and PERV.** eGenesis EGEN-2784 pigs carry 69 edits: three glycan antigen genes inactivated, seven human transgenes (immune and coagulation regulators) added, and 59 copies of porcine endogenous retrovirus (PERV) inactivated (Anand et al. 2023, Nature 622:393). PERVs are retroviral sequences integrated in the pig genome; transmission to humans has not been shown in recipients, so "a pig virus could spread beyond the patient; reduced by inactivating viral copies, not removed" is correct as a possibility. "The pigs are raised in sealed, disease-controlled facilities" is correct (designated pathogen-free facilities). The other company's pigs (United Therapeutics/Revivicor, 10 edits) do not inactivate PERVs; the card describes the eGenesis pigs only, which is consistent with the 271-day kidney.
- **Q3 consent and ethics.** Trial patients accept high, uncertain risks, so informed, voluntary consent is essential; a zoonotic risk falls on people who did not consent, which is why recipients and contacts are monitored. Objections in principle to using animals as organ sources are not settled by evidence (b10). All correct.
- **Q3 need.** "Many thousands of people with kidney failure wait years ... some die while waiting" is correct for the US (about 90,000 on the kidney waiting list) and Australia (median wait about 2 to 3 years); the card names no country.
- **Fractions.** 2 in 100 means 98 of 100 embryos fail; 271 days is about 8.9 months. Correct.
- **Absolute words.** "None until year 7" is data. "Can never increase" appears only in the crossed mistake. "Evidence can't settle that" is correct for principle objections. "Which removes threats cloning can't" is correct (finding 7 adds the other half). "So don't invent them" is advice. No other problems.
- **Memory aids.** All recalled aids are used within their limits (the s04 aid for judgement verbs only; b12's level and baseline; b13's checkable condition; b19's tests; b16's nuclear-donor rule with the mitochondria bracket). "Count the donors, not the clones" is correct given distinct donors and clones that breed.
- **Exam style and near-match.** All three are HSC Section II style, use NESA verbs (assess, evaluate) with mark values and carry the "HSC-style question, written for HSCScience" tag. Biology 2023 Q34 asked students to judge, from a table, how each of several biotechnologies can increase, decrease or maintain biodiversity in cattle (evaluate, 5 marks, per `../research/exam-skills/verb-frequency.md` and the fetched feedback). Q1 differs in organism, technology, verb and stimulus, and does not reuse its wording. Biology 2025 Q30(b) asked for a judgement about the impacts of genetic technologies on society (evaluate, 7 marks; feedback: "provide a consistent, explicit judgement about the impacts of genetic technologies to society"); Q3 is a single named case with given evidence, not a paraphrase. No close paraphrase found.
