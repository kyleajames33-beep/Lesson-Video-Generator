## Should fix

**D02-FILTER, scene 2, "Add that one of them slept four hours, and everyone knows who it is."** The third filter step uses the sensitive answer itself as the identifying detail. Knowing that one of three band boarders slept 4 hours does not tell anyone which one, so the sentence does not show what it claims, and an attentive student will notice. The idea being taught is that ordinary details combine to single out one person, whose answer is then exposed. Fix: make the third chip another ordinary detail, for example "plays the cello" (count 1), then say "Now that one row, and its four-hour answer, belongs to a person everyone can name." The counts 200, 24, 3, 1 stay.
