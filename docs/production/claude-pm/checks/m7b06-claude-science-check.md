## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Metchnikoff (scene 1).** Messina, 1882 (usually given as December): rose thorns pushed into transparent starfish larvae; by the next morning, mobile cells had gathered round them. Echinoderms make no antibodies. "Zoologist" is right. Nobel Prize 1908 with Ehrlich. (searched) Embryo Project https://embryo.asu.edu/handle/10776/8018 ; Encyclopedia.com https://www.encyclopedia.com/topic/Elie_Metchnikoff.aspx
- **Barriers (scene 3).** Shedding dead outer skin cells remove surface microbes; mucus traps; cilia move it to the throat to be swallowed; stomach acid kills many swallowed microbes; lysozyme in tears and saliva breaks down peptidoglycan. "Most microbes never get past the outside" is hedged by "Most" and fine. Physical (skin, mucus, cilia) against chemical (acid, lysozyme) grouping on screen is correct.
- **Mast cells and histamine (scene 5).** Tissue-resident cells beside small vessels; histamine (H1) widens arterioles and opens junction gaps in venules, so plasma leaks (finding 3 for the vessel picture). (searched) https://doaj.org/article/8999923eae7f448ca408c9497dec560c
- **Four signs (scene 6).** Redness and warmth from more blood; swelling from leaked fluid; pain from bradykinin stimulating and prostaglandins sensitising pain receptors, plus pressure. Purpose: delivery of cells and plasma proteins. Correct.
- **Neutrophil recruitment (scene 7).** Capture and rolling (selectins), arrest (integrins, switched on by chemokines on the wall), crawling and crossing mostly between wall cells, then movement up a chemical gradient. Neutrophils are usually the first cells to arrive in large numbers, within hours. "Stick, squeeze out, crawl towards the stronger signal" is a fair plain version. (searched) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leukocyte_extravasation ; neutrophil adhesion cascade review https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2851240
- **Phagocytosis (scene 8).** Receptors bind molecules shared by many microbes (PAMPs); membrane surrounds the microbe; phagosome; lysosomes fuse; enzymes (and reactive oxygen) destroy it. Sizes (neutrophil about 12 µm, macrophage about 20 µm) are within standard ranges.
- **Pus and fever (scene 9).** Pus is mainly dead neutrophils, bacteria and fluid. Fever: cytokines act on the hypothalamus to raise the set point; a moderate rise can help some immune reactions. All hedged.
- **Dendritic cells (scene 10).** Long branching processes; take up antigen in tissue; travel in afferent lymph to the draining node; show fragments to T cells that have not met their antigen; the primary adaptive response takes days. Correct. Dendritic cells were described by Steinman and Cohn in 1973 (site error 10 is right on this point). (searched) https://www.rockefeller.edu/our-scientists/ralph-m-steinman/2370-nobel-prize
- **Innate against adaptive (scene 11).** Fast, broad, no improvement with repeat exposure (classical view; trained immunity rightly kept in author notes); adaptive slow at first and specific. Correct.
- **Attempt 1.** Miles-type dye leak test: a dye that binds albumin is given into the blood, histamine and a saline control are injected into the skin, and the histamine site turns blue as plasma protein leaks. Mice are a standard model. Model answer and the crossed mistake ("widening alone does not let proteins out") are correct. (searched) Brash et al., JoVE 2018 https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6101766/ ; modified Miles assay with histamine https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11986696/
- **Attempt 2.** Leukocyte adhesion deficiency type 1 (CD18, β2 integrin): neutrophils cannot firmly attach and leave the blood, so blood neutrophil counts stay high, bacterial skin infections recur, and pus is absent or reduced. The 4-mark model is correct. (searched) EMJ review https://www.emjreviews.com/allergy-immunology/article/leukocyte-adhesion-deficiency-type-1-a-case-series-and-review-of-the-literature ; Frontiers in Immunology 2020 https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2020.612703/pdf
- **Memory aid.** "Block, alarm, eat, alert" matches the usual order after a breach; its limits (overlap, resident macrophages eat at once, "alert" is only the start of adaptive immunity) are correctly in author notes. Sound.
- **Absolute words.** "Most microbes never get past" (hedged), "makes no antibodies" (true), "Animals can do something plants can't" (true for circulating cells), "only leaky walls let the dye out" (true), "Neutrophils must stick ... to squeeze out" (true), "almost any invader" (hedged), "this exact pathogen" (fine). None flagged.
