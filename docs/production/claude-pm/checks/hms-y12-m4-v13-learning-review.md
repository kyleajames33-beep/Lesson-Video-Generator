A student who watched v10 hears the same lesson. Fix: one recall sentence, "Earlier you met the two words: the strategy is the plan, tactics are the moves that serve it", then keep only what is new here: the "serves it?" check line with the stray card dropping away, and the "once in a game" rule. Cut the tennis tactic list to one card. Spend the saved 25 to 30 s on a 1 s settle in scene 3 and a slower scene 5. Students should build on v10's banner, not meet it twice.

2. **Should fix, 8c's marking does not match its card or its feedback.** The card asks, for each case, a change, its cost, how quickly it takes effect and what should have been prepared. The marking gives the badminton player one mark, the netball team two (one for "a team can substitute or reassign roles", which the card never asks for and 8d never says), and preparation one. A student who answers the badminton half fully can earn only one mark for it, and the substitution mark is invisible. Fix the marking to the card: badminton change and speed (1), badminton cost in that setting (1), netball change, cost and speed (1), a rehearsed plan B with a trigger for both (1). 8d already models these four lines. Students should be able to see every mark in the demand.
