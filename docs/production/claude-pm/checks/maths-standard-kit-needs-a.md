# Maths kit needs: Year 12 Standard, Algebraic relationships (math-s12-a01 to a13)

Maths Standard start, 18 November 2026, methods of brief 243 section 5. Existing parts are named from `src/explainer/math/studio/` (exports in `index.ts`, names checked against the files on 18 November 2026), the older `src/explainer/math/` parts (`algebra.tsx`, `strand-parts.tsx`, `calculus-pilot.tsx`) and `src/explainer/sims/`. These are requirements for later build work, not implemented capability or a pilot pass. No source file was changed.

Root ruling 7 applies: visual parts, sims and plates may be reused from Advanced; scripts are new and gentler. Several Year 11 Advanced designs named below (w13, w14, w15, w16, w19, w21, w22, w23, e01) are designed but not built, so parts they propose but the kit lacks (`DoublingLadder`, `SignSweep`) are not available yet.

Every video uses the shared frame: `WorkingBoard` (TaskStrip, EquationStrip, GivensCard, WorkingRows, AnswerFrame, MistakeCard) from `board.tsx`, `QuestionCard` with `GivensStrip` and its countdown, `KeyNotesScreen` and `MemoryAidCard` from `cards.tsx`, role colours from `roles.tsx`, and `HandTag`, `DrawnMark` and `PlateImg` from `primitives.tsx`. Graphs use `StudioPlot` from `plot-core.tsx` (one coordinate transform). "With graphing applications" uses `ScreenApp` (finance-parts.tsx) in graph mode, unbranded; Standard 2 HSC calculators do not graph, so the app appears as a classroom tool.

## New parts for this strand in one list

1. **SpreadsheetGrid** (a04; Year 11 Standard f08, l05, e06, e12, m06, m08 and data analysis; Year 12 i05, i11, c06): the main new part. Coded, unbranded, column letters, formula bar, fill-down, sign shading. Generalise `FrequencyTable`.
2. **StoryGraphBuilder** (a08; later r12): story sentences that draw a piecewise graph one piece at a time.
3. **RepresentationQuad** (a05, a09, a12; Year 11 linear videos): equation, table, ordered pairs and graph with one point lit in all four.
4. **ConstantProductRectangle** (a12, a13): a fixed-area rectangle riding the hyperbola, from `ProductRectangle` and `FunctionRider`.
5. **EliminationStack** (a02): column subtraction of two equations with the sign change as its own beat.
6. Small options: table-to-plot flight on `StudioCandidateTable` (a01); **MidpointBracket** as an `IntervalPicture` mode (a10); an axis offset on `StudioSymmetryPairs` (a09) if it lacks one; interval shading on `StudioLevelLine` (a11).

No simulation engine is required except the optional population engine in a07, used with neutral labels. No painted plate is required for the maths itself. Everyday anchors in the packs (driving schools, a lawn-mowing round, a school formal, a kangaroo's hop, a garden archway, a rugby kick, muffin boxes) follow the existing plate rules if Claude pictures them: a Codex painted plate for objects and animals, never a coded icon, and no people. Existing Advanced plates that may fit after an appearance check: `ma12-phone-v1.png`, `ma11-trade-van-v1.png`, `ma11-road-side-strip-v1.png`, `ma11-garden-bed-v1.png`.
