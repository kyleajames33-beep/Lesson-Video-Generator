# m7p05b learning review: What can an inhibition zone show?

Reviewer: independent Codex reviewer, 28 October 2026. I did not author this design. This is a review of Revision 1 source, not student testing, a voiced preview or release approval. Human listening and playback-speed motion review remain pending. The review follows `docs/production/student-learning-review.md`, AGENTS.md and the m7c13 review format. Findings do not edit the design.

Input: `../lessons/bio-m7-p05.md`, Revision 1. Sister-design narration inspected for the boundaries named below. Existing polymer objects and sealed-plate artwork were visually inspected on `../assets/brief109-qa/existing-assets.jpg`.

Main question traced: what the three different zones support, and why the largest zone does not identify the best medicine. Entry b11 supplies targets and selective toxicity; p02 supplies practical safety concepts; s07 supplies the three evaluation questions. The visible zone and the plate-versus-patient contrast are effective teaching choices. This review covers educational interpretation and safety, not optimisation or operational validation of a culture method.

## Strengths to keep

The drug moving away from its disc explains the clear region before its name becomes a result. The negative control has an explicit purpose. The numerical example distinguishes repeat agreement from a clinical conclusion. Two full HSC-style cards persist during the ten-second countdown; each feedback crosses a relevant mistake. VRA questions match s07 in plain meaning.

## Checks against the review questions

The aim, IV, DV, controlled conditions, negative control, repeat agreement and risk categories are named, so a student can evaluate a plan. This source is not an approved laboratory procedure. The zone explanation and clinical distinction are new; the plate-handling sequence risks repeating p02 but is shorter there, and b11's drug-target content is recalled briefly. Terms lawn, diffusion and negative control have meanings. The questions are answerable from the cards. A2's feedback is near the upper thirty-second estimate and should be timed on the voiced preview. Its safety finding should be resolved by the teacher, without adding method optimisation.

Narration recount: 5,728 characters. U+2014 count: zero. Example means 25 and 15 mm, spreads 2 mm each, and ten-millimetre gap are arithmetically correct.
