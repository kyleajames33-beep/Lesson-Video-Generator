## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Large-cage result (Q1 card, 2b).** (searched) Hammond et al., *Nat Commun* 12:4589 (2021), "Gene-drive suppression of mosquito populations in large cages as a bridge between lab and field": the doublesex drive spread quickly and fully suppressed the caged *An. gambiae* populations within a year, with no resistance selected. Earlier small-cage populations collapsed in 7 to 11 generations (Kyrou et al. 2018). "Died out within about a year" is correct. Codex should confirm from the paper that the drive reached every mosquito (100 per cent carriers) in each replicate cage and that all replicates collapsed ("every population").
- **No wild gene drive release; Target Malaria author note.** (searched) The 2019 Bana release in Burkina Faso used non-gene-drive, genetically modified sterile males. In August 2025, after a further release of modified males (reported as about 16,000 at Souroukoudingan on 11 August), the authorities stopped the work on 18 August. On 22 August the ministry announced the end of all project activities, with samples to be destroyed. The author note is correct. Codex should confirm that the August 2025 release was also a non-drive strain. Sources: https://targetmalaria.org/target-malaria-activities-suspended-in-burkina-faso/ ; https://www.geneconvenevi.org/articles/target-malaria-activities-suspended-in-burkina-faso/
- **Wind-borne spread.** Huestis et al., *Nature* 574:404 (2019): Sahelian *Anopheles*, including malaria vectors, migrate at 40 to 290 m altitude, potentially hundreds of kilometres a night (for Codex to open). A 20 km crossing "can be carried by wind" is conservative and correct.
- **Ecological effect.** Collins et al., *Malar J* 18:2 (2019) found no predator that depends only on *An. gambiae* (for Codex to open). The spoken line "though studies so far suggest few depend on it alone" and the tag "few, if any ... (evidence so far)" are correctly hedged.
- **Staged trial.** WHO's 2021 guidance framework sets out a phased pathway (laboratory, physically confined, staged field release) with community engagement. "Justified ... as a staged trial, provided both islands' communities agree first, and the spread is monitored" matches it.
- **Q2 realism values.** New Zealand: 25 deaths in 2020 in about 5.1 million people, about 4.9 per million. Sweden: about 9,700 deaths in about 10.35 million people, about 940 per million. The card's 5 and 900 are inside these, and the card is labelled constructed. 14-day managed isolation, 90 per cent of eligible people fully vaccinated by December 2021, and staged reopening from February 2022 match New Zealand's pattern.
- **"Buy time until vaccinated", "costs fell unevenly".** Correct as general features of 2020 to 2022 elimination strategies (L21's verdict says the same).
- **Q3 science.** An extract that inhibits *S. aureus* but kills human skin cells at the same concentration shows no selective toxicity yet, so "not yet a treatment" is correct and correctly hedged. Topical antiseptics can be useful despite some cytotoxicity in culture, but the design says "yet" and "tested at a safe dose", which covers this. The protocol terms (consent before research, community control of knowledge and use, attribution, agreed benefit sharing, consent to one use is not consent to another) match b15 and L20.
- **Model answers a marker would credit.** Q1 (with findings 2 and 3) and Q3 earn full credit: each has criteria, case evidence, a limit and a conditional judgement. Q2 would lose the condition mark as written (finding 1). With the fix, its 8 marks are fairly distributed.
- **Numbers.** 5 against 900 per million is a 180-fold difference (not stated in the design, which is fine). Q has 5 million people, so 5 per million is about 25 deaths, consistent with the realism check. No other arithmetic.
- **Absolute words.** "can't easily be recalled" is hedged. "Always ask where the proof was tested" is fine as a habit. "every population died out" is finding 4's Codex confirmation. "The protocols are met" is true inside the constructed case.

## Verdict

**Not ready to voice until finding 1 is fixed.** Q2's condition, its key note and two narration lines attribute the strategy's breakdown to presymptomatic spread, which was not new in 2021. They also treat quarantine as the measure that presymptomatic spread defeats, when that measure is isolation of cases.
