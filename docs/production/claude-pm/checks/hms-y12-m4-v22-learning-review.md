## Findings

1. **Should fix, 8c is scene 5's last sentence, and its spoken demand differs from its card and marks.** Scene 5 explains that drugs "used in training and cleared before the event" are why no-notice testing exists, lists the limits, and ends "a negative test doesn't prove an athlete is clean", with "a negative test proves they're clean" crossed. 8c asks whether a swimmer's negative in-competition tests prove she is clean, and 8d repeats scene 5. Also, the narration asks for "two features of testing and two of its limits", while the card asks for "features and limitations" and the three marks reward one feature, the limits together and a judgement. Replace with an unseen case that uses different features, for example: "A cyclist's A sample is positive for a banned stimulant. She says it came from a contaminated supplement, and that she has approval to use an asthma inhaler. Explain how the testing system treats each claim. (3 marks)" (B sample confirms or rejects the A result; strict liability means contamination is not a defence, though it may affect the sanction; a therapeutic use exemption covers only the approved medicine, not the stimulant). Make the spoken demand and the card identical. Students should apply the features, not recognise the scene's conclusion.
