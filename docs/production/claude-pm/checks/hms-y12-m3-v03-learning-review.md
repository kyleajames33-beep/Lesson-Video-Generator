## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 7's culturally and linguistically diverse line has no mechanism in the narration.** The spoken line is "Some migrants arrive healthier than people born here, then lose that edge, the healthy migrant effect", and the mechanism ("language, unfamiliar system: care delayed") is written silently. A student asks: "So they are healthier. Where is the inequity, and why do they lose the edge?" The scene's own opening says each group has "its own mechanism", so this row breaks the pattern. Add one spoken clause: "then lose that edge over time, as language barriers and an unfamiliar system delay care." (about 3 s). Students should hear a mechanism for every group, which they need if they choose this group in an exam.

2. **Note, scene 7 disability wording.** "Health is often poorer for reasons unrelated to it" is hard to parse ("it" can read as "health"). Say "for reasons that have nothing to do with the disability itself".

3. **Note, scene 8 ends with four crossed error chips after the compounding idea.** The scene teaches compounding, then lists four errors that belong to the whole method. Move the four errors to the start of scene 11 (as crossed chips beside the frame's four stages, one per stage), or keep them and fade the rings first. Each error then sits next to the stage it spoils.

4. **Note, hook "Think of three things" has no time.** Add a 2 to 3 s hold with the two empty columns before "There's a third kind of difference".

5. **Note (science check), "about a decade".** The design already flags this as the site's estimate; "years, often a decade" is safer if the check cannot source it.
