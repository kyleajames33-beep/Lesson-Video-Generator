## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 9 and feedback 11d ("dynamic" is defined only in the feedback).** Scene 9 ends "So health is dynamic", but the meaning "changing, for reasons you can name" first appears in 11d, where the student is told to "Define dynamic first". A student answering 11c during the countdown has never heard a definition to give. Replace scene 9's last sentence with: "So health is **dynamic**, which means always changing, for reasons you can name: the dimensions interact, people move along the continuum, and the conditions around them keep shifting." On screen, gloss "dynamic: always changing, for reasons you can name" (34 px) under the existing line. About 3 s. Students can then open 11c with the definition the feedback expects.

2. **Should fix, scene 4 ("the same fitness").** A seventeen-year-old who is "back to jogging" and a seventy-year-old who "walks two kilometres a day" are not obviously the same fitness, and the coded equals sign asserts it. A student will ask "is jogging really the same as walking?" and miss the point (the judgement differs because expectations differ). Say "would call a similar level of fitness excellent" and change the equals sign's tag to "similar capacity". Students should take away that the same capacity is judged differently, not argue about the example.

3. **Note, 11d length (about 31 s).** Trim "Finish by joining them." to keep under 30 s; the step 4 line carries it.

4. **Note, scene 8 list of six circumstances.** The densest line; the chips carry it. Keep "access to services" as a chip, because v04's access gates pick it up.
