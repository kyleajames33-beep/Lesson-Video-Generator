### Quartile and standard-deviation convention

I inspected both rendered pages of the exact 2024-course reference sheet. It prints outlier fences, the z-score and normal-distribution percentages. It does not prescribe an algorithm for locating sample quartiles. The syllabus crosswalk's `ci3247f05b` and `ci955be675` require a five-number summary and IQR, without a competing quartile algorithm in the captured content. No conflict with the designs' stated halves method was found. This is not a claim that the sheet teaches that method.

The captured Year 11 syllabus explicitly requires population standard deviation, using a calculator or digital tools. Thus `σx` and `STDEV.P` agree with the course; `sx` and `STDEV.S` calculate a different quantity. Source: `programme/syllabus/maths-standard-2024-crosswalk.json`, with official NESA wording and provenance. The [NESA glossary](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/mathematics/mathematics-standard-11-12-2024/glossary) supplies the IQR and standard-deviation meanings, read 25 November.
