### Primary Triodia anatomy checked, 5 November 2026

Toon et al. (2015), *Key innovation or adaptive change? A test of leaf traits using Triodiinae in Australia*, doi:10.1038/srep12398, Figure 1 and full-text XML (PMC4648476) were read and the original figure inspected. T. bynoei is epistomatous: stomata occur on the adaxial surface, inside the rolled leaf, deeply sunken in longitudinal grooves with dense interlocking papillae. T. danthonioides has grooves/stomata on both surfaces. The plate uses the right-hand T. bynoei example; this is not a universal claim about every Triodia species. The earlier broad outer-surface assumption is not a safe basis for painting. Keep the existing neutral learner wording and the species-variation author note. No narration or design was edited.
