## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 3).** "The strongest single source is at least one stable, supportive relationship". The source cited (National Scientific Council on the Developing Child, 2015, *Supportive Relationships and Active Skill-Building Strengthen the Foundations of Resilience*, Harvard Center on the Developing Child) says "the single most common factor for children who develop resilience is at least one stable and committed relationship with a supportive parent, caregiver, or other adult". "Most common" is not "strongest". Corrected: "The most common factor in young people who do well is at least one stable, supportive relationship, the same trusted adult from earlier." The memory aid ("supplied mostly by who you have") stays true.
