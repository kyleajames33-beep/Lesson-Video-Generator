## Confirmed

- 1RM bands (above 85 per cent, 1 to 5 reps; 70 to 85, 6 to 12; below 70, 15 or more): consistent with NSCA guidance (at or above 85 per cent for 6 or fewer; 67 to 85 per cent for 6 to 12; 67 per cent or less for 12 or more). Correct as rounded.
- RPE out of ten; adaptation during recovery; overload, deload weeks; the week table arithmetic (90, 99, 99, 75, 108 min). Correct.
- Netball program (7): sound, with reasons; zone 143 to 173 bpm for 16.
- 9a/9b: 202; 141.4 ≈ 141; 171.7 ≈ 172. Correct. 9c/9d: specificity fault correct; the 400 m is predominantly anaerobic.
- Memory aids sound with stated assumptions. No people; plates named correctly.

## Findings
1. **Note (scene 3).** "beginners tend to under-rate hard efforts" has limited evidence; "beginners rate effort less accurately" is safer.
2. **Note (scene 3, author).** In adolescents, measured maximum heart rate is often near 200 to 205 regardless of age, so 220 − age can over-estimate slightly; the video's "starting point" framing covers this.
3. **Note (9d).** A 400 m still draws about 40 per cent of its energy aerobically; "depends mainly on the glycolytic system" is more exact. The model's marking is unaffected.
