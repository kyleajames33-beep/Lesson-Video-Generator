## Findings

**F1 (should fix). Scene 4 narration.**
Quoted: "**True-breeding** tells you each parent has two identical alleles, so every offspring is heterozygous."
Problem: the conclusion needs the parents to carry different alleles. Without it, the step reads as "homozygous parents give heterozygous offspring", which is false in general (red x red gives red). Same as learning review S4.
Replacement: "**True-breeding** tells you each parent has two identical alleles. The parents differ, so every offspring gets one of each: heterozygous."

**F2 (should fix). Scene 2: zero count stated beyond the pack's scope.**
Quoted: "Contrast and distinguish didn't appear as commands in the last five years of papers".
Problem: `verb-frequency.md` (Coverage limits) says the zero is "within these 10 Section II papers, not a claim about all NESA exams". The spoken line does not name Chemistry, Biology or written questions. Same as learning review S3.
Replacement: "In the last five years of Chemistry and Biology written questions, compare appeared seven times. Contrast and distinguish didn't, but they're still glossary words, so know them."

**F3 (should fix). Scene 9: a single example turned into a universal rule.**
Quoted: "So more marks usually means **more paired rows**, not longer descriptions."
Problem: the pack says adjacent-band differences "are specific to that question" and "not a fixed mark allocation". Neither cited guideline shows marks rising with the number of rows: Biology 2024 Q21(b) separates all from most of the requested features, and Biology 2025 Q23 gave three marks for a two-sentence sample answer. Same as learning review S1.
Replacement: "So the marks followed **complete pairing** of the features asked about, not longer descriptions."
