## Claims

1. **Four sources; only mutation makes new alleles.** Crossing over, independent assortment and random fertilisation recombine existing alleles; mutation changes the DNA sequence. Correct (crossing over within a gene can rarely create a new allele by unequal exchange; not relevant at this level).
2. **Pea genes:** seed colour (I, *SGR*) and seed shape (R, *SBEI*) assort independently; seed traits belong to the embryo, so seeds in one pod differ. Correct.
3. **Genes on one chromosome:** recombinant gametes arise only when a crossover falls between the genes, in a fraction of meioses, so they are usually less than half of all gametes; very distant genes approach 50 per cent ("usually" kept). Correct.
4. **No crossing over in male *Drosophila*:** correct (achiasmate male meiosis; rare exceptions reported). Revision 2 states the attempt as "a cell in a fruit fly's ovary is making eggs", which keeps the item true without the word "female".
5. **Morgan 1910:** a single white-eyed male found among red-eyed *Drosophila* in January 1910 (Morgan, Science 32:120, July 1910); the allele was inherited (red-eyed F1, white-eyed males reappearing in F2). Correct. "A new allele had appeared" is correct (a mutation, now known to be an insertion in *white*; author note).
6. **Germ line against somatic:** in animals only mutations in the germ line (cells that give rise to eggs or sperm) are inherited; a gut cell mutation is somatic and stays in that individual. Correct. Plants differ (no early set-aside germ line), which is why flies carry the point (author note f).
7. **Mutation rate:** about 10^-8 per base per generation in humans; "rare for any one gene". Correct.
8. **Strawberry runners:** stolons give genetically identical daughter plants; cultivated strawberries are octoploid and highly heterozygous, so seedlings vary widely. Correct.
9. **Variation and survival:** "more likely to survive", not a guarantee. Correct.
10. **Heritability correction:** the site's h squared 0.79 claim is not used. Correct decision (heritability does not separate reshuffling from new mutation).
