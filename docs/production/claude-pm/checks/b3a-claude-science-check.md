## UNVERIFIED (not passed, not counted)

- U1, scene 2c: "the sperm's nucleus joins the egg's nucleus". In mammals the two pronuclei come together and the parental chromosomes typically unite on the first mitotic spindle rather than by a nuclear membrane fusion. My search did not confirm this, so I cannot cite it. It matches the site ("fusion of sperm and egg") and the HSC marking convention, so it is probably acceptable; recording it in case a reviewer wants it.
- U2, scene 9a: "the embryo makes hCG only after it implants". In vitro, human embryos show a small hCG rise at about day 7 after insemination in partly hatched blastocysts, rising about 50-fold after attachment ([Chorionic gonadotropin secretion by human embryos in vitro](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2461389/)). So "only after" is slightly too strong at the day 6 to 7 boundary, but any pre-attachment hCG is tiny and cannot reach the ovary, so the conclusion "no hCG here to rescue anything" in item one is correct. Consider "in meaningful amounts only after it implants" only if you want it exact.
- U3, scene 7a graph: "total line keeps rising" through the hand-over. Serum progesterone is reported to plateau or dip slightly around weeks 7 to 9 gestational age while the shift occurs. I did not find a source I could cite, and the graph is schematic with no values, so I left it.
- U4, NESA 2017 Biology Module 5 dot point: wording matches the site extract's quote but the official page was not retrievable ([search result pointing to the dot point](https://hscbiology.podbean.com/e/module-5-fertilisation-implantation-pregnancy-and-manipulation-in-agriculture)).

## Other claims checked

- Embryo versus fetus: the embryonic period runs to the end of week 8 after fertilisation (week 10 gestational age); fetus from week 9 on. The learner copy's "organs forming" versus "grows and matures" is correct ([embryo/fetus definitions](https://www.osmosis.org/learn/Human_development_days_4-7)).
- Corpus luteum "yellow body": correct (Latin corpus luteum). It forms from the follicle that released the egg: correct.
- Progesterone name: from "progestational" (pro-gestation), Allen and Corner, 1930s ([history of progesterone](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7354701/)). The aid is accurate.
- hCG acts on the corpus luteum, progesterone on the lining: correct. Aid failure modes ("hCG makes progesterone") correctly warned.
- Pregnancy begins at implantation: standard medical definition; consistent with the lesson.
- Implantation "lining closes over it", embedded by about day 10 to 12: correct. Implant plates should not show the blastocyst's outer shell (it hatches at about day 5 to 6); the brief for plates 1 to 3 does not mention it, so no change.
- Placental exchange (7b, 9c): oxygen moves down its concentration difference from maternal to fetal blood, nutrients cross, carbon dioxide and wastes cross back; bloods do not mix in normal exchange. "Nutrients cross too" avoids any mechanism claim. Correct apart from finding 4's stage.
- Attempt item 1 model answer: without progesterone the lining is not receptive, so no implantation; hCG is not available because the blastocyst has not attached (see U2). Correct.
- Attempt item 3: correct (mother's blood does not flow into the fetus's blood).
- Cleavage plates: zygote, 2, 4, about 16 cells, then blastocyst with outer ring and inner clump: sequence is correct; skipping 8 cells is fine. The shell staying the same size through the blastocyst plate is accurate at day 5 (before it hatches).
