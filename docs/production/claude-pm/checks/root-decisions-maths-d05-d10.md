## Decisions

1. **Spoken "cos" and "natural log"** are voice-pilot items (brief 124), not design items. Scripts keep the current wording; the pilot decides "cos" or "cosine" for d05 to d10 together.
2. **d05 unit-circle scene stays.** The radian reason is a necessary condition for d/dx sin x = cos x.
3. **d06 scope:** log_a(f(x)) and 3^(2x) stay out of d06; their later home is the strand's exam-practice video. d06 is already on the chain rule with four outside rules, and adding them would crowd it.
4. **d06 ln(cos x):** keep −sin x / cos x as the result, with "= −tan x" as a grey identity beside it (tan x = sin x / cos x is Year 11 knowledge). No tan derivative is used.
5. **Double-angle identity (d06 site note):** the reviewer confirms against the 2024 Advanced content whether the double-angle identities are in Advanced before the site error is logged.
6. **d06 to d07 handoff:** d06 scene 14's window now matches d07 scenes 1 to 5 (x −0.5 to 3.5, y −2 to 8), so the 7.46 point at 3π/4 is visible. (The old 0 to 2π window would have clipped the curve badly.)
7. **d07 attempt 2:** the replacement xe^(−x) at x = 2 is confirmed, because the pack's question repeats scene 8.
8. **d08 we-2 tangent line:** left out. Tangent and normal lines have their own later home.
9. **d08 "push and pull" wording:** the learning reviewer checks that the scene states it for this example, not as "the bottom always pulls down".
10. **d08 to d09 handoff:** d08 scene 13 (xeˣ/(x + 1)² with x = −1 excluded, x = 0 marked and "?" on each interval) matches d09's assumed opening. Confirmed.
11. **d09 we-l17-3** (working backwards from conditions) stays out; its later home is the exam-practice video.
12. **d10 closing scene:** no stills from built lessons. It redraws one small coded graph motif from each of d01 to d10, so it has no build dependency.
13. **After d10:** the teaser for the fencing problem (we-l20-1) stays. The route gets a later id for the optimisation video when that strand is planned.
14. **d10 "ceiling and floor" wording:** the learning reviewer judges it.
15. **Site link form:** use the `.sa.html` form, as in d01 to d04, until the site's live maths path is confirmed in an upload brief.
