# hms-y11-m1-v12 science check (reflecting on your own health)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- Reflection cycle (scene 2): consistent with Gibbs (1988) and Kolb.
- Self-criticism against situation-focused reflection (scene 3): consistent with self-compassion and goal-pursuit research (Breines and Chen 2012). Fair.
- Usable plan properties (scene 7): consistent with goal-setting and behaviour change evidence.
- Implementation intentions (scene 8): Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006 meta-analysis, medium-to-large effect (d about 0.65). Correct. "A missed day is data": Lally and others 2010 found one missed opportunity did not materially affect habit formation. Correct.
- Practice 10a/10b (Revision 2 breakfast case; ordinary food, no diet advice) and 10c/10d: correct. Other weaknesses a student might name (no analysis of why, no condition) fit the model's two categories.

## Findings
1. **Blocker (scene 8 narration).** "Even then, almost everyone who intends to sleep more or move more doesn't." The evidence puts the gap at about half, not almost everyone: Rhodes and de Bruijn 2013 (*British Journal of Health Psychology* 18:296 to 309, meta-analysis of physical activity) found 36 per cent of participants were unsuccessful intenders against 42 per cent successful intenders, so about 46 per cent of intenders did not act. Sheeran 2002 reports a similar order. As taught, the line is false and would also undercut the plan the video recommends. Corrected: "Even then, many people who intend to sleep more or move more don't. For exercise, studies suggest close to half of intenders don't follow through. That distance is the intention-behaviour gap, and a plan should expect it." (adds about 70 characters; offset available by trimming "and a plan should expect it" to "so plan for it"). If root prefers no figure: "Even then, many people who intend to sleep more or move more don't." Keep consistent with hms-y11-m2-v21's "close to half" (same source).

## Verdict
Fail until fixed: 1 blocker, 0 should fix, 0 notes.
