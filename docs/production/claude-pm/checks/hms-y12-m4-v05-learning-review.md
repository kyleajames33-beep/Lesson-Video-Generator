## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 2 teaches four stretching methods, their risks, a three-step PNF sequence and the placement rule in one scene (about 1,000 characters, four glossed terms).** The student must hold static, dynamic, ballistic and PNF, each with a mechanism and a cost, before the rule that orders them arrives at the end. Fix: split at "Ballistic" into 2a (static against dynamic, ending on the two cards in the placement strip and a 1 s settle; that contrast carries the rule) and 2b (ballistic and PNF, then "So, dynamic before, static or PNF after"). No narration change. Students should hear the core placement contrast before the two specialist methods.

2. **Should fix, narration and screen name an internal video number.** Scene 3 opens "In v-three, load, reps, sets and rest decided ..." and shows a grey tag "v03" on the load ladder. Students never see these ids, and the course brief says "earlier". Say "Last time, load, reps, sets and rest decided ..." and use the grey tag "earlier".

3. **Note, 8a reduces to matching scene 3's tags.** Scene 3 tags fixed weights "beginners, rehab, no spotter" and elastics "rehab"; 8a gives a beginner with no spotter and a rehab case. The items are the site's, and the limitation demand adds some thinking, but a student can score by matching words. If root wants a harder transfer, make athlete B "a sprinter who wants hip strength near full extension, training alone in a hotel room" (elastics, because the load rises toward the end of the range where the sprint needs it).

4. **Note, 8d's rule mirrors scene 4's touch football rule.** "A goal counts double after three passes" is the body's "two points within three passes" in a new sport. That is a fair design transfer, and the three-touch option is new; accept either in the marking, as it does.

5. **Note, 8b is about 30 s.** Trim "and body weight builds control" (about 2 s); the marking still accepts body weight.
