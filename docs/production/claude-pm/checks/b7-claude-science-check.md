- **Himalayan rabbit:** the c^h allele of the tyrosinase gene gives a heat-labile (temperature-sensitive) tyrosinase that makes melanin only in cooler skin; extremities are cooler; fur regrown under cold is dark. Finding: Revision 1's "the cold changed how the gene was expressed" is imprecise (the enzyme is made, but is active only in cool skin); now "the cold changed where the enzyme worked, not the gene they inherit".
