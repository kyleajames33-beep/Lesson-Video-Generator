1. **Should fix:** The claim in scene 7 that infinitely many equal positive probabilities necessarily pass 1 is correct for countably infinite values, but the learner-facing explanation is compressed. At the `1/c` comparison, show the chosen number of bars accumulating past 1 and say that *enough* positive pieces must exceed 1. This is the stated visual, so it is an implementation and spoken-cue check, not a request for an extra scene.
2. **Should fix:** Scene 6 crosses `15/75` as a method even though `15/75 = 1/5`. The narration does explain the coincidence and visual gives `25/75 = 1/3`. Make the crossed reason and the counterexample simultaneously legible, otherwise the learner could reasonably think an equal numerical answer was marked wrong. No new narration needed.

The count test does not substitute for directly comparing probabilities where outcomes have different weights. The lesson stays within its one action and leaves expectation to s03.
