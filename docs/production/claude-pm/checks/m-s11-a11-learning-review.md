## Should fix

**SF1. Scene 2 says the block has "a straight front, eighteen metres deep".** The 18 m is the front's width, the perpendicular distance between the two parallel sides, which becomes h. "Deep" suggests the distance from front to back, which is the 28 m or 34 m side. A student will put 18 in the wrong place or think the sides are 18 m. Fix: "A block of land has a straight front, eighteen metres wide, and two parallel side boundaries at right angles to it, twenty-eight metres and thirty-four metres long."
