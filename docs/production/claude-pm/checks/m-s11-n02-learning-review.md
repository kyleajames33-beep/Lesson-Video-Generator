## Should fix

**N02-TWOWAY, scene 4.** The narration says "On a two-way street ... we draw a plain line", then two sentences later "Between the Bakery and the Pharmacy, the street is two-way, so the arrow points both ways", and the visual draws B–P with arrowheads at both ends. The student now has two rules for one situation, inside a directed network where a plain line could be read as "no direction given". Year 12 c01a and f01 build on this convention. Fix: choose one. Suggested: "In a directed network, a two-way street gets arrows at both ends, so every edge shows its direction" and drop the "plain line" sentence (keep plain lines for undirected networks, as in n01). The student should be able to read any edge in a directed network without guessing.
