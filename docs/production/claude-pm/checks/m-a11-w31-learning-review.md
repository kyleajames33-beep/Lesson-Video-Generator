## Findings

1. **Should fix, attempt 2 and feedback 2 (six demands).** The card asks for the vertex, the axis, the domain, the range and three solution counts. The speech is about 29 s, but the framed answer holds six items, and the domain and range of |ax + b| were already taught in scene 2 and in w30. Fix the card: "For y = |4 − 2x|, state the vertex. How many solutions does |4 − 2x| = k have when k = −1, k = 0 and k = 3?" Narration: "Last one. For y equals the absolute value of four minus two x, state the vertex. Then say how many solutions the absolute value of four minus two x equals k has, when k is minus one, zero and three." In the feedback, end the first sentence at "so the vertex is two, **zero**." and delete "The domain is all real x, and the range is y of zero or more." That leaves about 22 s, and the question keeps its main point: the level line counts solutions.

2. **Should fix, scene 4 (unclear reference).** "Here's one from the course." Students will not know which course is meant. Say "Here's another one."
