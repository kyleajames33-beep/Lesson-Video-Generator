The catalase equation 2 H₂O₂ → 2 H₂O + O₂ balances four H and four O on each side. Oxygen forms bubbles; visible foam also depends on the system's ability to trap bubbles. Peroxide removal can be measured rather than inferred solely from foam; [published catalase assays](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2830880/) measure concentration change. Catalase turnover depends on enzyme and conditions, so the separate "millions per second" claim needs its source and specified conditions, not a universal constant. A protein catalyst is not consumed stoichiometrically but can age or be inactivated. Some RNA molecules catalyse reactions; "almost all enzymes are proteins" is appropriately qualified in scene 2 and should not become an unqualified universal definition in the role board.

L15's statement that proteins denature above about 40 °C is too broad across organisms and proteins, and it implies an exact threshold; log the scope correction without inventing a universal replacement temperature. Grass stains include pigment and other matter; enzymes may aid removal of some components, so the protease wording is a minor precision issue rather than a claim that no grass stain contains protein. "Thousands" is not mathematically false merely because a larger fold change is possible, and the page provides no directly comparable test, so do not call that an established numerical error.
