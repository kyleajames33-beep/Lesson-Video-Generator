## Findings

1. **Blocker, scenes 3, 6 and 12 (a charity gift fails the test the video gives).** The video says every deduction "has to pass three tests", and the memory card defines allowable as "you paid it, for earning this income, with a record". A gift to a registered charity is not for earning income, yet scene 3 sorts it as allowable with no reason, scene 4 counts the teacher's $250 donation, and A1's correct answer needs the $40 donation counted. A student who applies the taught tests excludes it and gets $55 932 instead of $55 892, and the rule they were given says they are right. Fix (about 6 s). Scene 3, replace "Union fees are allowable, and so is a gift of two dollars or more to a registered **charity**." with "Union fees are allowable too. And there's one special rule. A gift of two dollars or more to a registered **charity** isn't for earning income, but the tax rules allow it anyway." Tag the charity receipt "special rule: gifts". Scene 6, end "... and you've got a record. Gifts to registered charities are a special **case**." (cue moves to "case"). Memory key line and key note 3: "Allowable: you paid it, for earning this income, with a record. Also: gifts of $2 or more to registered charities." Students should be able to apply the three tests and know the one exception they will meet in questions.

2. **Optional, A1 card ("a deductible gift recipient").** The card adds a term the narration never says. Either drop the bracket, or say it once in scene 3: "a registered charity, which the tax office calls a deductible gift recipient".
