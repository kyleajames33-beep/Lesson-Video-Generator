| 1 | science | Scene 3a: "the zygote has no supply from its mother yet and lives on the egg's food store"; scene 5: "From now on the embryo can begin to take food and oxygen from the lining around it" | The embryo already takes nutrients and oxygen from fluid in the mother's reproductive tract before implantation, so lack of maternal supply does not explain cleavage without growth ([human embryo metabolism research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9080207/), [human reproductive-tract fluid measurements](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0015028216580972)). | Explain that cleavage divides the cell contents into smaller cells; remove the claim that maternal nutrition begins at implantation. |
| 2 | science | Scene 9a: "the embryo makes hCG only after it implants, so there is no hCG here to rescue anything" | Human embryos can secrete hCG before implantation, so this explanation of why item one fails is false ([preimplantation human embryo experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6546453/)). | Explain that hCG cannot restore progesterone when the woman's body makes none, as the question states. |
| 3 | science | Scene 7a graph: "over 'two to three months' the corpus luteum share fades while the placenta share grows"; review notes: "ovulation, about 2 weeks, then 2 to 3 months" | The graph places the handover too late when its dates are counted from fertilisation: the main shift is about 5 to 7 weeks after fertilisation, and the placenta is the main source by about 8 weeks ([Endotext](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK278962/)). | Put the graph's transition around 5 to 8 weeks after fertilisation. |
| 4 | science | Tract asset 1: "mature fluid-filled follicle" and "about 5 percent of the ovary's width" | A follicle that small cannot represent the mature follicle releasing the egg: a preovulatory follicle is about 20 mm across, while a normal ovary is about 20 mm wide and 35 mm long ([follicle development](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK278951/?report=printable), [ovary anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK545187/)). | Draw a large follicle near the surface, occupying a substantial part of the ovary, and preserve its position in the aligned pair. |
| 5 | science | Scene 2c visual: "the sperm head slips through (a common simplification; Codex to say whether the whole sperm should be shown)" | Showing only head entry leaves out the tail's entry into a human oocyte ([human ultrastructure study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1169172/)). | Show the head entering first and the tail following into the egg. |
