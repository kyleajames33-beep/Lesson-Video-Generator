## Main question and useful outcome

*In an equilibrium calculation you have not seen, where is the trap, and what do you do first so it cannot catch you?* For part 2, afterwards a student should be able to name the first move for: flip or square K for a rewritten equation, perfect square or tiny K (with the 5 per cent check on the amount actually taken off), convert back at the end, and reuse K at the same temperature.

## Trace from entry knowledge (questions 3 and 4)

- **Q3, nitrosyl chloride.** Reversed K, then a 2 : 2 : 1 table, the tiny-K drop on 2x, a cube root and the 5 per cent check on the amount actually taken off (2x, not x). The "only just" pass at 4 per cent, plus the exact-value tag, models honest use of a rule of thumb.
