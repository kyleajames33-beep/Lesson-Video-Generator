## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 2 contradicts earlier videos.** "Every other strategy so far works on what a person does" is not true of the module: v05's corrective and v09's strategy levels (regulation, services, environment) already worked on conditions. A student who remembers v09 will be confused about what makes advocacy different. Say "Every personal skill so far works on what a person does. Advocacy works on what they're doing it within, and it's how young people push for the regulation, services and environments you met earlier." About 4 s. Students should see advocacy as the route by which young people reach the condition-level strategies.

2. **Note, 10a is close to scene 3.** Late buses to a sports ground against scene 3's later bus past the youth health centre; 10b's next step is the same template. The viral video and "40,000 views, decisions changed: 0" make it a different diagnosis (visibility is not impact), so it still tests application; a different condition (for example unlit courts) would make the transfer cleaner.

3. **Note, the ladder's top rung.** Here it is "lead"; v14 calls control "the top rung" and v16 relabels it "lead (control)". Use "lead (control)" from this first appearance so the label does not change (see the summary).
