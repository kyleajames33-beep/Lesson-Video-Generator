1. **Should fix, shared with science:** Scene 10 says every individual reaction releases energy and each release is caught in ATP. Glycolysis includes ATP-investment reactions and many steps do not directly make ATP. Say that the overall pathway releases energy through controlled steps and some is transferred to ATP. Replace the existing wording, not the visual sequence.
2. **Should fix, shared with science:** Scene 13 says that when oxygen merely runs short all mitochondrial steps stop and the cell can use only cytoplasmic steps. Oxygen limitation changes the rate, not an automatic complete switch, and mitochondrial chemistry is not wholly disabled. State that without enough oxygen aerobic ATP production falls and cells can obtain less ATP from anaerobic pathways. Preserve the brief comparison and do not claim lactate only appears when oxygen is absent.
3. **Should fix, shared with science:** The handoff says a plant runs respiration's equation "the other way", which can imply the same biochemical pathway in reverse. Say the next video compares the overall photosynthesis equation and its contrasting inputs and outputs. Replace the one line.
4. **Should fix, shared with science:** Scene 7 attributes all the hydrogen in metabolic water directly to glucose. Oxygen accepts electrons and protons in the terminal respiratory reactions; exchange with cellular water makes atom-by-atom hydrogen provenance more complicated than the summary equation shows. Say oxygen accepts electrons and hydrogen to form water, without tracing every hydrogen atom directly from the glucose molecule.


Independent atom count in `work/brief150/v16/calculations.json`: each side has C=6, H=12 and O=18; two glucose molecules require 12 O₂ and give 12 CO₂ and 12 H₂O. The equation is an overall account, not an atom map of every intermediate. [Glycolysis measurements and mechanism](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8091952/) describe two ATP invested before four ATP formed; [photosynthesis research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5264509/) distinguishes the overall reverse relationship from the reactions themselves. Oxygen acts as terminal electron acceptor and becomes water in the simplified aerobic account. Germinating seeds' oxygen fall and CO₂/heat rise are consistent with aerobic respiration in the stated sealed, insulated flask, although non-respiratory heat and experimental controls would matter in a real quantitative investigation.

Muscle lactate is present predominantly in its ionised form at physiological pH; the conventional Year 11 "lactic acid" name can be introduced with "lactate" rather than treating the familiar word alone as a substantive blocker. Lactate is not proof of zero oxygen. [Physiological evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/8225492/) discusses dissociation and production. For the site, its claim that a breathalyser detects aerobic respiration confuses ethanol breath testing with a CO₂ sensor, and its single-glucose heat-and-denaturation claim is unsupported. ATP energy phrasing should refer to the reaction and ATP overall, not imply bond breaking itself releases energy.
