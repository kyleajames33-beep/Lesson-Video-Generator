## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 6 (internal reference and a muddled callback).** Narration: "Here's the site's example, and you may recognise it. At a price of p dollars, a stall sells forty minus two p items." YouTube viewers do not know what "the site" is, and the example is called a stall selling items, then "the kayak model". Fix: "Here's a model you may recognise. At a price of p dollars, the kayak hire gets forty minus two p hires a day. Find the price that maximises its takings, and the maximum takings." Then keep "That's the kayak model from the discriminant video." and replace "revenue" with "takings" in this scene for consistency with w05.
