## B15

1. **Scene 4, licensing AMRAD.** The narration says the US government "licensed an Australian company, AMRAD, to develop it", and the screen labels an exclusive NCI licence as an accomplished fact. The brief and Decisions error 3 date this to 1993. The 22 December 1993 statement describes only a provisional allocation. The 1 March 1995 statement says AMRAD withdrew because it could not obtain satisfactory NCI licence terms. Correct text: "The NCI provisionally offered AMRAD an exclusive development licence. AMRAD separately paid WA $1.15 million at the December 1993 signing for plant access and research, but withdrew in 1995 after failing to agree satisfactory NCI licence terms." Keep the NCI negotiation separate from the signed WA agreement. Sources: [WA Government, 22 December 1993](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Court%20Coalition%20Government/Agreement-on-development-of-anti-AIDS-drug-19931222), [WA Government, 1 March 1995](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Court%20Coalition%20Government/Withdrawal-of-Australian-company-from-AIDS-treatment-project-19950301).

2. **Scene 8 and the hook's patent dates.** "The patent, filed in 2003" and "patent, 2003: owners" conflate the Australian provisional priority application with the later international application and granted US patent. The record gives priority 27 November 2003, PCT filing 26 November 2004 and US grant 17 November 2009. Correct text: "The patent family began with an Australian provisional application in 2003. The US patent, granted in 2009, names Griffith University and Jarlmadangah Buru Aboriginal Corporation as co-assignees." Label the comparison "1993 application / 2003 priority application" if keeping the ten-year interval. The patent record spells its assignee "Buru"; the community's preferred name in narration remains for the cultural reviewer. Source: [US 7,618,946 B2, dates, assignees and application family](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7618946B2/en).

3. **Scene 8's missing chronology tag.** The design expressly asks for "begun before the smoke bush patents" if the partnership predates 1993. It does. Correct the pending tag to "partnership began before 1993". The chemist's laboratory dates it to 1986; ANU describes the partnership as beginning 35 years before its 2023 award report. These establish the required ordering without confusing the first commercialisation discussions with a later formal agreement. Sources: [Ron Quinn laboratory](https://ronquinnlab.com/ron-award), [ANU, research partnership](https://asiapacific.anu.edu.au/Content-Centre/Article/News/National-spotlight-Aboriginal-medicines-research-project).

## P01

1. **Scene 11 risk table and author safety facts: indicator mixture codes.** The table gives H341 and H361 for phenolphthalein indicator in ethanol without identifying a formulation. These are phenolphthalein ingredient hazards, but are not automatically the finished mixture's classification. ChemSupply's current PL101 0.5% indicator SDS (30 July 2026) lists H225, H319 and H350 in section 2; H341 and H361f appear only under its ingredient in section 3. Correct the table for that product to "highly flammable; causes serious eye irritation; may cause cancer (H225, H319, H350)". If another formulation is intended, use its section 2 codes. The narration's "flammable and may cause cancer" remains correct. Do not infer all mixture classifications from a single 0.1% threshold. Source: [ChemSupply PL101, current SDS, sections 2 and 3](https://shop.chemsupply.com.au/documents/WFC0JFEX.pdf).
