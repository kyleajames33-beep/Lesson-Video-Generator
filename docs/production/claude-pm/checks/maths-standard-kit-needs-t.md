# Maths kit needs: Year 12 Standard, Trigonometry (math-s12-t01 to t11)

Maths Standard start, 18 November 2026, methods of brief 243 section 5. One author. Existing parts are named from `src/explainer/math/studio/` (names checked against the exports on 18 November 2026: `a11-trig-triangle.tsx`, `a11-trig-bearings.tsx`, `a11-trig-circle.tsx`, `integration-parts.tsx`, `finance-parts.tsx`, `rig.tsx`, `primitives.tsx`, `board.tsx`, `cards.tsx`). These are requirements for later build work, not implemented capability or a pilot pass. No source file was changed.

Root ruling 7 applies: visual parts, sims and plates may be reused from Advanced; scripts are new and gentler, and Advanced anchors are not reused. The Year 11 Advanced trigonometry kit (`StudioTriangle`, `BearingRose`, `BearingJourney`, `ElevationView`, `StudioCirclePoint`, `HingeBars`, `MinutesDial`) covers most of this strand.

Every video uses the shared frame: `WorkingBoard` (TaskStrip, EquationStrip, GivensCard, `WorkingRows`, AnswerFrame, MistakeCard) from `board.tsx`, `QuestionCard` with `GivensStrip` and its countdown, `KeyNotesScreen` and `MemoryAidCard` from `cards.tsx`, role colours from `roles.tsx`, and `HandTag`, `DrawnMark` and `PlateImg` from `primitives.tsx`. Values come from exact arithmetic, rounded only for display (`work/brief-standard-t/recalc.py` and `recalc_pack.py` hold every pack value).

### New parts in one list

1. **RadialSurvey** (t10): field book to rays, angle arcs, 360° ring, area chips and running total, one boundary.
2. **MapPins** (t04, t05, t10): landmark pins with bearing arms on a top-view plate.
3. Small options: a ratio readout on StudioTriangle (t01, t06), a two-object ElevationView mode (t03), a component split on BearingJourney (t05), an area readout on the hinge (t09), a RuleChooser card (t11).

No new simulation engine is required. All trigonometry geometry (`triRight`, `triSAS`, `triSSS`, `triHinge`, `journeyPoints`, `elevationAngle`) already exists in the Advanced kit.
