## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Three levels.** Genetic, species and ecosystem diversity (Convention on Biological Diversity, Article 2; OpenStax 47.1). Correct.
- **Cavendish and TR4.** Cavendish bananas are sterile triploids propagated vegetatively (suckers and tissue culture), so plants are near-identical; *Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *cubense* TR4 kills susceptible Cavendish. "Grown from suckers, so every plant is a copy, with the same alleles": correct at this level (tissue culture is also used; both copy one genotype).
- **QCAV-4.** Grand Nain Cavendish carrying *RGA2* from *Musa acuminata* ssp. *malaccensis*; OGTR licence DIR 199 for commercial cultivation and FSANZ food approval (notified 16 February 2024) (searched: ISAAA and AgbioInvestor reports; FSANZ A1274 executive summary, https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/food-standards-code/applications/Documents/A1274%20Event%20QCAV-4-Executive%20summary%20PD.pdf). QUT: "no plans to grow or sell QCAV-4 bananas to consumers in Australia at this time"; a "safety net" (QUT, 2023, searched: https://www.qut.edu.au/news?id=186769). No 2025 or 2026 commercial planting found. "It was approved for growing in Australia in 2024, as a back-up if TR4 spreads": correct; recheck commercial status near recording.
- **Bt cotton and insects.** Reduced broad-spectrum spraying in Bt cotton is associated with more non-target and beneficial arthropods than in sprayed conventional cotton (Marvier et al., Science 2007;316:1475; Lu et al., Nature 2012;487:362). "Can leave more insect species alive than sprayed conventional cotton": correct with "can" and the named baseline (most evidence is on abundance; "species" is acceptable at this level, hedged).
- **Land sparing.** Yield gains spare land only where policy and land use protect habitat (National Academies 2016). The narration carries the condition. Correct.
- **Northern white rhino.** Sudan, the last male, died on 19 March 2018 at Ol Pejeta. Najin and Fatu cannot carry a pregnancy. Eggs are now collected from Fatu only (Najin retired from collection in 2021); embryos are made by ICSI with frozen sperm from dead males. 39 pure embryos as of April 2026 (fetched). A southern white IVF pregnancy was confirmed in January 2024 (Curra, after a September 2023 transfer; searched). "They've made dozens ... no northern white pregnancy has lasted yet": correct as of April 2026.
- **Southern white surrogate.** *Ceratotherium simum simum*, the closest living relative of the northern white (*C. s. cottoni*, a separate species in some classifications). "A close relative": correct.
- **Bottleneck.** All current embryos come from one female's eggs and a few males' sperm. "Every calf would come from one female's eggs and a few males' sperm. That's a tiny gene pool, a bottleneck from the start": correct for the embryo route ("would" is conditional).
- **Ecosystem role.** White rhinos are bulk grazers that create grazing lawns. "Decades away at best": correct as a hedge.
- **Yield.** Yield measures production, not biodiversity. Correct.
- **Attempt 1.** Gene flow from herbicide-tolerant canola to *Brassica rapa* and other relatives is documented; introgression with selection and demographic swamping are the site's pathways (Ellstrand, Dangerous Liaisons? 2003; Warwick et al., Mol Ecol 2008;17:1387). 10b's chain (gene flow; selection by the edge spray makes tolerance common as non-carriers die; swamping of a small population) is correct; "only wild plants carrying it survive" is correct for the stem's spraying condition.
- **Attempt 2.** Chestnut blight (*Cryphonectria parasitica*), found in New York in 1904, killed an estimated 3 to 4 billion American chestnuts in the first half of the twentieth century. A transgenic line with a wheat oxalate oxidase gene was developed; in 2023 the distributed material was found to be a different line, and the American Chestnut Foundation withdrew support. The card names no line and makes no status claim (project manager decision). "Were once common ... killed billions of them": correct. 10d's "animals could regain a food tree, but other effects are uncertain": correct as hedged.
- **Absolute words checked.** "Every Cavendish banana plant is a clone" (correct for the cultivar); "TR4 can kill any of them" (susceptible, hedged by "can"); "isn't a gain at every level" (correct, the crossed misconception); "every calf would come from" (conditional, correct for the embryo route). Finding 5 removes "every plant or animal". No "always", "completely" or discontinued-product claims.
- **Memory aids.** "The banana problem returns" (bank insight) is correct after finding 5, limited to the genetic level; "Which level? Compared with what?" (site L11) is a correct tracing rule.
- **Em dashes:** none in the design.
