## Learner trace and boundaries

From C04 and G02, the learner can read standards and select the right guideline. Extra limiting nutrient removes a growth brake; algae grow; dead material feeds microbial respiration; oxygen falls; the student chooses temperature, nutrients and algal counts for warning. The matched lake states and day/night trace serve this chain. The spatial table needs the inference repair above.

No re-teaching of DO measurement or treatment processes is needed. The one treatment link stays. C04 colourimetry is a brief recall.
