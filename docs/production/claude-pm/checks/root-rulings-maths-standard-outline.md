# Root rulings: Mathematics Standard outline (18 November 2026)

Root read the crosswalk (`programme/syllabus/maths-standard-2024-crosswalk.json`, 333 content points), the outline (`programme/maths-standard-course-outline.md`, 79 Year 11 and 92 Year 12 videos plus optional practice) and `site-corrections-maths-standard.md`. Claude is designer of record (owner, standing). These rulings bind the packs and designs.

1. **Order: Year 12 first.** Students sitting the 2027 HSC are on the new course now. Design and build Year 12 Standard (strand order as the outline recommends: algebraic relationships, then investment, loans and annuities, then the rest), then Year 11. Within the overall queue, Standard starts now in design; it builds only as voicing capacity allows, after the in-flight courses.
2. **Standard 1 is in scope** through the shared videos: Year 12 videos are built on Standard 2, and the 45 that also cover the matching Standard 1 points say so in their descriptions (both codes). math-s12-a08 is the only Standard 1-only video. A Standard 1 playlist can be filtered later.
3. **Site gaps.** Where the site has no lesson (12 Year 11 points, mainly spreadsheets, BAC and medication formulas, metric prefixes, interpolation and extrapolation, infographics), the video teaches from the syllabus alone. This is a recorded exception to the "site is the source of truth" rule, limited to missing content; where a site lesson exists, the video mirrors it. Gaps are logged in `site-corrections-maths-standard.md` for the website AI.
4. **BAC and medication.** BAC is framed around road safety: P-platers must have zero BAC, and the formula is an estimate, never a guide to how much is safe to drink. Medication dose formulas use invented, clearly labelled examples, with "your doctor or pharmacist sets real doses" on screen and in narration.
5. **Money figures.** Use the current Australian rates for the 2026-27 financial year (tax tables, Medicare levy, stamp duty for NSW), cite the source in the design, and say once in each video that rates change every year. Prices and wages are realistic for NSW in 2026.
6. **Everyday anchors.** Standard students need practical contexts: work, money, phones, sport, travel, cooking, renting, cars. Mostly no science. No gambling (as in the Advanced rulings).
7. **Reuse from Advanced.** Visual parts (kit, sims, plates) may be reused freely; scripts are new and gentler. No Advanced video is relabelled as a Standard video.

## Rulings on the a-strand packs (18 November 2026)
8. **Splits accepted** for a gentler Standard pace: a02 into a02a (substitution) and a02b (elimination); a04 into a04a (break-even, profit and loss) and a04b (break-even in a spreadsheet); a11 into a11a (reading the graph) and a11b (valid values and limitations). The a and b parts share the outline number. Other videos split only if a design cannot be taught well under 570 s.
9. **Standard 1 path:** Standard 1 viewers go from a08 to math-s12-i01; a09 to a13 are Standard 2 only. a03 gives a graph route in every worked example so Standard 1 students (who skip a02's algebra) can follow.
10. **Notation:** the syllabus letters on screen (y = a^x in a05, y = k·a^x in a06), not the site's A·b^x.
11. **Site examples:** do not use the science or sensitive site examples (bacteria, a drug dose, a bushfire, Boyle's Law) as worked examples; the site's beyond-syllabus x = −b/(2a) in a09 stays out of the videos.
12. **a06's car example** may preview depreciation in one sentence only; math-s12-i10 teaches it.
