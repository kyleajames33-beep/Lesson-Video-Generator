## The blockers

**v03 8a session 1 (HIIT by a ratio that breaks the video's own HIIT rule).** Scene 3 and key note 3 teach HIIT as 30 s to 4 min efforts at 1:1 to 1:2 (rest equal to or longer than work). 8a session 1 is 6 × 4 min with 2 min jogging (2:1), the card demands a justification "with reference to the work-to-rest ratio", and the feedback calls it HIIT "about two to one". A student who applies the taught rule cannot reach the marked answer, and v04 then defines the same session ("five four-minute efforts near the threshold", 2 to 3 min recovery) as aerobic intervals. Fix: session 1 becomes "8 × 60 s hard, 60 to 90 s jogging" (about 1:1), and v04 adds one clause separating aerobic intervals (just below the threshold, rest shorter than work) from HIIT.

**v04 scene 2 and key note 1 (an "anaerobic threshold" at 85 to 90 per cent beside Year 11's lactate threshold at a different number).** Root's Module 2 ruling 3 says "lactate threshold" only, never "anaerobic threshold", with numbers as a share of maximum aerobic capacity; hms-y11-m2-v14 places the trained lactate threshold at about 70 to 80 per cent of maximum aerobic capacity. v04 draws "anaerobic threshold ~85 to 90%" (of maximum heart rate) and aligns Year 11's curve's bend with it, and students copy the term and number into their books. A returning student will write two thresholds or think one number is wrong. Fix: name it the lactate threshold throughout (one silent chip "also called the anaerobic threshold", since the site uses that term), label the axis "% of maximum heart rate", add a small second scale at the threshold line in "% of maximum aerobic capacity", and say once that 85 to 90 per cent of maximum heart rate is the same point as Year 11's 70 to 80 per cent of maximum aerobic capacity. v06 and v08 inherit the term and need the same change.

## Module-level findings

1. **Internal video numbers spoken and shown (should fix in seventeen designs: v04, v05, v06, v07, v09, v10, v11, v13 to v22).** Narration says "v-three", "v-eight", "v-seventeen" and so on, and grey recall tags read "v04", "v08", "(v22)"; v19's key note 3 ends "(v17 ideas)", which students will copy. Neither Year 11 Module 2 nor Year 12 Module 3 does this (checked by script: 0 occurrences), and the course brief says "earlier", never module or video numbers. Fix everywhere: "last time", "earlier", "later", and grey "earlier" tags. Only v01 to v03, v08 and v12 are clean.

2. **"Unseen" practice items answered by the body (should fix in eleven designs: v02 8a, v04 8a and 8c, v06 8a, v07 8a, v15 8a, v16 8a, v17 8a, v18 8c, v19 8a, v21 8c, v22 8c; notes in v05, v09, v11, v18 8a, v19 8c, v21 8a, v22 8a).** This is Year 11 Module 2's first finding again, at the same scale. The usual shape: the body works a case (a sprinter doing only long runs; a late post-training meal; low iron found by a blood test; a recreational over-strider; a six-hour sleeper with an ice bath; a negative-tested athlete), and the attempt sets the same case in another sport with the same answer. Each review gives a replacement that tests the same rule from a new side. The genuinely unseen items (v01, v03 8c, v08 8c, v10, v11 8a, v12, v13 8a, v14 8c, v17 8c, v20) are the model.

3. **Year 11 re-taught as new (should fix in v08, v14, v17; v16 note).** v08's main question and worked answer (a trained runner's lower resting heart rate: same output, bigger beats) is hms-y11-m2-v05's hook and calculation, and its overlay thickens the ventricle wall first, the feature Year 11 tied to high blood pressure; v08 also re-teaches Year 11's fibre types. v14 restates glycogen, hitting the wall, "training is the signal, protein is the material" (hms-y11-m2-v06, v10, v11) and teaches the sweat and plasma chain box by box, against root's ruling 6. v17 re-teaches summation of forces, absorption over time and area, and the box held close (hms-y11-m2-v03) under new names. Within the module, v13 re-teaches v10's strategy and tactic. The protein line is now said three times (Year 11, v14, v16). Fix: one recall sentence with grey "earlier" tags, and spend the time on what is new; the time saved also pays for the additions other findings need (every design is 560 to 569 s).

4. **Cross-course consistency with the Module 2 rulings (should fix in v04, v06, v08; needs a root ruling).** Beyond the blocker: (a) the aerobic floor. v04 and v06 place the aerobic training threshold at about 70 per cent of maximum heart rate (and v06 8c calls 60 per cent "below the aerobic threshold"; v08 8a uses 65), but hms-y11-m2-v12 (Revision 2) still teaches "the aerobic training zone, sixty to eighty-five per cent", while v13 uses 70 to 85. A Year 11 student loses marks in v06 8c and v08 8a. Recommended ruling: 70 to 85 per cent of maximum heart rate course-wide, amending Year 11 v12 scene 4 and 9b. (b) Percentages without their maximum: v08 8a ("65 per cent of maximum") and scene 6 ("a lower share of maximum"). Other rulings are respected: carbohydrate's wording is not contradicted (v14 does not use "only fuel"), breathing drive is not restated, and "mostly taught as a whole" is not contradicted.

5. **Cards, marks and feedback that do not line up (should fix).** v01 8d omits the structure mark; v13 8c's marking gives the netball half two marks (one for substitution, never asked or modelled) and the badminton half one; v22 8c's narration asks for "two features and two limits" while the card and marks reward one and one; v03 8c's "beaten off the first step in the second half" points to fatigue, which the marking does not accept. Root should require that every mark appears on the card and in the feedback, and that the spoken demand matches the card word for word.

6. **Science and currency items that a beginner would copy (should fix, then the science check).** v02: elite tests "catch a change smaller than normal day-to-day variation" (impossible; the test's error must be smaller than the change). v06: key note "strength ~85% 1RM" as the threshold (false for beginners; skips v03's 65 to 85 band). v10: the model constraint "a three-second hold" is netball's existing rule. v15: the teen sleep range is on screen and in a key note, against root's seventh-set ruling. v16: the hook's ranking puts creatine first for a 17-year-old with no junior-athlete caution. v20: "overuse" as a separate cause versus an onset axis (confirm marking accepts both). v21: RICER is kept, correctly hedged; newer guidance (protect, early pain-guided loading, less prolonged icing) is flagged for the science check. v20's concussion wording and v01's APSS question wording also need confirming.

7. **Scene density (should fix where five or more glossed terms or a calculation share one scene).** v01 scene 3 (the whole APSS plus the weighted-minutes calculation, about 1,040 characters), v02 scene 3 (four tests plus the fatigue index), v05 scene 2 (four stretching methods), v06 scene 4, v09 scene 4 (five hazard categories), v12 scenes 3 and 4 (five terms each), v20 scene 2 (six terms). 8. **The course close (should fix in v22).** Root confirmed the strip (Module 4's models plus Year 11's meanings ladder, no Next chip), but the narration closes the course as if it were only about athletes ("how do we look after people who move?"). Half of HMS (individual and community health, Australia's health system, the SDGs) is not mentioned. The v22 review gives a four-second spoken fix that names both halves; root may also add one anchor thumbnail each for Year 11 Module 2 and Year 12 Module 3.

9. **Hooks and feedback length (notes).** Every hook is a real puzzle and nearly all are answered on screen by filling the hook's own slots, which is a module strength. Almost all ask students to write two or three things with a one-second hold; add 2 s. Feedback at or over 30 s by the authors' counts: v05 8b, v07 8b, v08 8d, v10 8b, v12 8d, v13 8d, v14 8b, v15 8b, v16 8d, v21 8d (31 s); trims are in each review. Every feedback gives the answer, one or two reasons and one crossed classic mistake, and none re-teaches.

## Module coherence

- **Order and joins.** All twenty-two joins match by script, including hms-y12-m3-v22 to v01 ("Why screen people before they exercise?"), and every opening reproduces the previous handoff frame. The order follows the syllabus's five inquiry questions: personalising assessment (v01, v02), training methods and principles (v03 to v08), individual and group sports (v09 to v13), sleep, nutrition and supplementation (v14 to v16), and sustained performance (v17 to v22). Each video keeps one main question.
- **Recurring models.** Introduced once and reused well: screening pathway (v01, v02), assessment cycle (v02, v03), demand-profile routine (v03 to v05, v11), threshold band (v04, v06, v08), adaptation staircase (v06, v07 backwards, v08, v18), adaptation chain (v08, v09), session strip (v09, v10), strategy and tactic (v10, v13), year calendar and fitness-fatigue (v11, v12), inverted U (v12, v22), factors board (v13, v14), fuel windows (v14, v15), supplement ledger (v16, v22), efficiency readout (v17, v18), recovery pyramid (v18, v19), load chart (v19, v20), TOTAPS gate (v20, v21), rehabilitation staircase (v21, v22). The cross-video threads (the load spike that becomes v20's injury; the staircase whose shortcut becomes v22's drugs) are strong.
- **Syllabus coverage.** Every dotpoint and sub-point of the focus area is owned and taught, with the syllabus examples (Yo-Yo and Wingate tests; environmental conditions and group strengths; physical activity, sport-specific and functional movements; TOTAPS). No gaps found.

## Checks across all twenty-two

- **Causes before reliance:** met throughout; the module names mechanisms (PCr refill, the stretch-shortening spring, fatigue masking fitness, the blood-flow valve, masking pain). Exceptions are in findings 4 and 6.
- **Visual medium:** every video has a moving model that carries its main relationship, and several make visual arguments: v04's two histograms whose eight-week lines diverge, v08's concentration gauge falling as the total rises, v11's performance line rising into the fitness-fatigue gap, v12's two tokens riding their own inverted Us, v13's change-latency strip, v17's projectile sim (ten per cent more speed adds about 3.5 m; seven degrees add almost nothing), v19's evidence cards, v21's valve. Plate issues found on inspection: the Achilles spring must sit on the tendon band at the calf plate's lower left (v03); the left ventricle is on the heart plate's right and the overlay should widen the chamber first (v08); the shoulder plate's ball is painted in its socket, so the dislocation overlay needs the painted head greyed (v20); v08 must wait for the mosaic fibre plate (v2), not fall back to v1. Motion still needs judging at playback speed.
- **Response opportunity:** all 44 cards keep the full demand on screen through the 10 s countdown and after; all end "Pause here to answer."; no picture slot shows an answer.
- **People, culture, sensitive topics:** nobody is drawn; athletes are tokens, linkages and capsules; anatomy is regional. Drugs, supplements and injury are handled generally, with help-seeking framed positively (v16, v22) and no doses on screen.
- **Memory aids:** two framed lines per video from the site's own sentences, after the explanation, each with its assumption recorded. All are sound for the cases taught; v06's strength-threshold key note is the exception.
- **Style and length:** 0 em or en dashes in all twenty-two designs and all twenty-three review files (searched by script). Narration 6,122 to 6,234 characters, about 560 to 569 s, so every design is near the cap: added speech (v04's bridge, v16's junior caution, v22's close, the 2 s hook holds) must come from reserve cuts or from the time freed by finding 3.
