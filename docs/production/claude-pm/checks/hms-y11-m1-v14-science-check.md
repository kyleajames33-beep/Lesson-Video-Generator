Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- Three levels of government (scene 2): Commonwealth funds health nationally, runs Medicare (through Services Australia), regulates medicines (TGA) and cross-border tobacco matters; states and territories run public hospitals and public schools, alcohol licensing and driver licensing; councils run parks, lighting, footpaths, pools and sportsgrounds. Correct in general terms (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing, "Australia's health system").
- Tobacco excise working especially well for young people (scene 3): Cancer Council, *Tobacco in Australia*, section 13 (price elasticity higher in young people). Correct.
- NGOs and the funding tension (scene 4): correct as a general claim.
- Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisations (scene 6): primary health care services initiated and operated by the local Aboriginal community, governed by an elected board (NACCHO definition). "Self-determination ... which national policy commits to strengthening": National Agreement on Closing the Gap (2020), Priority Reform Two (building the community-controlled sector). Correct; stated generally. ACCHO names a sector, not a community; compliant with the 18 Nov rule.
- Impact staircase (scene 7) and checklist growth (scene 8): standard evaluation logic.
- Vaping chain (scene 9): only government could restrict import and supply. Correct (TGA: disposable vape import ban from 1 January 2024; non-therapeutic vape import ban from 1 March 2024; pharmacy-only supply from 1 July 2024 under the Therapeutic Goods and Other Legislation Amendment (Vaping Reforms) Act 2024; adults able to buy lower-strength vapes from pharmacies without prescription from 1 October 2024 where state law allows; https://www.tga.gov.au/products/unapproved-therapeutic-goods/nicotine-vaping-products-hub/reforms-regulation-vapes).
- Practice 11a/11b and 11c/11d: correct; no reasonable alternative crossed.
