## Summary

- UK Biobank, the Piel et al. 2010 map, heterozygote advantage, the Tasmanian devil case and the 1000 Genomes figures are all supported.
- Should fix: scene 5 "they line up" overstates the map match outside Africa; scene 8 "other populations mostly carry a subset of it" is wrong for all variants (true for common variants); feedback 11d says pairing the least related animals "adds" diversity.
- Minor: "began in 2006" (recruitment began then); "850,000" is UK Biobank's own rounded figure (the array has about 821,000 to 826,000 markers); scene 6 "only after" is a shaky historical claim; scene 5 "lives inside red blood cells" omits the liver stage (acceptable).
- Syllabus quotes match the JSON word for word (including the double space before "for example").
