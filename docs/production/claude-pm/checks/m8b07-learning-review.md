## Findings

### 1. should fix: 6

Text: "Think of each exposure as a ticket in a lottery"

The explanation immediately talks about repair and cells collecting mutations. It fits carcinogenesis but follows COPD and vascular disease, where risk is not exhausted by unrepaired mutations.

Repair: Start "For cancer, think of each exposure ...". Keep dose as the general principle, then explicitly attach mutation/repair lottery to cancer. No added scene.

### 2. should fix: 10a graph

Text: "percentage of Australian adult men ... from 1945 to 2020"

The graph combines invented exact values and an Australian population label. The small "teaching data" tag does not tell a student which axis is age-standardised or which smoking definition applies, so the lesson appears to ask analysis of real national measurements.

Repair: Label both series "illustrative" and remove the Australian measurement claim from the stem, or replace them with referenced actual series with age group, smoking status and cancer standard population. Keep the lag contrast. No new narration needed.

### 3. should fix: 10b

Text: "people quit or never started"

Cancers caused by earlier smoking can continue after quitting, but cannot be attributed to that earlier smoking in people who never started. The student loses the cohort-latency explanation.

Repair: Replace with "people had quit, even while fewer young people took up smoking". Keep the population lag and crossed classic mistake.
