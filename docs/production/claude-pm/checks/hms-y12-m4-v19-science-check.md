## Confirmed
- Three categories (2). Matches the syllabus.
- Equipment (3): energy-return footwear lowers the metabolic cost of a given speed (about 4 per cent in a laboratory trial; Hoogkamer and others, *Sports Med* 2018); drag reduction; governing bodies have restricted equipment (for example full-body polyurethane swimsuits, banned from 2010). "technological doping" is an established term. Correct.
- Monitoring (4): GPS brands and units give different values for the same session; compare within one system (Scott, Scott and Kelly, *J Strength Cond Res* 2016). Privacy and consent for minors. Correct.
- Access (5) and causal inference (6). Correct reasoning.
- Handoff (9): a week at about twice the usual load; no ratio claim, which avoids the contested acute-to-chronic ratio (Impellizzeri and others, *Int J Sports Physiol Perform* 2020). Correct.
- 8a to 8d: correct. Memory aids sound. No people.

1. **Note (line 98, 8d).** "a real but small gain": the laboratory energy saving is about 4 per cent, translating to roughly 2 to 3 per cent in race time for trained runners and less certain for juniors. "small" is fair.
