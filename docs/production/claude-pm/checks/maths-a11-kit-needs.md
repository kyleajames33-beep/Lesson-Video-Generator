# Maths kit needs: Year 11 Advanced first strand (w01 to w14)

Brief 243, 16 November 2026. Existing parts are named from `src/explainer/math/studio/` (exports in `index.ts`), the older `src/explainer/math/` parts (`algebra.tsx`, `graphs.tsx`, `strand-parts.tsx`, `lesson-parts.tsx`) and `src/explainer/sims/`. These are requirements for later build work, not implemented capability or a pilot pass. No source file was changed.

Every video uses the shared frame: `WorkingBoard` (TaskStrip, EquationStrip, GivensCard, WorkingRows, AnswerFrame, MistakeCard), `QuestionCard` with its countdown, `KeyNotesScreen` and `MemoryAidCard` from `cards.tsx`, role colours from `roles.tsx` and `HandMark` / `DrawnMark` from `primitives.tsx`. Graphs use `StudioPlot` from `plot-core.tsx` (one coordinate transform). No simulation engine in `src/explainer/sims/` fits this strand: they are physics, chemistry, biology and chance engines. `chance.ts` becomes relevant for the probability strand (s04 to s08), not here.

## New parts for the trigonometry strand in one list

1. **StudioCirclePoint** (t03, t04, t08, t10, t12, t13, t15): circle of radius r; ray at any signed angle with a lap counter; point (x, y) and its drop triangle; readouts of the six ratios; related-angle mode with the four copies of A and quadrant signs; mirror modes in the x-axis and in y = x; a level line y = k; degree or radian labels; undefined flags. The main new part.
2. **StudioTriangle** (t01, t02, t05, t06, t07, t11, t13, t14): a labelled triangle from side and angle data, with role colours relative to a focus angle, special-triangle presets, a perpendicular drop with its foot and segments, the SSA arc construction, a hinge mode, a fixed-hypotenuse limit mode, scale modes and a degrees-and-minutes readout.
3. **BearingRose** (t02, t04, t06): north lines at each vertex, clockwise bearing sweeps with three-digit readouts, compass-bearing labels, reverse bearings, two-leg journeys and an elevation and depression mode.
4. **RadiusWrap** (t08, t09): radius lengths wrapping the circumference, and an arc straightening to length rθ.
5. **SectorSegment** (t09): sector, chord, triangle and segment, minor and major.
6. **CircleToGraph** (t10): a projector from a **StudioCirclePoint** to a graph, plus a `TAN_FAMILY` constant and a degree-tick helper in `trig-parts.tsx`.
7. **Surd formatter** (t01, t04, t08, t11, t12): exact values with roots (√3/2, 2√3/3, (1 − √3)/2), since `ExactFraction` takes rational values only.

No painted plate is required for t01 to t16. If Claude uses an everyday anchor as a picture (a pool rack, a compass app, a give way sign, a running track bend), it follows the existing plate rules: a Codex painted plate for objects, never a coded icon, and no people.
