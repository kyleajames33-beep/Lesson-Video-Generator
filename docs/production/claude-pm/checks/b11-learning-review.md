## Association against causation; "evidence, not proof"

Clear. Scene 8 names the trend, calls it an association with the gloss "found together more often", crosses "T causes the disease", and gives a concrete alternative cause the student can picture (linkage, with B8's swap playing far from the pair). Attempt 2 asks the question cold and the feedback answers with the same reason. Revision 2 adds one unspoken tag on what would be needed to show cause ("what the variant does to a protein"), which hands off to B12 scene 9b without adding speech. "Strong evidence, not proof" is the B12 framing for a profile match; in B11 the right framing is "an association is a pattern, not a cause", which scene 13 says. No change needed.
