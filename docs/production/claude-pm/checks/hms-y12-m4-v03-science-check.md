# hms-y12-m4-v03 science check (anaerobic training methods)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: script, on-screen text in the visual lines, practice, key notes, memory aids and facts section; no build or playback; plates checked by name and description). Independent check; the design was not edited. Line numbers refer to `lessons/hms-y12-m4-v03.md` as of this check.

## Confirmed
- Energy-system bands (2): ATP-PC dominant to about 10 s, glycolysis about 10 s to 2 min, aerobic beyond about 2 min; phosphocreatine "runs low" and refills over minutes (about half in about 30 s; Harris and others 1976; Bogdanis and others 1995). Consistent with hms-y11-m2-v09, v10 and v12.
- Interval methods (3): the site's ranges with "about"; the rule that the rest decides the system is correct. Hook sessions: 30 s/30 s is HIIT, 6 s/3 min is SIT. Correct.
- Plyometrics (4): stretch-shortening cycle, elastic energy in the tendon, stretch reflex, amortisation, energy lost as heat if slow; contacts, fresh, full rest, strength base first. Correct (NSCA, *Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning*, 4th ed.). 8c's 80 to 100 contacts is the NSCA beginner volume.
- Resistance loading (5): strength about 85 per cent of 1RM or more, 1 to 5 reps, 3 to 5 min (root ruling; NSCA 2 to 5 min); hypertrophy 65 to 85 per cent, 6 to 12 reps, short rests; power with light loads moved fast. Correct as rules of thumb. Worked loads (85 kg; 65 to 85 kg) correct.
- 8a ratios: 60 s : 60 to 90 s (HIIT), 10 s : 90 s = 1:9 (SIT), 60 s : 4 min = 1:4 (anaerobic intervals). Correct.
- Memory aids sound. No people.

## Findings
1. **Note (line 58, SIT; flagged tag).** "recovery is nearly complete and every effort stays maximal" fits 6 to 10 s efforts. With 30 s all-out efforts at about 1:8, power still falls across repeats, and 30 s SIT produces large aerobic adaptations too (Gibala and others, *J Physiol* 2006). The Revision 2 tag "trains: peak power, repeated maximal sprints" is acceptable; "nearly complete" could become "much more complete".
2. **Note (line 68, power).** 30 to 60 per cent of 1RM moved fast is a recognised ballistic power load; NSCA also lists heavier loads for multi-effort power lifts. Acceptable as one prescription.

## Verdict
Pass. 0 blockers, 0 should fix, 2 notes. The flagged SIT tag is acceptable.
