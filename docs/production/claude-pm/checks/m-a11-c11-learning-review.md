## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 3 ("So the income grows thirty-eight dollars a week, each month.").** Thirty seconds earlier the student worked out that the first month added $40, and scene 5 says the rate rises as 38 + 4t. As worded, $38 sounds like the actual monthly increase, which contradicts both. The point of the shrinking corner is that 38 is the rate right now, at t = 0. Fix: "So right now, her income is growing at thirty-eight dollars a week, per month. The real first month adds a bit more, forty, because both factors keep growing." Change the row to "rate now = 8 + 30 = $38 a week, per month". This also settles open decision 2 ("per month" is clearer than "each month").

2. **Optional, feedback 2 (the factorising step is missing on screen, and one phrase is ambiguous aloud).** The rows jump from "3x²(1 − x)⁴ − 4x³(1 − x)³" to "x²(1 − x)³(3 − 7x)", and "minus four x cubed, times one minus x cubed" can be heard as 1 − x³. Add an unspoken row "= x²(1 − x)³[3(1 − x) − 4x]" and say "times one minus x, all cubed".

3. **Optional, scene 6 (about 69 s, the longest stage in the strand).** It covers a chain rule inside a product, factorising, then zeros. It works on the g01 board with dimmed rows, but if the voiced take runs long, cut the zeros sentence ("If a question asks where the gradient is zero...") and keep its row.
