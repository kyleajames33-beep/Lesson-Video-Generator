## Confirmed
- Stretching (2a, 2b): static holds about 15 to 30 s (ACSM 10 to 30 s); long static holds just before explosive work can briefly reduce power, while short holds inside a full warm-up have small effects (Behm and others, *Appl Physiol Nutr Metab* 2016); dynamic suits the warm-up; ballistic carries the highest risk; PNF is contract-relax with a partner. Correct.
- Equipment (3): free weights demand stabilisation; machines guide the path; body weight scaled by leverage, range or speed; band tension rises with stretch, so load peaks near end range, "often" where the joint is strongest (ascending strength curves). Correct.
- Skill (4): drills, modified games, games for outcomes; the touch football rule. Consistent with Year 11 practice types.
- 8b sprinter: elastics load most near full hip extension. Correct.
- 8c/8d: constraint-led design. Correct.
- Memory aids sound. No people.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (line 88, 8a; line 93, 8b; line 128, marking).** The 14-year-old trains "with no coach present and no spotter", and the model answer prescribes "fixed weights and body weight, because a guided path is safe without a spotter". Youth resistance-training guidance makes qualified supervision the first condition whatever the equipment, and adult machines often do not fit a 14-year-old (Faigenbaum and others, NSCA position statement, *J Strength Cond Res* 2009; Lloyd and others, international consensus, *Br J Sports Med* 2014). As written, the video models unsupervised training for a young teenager as the safe answer. Corrected: stem "is starting resistance training at school, with a teacher supervising but no spotter available"; 8b "The fourteen-year-old suits body weight and fixed weights, because a guided path is safe without a spotter, with a teacher supervising." Marking line A adds "under qualified supervision". The crossed mistake "heavy free weights with nobody watching" stays.
2. **Note (line 58).** "It's generally the most effective for range": PNF often shows the largest acute gains, but long-term differences between PNF and static stretching are small. Acceptable with "generally".

Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 1 should fix, 1 note.
