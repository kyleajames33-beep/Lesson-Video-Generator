# Maths kit needs: Year 11 Standard, Formulas and equations and Linear relationships (math-s11-f01 to f08, l01 to l07)

Maths Standard Year 11 start, 19 November 2026, methods of brief 243 section 5. Existing parts are named from `src/explainer/math/studio/` (exports checked against the files on 19 November 2026). These are requirements for later build work, not implemented capability or a pilot pass. No source file was changed.

Root ruling 7 applies: visual parts, sims and plates may be reused from Advanced; scripts are new and gentler. The Year 11 Advanced linear designs named below (w10, w11, w12, w22) are designed but not built.

Every video uses the shared frame: `WorkingBoard` (TaskStrip, EquationStrip, GivensCard, WorkingRows, AnswerFrame, MistakeCard) from `board.tsx`, `QuestionCard` with `GivensStrip` and its countdown, `KeyNotesScreen` and `MemoryAidCard` from `cards.tsx`, role colours from `roles.tsx`, and `HandTag`, `DrawnMark` and `PlateImg` from `primitives.tsx`. Graphs use `StudioPlot` (plot-core.tsx). "With graphing applications" uses `ScreenApp` (finance-parts.tsx) in graph mode, unbranded.

## New parts in one list

1. **SpreadsheetGrid** (f08, l05; first requested by math-s12-a04b; later Year 11 e06, e12, m06, m08 and data analysis; Year 12 i05, i11, c06): the main dependency. Year 11 adds `^`, `/`, an in-cell bar option and a linked chart (l05). Build before f08.
2. **StoppingDistanceStrip** (f04, with callbacks in f05 and f08): the two-part stopping bar on a road strip.
3. **FormulaSlots** (f01, f05, f06, f07): a value dropping into a bracketed slot, then evaluation in order; probably a `ModelCard` prop.
4. Small options: solid-then-dashed line on `StudioPlot` (l04; also b07); ConversionGraph reading paths (l07); a ± fork on `RootExtract` (f03).
5. Plates (Codex, objects only, no people): a P plate on a car's rear window; a roadside breath-test sign; an unbranded "Medicine A" bottle with a measuring syringe; a first-aid kit. Anchor plates in the packs (a roasting tray, a scoreboard, a trampoline, a swim cap and kickboard, an aquarium, a hire boat, pick-and-mix lollies, cherries) only if Claude chooses those anchors.

No simulation engine is required. Every graph, table, formula and spreadsheet is coded; real objects are painted plates (owner rule: no basic coded icons for real objects).
