# Brief 246: measured spectra check, c10 to c12

16 November 2026. Research and checking only. No lesson, narration, scene code or recorded audio changed.

**Result:** measured liquid-film references now cover all seven missing c10 compounds. The ethanoic acid C–O feature is 1294 cm⁻¹, not near 1100. Several provisional depths and missing fingerprint bands need correction. The carbon-13 comparisons retain all c11a answers and rounded spoken shifts. c11b and c12 still have the specific record gaps listed below. This report does not lift all four recording holds.

IR notation below is centre in cm⁻¹ followed by minimum transmittance in parentheses, for example 1714 (4). Lower %T means stronger absorption. Values read from a graph are approximate and rounded to whole %T; they are not instrument-export precision. A depth comparison applies to that particular liquid-film record, not every possible film thickness. None of the transmission curves has been replaced by a gas, solution, ATR-depth or optical-constant curve.

NMR shifts are ppm. All accepted NMR comparisons use CDCl₃. Proton field strengths are given explicitly. HMDB line intensities are peak heights, not integrated areas. Multiplet centres are inferred from the principal lines; structural hydrogen assignments are separate from measured integration. The stricter design limits, 1 ppm for c11a and 0.1 ppm for c11b, were retained alongside brief 246's limits.
