## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 3 carries the whole APSS and a worked calculation in one scene (about 1,040 characters).** In one breath the student meets the APSS, two stages, six Stage 1 questions, question 7, weighted minutes with its doubling rule, a worked case, two advice branches, six Stage 2 factors and "no fixed risk categories". The calculation (the rule the student most needs to apply in 8a and 8c) arrives in the middle of a list and is gone within ten seconds. A student will ask: "Why is vigorous doubled, and what happens at exactly 150?" Fix: split at "Stage 2 is recommended" into 3a (Stage 1 and question 7, ending on the framed "150 min" and a 1 s settle with the two branches visible) and 3b (Stage 2, its factors, the three dials and the crossed category chip). Add one clause to 3a so the doubling has a reason: "vigorous minutes count double, because they're about twice the work." No other narration change. Students should be able to work a weighted-minutes total and read the branch before Stage 2 begins.

2. **Should fix, 8d does not model the fourth mark the marking rewards.** The marking gives mark 4 for "a sensible first-four-weeks structure linked to the 10 km goal (frequency, duration building)", and the card asks how Stage 2 "should shape the first four weeks". The feedback's four lines are APSS, knee, knee strength and blood pressure; no line gives a week-by-week structure, and lines 2 and 3 are the same mark. A student who writes a good structure sees nothing in the feedback that matches it. Fix: merge lines 2 and 3 ("2 knee: low impact, gradual distance, knee strength") and add line 4: "4 weeks 1 to 4: about three walks a week, adding about ten minutes a week toward the goal" (about 3 s; the feedback is about 27 s, so it stays near 30 s). Students should see every mark in the model answer.

3. **Note, the hook asks students to write three things with a one-second hold.** Add a 2 s quiet hold with the three empty slots lit. The design is about 563 s, so this fits.

4. **Note, scene 2 is dense (about 920 characters, two glossed terms and three purposes).** Each purpose card writes on its cue, so it reads; fade the population field to grey before "It does three jobs" so the purposes are the only bright items.

5. **Note, the worked case sits exactly on the boundary.** 90 + 60 = 150 lands on "150 or more: continue". That is a useful edge case, but it is the only worked number, and both attempts are under 150. Say "exactly a hundred and fifty, so it counts" in scene 6, or keep the case and let 8a and 8c carry the under-150 branch, as they do.
