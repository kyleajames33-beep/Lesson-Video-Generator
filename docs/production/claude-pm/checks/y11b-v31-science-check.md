1. **Blocker, shared with science:** The assessment answer says meiosis is essential for every species and is the only way to reshuffle alleles each generation. Some species reproduce asexually without regular meiosis, and other mechanisms also produce genetic variation. Restrict the judgement to the sexual life cycle being evaluated, explain that meiosis maintains chromosome number and creates combinations there, and accept other evidence-based judgements. Replace scenes 9, 10 and A2 feedback.
2. **Blocker, shared with science:** The banana example says every Cavendish plant is genetically identical and all are killed by TR4. Vegetatively propagated commercial Cavendish plants are genetically similar and susceptible, but somaclonal variation and TR4-resistant modified Cavendish exist. State that low genetic diversity can leave susceptible plantations vulnerable to TR4, without claiming universal identity or inevitable death. Use the design's fallback and recheck the current facts at voicing.
3. **Should fix, shared with science:** Scene 6 says meiosis makes every gamete different and four separate cells in ovaries or testes. Different outcomes are likely, not guaranteed; human oogenesis typically produces one egg and polar bodies. Keep the four-product model and distinguish human sperm and egg outcomes with the existing note.
4. **Should fix, shared with science:** Scene 7 says replication comes first and cytokinesis finishes every division. The standard cycle illustrates both, but nuclear division without cytokinesis and other life cycles occur, including the very cytokinesis-blocking scenario in A1. Say these steps are needed for the ordinary separate daughter cells in the two examples.
5. **Should fix, shared with science:** The switch-off argument assumes checkpoints invariably stop unreplicated cells and says wound closure is impossible under all circumstances. State the stipulated experimental consequence and that normal checkpoints can halt division, while failures and other repair processes are possible. Say unrepaired replication errors can be inherited by descendant cells rather than guarantee every descendant keeps the change.

[Cavendish somaclonal research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9233791/) identifies genomic differences in TR4-resistant variants, and the [QCAV-4 report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12392930/) describes a genetically modified TR4-resistant Cavendish approved for commercial production and consumption. Conventional susceptible Cavendish plantations remain a suitable example of reduced diversity and disease vulnerability. This is time-sensitive and should be checked again at voicing, as root decision 34 requests. These counterexamples do not mean every variety is protected or available everywhere.

L25's claim that meiosis alone generates anything new conflicts with mutations producing new alleles. Its definition of all asexual reproduction as mitosis excludes prokaryotic binary fission. Its unreplicated-cell outcome and claim that removal of any process is fatal need a stated context and checkpoint condition. Log the first, second and fourth; treat the third as a hypothetical unless the text states it must occur. An assess answer needs a criterion and a supported judgement, not a single universal ranking.
