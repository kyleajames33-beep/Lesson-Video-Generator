## Findings

1. **Should fix, the six tests and six failure modes restate Year 11's strategy checklist and each other.** hms-y11-m1-v18 ended with a strategy checklist that "you've been building": who decided (consultation is not a vote), who does it reach and who does it miss, what changes and how would you know (a baseline, an indicator, results by group), what survives the funding, and who acts outside health. v22's tests 4 (reach), 5 (funded past the first cycle) and 6 (measures set in advance, by group) are that checklist, and scene 5's failure modes 2 to 5 (wrong people, ends too soon, no community, wrong measures) are the same items again, now as failures. Students meet one idea three times in two videos and once in Year 11, as different lists; the Year 11 summary's top fix was "one checklist that grew". Fix: in scene 4, show tests 4 to 6 with grey "earlier" tags and say "Three of these you've used since Year 11: who it reaches, whether it lasts, and how you'd know." Spend the time on the new tests (a stated mechanism; worked elsewhere, using v21's transfer test; acting where the problem is), which are what Year 12 adds. In scene 5, keep the question "What would have to be true for this to fail?" and show the failure cards as the flip side of the tests (each card links to its test), rather than as a second list of six; treat the symptom-not-cause and one-person cards as the two genuinely new failures. Key notes 3 and 5 then merge into one line. Students should leave Module 3 with one strategy checklist that grew from Year 11, not three.

2. **Note, 8d at about 30 s.** At the limit. Trim "or fluoridate the water supply" to "or water fluoridation".

3. **Note (science check, currency), water fluoridation as an advocacy target.** Correct and well evidenced; some councils have debated or removed fluoridation, so a 2026 student may meet it as contested. The feedback's wording ("push the state to fund public dental or fluoridate the water supply") is fine; no change unless the check prefers "keep or extend".

4. **Note, 8a's "fails most clearly".** The sunscreen proposal also arguably fails test 3 (sunscreen at the beach reaches beach-goers, not everyone exposed), and the marking accepts reach or the barrier for the sport program. "Most clearly" handles this; keep the marking open to a well-argued second test.
