| 1 | science | Scene 5: "A squeeze speeds up most the reaction that depends on more gas particles"; ammonia: "the forward reaction depends on more gas particles and gains more"; scene 12 repeats this reason. | Balanced gas coefficients determine the equilibrium shift but do not determine the concentration dependence of a multistep reaction's rate ([OpenStax rate laws](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry/pages/12-3-rate-laws)). | Keep the gas-count direction rule, and limit the demonstrated rate factors to the stated syringe rate equations. |
| 2 | science | Scene 6b: "The forward reaction needs one carbon dioxide to meet the solid, so it doubles. The reverse reaction needs two carbon monoxide to meet, so it goes up four times"; visual "forward x2", "reverse x4". | These rate factors and collision steps are inferred from the overall equation rather than established carbon-surface kinetics ([original Boudouard kinetics research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/000862238790011X)). | Remove the collision mechanism and rate factors; retain the one-to-two gas count and left shift. |
| 3 | science | Scene 10a: "because nothing here removes the N O two". | The reverse reaction consumes NO2 even while the net reaction forms it ([OpenStax dynamic equilibrium](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/13-3-shifting-equilibria-le-chateliers-principle)). | Say more NO2 forms each second than is consumed until the rates match. |
