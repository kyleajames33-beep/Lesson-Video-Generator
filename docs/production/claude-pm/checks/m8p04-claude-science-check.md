## Findings

### 1. should fix: 4 wavelength tag

Text: "about 800 nm (red to near infrared)"

800 nm is near infrared, beyond the usual red visible band. A common school colourimeter has a 635-nm LED, not an 800-nm setting.

Repair: Use "near infrared, about 800 nm" for the UV-visible instrument and "red filter, e.g. 635 nm; recalibrate" for the school colourimeter. Keep distinct numerical calibration datasets.

### 2. should fix: 10

Text: "Then the standards' line applies to it."

Same wavelength, cuvette and blank plus filtration are insufficient to establish absence of dissolved matrix interference.

Repair: Use a teacher-checked copper-only/artificial matrix for this school method, or require a suitable matrix validation before applying the line. Do not describe filtration as removal of dissolved metals.
