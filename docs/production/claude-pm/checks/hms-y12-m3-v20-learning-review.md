## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 6's method re-teaches Year 11's localisation method as new.** hms-y11-m1-v18 taught "start from a local need, not from the goals" (its memory line), mapping the need to goals with mechanisms, acting outside health, and measuring change from a baseline by group, and v17 crossed "a label on existing activity". v20's five steps open with "Start with the health issue, not the goals" (with the crossed chip "start from the goals") and end with "measure, by group, over a stated time". Only step 3 (check what each action depends on) is new, and it comes from this video's interlock. Fix: say "In Year 11 you learned to start from a local need, not from the goals, and to measure by group. Two checks are new." Show steps 1, 2 and 5 with grey "earlier" tags and spend the time on step 3 (dependencies: the cut transport edge) and step 4 (who would this miss, with the gap bracket). The memory card stays as is. Students who did Year 11 see the method grow, and v21 and v22 can then use one method rather than another new list (see the module summary).

2. **Note, the SDG 11 mechanisms overlap Year 11's goal 11 practice item.** hms-y11-m1-v17's attempt 1 asked how goal 11 improves young people's health (safe spaces and active transport, crowding and stress, transport to services). Scene 3 goes further (cold and damp, heat, rent squeeze, walkability against campaigns, green space), so it is not a repeat, but one clause "You traced some of these in Year 11" would acknowledge it.

3. **Note, scene 5 names "car dependence ... cuts daily activity" and "tree cover thinnest in lower-income suburbs".** Both are flagged for the science check as illustrative patterns; keep the heat and canopy overlay labelled "illustrative pattern" since it implies a geography.

4. **Note, 8b's answer for the buses lists four goals.** The card asks for "the goals it contributes to" and "TWO health mechanisms"; the feedback names SDG 11, 10, 3 and 4 in two sentences. That is correct, but a student may think all four are required. Say "at least SDG 11 and SDG 10" to show the minimum.
