## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 5 and the design notes ("ln" read aloud).** Open decision 1 and author note (f) say "The narration says 'the natural log of' throughout". It does not: scene 5 says "its own short label, ln. So ln of a means log base e of a", "ln of e is one", "ln of one is zero", "ln of e to the four", "an ln key", "Try ln of five" and "That x was ln of five"; scene 6 says "e to the ln x". The voice will read "ln" unpredictably ("lun", "L N" or "ln"), in the very scene that teaches the label. e04, e06 and e07 also say "ln" aloud ("That's the ln key", "ln twelve hundred", "ln x over ln a"), so this is a strand decision. Fix: in scene 5 say "its own short label, written l n, and read ell en", then respell "ln" as "ell en" in every e-strand voicing request (or as "the natural log of" everywhere), and correct the open decision and author note to match. Students should hear one consistent name for the key they press.

2. **Should fix, scene 3 and A1 (c) (a fractional log is asked but never shown).** A1 (c) asks for log₉ 3. The worked examples cover whole-number and negative logs only; no fraction value is modelled before the countdown, although the memory aid check claims "scenes 3 and A1 (c) show both". A student who reaches "9 to what power is 3?" may not think of a root. Fix: add one line to scene 3 after the negative case: "And a log can be a fraction. Log base four of two is a half, because four to the half is the square root of four, which is two." Visual: row "log₄ 2: 4^? = 2; 4^½ = √4 = 2; = ½" with the grey chip "w01: a^½ = √a". About 9 s; scene 1's "free pass" sentence is the named trim if needed.

3. **Should fix, scene 5 (a callback to a number e02 never showed).** Narration: "Remember the point on e to the x where the height was five? It was at x equals about one point six one." e02 scene 6 stops its rider at height 5, but its readouts are height, gradient and ratio; x ≈ 1.61 is neither said nor shown. Students cannot remember it. Fix (no e02 change needed): "Last time, the rider on e to the x stopped where the height was five. Where was that? At x equals about one point six one." If root prefers the recall wording, add an "x ≈ 1.61" readout to e02 scene 6 instead (e02 finding 4).
