## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 2 narration).** "when it contracts, it shortens and pulls the bone towards itself." Scene 6 and the hook's answer (the biceps lengthening while working) teach that a contracting muscle can lengthen or stay the same length. Corrected: "when it contracts, it pulls on the bone, and if it shortens, it draws the bone towards itself."
2. **Note (scene 5).** "The proportion in each person is largely inherited": twin studies put heritability of fibre type proportion at roughly 40 to 50 per cent. "strongly influenced by your genes" is safer if the line is touched.
3. **Note (gloss "isotonic").** Literally "same tension"; tension in fact varies through a lift. The gloss "changes length while moving a load" is the school usage and acceptable.
