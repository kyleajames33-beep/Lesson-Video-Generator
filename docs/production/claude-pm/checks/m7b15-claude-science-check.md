## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Smoke bush history.** (opened, Janke 2018) Every Janke quotation in the design's Numbers list is accurate: "predominant to the coastal areas between Geraldton and Esperance"; "traditionally used by Aboriginal people as medicine"; NCI collecting "Since the 1960s"; 17 specimens tested for cancer in 1981, negative; the late-1980s rescreen "for treating the AIDS virus"; DHHS "filed for a US patent in 1993" and "for an Australian patent in 1994"; AMRAD, "an Australian pharmaceutical company based in Victoria"; "Amrad paid $1.65 million to the WA Government" (citing Blakeney); "No royalties or other compensation or even acknowledgement were targeted as forthcoming for the Aboriginal people of WA". The US patent 5,672,607 was filed 29 January 1993 by the US DHHS and granted 30 September 1997 (opened). "In 1993, the United States government applied to patent a compound" is correct.
- **NCI species.** (opened, US 5,672,607) The main source is "*Conospermum* sp. (Spjut 7139)". The patent says the compounds can also come from *C. incurvum*, *C. stoechadis* and *C. triplinervium*. Labelling the plate to genus only is correct, and either named species is acceptable for the painting.
- **Mudjala patent.** (opened) US 7,618,946 B2, "Analgesic compounds, extracts containing same and methods of preparation": assignees Griffith University and Jarlmadangah Burru Aboriginal Corp; inventors Ronald Quinn and Clive Mills; priority AU 2003906558, 27 November 2003; PCT/AU04/01660 filed 26 November 2004; granted 17 November 2009. The sample is identified as *Barringtonia acutangula* ssp. *acutangula* from the Kimberley, so the species is confirmed. Janke: "The Jarlmadangah community and Griffith University became joint patent holders ... and additional patents were registered in other countries". "Filed in 2003" (priority) and "Ten years later" are fair.
- **Mudjala people and award.** (searched) ATSE: "Nyikina Mangala West Kimberley Elder John Dadigar Watson and Professor Ronald Quinn AM FTSE", inaugural Traditional Knowledge Innovation Award, 26 October 2023; mudjala is described as a mangrove tree (*B. acutangula* is the freshwater mangrove). "Griffith University chemist Ron Quinn" and "new pain-relieving compounds" are correct.
- **Spinifex.** (opened, Janke 2018) Indjalandji-Dhidhanu people "of the Camooweal/Upper Georgina River area"; the Dugalunji Aboriginal Corporation's commercial entity Myuma Pty Ltd; the 2015 patent on "a composite material comprising an elastomer and nanocellulose derived from spinifex plants", "owned solely by University of Queensland"; benefits "as negotiated in the research and collaboration agreement"; "the right to veto commercialisation"; benefits including "employment for Aboriginal youth". The veto, the jobs and the sole university ownership are correct. Finding 4 covers the attribution of the glue use.
- **Nagoya Protocol.** (opened, CBD signatories page) In force 12 October 2014; Australia signed 20 January 2012, with no ratification, acceptance, approval or accession date; 143 Parties. "Not ratified as at October 2026" is correct today.
- **ICIP and True Tracks.** Janke, *Our Culture: Our Future* (1998), is the standard origin of the ICIP framework, and *True Tracks* (UNSW Press, 2021) sets out ten principles matching the design's list. Both are as cited. The plain meaning of ICIP is for the reviewer.
- **Prior art.** Since 15 April 2013 (*Raising the Bar* Act), Australian prior art includes information made public by any means, oral included, anywhere. Under pre-2013 US law (35 USC 102, pre-AIA), foreign knowledge or use that was not published was not prior art at all. That strengthens the smoke bush point. "Can look new when it isn't" is fair.
- **Drug pipeline.** Isolation, cell tests, animal tests, human trials, then TGA approval: correct.
- **Attempts.** Both models follow the protocols correctly. Finding 5 adds the isolated-compound path to 12c. 12a's judgement ("each is a separate obligation") is correct.
- **Absolute words.** I searched for "only", "never", "all", "every", "must" and "can't". "It never became" is finding 7. "should be used only with" (finding 13) reports the law. No others need changing.

## Exam style

12a is an assess item (4 marks) and 12c an explain item (3 marks). Both are HSC Section II style, original, and tagged "HSC-style question, written for HSCScience" and "invented example". Each model gives a mark to each protocol or reason, plus a judgement. The crossed mistakes ("the payment makes it fair"; "the community should have patented it first") are the common wrong chains. Each feedback is about 27 to 28 s, inside the brief-answer rule. Finding 5 adds the isolated-compound reason to 12c.
