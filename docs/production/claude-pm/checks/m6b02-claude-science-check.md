## Facts confirmed (no change)

- **Thymine dimers from UV.** UVB forms cyclobutane pyrimidine dimers (and 6-4 photoproducts) between adjacent pyrimidines on the same strand. T-T is the commonest, and CPDs are the main sunlight DNA lesion. Nucleotide excision repair removes them. UV reaches mainly the epidermis. Correct (see finding 5 for the nuance). Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2011/pp/c1pp05144j
- **Hook ("many times over in each exposed cell", "almost all ... never becomes a mutation").** No number is spoken; the claim is qualitative and correct.
- **Ionising radiation: strand breaks and free radicals.** X-rays and gamma rays ionise. They break the backbone directly, and radiolysis of water gives hydroxyl radicals, which cause most of the DNA damage from low-LET radiation (about two-thirds is often cited). Single- and double-strand breaks result, and misrepair gives deletions and rearrangements. Correct as spoken. Sources: https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-00614957 ; standard radiobiology texts
- **Electromagnetic spectrum.** UV, X-rays and gamma rays are all electromagnetic radiation with higher photon energy than visible light. Correct (site correction 5 is right).
- **Base analogues.** 5-bromouracil resembles thymine, is incorporated opposite A, and in its rare form pairs with G (A-T to G-C). Correct.
- **Base-modifying agents.** Benzo[a]pyrene from tobacco smoke, after activation to its diol epoxide, forms bulky guanine adducts (mainly G to T). "Attach to a base and change how it pairs" is correct at this level.
- **Intercalating agents (acridine dyes).** Flat acridines (proflavine, acridine orange) and ethidium slide between stacked base pairs, and acridines are the classic frameshift mutagens. The intercalation model was proposed to explain exactly this. Correct. Mustard compounds are alkylating agents, not intercalators, so site correction 4 is right. Source: https://www.docketalarm.com/cases/PTAB/IPR2018-01156/Thermo_Fisher_Scientific_Inc._v._The_Regents_of_the_University_of_California/09-10-2018-Patent_Owner/Exhibit-2002-Berman_and_Young,_Ann_Rev_Biophys_Bioeng_1981/ (Berman and Young, Annu Rev Biophys Bioeng 1981); https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC348606
- **Aflatoxin.** Made by *Aspergillus flavus* and *A. parasiticus* on poorly stored peanuts and grain. It is a mutagen through guanine adducts. Correct (finding 4 is optional wording).
- **Background radiation and granite.** Granite and shale areas usually contain more uranium and can have higher radon. The granite plate is acceptable context. The Australian natural dose averages about 1.7 mSv a year. Source: ARPANSA links in finding 1.
- **Viruses inserting into DNA.** Hepatitis B virus DNA integrates in most HBV-related liver cancers. Retroviruses integrate as part of their cycle, and HPV integrates in cervical cancer. "Some viruses insert their own DNA into ours" is correct (finding 2 fixes the consequence). The enveloped virus plate suits HBV.
- **UV yeast data and its interpretation.** 142, 98, 61, 24, 5 (mean of three plates) at 0, 15, 30, 60 and 120 s. The drops are 44, 37, 37 and 19 over intervals of 15, 15, 30 and 60 s, so the fall is steepest at first and then levels off. "After two minutes" equals 120 s. Colony counts measure survival, so the mutation-rate conclusion is not supported; mutagenesis needs a scored visible or selectable trait against the 0 s control. The design's (a) and (b) model answers and feedback 11d are correct. These are illustrative site values and are labelled honestly as a student's results.
- **Repair before copying.** A lesion becomes a fixed mutation only after mis-insertion during replication and a second round of copying. Correct.
- **Germ-line reach.** X-rays and gamma rays penetrate to the gonads. Correct.
- **Site corrections 1, 2, 3 and 6** (not used in learner copy). RERF has found no radiation-related genetic effect in survivors' children: https://www.rerf.or.jp/en/programs/research_activities_e/outline_e/progf1-en/ . IARC placed X- and gamma radiation in Group 1 in Monograph 75 (2000), not "Class 1 in 1979". The corrections are right; root should add them to `site-corrections-m6.md`.
- **Em dashes:** none in the design.
