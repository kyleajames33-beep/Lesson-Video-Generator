## Should fix

**SF1. The ladder's orientation is not fixed, and the obvious build reverses the rule.** Decisions say "Arrows pointing down a ladder (to a smaller unit) are labelled ×", which needs the biggest unit at the top. But scene 2 lists the rungs "mm, cm, m, km", and the narration says "Put them in a ladder, from small to big". A builder who draws them top to bottom in that order puts mm at the top, so the "× 10" arrows pointing down would run from mm to cm, which is wrong and would teach the opposite of the memory aid. The same ambiguity applies to the mass and capacity ladders here and to the ladders reused in a02 to a04. Fix: state in scene 2's Visual "km at the top, mm at the bottom; down = to a smaller unit = ×", list rungs top to bottom in the design (km, m, cm, mm; t, kg, g, mg; ML, kL, L, mL), and change the narration to "Put them in a ladder, with the biggest at the top." Add a hand tag "down: smaller unit, ×".

## Notes

- N1. The hook repeats m09's last lines almost word for word ("Money works in hundreds. A hundred cents make a dollar ... in tens, hundreds and thousands"). A student watching in order hears it twice within a minute. Shorten a01's opening to one sentence ("Like money, measurement works in tens, hundreds and thousands.") and go straight to the ribbon.
- N2. The muesli bar label says "salt 95 mg" on screen and in narration, while the author note says sodium. Australian nutrition panels list sodium in mg, not salt. Say and show "sodium 95 mg" so the label matches what students see on real packets.
- N3. Scene 5 (890 characters) carries four examples (mandarins, muesli bar, ute, parcels). Each covers a different skill (kg to g, mg to g, kg to t, matching before adding), so the load is purposeful, but check in the voiced preview that each example leaves the screen before the next arrives. Scene 3's "longer than a trip to Brisbane" assumes a Sydney start; add "from Sydney" or say "longer than Sydney to Brisbane, twice".
