## Student learning trace (entry knowledge: C1 to C4b)

- Main question: when every amount in a settled mixture changes, what stays the same, and how do you write it for any reaction? The hook poses it, and the answer is derived (equal rates, divide, K = 0.3 / 3) before the name K is used.
- Cause chain is followable: the ratio is fixed because the rates are equal and the two rate numbers are fixed; the coefficient becomes a power because that many particles must meet; solids and solvent drop out because a steady concentration folds into the fixed numbers. Each link appears on screen on its words (except the blank opening of the rates scene and the any-equation scene, SHOULD FIX 1 and 4).
- The check scene cannot be passed by a wrong form, which also supports feedback one's rejection of "add underneath".
- The size-of-K scene gives the three bands, applies them to the lesson's own tube, NO and the copper complex, with the rough-guide caveat visible.
- Application: two unseen systems with all conditions visible, "Pause here to answer." and a 10 s countdown. Feedback names the likely wrong answers. The one learning gap is feedback two's unexplained algebra (SHOULD FIX 2).
- Memory aid ("products over reactants, coefficients become powers, pure solids, a pure liquid and the solvent drop out") is placed after the reasoning, carries its condition ("for the equation as given, concentrations in mol/L, water as solvent or gas"), and ends the notes.
