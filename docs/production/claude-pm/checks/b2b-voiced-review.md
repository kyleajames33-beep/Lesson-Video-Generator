## Findings

### BLOCKER

### SHOULD FIX

1. **Hook, f0 to f220.** The first 7 s ("A gardener has one strawberry plant, and wants more. There are two ways the plant can make them. It can send a runner") show a blank page with only the BIOLOGY label; no title, no plant (f100). The strawberry runner picture arrives at about f220, after the word "runner" (f154). Fix: bring the strawberry plant in on "one strawberry plant" (f0 to f30) and draw the runner on "send a runner".
2. **Hook, f250.** The "parent plant" label is drawn over the leaves and is unreadable (grey on green). Fix: move the label above or beside the plant, or give it a chip background.
3. **Pollen-tube scene, f4378 to f4560 ("the two sperm cells form inside the tube as it grows, and they travel along it, so they are protected").** The sperm cells are only tiny teal dots in the style at half scale (about 6 px), and the "two sperm cells form" label sits under the grain inset, away from the tube. A student cannot see sperm travelling along the tube, the key step of the video. Fix: show two clearly drawn sperm cells (same blue drawn sperm used in the ovule inset) travelling down the tube, with the label next to the tube.
4. **Seeds-better scene, f9806 to f10022.** "Alike and close" is shown under the paddock card, but "varied and far" and the "new place" / fence icons are placed inside the card headed "New disease arrives". Seeds travelling to new places is a different condition from a disease, and the fence and dotted "new place" icons are about 20 px and unclear. Fix: add a third small card "Need to reach new places" (or move the icons out of the disease card), and enlarge the icons.
5. **Key notes, f16843 to f17604 (25 s total, about 12 s with all five bullets and the mnemonic visible).** About 105 words of 28 px text. The "pause if you need more time" line helps, but the hold is short for copying. Fix: lengthen the hold by about 15 s (to roughly 40 s) or trim bullet 1 (it joins three ideas: pollination, fertilisation, "not fertilisation").
6. **Worked outline, f10650 to f11250.** In the "Then" line the highlighted term splits as "sperm cell" plus a detached "s" ("sperm cell s"), a visible rendering glitch in a model answer students are asked to copy. Fix: highlight "sperm cells" as one chip or leave the plural unhighlighted but joined.

## Student learning trace (entry knowledge: B1, B2a, Year 11 genetics basics)

- One question carried through: why does the seed not start at pollination, how does the sperm reach the egg (tube), what the seed and fruit become. Each step has its own picture and the "pollination is not fertilisation" cue is built into the screen at f3539 to f3742, in the attempt (Q2) and again in feedback.
- Vegetative propagation and the "when is each route better" scene use the same plant (strawberry) as the hook, and tie back to B2a variation.
- Exam transfer: the model outline answer and the three "your turn" items test pollination versus fertilisation, pollen tube order, and clones versus variation. Feedback names the likely wrong answers.
- Reading load is generally low (labels, not paragraphs); the heaviest text is the key notes and the three attempt cards, which are acceptable at their hold lengths.

## Verdict

**Fix first** (small). No blockers, but SHOULD FIX 1 (blank hook opening), 3 (sperm hardly visible in the tube, the main mechanism) and 4 (seeds-better card mixes disease and travel) change what the learner sees at the key moments, and 5 and 6 are quick. Resolve them, re-check at playback speed and in listening, then ready for owner approval.
