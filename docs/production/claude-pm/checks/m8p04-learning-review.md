## Findings

### 1. should fix: 4

Text: "set the instrument there"

The spoken instruction sets about 800 nm, but the picture and practical alternatives include school colourimeters that cannot select that wavelength. A student cannot carry out the method on the illustrated instrument.

Repair: Name the instrument being used: "This spectrophotometer reaches the peak near eight hundred nanometres. With a school colourimeter, use its red filter and make a new calibration." Replace the first three wavelength sentences and shorten scene 17's repeated selection rule to keep the 568-second cap. Do not reuse the 800-nm slope for a red filter.

### 2. should fix: 3

Text: "Two, three and four millilitres of stock give the next three standards"

Only the first tube gets an explicit water volume. Students planning the method need to know that each tube is made to the same total, not merely that more stock is added.

Repair: Replace with "Use two, three, four and six millilitres of stock, adding water to ten millilitres in every tube." Keep the concentration labels on their existing cues. This is shorter.

### 3. should fix: 10 and risk table

Text: "filter it first"

The sample is real plating rinse water, acknowledged to contain acid or other metals. Filtering removes suspended particles but cannot establish that copper is the only dissolved absorber or that the matrix matches.

Repair: Keep filtering as the turbidity fix. State that the teacher's sample has been checked for dissolved interference and matched to the standards; otherwise label it an artificial copper-sulfate rinse sample. Replace the generic "Then the standards' line applies" with the condition. Avoid a new separate mechanism scene.
