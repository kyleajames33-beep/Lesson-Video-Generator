# hms-y12-m3-v11 science check (Medicare, private insurance and the NDIS)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited. Line numbers refer to `lessons/hms-y12-m3-v11.md`.

## Confirmed
- Medicare's aims and funding: general revenue plus the Medicare levy (2 per cent). Correct.
- Inside and outside the boundary (3): public hospital care, MBS rebates, PBS co-payment capped and lower for concession holders, safety nets; most adult dental, allied health beyond limited sessions, glasses, ambulance (not Medicare-covered; free for residents only in Queensland and Tasmania), private hospital care. Correct.
- Gap arithmetic (4): 90 minus 42 = 48; 12 x 48 = 576. Correct; illustrative, not stated as current.
- Rebates both ways (4; currency item): bulk-billing incentives tripled in November 2023 and extended to all Medicare-eligible patients from 1 November 2025, with a Bulk Billing Practice Incentive Program (12.5 per cent quarterly loading). "When government lifts rebates or pays more for bulk billing" is true and names nothing that will date.
- Private insurance (5): premium rebate, Medicare Levy Surcharge, Lifetime Health Cover loading, community rating (same premium for the same policy regardless of health). Correct.
- NDIS (6): permanent and significant disability, goals-based plans, supports not treatment. My Aged Care front door, then help at home (Support at Home since 1 Nov 2025) or residential care. Correct.
- 9b and 9d: layered funding (Medicare 75 per cent of the schedule fee for in-hospital private patients, insurer at least the remaining 25 per cent plus hospital costs, patient the gap). Correct.

## Findings
1. **Note (scene 6, line 72).** "Both funded nationally": the NDIS is jointly funded by the Commonwealth and the states and territories and administered nationally. Acceptable simplification; "both run nationally" would be exact.
2. **Note (scene 6).** NDIS access also requires being under 65 when applying. Omission does not teach a wrong idea; author note only.

## Verdict
Pass. 0 blockers, 0 should fix, 2 notes.
