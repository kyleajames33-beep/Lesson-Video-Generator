## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 6 (the tangent's read points sit almost on the curve, at the same times as c01's secant).** Narration: "It passes through about seven o'clock, thirty-six cups, and nine o'clock, a hundred and sixty-one cups." The curve itself passes through (7, 40) and (9, 160), which are c01's secant ends, and the grey chip "c01: average 7 to 9 = 60" sits beside the result. On a 0 to 250 cup axis 760 px tall the read points are about 12 px and 3 px from the curve, so the tangent and c01's secant will look like the same line, giving 62 and 60. c02 and this video's scene 7 teach "two points on the tangent, not on the curve"; here a student sees almost no difference and may conclude that curve points are good enough. Fix: read the tangent further out, where it visibly leaves the curve. Narration: "It passes through about half past six, five cups, and half past nine, a hundred and ninety-two cups. Keep the c01 chip; the secant is now visibly a different line.
