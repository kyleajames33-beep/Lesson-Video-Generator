## Biology B09

- **Scene 16 feedback, and scene 10 explanation:** “her own B cells weren't selected and no memory cells formed” treats the antivenom as if it also prevents any response to the venom introduced by the bite. The antivenom supplies ready-made horse antibodies and does not itself make the patient form memory cells. A bite can still trigger the patient's own antibody response, although that is no guarantee of protection from a later bite. Correct the feedback to: “The antivenom supplied horse antibodies, which fade. It did not give her dependable protection against another bite, so her friend's claim is wrong.” In scene 10, change “your own B cells weren't selected, so no memory cells form” to “receiving those antibodies does not itself make your B cells form memory cells.” [Human venom-antibody measurements after envenoming](https://repositorio.unesp.br/entities/publication/27bba537-b5fe-4726-a292-516aa4ab78bc), [Australian brown snake antivenom product record](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/artg/74897).
