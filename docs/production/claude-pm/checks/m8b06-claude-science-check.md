## Verification and recalculation

The Cystic Fibrosis Foundation supports the CFTR cell-surface chloride/hydration chain and F508del misfolding/degradation. Four normal illustrated channels versus none is a schematic, not a channel count measurement. General CFTR dysfunction includes processing, gating and conductance defects, so the missing-channel drawing must remain attached to this F508del example. Omitting ENaC and bicarbonate from this introductory airway account does not by itself make the causal chain wrong. The CFTR gene is on chromosome 7; the protein is not on a chromosome. Two disease-causing alleles support autosomal-recessive inheritance; carrier parents are a valid recall.

Silica inhalation can initiate inflammation and fibrosis; scarring reduces lung function. The national ban began 1 July 2024. Existing stone and other crystalline-silica work still matter. Iron contributes to haemoglobin, and established deficiency commonly produces microcytic, hypochromic cells and fatigue/breathlessness. UV is the main avoidable environmental cause of cutaneous melanoma; not every melanoma is UV-caused. Growth-signalling damage and loss of division control are a suitable introductory account, with invasion distinguishing malignancy.

The broad historical mortality contrast is credible: infection control and longer lives contribute to the current predominance of non-infectious causes. Do not substitute the site's unsupported 89% or 1940-to-1990 transition figure. ## Findings

### 1. blocker: 2, notes and decision rule

Text: "That's the test."

Infectious and communicable are conflated. Infection can originate in the environment without spread between people.

Repair: Use pathogen causation as the infectious criterion and qualify transmission as common, not necessary. Coordinate labels, notes and memory aid with learning finding 1.

### 2. should fix: 7

Text: "engineered stone, used for kitchen benchtops"

The unqualified present-day example can suggest normal manufacture/installation still occurs. Australia prohibited manufacture, supply, processing and installation of engineered-stone benchtops, panels and slabs from 1 July 2024; controlled legacy work can remain.

Repair: Use "engineered stone formerly used for new benchtops" and an author source note for the ban. Do not replace the settled silicosis example or imply legacy removal is risk-free.

### 3. should fix: 8

Text: "A nutritional disease comes from the diet."

Iron-deficiency anaemia is not necessarily caused by inadequate dietary intake.

Repair: Scope this example to inadequate intake or absorption and do not imply all anaemia is nutritional.

Counts: **1 blocker; 2 should fix; 0 optional**. Findings repeated between the two reviews describe the same issues and must not be added as separate defects.
