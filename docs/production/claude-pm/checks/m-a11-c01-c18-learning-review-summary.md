## Should fix, by design (most important first)

- **c09, attempt 2:** the step from an obtuse angle to a gradient (135° → m = −1) is never taught before the countdown. The video only goes from gradient to angle for a falling line, and the memory card's "Angle → gradient: tan" leads straight to the crossed slip. Add two sentences and a row to scene 6, then extend the card and key note 4.
- **c07, scenes 1 and 7 (with c06, scene 11):** h, the step that shrinks to zero in c04 and c06 and is always coloured blue, becomes the lookout's height one video later. Use x for the height in c07 and in c06's handoff chip.
- **c07, attempt 2:** the attempt tests "expand first" on x²(x − 3), but that step appears only in the feedback. Add one sentence and a row to scene 5.
- **c05, scene 4:** "Everywhere else, the slide is smooth" implies the slide has a kink and a vertical drop, but the plate has neither. A vertical segment is also not a function, so it shows the wrong picture of a vertical tangent. Replacement narration is given, plus a ∛x-shaped inset and a rounded platform join on the slide profile. This keeps root ruling 3's "No corner, no cliff".
- **c03, scene 6:** the tangent's read points (7, 36) and (9, 161) are almost on the curve, at c01's secant ends, so the tangent and the secant will look identical. Read the tangent at 6:30 and 9:30 instead (about 5 and 192 cups; 187/3 ≈ 62).
- **c06, scene 4:** the expansion of −5(t + h)² is skipped in both speech and board. This is the hardest step in the video, and A1 tests it.
- **c10, scene 2:** "each second, the area grows by π" calls an instantaneous rate a one-second change; the real growth is about 3.9 m². Say "at that moment ... is growing at".
- **c11, scene 3:** "the income grows $38 a week, each month" contradicts the $40 just computed and scene 5's 38 + 4t. Say "right now ... growing at".
- **c13, scenes 12 and 7:** "every rule for differentiating this year" and "next year you'll meet new functions" are both wrong: e02 teaches eˣ in Year 11.
- **c02, scene 3:** the phone's slow-motion film strip is never narrated. Name it in one sentence or remove it.

## Cross-video consistency

- **Instant against interval wording.** c03 teaches "at" (a tangent) against "from ... to" (a secant). c10 and c11 then slip into "each second / each month ... grows by" for an instantaneous rate. Fixing both keeps the strand's own rule.
- **Letters.** h is the step in c04 to c06; keep it that way (c06 and c07 fix). The switch from x to t and then to x as displacement in c18 is handled well ("with t in place of x").
- **"The sign of f′ only changes at its zeros"** (c14, scene 5) is true for polynomials, but c15's |x| breaks it one video later. An optional rewording is in c14.
