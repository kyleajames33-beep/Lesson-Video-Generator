## Learner trace

Main question: how did improved microscopy change understanding of cell structure and function? Entry is Stage 5 cells and light microscopy, v02 organelle names/functions, v06 magnification and measurement. A learner should distinguish enlarging an image from resolving new detail, connect an image to a changed explanation, and justify instrument choice for a new task.

The dots-to-blob prediction provides a causal case: two nearby points merge before enlargement; the enlarged image cannot restore information not recorded. The blurry-photo analogy follows that cause and supports the new photograph question. The size ladder then compares known cells with ribosomes and membranes. The electron-image reveal earns the change from simple cell picture to internal compartments, and cristae supply one structure/function link without repeating v02. The mesosome pair provides an observation/preparation counterexample that makes assessment meaningful. The assessment criterion and final two-question rule can transfer to pondweed tasks.
