Lithium’s red line rounds to 671 nm; its weaker orange line is about 610 nm. Strontium has an atomic blue line at 460.7 nm and molecular flame bands near 605/606 and in the 640 to 690 nm region. The original 1956 flame study assigns maxima at 646, 659, 667.5 and 682 nm to SrOH, so a broad-band strip is appropriate, not a set of invented sharp atomic lines. The red-colour overlap and sodium masking are correct. The cobalt-glass tag belongs as a viewing aid, not proof of identity. [Primary flame-band study](https://doi.org/10.1088/0370-1298/69/7/303).
