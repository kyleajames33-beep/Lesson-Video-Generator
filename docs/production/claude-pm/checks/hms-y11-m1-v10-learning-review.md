## Findings

1. **Should fix, 10a asks for "the skill" but the answer is two.** The demand says "Identify the skill that would most help" and 10b answers "Problem-solving, together with help-seeking for someone else." A student who answers help-seeking alone (a reasonable reading of scene 6, "let's go together") cannot tell if they were right. Change the demand to "Identify the skill or skills that would most help" and begin 10b "Problem-solving, joined with help-seeking for someone else; either earns the mark if you explain how it changes the outcome." About 3 s. Students should see that the mark is for skill plus mechanism plus condition, which the crossed mistake already says.

2. **Note, the hook's kept-or-not sentence returns only as a glowing chip.** Scene 8's grey tag "skill or condition?" carries it silently. One spoken line in scene 8 ("Look at your kept change: was the difference a skill, or a condition?") would close it (about 3 s); optional given length (about 560 s).

3. **Note, term load.** Nine terms, four of them screen-only glosses (self-esteem, critical health literacy, stigma, pre-decided response). Acceptable because each is attached to one card; check at playback that scene 4's four-box chain and scene 5's barrier wall are not both in view.
