## Findings

**F1 (should fix). Excess calcium carbonate pH.**
Quoted (card): "pH of the mixture if too much is added | about 9 | above 12"; scene 6: "the mixture stays near pH **nine**".
Problem: excess CaCO3 in water open to the air settles near pH 8.3. While CO2 from the neutralisation is still dissolved, the mixture is closer to neutral. Only a sealed, CO2-free suspension approaches 9 to 10. The author note's "about pH 8 to 10" overstates the top of the range.
Replacement: "about 8" on the card, in scene 6 ("near pH **eight**"), in the ghost line, in model answer line 3 and in the teaching checks, with the science note rewritten. The teaching point, "only mildly basic", is unchanged.

**F2 (should fix). Drill 1: ethanoic acid pKa.**
Quoted: "Ethanoic acid has a pKa of four point seven six" and the card value 4.76.
Problem: C10 teaches pKa 4.74 (from Ka 1.8 x 10^-5) and C11 uses 4.74 throughout. 4.76 is not wrong, but students would meet two values for the same acid within two videos. Same as learning review S5.
Replacement: 4.74 throughout drill 1, feedback 1, the number line and the model answer. "Within 0.1 of pH 4.8" still holds (0.06).

**F3 (minor). Model answer wording.**
Quoted: "so CaCO3 is safer to apply by hand during the clean-up" and "for a small spill in a ventilated laboratory".
Problem: "by hand" suggests touching the chemical; ventilation is not given on the card, and an evaluation should use the case.
Replacement: "safer to handle during the clean-up" and "for a 50 mL spill the CO2 released is small (about 0.6 L)".

**F4 (minor). Author note on NH4+ pKa.**
Quoted: "NH4+ pKa 9.25 (Kb of NH3 1.8 x 10^-5)".
Problem: Kb 1.8 x 10^-5 gives pKa 9.26.
Replacement: "NH4+ pKa 9.25 (Ka 5.6 x 10^-10, as in C11's attempt A1)".

**F5 (minor). Stale note on the research pack.**
Quoted: "Note for root: the pack's Evaluate row says "well-supported judgement" for this question" (Decisions), the research table row, and open decision 5.
Problem: `what-markers-reward.md` now reads "a well-informed judgement (the guideline's wording)" in the working tree. The note and open decision are stale.
Replacement: record that the pack matches the guideline and close open decision 5.

**F6 (should fix). Scene 9: two guidelines turned into a general rule.**
Quoted: "So reasons with no verdict lose marks, and a verdict without reasons earns **little**."
Problem: the pack says band differences are question-specific. Biology 2025 Q34(b) separates "describes" from "evaluates" and has no separate judgement criterion. Same as learning review S4.
Replacement: "In both, answers without a judgement stopped short of the top, and no band paid for a verdict on its **own**." Key note 4 hedged to match.

**F7 (should fix). Scene 10: "often with the verdict handed to you".**
Problem: there is no support in the pack. In Chemistry 2025 Q23, Chemistry 2024 Q31 and drill 1, the student makes the choice. Same as learning review S2.
Replacement: "whether the conclusion is given or you choose it, your job is the support".

**F8 (should fix). Scene 7: glossary meaning overstated.**
Quoted: "because evaluate means judging both ways".
Problem: the glossary paraphrase is "judge against criteria or determine value". Weighing both sides is advice, not the glossary meaning. Same as learning review S3.
Replacement: "because a fair evaluation looks both ways"; key note 3 labels this an HSCScience tip.
