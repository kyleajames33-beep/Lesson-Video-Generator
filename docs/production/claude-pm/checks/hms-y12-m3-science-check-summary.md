## Blockers in full

### 1. v06, scene 3 (line 58): injury "mostly affects the young"
Current: "Injury sits on the prevention side, because road deaths fell with laws and safer cars, and it mostly affects the young." Falls are the leading cause of injury deaths in Australia, and over 95 per cent of fall deaths (about 6,400 in 2023 to 24) are in people aged 65 and over (AIHW, *Injury in Australia*). Injury deaths are therefore concentrated in older people; injury is the leading cause of death for young people, which is a different claim.
Corrected: "Injury sits on the prevention side, because road deaths fell with laws and safer cars. It's the leading cause of death for young people, while falls hit older people hardest." Adjust the token's tag to match.

### 2. v12, scene 2 (line 53): "an approved product has been checked"
Current: "And in Australia, an approved product has been checked for safety and that it contains what it says, not that it works." The TGA states that listed (AUST L) medicines are "not 'approved' by the TGA" and are not individually assessed before sale. The sponsor self-certifies, ingredients come from a pre-approved low-risk list, manufacture must meet good manufacturing practice, and the TGA may review products after they are on sale.
Corrected: "And in Australia, a listed product, with AUST L on the label, can only use ingredients approved as low risk and must be made to quality standards, but nobody has checked that it works." Replace any "approved" stamp wording with "listed".

## Should fix (one line each; full wording in the per-video files)
- v01 scene 3 and source tag: ABS *Causes of Death, Australia, 2025* (released 27 Oct 2026) supersedes the 2024 tag; same five causes, with stroke now above lung cancer. Say "In 2025, ... dementia, coronary heart disease, stroke, lung cancer and lung disease".
- v02 scene 2: on a child deaths axis "both lines rise" reads as deaths rising, and other children improved faster, not "just as fast" (the gap did not narrow from 2008 to 2018). Say "often faster ... both groups improve, and the gap between them hasn't narrowed", and label the axis so that higher is better.
- v04 scene 3: Australia's prevention share (3.1 per cent) is close to the OECD average (3.4), so it does not belong in the weaker column. Say "as in most OECD countries".
- v04 scene 3: the out-of-pocket share is near or below the OECD average, though higher than several Western European countries. Say "higher than in several similar countries, and heaviest for dental care".
- v05 scene 8: modelling splits the fall in coronary deaths roughly evenly between risk factors and treatment (US) or gives half to three-quarters to risk factors elsewhere. Say "in large part a prevention story: about half or more of the fall".
- v06 scene 5 and key note 4: the age-standardised prevalence of diabetes has been stable since 2011, while the number living with it rises. Say "the number of people living with it keeps rising".
- v06 scene 6: "more of them in younger adults" is not supported by the AIHW release. Say "but when it starts in younger adults, they live longer with it".
- v18 scene 1: national dispensing data cover PBS-listed medicines, not fully private scripts. Say "Every subsidised prescription".
- v21 scene 2 and key note 1: Healthy Cities Illawarra still operates. It has existed since 1987 and was renamed Healthy Cities Australia in 2025, so drop the past tense "For decades it was" and add the tag "now Healthy Cities Australia". Do not use the design's fallback clause about funding ending.

- v04 child dental: the Child Dental Benefits Schedule continues (ages 0 to 17, qualifying payment, $1,158 cap over two years in 2026). The narration stays true.
- v09 Support at Home: began 1 November 2025 with means-tested contributions by service type (clinical care free). The Commonwealth Home Support Programme transitions no earlier than 1 July 2027. The narration stays true. Site correction 28 confirmed.
- v11 rebates and bulk billing: incentives were extended to all Medicare-eligible patients from 1 November 2025, with a practice incentive program. The narration stays true. Site correction 29 confirmed.
- v17 sharing by default: the Act passed February 2025 and the rules were made December 2025. The obligation for pathology and diagnostic imaging reports applied from 1 July 2026, with a transition period. The narration stays true. Correct site correction 30's "phased 2025 to 2026" to these dates.
- v21 Healthy Cities Illawarra: it is still operating, renamed. See the should-fix above.
- Also flagged. v01's "Some of those extra years": confirmed (health-adjusted life expectancy). v06's dementia burden: 59 per cent fatal in 2024, so the mid or upper position is right. v06's incidence fall of 45 per cent is confirmed (site correction 26), but the young-adult trend is not (see the should-fix). v05's "largely a prevention story" (see the should-fix) and its spending claim: confirmed.
