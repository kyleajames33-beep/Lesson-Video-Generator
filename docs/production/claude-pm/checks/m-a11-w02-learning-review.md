## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 8 (restrictions without a reason).** Narration: "That leaves x minus two, over x, with x not equal to minus two, zero or two." Scene 7 taught restrictions only from the original bottom. Here x = 0 and x = 2 come from the divisor (x² on its bottom, and the divisor itself cannot be zero), which nobody explains. A student will ask: "Where do zero and two come from? Neither is in x + 2." Fix: replace the last sentence with "That leaves x minus two, over x. The restrictions come from every bottom, and from the fraction we divide by, which can't be zero. So x can't be minus two, two or **zero**." Keep the amber chips "x ≠ −2, 0, 2" on the final cue. The student should see that dividing adds restrictions that the final answer hides.

2. **Should fix, scene 6 ("outside numbers").** Narration: "Multiply the outside numbers, two times three, which is six." "Outside numbers" is not defined and could mean the outer terms of a bracket. Fix: "Multiply the number in front of x squared by the number on its own, two times three, which is six." The product-sum box already shows "product 2 × 3 = 6".

3. **Optional, scene 6.** The tile rectangle appears only at the end, as a check. If the build allows, start `AlgebraTileGrid` on "Split seven x", so the six-and-one split is seen as two groups of strips while it is spoken.
