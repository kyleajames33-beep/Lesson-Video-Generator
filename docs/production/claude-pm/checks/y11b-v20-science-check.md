Activity rates depend on time and assay conditions. For a simple fixed-enzyme Michaelis-Menten model, v/Vmax = [S]/(Km+[S]); at [S]=9Km the rate is 90% of the limiting maximum, so the practical plateau is an approximation rather than literal continuous 100% occupancy. More active enzyme increases Vmax under otherwise matching conditions. The calculation explains the model and is not a claim that every enzyme follows it.
