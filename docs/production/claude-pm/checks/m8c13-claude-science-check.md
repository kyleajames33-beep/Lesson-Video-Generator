### 1. Should fix: attempt 2 card and feedback

The card calls the hydration process product "nearly pure ethanol", while describing fermentation before distillation. That can compare two different stages. Hydration also produces an ethanol/water stream that must be separated. The finished-process claim need not be false, but the comparison needs the same basis. In the model compare the separation burdens and energy, without saying the hydration reactor directly delivers nearly pure fuel ethanol. Cut the repeated purity claim to fund this repair.
