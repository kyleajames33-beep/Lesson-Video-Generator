# Brief 101: error-only check of Revision 2 designs

Checked 22 October 2026. Findings concern the six named designs. Site lesson corrections are in the separate log.

## chem-m7-p01

- **Attempt 2 and feedback:** The question asks students to compare the *volatility* of pentane, hexane and 2-methylpentane. The feedback calls this invalid solely because one compound is branched. Branching is a valid variable in a comparison of volatility; it only confounds a test of the effect of *chain length alone*. Correct the question to ask whether the plan can isolate the effect of chain length, or make the feedback judge safety separately without rejecting the comparison on validity grounds. Source: [NSW Chemistry Stage 6 syllabus, 2017, Hydrocarbons](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-word.docx).

## chem-m7-p02

- **Scene 4, equation, feedback and key notes:** The design treats 1,2-dibromocyclohexane as the one product of mixing cyclohexene with *bromine water*. Aqueous bromine also forms hypobromous acid and hydrobromic acid, which add across the double bond. The NSW Department of Education guide says 1,2-dibromocyclohexane is produced but is not the most common product under these conditions. Correct the learner copy to say that bromine is consumed by addition and that dibromocyclohexane is one possible product. Use bromine in an organic solvent if the single dibromo product is needed for the equation. Source: [NSW Department of Education, Chemistry Module 8 study booklet, pp. 35 to 36](https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/key-learning-areas/science/media/documents/chemistry-s6-aurora-hsc-study-day-booklet.pdf).
- **YouTube syllabus quotation:** The description joins the four printed examples after `including but not limited to:` into one comma-separated sentence. Correct text: retain the stem and list `hydrogen (H2)`, `halogens (X2)`, `hydrogen halides (HX)` and `water (H2O) (ACSCH136)` on their own lines. Source: [NSW Chemistry Stage 6 syllabus, 2017, p. 55](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/chemistry-stage6-syllabus-word.docx).

Correct text: `Yeast supplies the enzymes that catalyse fermentation. Write yeast over the arrow as a condition, not as a stoichiometric reactant.` Source: [Original study of yeast growth during fermentation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15187181/).

## bio-m7-b07

- **Attempt 1 feedback and model:** Tube 2 shows no visible clumping, but the answer concludes that antibodies `didn't bind` bacterium B and that B's antigens have different shapes. Lack of agglutination does not prove lack of binding: binding may occur without enough cross-linking to form visible clumps. Correct text: `The serum did not visibly agglutinate bacterium B, so it gives no evidence of effective cross-linking to B. B may lack the matching antigen, but this test alone cannot establish that.` Source: [Original study on false-negative agglutination](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4703582/).
