## Findings

1. **Should fix, the memory card conflicts with scene 5's progression.** Scene 4 and the card say "Raise one dial, and name the one you lowered." Scene 5 then progresses by raising time 10 per cent with nothing lowered, and later intensity alone. A student will ask which dial to lower when progressing. Scope the rule to a session: "In one session, if you raise one dial, name the one you lower to pay for it", and keep scene 5's "one variable at a time, in small steps" for weeks. The card could read "Raise one at a time; a big rise costs another dial."
