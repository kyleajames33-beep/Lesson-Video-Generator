## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 6 narration).** "free youth mental health centres": the national youth mental health centres are free or low cost, not universally free (some services charge a gap). Corrected: "and free or low-cost youth mental health centres". (Scene 3's constructed service with "no cost" can stay.)
2. **Note (11a card).** All Australian states and territories already require helmets for cyclists of all ages (from 1990, Victoria first), so "A state makes bicycle helmets compulsory for riders under eighteen" is hypothetical. Consider "In a hypothetical state, ..." so a student who knows the real law is not distracted. Answer unchanged.
