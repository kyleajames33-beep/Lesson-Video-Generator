## Strengths to keep

- The banana hook is concrete and creates a real question ("why would sharing DNA be a problem?"). Scene 5 returns to the plantation as the payoff.
- Scene 2 makes continuity two parts (DNA passed on, offspring survive) and the fading branch shows the second part.
- Scene 5 split board (stable versus change) uses video well: identical clones all affected, then varied seedlings with survivors highlighted.
- "Not a blend, not a copy" directly addresses the site's misconceptions. The chromosome and gene meanings from the fact check are included.
- Narration is conversational and natural for speech. Key notes are short, copyable and use "usually" correctly.
- Hand-off to B2 is clear and the lesson ends on a decision rule ("depends on whether conditions stay the same or change").
