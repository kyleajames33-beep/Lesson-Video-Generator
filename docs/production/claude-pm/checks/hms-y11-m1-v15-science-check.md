# hms-y11-m1-v15 science check (approaches to health promotion)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: the Revision 2 section, script, practice, key notes and the design's facts section; no build, audio or playback). Independent science and fact check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- **Smoking figure (scene 1, dated official figure).** AIHW, *Tobacco smoking: National Drug Strategy Household Survey 2022 to 2023* (https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/smoking/tobacco-smoking-ndshs; page last updated 20 August 2026): daily smoking among people aged 14 and over 24.3 per cent in 1991 and 8.3 per cent in 2022 to 2023. Confirmed 24 Nov 2026. "about a quarter" and "about one in twelve" are true. The card's "24%" is a fair rounding; "24.3%" is the exact figure. Keep the release re-check.
- WHO health promotion definition (scene 2): Ottawa Charter 1986, "the process of enabling people to increase control over, and to improve, their health". Correct.
- Three approaches and the two shifts (scenes 3 to 5): standard account; infectious disease dominant early in the twentieth century and cardiovascular disease and cancer leading by the 1970s (AIHW historical mortality). Correct.
- Smoking across three bands (scene 6): excise making Australian cigarettes among the most expensive in the world, advertising bans, smoke-free laws, plain packaging from 1 December 2012. Correct (*Tobacco in Australia*).
- Salutogenic model (scene 7): Antonovsky, sense of coherence (comprehensibility, manageability, meaningfulness). Correct. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander approaches: holistic definition (National Aboriginal Health Strategy 1989), stated generally. Compliant.
- Global policies (scene 8, key note 4): Alma-Ata 1978 (primary health care, Health for All, intersectoral action); Ottawa 1986; Jakarta 1997 (partnerships including the private sector); Bangkok 2005 (globalisation). UNESCO and WHO jointly published the *Global Standards for Health Promoting Schools* (2021). Correct.
- Prevention a small share of spending (scene 9): AIHW health expenditure reports (under 2 per cent before the pandemic). Not spoken as a figure. Correct.
- Practice 11a/11b and 11c/11d: correct; no reasonable alternative crossed.

## Findings
1. **Note (scene 1).** "Surveys show most smokers already knew": high awareness of smoking harms among Australian smokers is documented (*Tobacco in Australia*, chapters 1 and 3), though awareness of specific harms varied. Acceptable.
