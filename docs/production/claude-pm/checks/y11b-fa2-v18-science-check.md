The [2014 surface study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24694282/) supports about 32 m² total gut mucosa, about 2 m² of it large intestine. A smooth 4 m by 0.025 m tube has area 0.314 m², so 30 m² is about 95.5 times larger. [Transport modelling](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/ajpcell.00068.2013) distinguishes SGLT1 cotransport from the basolateral ATP-dependent pump.

1. **Blocker:** Feedback 10b says digestion is intact in untreated coeliac disease. The source facts acknowledge loss of brush-border enzymes. Replace the absolute statement with "Even when nutrients have been digested, damaged villi absorb less"; retain surface area as the central reason.

2. **Should fix:** Scenes 4-5 present a universal switch from downhill glucose entry to directly ATP-powered glucose carriers. Main apical uptake is sodium-glucose cotransport, indirectly supported by the sodium gradient. Keep energy-dependent uptake without claiming every carrier directly uses ATP or that the route switches universally.

3. **Should fix:** Scene 3 says only one cell is crossed to reach blood. The gut epithelium is one cell layer, but capillary endothelium is another barrier. Say "one layer of gut lining cells, close to capillaries" instead.
