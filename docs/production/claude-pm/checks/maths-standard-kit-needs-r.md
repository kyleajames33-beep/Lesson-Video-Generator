### New parts in one list

1. **RatioBar** (r01, r02, r09): tiles, HCF regrouping, equivalents, share and part-given modes, unit-rate mode.
2. **ScalePlan** (r04 to r08): a measuring ruler on a plate or plan with a scale chip and real-length readout; fit-the-box mode (r05) and offset-survey mode (r07).
3. **TripGraph** (r12, r13): TripSim's state drawn live as a distance-time graph.
4. **PlanSymbols** and **WallStrip** (r06).
5. **MarkRecapture** (r03).
6. Small options: **ShelfLabel** (r11), **RateFraction** (r10), **RainSlab** (r08), **EnergyMeter** (r14), a fuel gauge on TripSim (r13).

No new simulation engine is required. `TripSim` already holds a trip state; the trapezium strip rule already exists.
