## Should fix

**T03-SUNRISE, scene 6, "Brisbane ... is a little further east than Sydney, so the Sun rises there a little earlier."** This is not true all year. Sunrise also depends on latitude and season: around the December solstice Sydney's sunrise (about 4:41 am in eastern standard time) comes before Brisbane's (about 4:47 am), because Sydney's summer days are longer. The visual then shows a sunrise glow reaching Brisbane first. What longitude does fix is solar noon. Fix: "so the Sun reaches its highest point there a little earlier" and relabel the glow as a noon line, which also matches t03 scene 12 and t04's "noon reaches Sydney first". The student should link longitude to noon, not to sunrise.

**T03-PERTH, scene 4, "then across to just past one hundred and ten east, and you land on Perth".** Perth is at about 116°E, more than halfway from 110° to 120°. A student following "just past 110" on the grid would land well west of Perth, on the coast north of it or in the ocean, and decide the method does not work. Fix: "then across to a bit more than halfway between one hundred and ten and one hundred and twenty east". Keep the readout "116°E". The student should learn to estimate between grid lines in proportion, which the Darwin reading then repeats.
