## Learner trace and boundaries

From C08, P02, P04, P03 and S07, the learner can choose blank/known/unknown, hold method conditions constant, record observation before inference, and evaluate a contaminated blank. The unknown D comparison makes a negative result concrete. Exact alkene selectivity and safe gas setup need repair.

P02/P04 quantities can remain on recalled method cards. S07 terminology is applied to this method. The new learning is control quality and defensible inference, not another mechanism explanation.
