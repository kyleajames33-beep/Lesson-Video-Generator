## Findings

1. **Should fix, scene 4 (a Standard 1 student cannot draw the money line).** "Draw both equations as lines" assumes a method. a01 taught lines from tables for equations that start "y =", and 22a + 12c = 5900 is not in that form; its points at round numbers are not obvious. The narration then skips to "Type both into a graphing app, or read a graph you're given". A1's picture (empty axes "for Standard 1 students who will graph") needs the same skill for 28w + 35s = 903 (intercepts 32.25 and 25.8). Fix, after "with adult tickets across and child tickets up": "To draw a line, two points are enough. If every ticket sold were an adult's, that's three hundred and fifty adults, and if every one were a child's, three hundred and fifty children. That's the count line. For the money line, five thousand nine hundred dollars would buy about two hundred and sixty-eight adult tickets on their own, or about four hundred and ninety-two child tickets." Visual: ring each intercept as spoken, with chips "all adults" and "all children". Time: about 18 s against a 19 s margin; take the named reserve trims in scenes 1 and 2. Students on either course should be able to draw both lines from the words.

2. **Should fix, scene 7 (the units trap cannot be fallen into).** The narration says "takes four and a half hours" and the coded total already reads "4.5 h, 400 km", then warns "Four and a half hours is four point five, not four point three." No student writes 4.3 from "four and a half". The trap only exists when the time is given in hours and minutes. Fix: state the trip as "four hours and thirty minutes" in narration ("A drive to the coast takes four hours and thirty minutes"), and on the coded total "4 h 30 min, 400 km". Then: "Watch the units. Thirty minutes is half an hour, so that's four point five hours, not four point three." The crossed slip "m + r = 4.3" then corrects a real mistake.

3. **Optional, feedback 1 (no route shown to s = 9).** "Solving, by graph or by algebra, s equals nine" shows only the crossing. Add a grey unspoken row, as scene 7 does: "Standard 2: 28(30 − s) + 35s = 903, 840 + 7s = 903, s = 9".

4. **Optional, feedback 2 (check chip).** "960 + 480 = 1440 ✓ (screen only)" is unclear; drop "(screen only)".
