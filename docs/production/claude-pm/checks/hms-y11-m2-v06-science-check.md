# hms-y11-m2-v06 science check (digestive and endocrine systems)

Revision 2 design checked 24 November 2026 (source-only: script, on-screen text, practice, key notes, memory aids and facts section; no build or playback). Independent check; the design was not edited.

## Confirmed
- Tract (2): mechanical and chemical digestion, peristalsis, stomach churning and slow release, most digestion and almost all nutrient absorption in the small intestine across villi, water reabsorption in the large intestine, bile from the liver stored and released by the gallbladder, pancreatic enzymes. Correct.
- Macronutrients (3): fat about 37 kJ/g, carbohydrate about 17 kJ/g; carbohydrate the main fuel at moderate to high intensity, fat at rest and low intensity, protein a small share; fat oxidation needs oxygen; glycogen depletion despite large fat stores. Correct.
- Micronutrients (4): iron, calcium (bone and contraction), vitamin D (calcium absorption), B vitamins (energy release), sodium and potassium lost in sweat. Correct.
- Endocrine (5): hormones act on cells with receptors; nerve against hormone speeds and durations; growth hormone from the pituitary; adrenaline and cortisol from the adrenals; insulin and glucagon from the pancreas; thyroxine sets metabolic rate. Correct.
- Glucose control and negative feedback (6). Correct.
- Session hormones (7): adrenaline raises heart rate and releases glucose; glucagon rises and holds glucose in prolonged exercise; cortisol mobilises fat; ADH conserves water; aldosterone conserves sodium; insulin restores glycogen; growth hormone pulses mainly in slow-wave sleep. Correct.
- Low energy availability effects (9): menstrual, bone and immune effects, stress fractures. Correct (IOC consensus on REDs).
- 11a/11b and 11c/11d: correct. 11d's "the hormones replace the lost water" is a real error.
- Memory aids sound. No people; plates named correctly.

## Findings
1. **Should fix (scene 7 narration).** "So a meal still in your stomach stops being digested, and that's why you feel sick." Exercise and sympathetic activity slow gastric emptying and digestion; they do not stop them. The on-screen tag already says "digestion: slowed". Corrected: "So a meal still in your stomach is digested much more slowly, and that's why you feel sick."
2. **Should fix (scene 8 narration).** "Then the gut gets too little blood for too long, causing nausea and cramps." Chronic psychological stress disturbs the gut mainly through nerve and hormone effects on its movement, secretions and sensitivity, not through sustained low blood flow. Corrected: "Then digestion stays disturbed for too long, with the gut's movement and blood flow upset, causing nausea, cramps and changes in appetite." On screen: "digestion disturbed for weeks".
3. **Note (scene 6).** "It digests carbohydrate into glucose" compresses pancreatic amylase (starch to maltose) and gut-wall enzymes (to glucose). Optional: "Its enzymes help digest carbohydrate into glucose."
4. **Note (11d).** Aldosterone release is triggered mainly by falling blood volume through renin and angiotensin, as well as by sodium levels. An answer naming falling blood volume should earn the mark.

## Verdict
Pass with fixes. 0 blockers, 2 should fix, 2 notes.
