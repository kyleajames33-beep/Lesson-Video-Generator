## Findings

1. **Should fix, scenes 2 and 4 re-teach Year 11 nutrition and the sweat mechanism.** Year 11 taught glycogen as the small store in muscle and liver, fat as huge but slow, hitting the wall (hms-y11-m2-v06 and v10), protein for repair with "The training is the signal to grow, and protein is the material" (v11), and the sweat chain (plasma down, stroke volume down, heart rate up, core temperature up; v05, recalled in v11). Scene 2 recalls the tanks in one sentence, then restates all of it, including a near copy of Year 11's protein line ("The training supplies the stimulus, and protein supplies the material"). Scene 4 teaches the chain box by box as new, against root's ruling 6. A returning student hears about a minute of repeat. Fix: scene 2, "In Year 11 you saw that glycogen is a small, fast store, fat a huge, slow one, and that training, not extra protein, is what builds muscle. So nearly every nutrition decision is about timing carbohydrate", with the tanks, wall and protein chips as grey "earlier" recall. Scene 4, "In Year 11 you saw sweat shrink the plasma, so stroke volume falls and heart rate drifts up", with the first four chain boxes pre-filled grey, then narrate only what is new: skill and decisions worsening, and hyponatraemia. Spend the time on scene 3's windows (the dotpoint's core) or a settle in scene 5. Students who did Year 11 should build; students who did not still get each idea in one plain sentence.

2. **Should fix, narration names internal video numbers, and one points to the wrong video.** Scene 2 says "the adaptations from v-eight" and scene 4 "as in v-eight"; the board shows "repair, adaptation (v08)" and a grey tag "v08". Students never see file ids, and the dehydration chain was not taught in v08 (it is Year 11's). Say "the adaptations you saw earlier" and "as you saw in Year 11", and tag "earlier".

3. **Note, 8b is about 30 s.** Trim "matched to a measured sweat rate" to "matched to their sweat rate" (about 1 s); the line on screen keeps "measured".

4. **Note, the hook asks for three differences and a judgement with a one-second hold.** Add 2 s with the slots lit.
