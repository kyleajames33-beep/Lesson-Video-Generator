20 November 2026. Compared the 26 current money-strand designs against 238 other local Advanced and Standard designs. The search terms and matching anchor lines are in `work/brief252/anchor-comparison.json`. Read both money-strand decisions files and the relevant root rulings before judging the overlaps.

- i01 through i05 use a gap-year fund, birthday money, a phone savings goal, grocery inflation, a salon equipment fund, and a coffee-cart account comparison. The Standard r11 grocery unit-pricing example asks a different question. The coffee-cart art also appears in Advanced examples, where its role is different.
- i06a through i08 move from a share yield to a share graph, acquisition costs, then four investment choices. The gym and phone-network motifs are depicted with reused plates. The invented company, fixed dividend and brokerage story define the teaching use. The reuse between i05 and i08 belongs to the two-route handoff.
- i09 through i11 compare fixed and percentage depreciation with a tool kit, a ute and a delivery van. Advanced e06 has exponential depreciation as its phone comparison. Advanced g03 and Standard r12a use a delivery van in motion contexts. Flag the shared depreciation method and vehicle subject as near overlaps with distinct teaching uses.
- i12 and i13 deliberately share the e-bike, as accepted under ruling 39. Advanced e-bike appearances teach movement or model behaviour. The Advanced f08 camper-loan example and f09 used-car loan come close to i14 and i15. i14 keeps a camper and teaches payment changes. i15 switches to a small SUV and reads a repayments-per-$1,000 table. Ruling 39 accepts the i15 table. Flag i14's repeat camper-finance setting for root.
- i16 through i18 give the card, television purchase, card statement and car repair different uses. The i17a to i17b carry-over is a continuous card story. i18's right-hand cordial jug leads to r01 for Standard 1. Standard 2 continues into annuities.
- i19 through i22 deliberately carry the pay-envelope artwork under ruling 39. i21 and Advanced f12 both anchor on saving for a first home. i21 solves for the deposit amount per period and checks it against a target. f12 uses its calendar to establish the structure of an annuity.
