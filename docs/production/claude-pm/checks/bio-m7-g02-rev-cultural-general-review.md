## What Q3 is meant to practise

Evaluate environmental management with case data: the link (chlorine kills *Vibrio cholerae* in drinking water; sewage repairs stop faeces reaching water), the proof (a staggered start in two districts rules out a shared cause), who pays and when (walking, daily refilling, District B's wait; holds only while tanks are kept up), and a verdict with its condition. The trap: judging from District A's single drop.

## Strengths to keep

- **The best possible Q3 for this video.** It practises the half of B7-IQ4-04 no question practised before, and it makes students use b14's "could something else explain it?" with real-looking data. A staggered start is the natural next step after b14's randomised rings.
- **The crossed mistake is a real student error.** Students routinely cite one fall after one intervention. "It's District B, falling later, after its own tanks, that makes the proof strong" is the right one-line correction. (Author point 2: keep it.)
- **Who pays is concrete and blame-free.** Walking up to 1 km, daily refilling and chlorine, and District B's extra cases while it waited. (Author point 4: it does not read as the team's fault.)
- **The verdict carries its condition**, and the `PenArrow` loop to "when?" makes the routine visible.
- **No people**: bucket and refill outlines, a tank plate, a bacterium plate, a coded graph.
