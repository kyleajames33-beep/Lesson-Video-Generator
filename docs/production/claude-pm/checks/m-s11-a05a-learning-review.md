## Should fix

**SF1. The hook says mulch is "measured in square metres".** Mulch is bought by volume (cubic metres, or litres in bags), and a11's A2 computes "the volume of mulch in cubic metres". A student who meets a11, or a garden centre, will see the contradiction. The point the hook needs is that the bed's area is the first step. Fix: "Mulch has to cover the garden bed, so first you need the area it covers, in square metres." The chip can read "mulch: covers an area (m²)". Alternatively switch the bed's need to "weed mat", which is sold by area; scene 6 already uses turf.
