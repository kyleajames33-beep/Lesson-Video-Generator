## Findings

1. **Should fix, 8c's model constraint is netball's existing rule.** 8d's constraint is "a three-second limit on holding the ball". Netball's rules already limit possession to three seconds, so a student who plays netball will say the squad is already under that limit and the "constraint" changes nothing. The design's own facts list flags this. Fix: "a two-second limit on holding the ball, in a smaller space" in 8d and its on-screen line ("1 constraint: 2 s hold limit, reduced space"), or a scoring constraint such as "a goal only counts if the ball moved in under two seconds per pass". Students should see a constraint that is stricter than the game, so it forces the earlier decision.

2. **Should fix, narration names an internal video number.** Scene 5 says "That's why v-nine's goal-specific aim matters". Students never see file ids; the brief says "earlier". Say "That's why the goal-specific aim from last time matters."

3. **Note, 8b is about 30 s.** Trim "and lighter, three days out" to "and lighter" (about 1 s); the line on screen keeps "too hard 3 days out".

4. **Note, 8a's stem is a five-row plan with an aim, read during a 10 s countdown.** The card ends "Pause here to answer", which covers it, and the marking accepts any three of six problems, so a student who reads half the plan can still score. Keep it; the plan is a realistic exam stimulus.
