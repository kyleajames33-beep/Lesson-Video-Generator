# Draft for owner review: chem-m5-c04b

## 1. Explain (3 marks)

**Question:** Explain how a catalyst changes the time taken to reach equilibrium without changing the equilibrium composition.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for lower activation energy pathway; 1 for faster forward and reverse reactions; 1 for same equilibrium composition at fixed temperature.

**Sample answer:** A catalyst provides a lower activation energy pathway. It speeds both directions, so the rates become equal sooner, but the equilibrium composition stays the same at the same temperature.

## 2. Apply (4 marks)

**Question:** Two identical sealed vessels start with the same reacting gases. One contains a catalyst. After 5 minutes the catalysed vessel contains more product, but after 2 hours both contain the same amount. Explain both observations.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for faster early reaction; 1 for earlier approach to equilibrium; 1 for catalyst affecting both directions; 1 for unchanged final composition.

**Sample answer:** The catalyst makes product form faster initially, so that vessel approaches equilibrium sooner. It also speeds the reverse reaction. Both vessels eventually reach the same equilibrium composition because the catalyst does not change the position of equilibrium.

## 3. Analyse (5 marks)

**Question:** At a fixed temperature, a reaction without catalyst reaches 40% product after 80 minutes. With catalyst it reaches 40% product after 12 minutes. A student claims the catalyst has increased K. Analyse the claim and state two further measurements that would test it.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for faster rate; 1 for 40% possibly equilibrium in both; 1 for K unchanged by catalyst; 1 for measuring final concentrations in both vessels; 1 for measuring at the same temperature after each mixture has settled.

**Sample answer:** The time difference shows a rate effect, not a larger K. A catalyst speeds both directions and leaves K unchanged at fixed temperature. Measure all relevant concentrations after both vessels have settled, at the same temperature, and calculate K from each final mixture.
