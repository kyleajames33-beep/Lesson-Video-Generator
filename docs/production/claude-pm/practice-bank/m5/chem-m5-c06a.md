## 1. Explain (3 marks)

**Question:** A student places the measured final concentration of a reactant in the I row of an ICE table. Explain why this placement is incorrect and how the C and E rows should be used.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for I holding starting concentrations; 1 for C holding stoichiometric changes; 1 for measured final value belonging in E.

**Sample answer:** I records the starting concentration, not a final measurement. C records the changes linked by the balanced equation. The measured concentration after the mixture settles belongs in E and can be used to find the change.

## 2. Calculate (4 marks)

**Question:** For A(aq) ⇌ B(aq), a vessel begins with [A] = 0.80 mol L⁻¹ and [B] = 0.10 mol L⁻¹. At equilibrium [B] = 0.35 mol L⁻¹. Calculate [A] at equilibrium and Kc.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for B increasing by 0.25; 1 for A decreasing by 0.25; 1 for [A] = 0.55; 1 for Kc = 0.636 or 0.64.

**Sample answer:** B increases by 0.25 mol L⁻¹, so A decreases by 0.25 mol L⁻¹. [A]e = 0.55 mol L⁻¹ and Kc = 0.35/0.55 = 0.636.

## 3. Analyse (5 marks)

**Question:** For 2A(g) ⇌ B(g), a sealed vessel starts with [A] = 1.20 mol L⁻¹ and [B] = 0.10 mol L⁻¹. At equilibrium [A] = 0.80 mol L⁻¹. Use an ICE table to calculate [B] at equilibrium and Kc.

**Marking criteria:** 1 mark for A change −0.40; 1 for B change +0.20 from 2:1 ratio; 1 for [B] = 0.30; 1 for Kc = [B]/[A]²; 1 for Kc = 0.469.

**Sample answer:** A falls by 0.40 mol L⁻¹, so B rises by 0.20 mol L⁻¹. Thus [B]e = 0.30 mol L⁻¹. Kc = 0.30/(0.80)² = 0.46875, or 0.469.
