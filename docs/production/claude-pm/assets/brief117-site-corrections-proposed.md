## Method and source notes, not clear factual-error additions

- L04 origin: an ideal analyte-only Beer-Lambert line is through zero after suitable blank correction. Real calibration intercepts need checking. The page's conditional "If Beer-Lambert holds" is not by itself false; qualify the worked instruction rather than logging all ideal-origin wording as wrong.
- L04 Flint: the 397 micrograms/L residence reading is documented, but the exact analytical method and "triggered federal action" attribution were not established. Virginia Tech/EPA's later study used ICP-MS. Do not claim that every Flint sample was ICP-MS or that AAS was essential for this particular result. [Published study](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/items/15a3581f-884a-4dc5-8b86-90f2794a3790).
- L03 safety: add a lead hazard wherever lead results are discussed, but follow the settled rule: student unknowns contain no lead. Do not copy the design's teacher-dispensed student-lead recommendation into the site log. Product SDS review remains incomplete.
- L05: the capillary's bore and shoot determine mm/min, so "typical" speeds cannot be universal. A practical setup and equal-area repeated stomatal counts are coverage improvements. Wind thins the humid boundary layer; it cannot guarantee a maximum gradient regardless of humidity and temperature.
- L06 89% and the 1940-to-1990 transition period: a reproducible ABS/AIHW calculation was not found. Remove or source these exact denominators and years; they are unsupported here, not independently proved numerically false.
- L06 WHO figures are historical, not current. The WHO fact sheet updated 25 September 2025 gives at least 43 million NCD deaths in 2021, about 75% of non-pandemic deaths. Preserve that denominator; do not substitute 75% of all deaths. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/noncommunicable-diseases).
- L06 BRAF is a growth-signalling gene and CDKN2A a division-control tumour suppressor. Their inclusion under genes controlling cell division is not a clear error. Do not assert that every BRAF mutation is a direct UV-signature mutation.
- L07 3500 people is an older rounded count. The ACFDR 2023 report gives 3738 registry participants in 2022, 3798 in 2023. These are registry counts, not automatically complete prevalence. Current birth incidence and the 1960 survival estimate remain unverified.
- L08 BRAF "about 60%": not a confirmed error. The NCBI medical-genetics chapter reports approximately 50 to 60%; estimates depend on subtype. [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK447416/).
- L08 latency 20-to-40 versus 20-to-50 years is a consistency repair, not proof one typical range is false. Cancer Australia supports 20 to 50; ASSEA also uses 20 to 60. Use "often decades" or a sourced typical range, not a hard limit.
- L08 smoking/asbestos multiplier: reject a universal 50-to-90-fold rule; retain a sourced historical cohort if explicitly identified. NCI supports a more-than-additive interaction for lung cancer, not mesothelioma. [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/causes-prevention/risk/substances/asbestos/asbestos-fact-sheet).
- L08 IARC-2020/22-cancer attribution remains unverified. Do not turn an unlocated attribution into a false numerical certainty. Commercial solaria were banned nationwide by 2016, not uniformly since 2015; private beds can still exist. [ARPANSA](https://www.arpansa.gov.au/understanding-radiation/radiation-sources/more-radiation-sources/solaria).
- L09 500000 undiagnosed, current re-emergent iodine deficiency, and "the leading cause" of each diabetes complication need their own dated sources. No unsupported replacement figures are proposed. The 2009 bread rule addresses pre-existing iodine deficiency; organic bread and home bread mixes are exceptions. Vitamin-D deficiency causes osteomalacia in adults and can contribute to osteoporosis, which is not synonymous with osteomalacia.
- L07 Winkler: keep MnO(OH)2 as a method note for c06, not a new G02 teaching segment. The hydrated operational precipitate is not necessarily one pure compound; MnO2 shorthand does not change the oxygen/thiosulfate stoichiometry. [NOAA](https://www.nodc.noaa.gov/archive/arc0001/9900162/2.2/data/0-data/jgofscd/Files/protocols/chap6.html).
