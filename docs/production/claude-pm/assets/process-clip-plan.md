# Process clip plan (Module 5, 15 Oct)

Owner rule (15 Oct): AI video is used only for occasional process clips. A clip is 5 seconds, Kling 3.0 image-to-video (`kling-v3`, `std`, no sound, single shot), and starts exactly on one painted plate and ends exactly on a matching plate. Use one only where watching the change is the explanation, roughly one per two or three videos. Not for background loops, molecules or particles, dot counts, graphs, labels, chromosome detail, characters, or lab colours that are not well known and checkable.

Method: each transparent plate is composited centred on a pure white 1920 x 1080 canvas (same scale and position for both frames), so the clip can be multiply-blended onto the paper colour `#f6f1e7` in Remotion and cut cleanly into and out of the coded scene on the same plate. Clips live in `public/assets/clips/`. Generations are logged in `process-clips-provenance.json` next to this file. Plates still to paint are in Codex brief 35 (`../codex-prompts/35-process-clip-plate-pairs.md`).

Clips are not approved for any video until Claude watches them at playback speed inside the scene. Frame checks (0.3 s, 2.5 s, 4.9 s and a 13-frame strip) catch background and drift problems only.

## Considered and rejected

- C6b, G03, G03b: calculation lessons. Nothing changes that is not a number.
- G02: scene 6a reuses the C9 clip. Scene 6d (open crucible against sealed vessel) is not visible as a painted change because CaCO3 and CaO are both white powders; keep it coded with CO2 dots. Scene 6b (HI squeeze) depends on a compression, which the coded syringe already shows exactly.
- C6a: the colourless to brown N2O4 vessel pair exists, but the lesson uses it as a static attempt vignette; a colour fade adds nothing to an ICE calculation.
- B5b and the rest of B5a: the teaching is chromosome separation, which is excluded and must stay coded so counts stay exact.
- B6a scenes 7 and 8 (winding on histones, condensing for division): molecular and chromosome detail, excluded.
- B3b: the processes (insemination, pollination by hand, cloning) are procedures; an AI clip of a procedure is not the explanation, and the potato blight pair shows wilting, not why a clone row is vulnerable.
- B3a2 term cervix and milk gland pairs: the painted differences are subtle at video size and the teaching is hormonal cause, which the code carries.
- C12 (food detoxification): cultural content that must come from reviewed direct sources; no generated depiction.
- P01, P02, P03: practical videos where real colours and apparatus matter; use real photographs or coded diagrams.
- Biology B06b onward (transcription, translation, inheritance): molecular or symbolic; no macroscopic process to watch.

## Use in Remotion (for the builder, not for this agent)

Place the clip in the same frame position as the plate it starts on, `mixBlendMode: "multiply"` over the paper, hold the start plate before it and the end plate after it so the cut is invisible. The darkening in the C9 clip is weighted to the last 1.5 s; use `playbackRate` or a held first frame to match the narration cue. For "in ice it fades", play the same clip reversed.
