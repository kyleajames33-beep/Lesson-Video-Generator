# Independent sanity check of Brief 121 review findings

30 October 2026. Read all twelve root-authored review files for C12, G03c, C13, B16, B17 and G02b. Checked the disputed findings against their design passages, actual NIST data, the official 2024 data sheet and the primary trial. No root file was edited. This is a review of findings, not a complete second science pass.

1. **C12 science finding 3: reduce the claim.** The spoken acid OH range 2500 to 3000 cm^-1 exactly matches the official NESA data sheet. The design explicitly distinguishes that window from a trace extending to 3300. This is at most an optional consistency note, not a mandatory correction or reason to re-record. The official table was visually inspected in `brief120-qa/exam-page-42.png`.

2. **B17 rounding: optional.** About 15,000 is a defensible rough spoken approximation to 15,623. About 16,000 is nearest-thousand rounding. The useful required action is to date the registry number correctly, not to describe the rough approximation as a science error. This check did not independently reproduce the full ANZDATA table.

3. **G02b trial denominator: distinguish illustration from the paper.** Russell 2017 randomised 21 intervention and 10 control participants. One from each arm withdrew, leaving 20 and 9 in the modified intention-to-treat analysis. The 13/20 = 65% result is correct. The lesson's illustrative 20/10 card can remain explicitly illustrative, but it is not the paper's actual analysed group size. [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5726391/), [trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28712537/).

4. **C13 hydration: a teaching-clarity issue.** A whole industrial process can deliver purified ethanol, so the card's process-level statement is not disproved merely by finding water in the reactor stream. However, the comparison makes separation explicit for fermentation and implicit for hydration. Clarify that both need separation, without claiming purified hydration product is impossible. The existing Sydney citation does not discuss that separation. The [original EPO process patent](https://data.epo.org/publication-server/rest/v1.2/publication-dates/19831012/patents/EP0042185NWB1/document.pdf) explicitly describes aqueous crude hydration product followed by fractional distillation to an ethanol-water azeotrope. The negative iron(III) test versus general purity finding remains valid.

5. **G03c: first-order versus second-order.** The learning review's phrase "an exact second-order line count" should be "an exact n+1 line count". Coupling to different CH2 and CH3 sets can produce a complex first-order multiplet; that is not automatically second-order behaviour. The cited Purdue page describes 3-methylbutan-2-one, while this finding is about pentan-2-one, so its source should be changed to the relevant page or a suitable account of unequal couplings.

6. **C13 intermediate arithmetic.** With the rounded masses printed in the review, 180.16 / (138.12 + 102.09) x 100 = 75.001040756%, not 75.0002%. The learner-facing 75.0% is correct. Only the review's intermediate value needs correction.

7. **C12 actual 2-chloropropane mass data obtained.** NIST spectrum 229273, CAS 75-29-6, source AIST/NIMC MS-NW-4938. The saved JCAMP is `brief120-nist/2-chloropropane-extra-for121.jdx`. Peaks at or above 10% are m/z 27:29.29%, 41:21.79%, 43:100%, 63:16.99%. The named isotope peaks are 78:8.99% and 80:3.09%, ratio 2.91, consistent with approximately 3:1. Further named weak peaks are 39:8.49%, 42:6.59%, 65:5.69%. This resolves that compound's mass-height lookup, not its IR or NMR. [Actual NIST data](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?JCAMP=C75296&Index=0&Type=Mass).

8. **Butanone correction checked directly.** NIST 50206 gives 43:100%, 57:8%, 72:25%, 29:17%. The saved raw data and JSON agree. My earlier Brief 120 draft wrongly transcribed 57 as 3%; that draft error was corrected before the Brief 120 media checkpoint. Do not restore it from a message or old draft.

Other checked arithmetic: G02b daily use 28/30 = 93.33%; quiet-score gain 70 minus 15 = 55 percentage points; noise-score gain 35 minus 5 = 30 percentage points. These do not prove identical gains for every patient. The reviewed molecular masses, carbon-environment counts and simple isopropyl/ethyl deductions have no additional contradiction identified by this check.
