# Brief 120 further primary verification

30 October 2026. These confirmations supplement the science files and do not resolve their recorded factual findings.

- **SunSmart:** its own [program history](https://www.sunsmart.com.au/about-sunsmart) dates establishment to 1988. Its [1980s campaign archive](https://www.sunsmart.com.au/about-sunsmart/media-and-communications/sunsmart-campaigns/1980s) dates the original Slip Slop Slap advertisement to 1981. The dates in B13 are supported. The original Melbourne and Queensland studies are already linked in its science review.
- **Mackenzie's Mission:** the [2024 primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39565987/) reports 10,038 couples enrolled, 9,107 screened and 175 newly identified higher-chance couples (1.9%). Screening covered at least 1,281 genes and more than 750 conditions. This is not the three-condition Medicare panel. The separate [Archibald original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29261177/) reports 610 carriers among 12,000 individuals (5.08%, about 1 in 20); approximately 88% had no family history. This resolves the proposed site's carrier-frequency attribution.
- **PGT:** [HFEA, reviewed July 2026](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/treatments/embryo-testing-and-treatments-for-disease/pre-implantation-genetic-testing-for-monogenic-disorders-pgt-m-and-pre-implantation-genetic-testing-for-chromosomal-structural-rearrangements-pgt-sr/) confirms IVF, embryo testing and transfer of unaffected embryos. [NHS Genomics Education](https://www.genomicseducation.hee.nhs.uk/genotes/knowledge-hub/visual-communication-aid-preimplantation-genetic-testing/) confirms that the family's causative genetic change must be known for PGT-M. The [2026 Council of Europe technical guide](https://www.edqm.eu/documents/52006/52042/PUBL-77_TechnicalGuide_Genetic_testing_A5_2026-EN-Final%2B%281%29.pdf/ba3f7e48-d56d-74f0-5b5e-d54551597a90) describes trophectoderm biopsy and later placental development. No DNA is edited by this selection procedure.
- **Australian law:** the Federal Register identifies compilation C2022C00295 as the latest version. [Section 15](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01081/2022-10-01/2022-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) prohibits intended heritable genome alterations, with the explicit mitochondrial-donation licensing exception. [NHMRC](https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/about-us/publications/embryo-research-licensing-key-terms) distinguishes potentially legal research from prohibited reproductive use of edited embryos. The learner case of reproductive germline gene editing is prohibited. Keep the mitochondrial-law exception in author notes rather than adding an unrelated learner digression.
- **Huntington's disease:** the [June 2026 NIH account](https://newsinhealth.nih.gov/2026/06/understanding-huntingtons) states that no therapy prevents, slows or stops the disease at that time. The [current NHS page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/huntingtons-disease/) supports no cure or treatment stopping worsening. Symptom management and experimental trials do not justify a current prevention claim. B14's no-preventive-treatment contrast remains supported.
- **Gene-therapy dates:** FDA approved Casgevy for sickle-cell disease on [8 December 2023](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-gene-therapies-treat-patients-sickle-cell-disease), and for transfusion-dependent beta-thalassaemia on [16 January 2024](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-roundup-january-16-2024). B14's author note must separate those dates rather than applying December 2023 to both. This date error is not in its narration. An Australian Casgevy approval was not established by this search; no absence-of-approval conclusion is inferred from that.
- **Cost:** the Australian Government's [2022 budget document](https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2022/04/budget-2022-23-stakeholder-pack_0.pdf) and [2024 HTA review](https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-07/hta-policy-and-methods-review-emerging-health-technologies.pdf) support a Zolgensma price above A$2.5 million before subsidy. Date that amount in author notes; the spoken general term millions is supported without asserting a current patient charge.
- **Hearing history:** the [National Museum collection account](https://www.nma.gov.au/explore/collection/highlights/bionic-ear-prototype) supports Clark's team at Melbourne, its 1978 prototype patient, commercial Nucleus production in 1982 and later regulatory approval. The [manufacturer's history](https://www.cochlear.com/us/en/about-us/proven-over-time) specifically dates its first commercial recipient to 1982. [MED-EL](https://www.medel.com/en-au/about-medel/our-history) and [Medical University of Vienna](https://www.meduniwien.ac.at/web/en/about-us/news/detailsite/meduni-vienna-innovations-in-treatment-with-hearing-implants/) identify an earlier multichannel implantation in Vienna in December 1977. The design correctly avoids an exclusive world-first claim; the site's unqualified first claim should be repaired.
