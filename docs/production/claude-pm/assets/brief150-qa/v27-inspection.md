# v27 cell and stage checks

The cell and stage strip is `v27-cells-and-stage-strip.png`. The animal-cell plate has an empty nucleus, whereas the interphase plate contains chromatin and a nucleolus. Their cell and nucleus positions and sizes do not match without a transform, and organelles also differ. Use a quick dissolve; a seamless registered push-in is not demonstrated.

The plant-cell plate has transparency outside the cell and a transverse partition. It reads more like two largely separated daughter compartments than a plate visibly forming from the centre, so it should not alone establish the direction of formation.

The six stage images are shown in order in the strip. Cell outline and scale change markedly between metaphase and anaphase and the later stages, and organelles change position. A one-second-per-stage playback test has not been performed, so the strip does not establish that the transitions are jump-free.
