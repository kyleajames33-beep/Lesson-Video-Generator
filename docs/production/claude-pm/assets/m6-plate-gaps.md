## Review before scene design

The table names primary examples for each focused video. Some packs list more optional applications. If a scene chooses a different example, inspect the existing catalogue and commission that specific plate before building. The gas reaction and scrubber plates show apparatus and a visible change; they still need causal diagrams or measured evidence when explaining the process. Plate presence does not approve a script, visual sequence, teaching claim or release.

Generation details and SHA-256 hashes are in [brief80-provenance.json](brief80-provenance.json). [QA previews](brief80-qa/) show cream and checker backgrounds at 200 px. The twelve new PNGs have true alpha, are 2000 by 2000 px, and retain at least 8 percent clear margin on each side.

The PNGs and QA previews are ignored by Git. [brief80-media-checkpoint.json](brief80-media-checkpoint.json) records every file and hash in the local archive `out/archives/brief80-plates-2026-10-18.zip`. Transfer or back up that archive with the working tree before moving production to another computer.
