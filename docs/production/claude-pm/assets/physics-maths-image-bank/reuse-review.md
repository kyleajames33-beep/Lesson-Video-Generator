# Cross-subject raster reuse: direct appearance check

25 October 2026. Root inspected these four actual local PNGs through the image viewer. This is a bounded appearance/purpose check, not final scene-size, alpha-edge, science, animation or release approval. No images were edited. The raw inventory is not an appearance pass for the other files.

## b4-car-blue-v1.png

File: `public/assets/biology/b4-car-blue-v1.png`; 1600 by 800; SHA-256 `7f6e318834c46def51bbf15740269c8a8de1bdacfd34fb8e696cafced660d141`.

Observed: Side-view blue hatchback, full vehicle, with faint occupant silhouettes.

Reuse decision: Useful candidate for a depreciation context or a stationary vehicle reference. Keep the same physical car while coded values change. Depreciation must not be represented by shrinking or destroying the car. This side view cannot supply bank-angle geometry or exact force directions.

## c8-stopwatch-v1.png

File: `public/assets/chemistry/c8-stopwatch-v1.png`; 1367 by 1800; SHA-256 `c6e1169d5f1410cdfcb1af27ea2defcd0f0294cf32b067a3876cb40c62896925`.

Observed: Analogue stopwatch with a blank dial and a painted hand.

Reuse decision: Context only as delivered. It has no calibrated scale or truthful changing reading. Exact elapsed time needs a coded timer, or a separately reviewed edited face/hand workflow. Do not treat this raster as measured data.

## m6-burette-clamp-stand-v1.png

File: `public/assets/chemistry/m6-burette-clamp-stand-v1.png`; 2048 by 2048; SHA-256 `3f14d65c8f7461ff47aa88755cedafaa74436e0892b096ac460b449d102a16ed`.

Observed: Retort stand with a clamped burette, stopcock and nozzle already attached.

Reuse decision: Not a generic bare stand or Physics apparatus as delivered. A different apparatus requires its own selected construction and a controlled edit/new plate. Do not hide the burette in a new experiment and imply an unchanged reusable stand.

## m6-beaker-v1.png

File: `public/assets/chemistry/m6-beaker-v1.png`; 2048 by 2048; SHA-256 `ee3d5de1168f964ab02f9a9c02cb4bee5a6638c35eb9a9acd9501284738239a1`.

Observed: Clear empty beaker in perspective with a pouring spout and reflective glass walls.

Reuse decision: Candidate for recognisable vessel context, not an exact mathematical cylinder or a calibrated volume measurement. Optical boundaries, rays, fluid level and any scale must be explicitly modelled and checked separately.

Neither first-lesson pack needs a new raster merely to fill a scene. Their missing capabilities are paired model-based motion and an exact secant/local view. Existing Physics coded visuals and Maths graph/algebra should remain the teaching layer. Reuse candidates in the subject plans that were not visually inspected retain that explicit status.
