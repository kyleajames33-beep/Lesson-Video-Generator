## Appearance and retirement findings

- The 49 Module 8 PNGs depict isolated apparatus, organs, test tubes and objects. The contact sheet shows no person or human hand in these files. None is retired for that reason.
- `m8-river-sampling-bottle-v1.png` shows a pipette or sampler next to a bottle. The v2 plate shows a filled sampling bottle. Choose the plate that matches the narrated action; the route inventory uses v2 for the sample.
- The `m8-cgm-reader-high` and `-low` plates show similar small blue display marks at thumbnail size. Do not rely on tiny displayed values; draw the readable glucose number and trend in code.
- The `m8-flame-*` plates show distinct flame colours, but colour alone cannot identify every listed syllabus ion. Pair each with the actual test and controls.
- The `m8-ppt-*` plates show cream, pale-yellow and green precipitates; they cover only some required ions. Do not reuse them for a different ion or precipitate.
- `m8-hospital-bed-v1.png` is an empty bed, not a patient or dialysis machine.
- Do not reuse the previously retired `m6-hand-pollination-v1.png` or `m6-agricultural-liming-v1.png`; the [Module 6 audit](m6-plate-gaps.md) found visible hands.
