# Brief 117 exam-question comparison

30 October 2026. Downloaded all fourteen official NESA Chemistry and Biology papers for 2019 to 2025 from their exam-pack links. PDFs were processed one at a time in memory and not retained. Extracted text, exact PDF URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `brief117-exams/`; the inventory is `brief117-exam-inventory.json`. This was a text and question-structure comparison, not a visual audit of every embedded graph. Image-only labels or redacted copyright material limit a universal no-match guarantee.

| Design | Comparison and conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| P03 | Searched unknown-ion, flame, precipitation and identification contexts. The proposed dirty-loop evaluation and calcium/chloride evidence table share taught chemical tests with exam questions, but no distinctive matching stem, numerical dataset or demand sequence was found. The site's ion activities share the investigation topic; a standard test or observation is not by itself copied question authorship. |
| C04 | Compared the copper dilution attempt and technique-selection attempt with calibration/AAS contexts across all seven Chemistry papers. The closest general forms include 2019 Q29 (two metals, treatment and discharge comparison), 2021 MC14 (nickel mass after dilution), 2023 Q31 (copper-lactate equilibrium from absorbance), 2024 Q25 (phosphate dilution plus unit conversion), and 2025 MC20 (silver oxalate Ksp from AAS). C04 has different samples, data and demands. No close paraphrase found. |
| P04 | The red-filter copper dilution task uses a common calibration move. Its dataset, 10-to-25 mL dilution and requested original concentration differ from the official contexts above. The fingerprint evaluation is a general method error, with no distinctive matching official stem located. No close paraphrase found. |
| G02 | Explicitly re-read 2023 Q31 and 2024 Q25. Q31 requires a copper-lactate equilibrium calculation using initial concentrations; G02 does not. Q25 uses phosphate in washing-machine water, a 1000-fold dilution and conversion to mol/L; G02 uses copper AAS, an over-range decision and fivefold dilution. Q2's reagent intercept and Q3's bubbler sampling judgement have no matching official dataset found. Sharing the site's 10-to-50 mL ratio does not by itself make Q1 a close paraphrase: metal, data and graph-construction demand differ. |
| Biology P02 | Searched potometer, bubble, fan, plastic bag, transpiration and stomatal contexts in all seven Biology papers. 2024 MC7 asks a short stomatal water-balance choice, not either proposed practical-evaluation stem. No matching bag-rate dataset or warm/cool day design found. |
| B06 | 2019 Q28 is Huntington/Stargardt linkage and meiosis, not the proposed three-disease classification. 2022 Q27 evaluates HPV vaccination/screening campaign data, not whether cancer is transmissible. 2023 MC14 classifies three different named conditions from brief descriptions; this is a shared question type, with different conditions and no copied numerical evidence. 2024 Q28 concerns CFTR genetics, not LDL receptor function. No close paraphrase found for the proposed classification and FH transfer questions. |
| B07 | 2022 Q31 uses a Japanese passive-smoking cohort and asks epidemiological-method evaluation. The proposed illustrative Australian smoking/cancer lag graph is a different dataset and demand, though its factual labelling needs repair. 2025 Q25 evaluates a sunscreen time claim against a daily UV graph. The proposed gardener question instead traces DNA damage and probabilistic risk. No close paraphrase found. |
| B08 | 2019 MC12 uses glucose-tolerance-test graphs. 2024 MC11 reads age/sex obesity prevalence; Q29 extrapolates global diabetes prevalence through time. The proposed obesity/family-history incidence comparison has different variables, values and inference demands. No matching indoor teenager/vitamin D stem found. |

The comparisons support retaining the questions as newly written HSC-style practice, subject to the scientific and learning repairs in the sixteen reviews. They do not grant NESA endorsement or establish that every possible prior question has been excluded.

Official exam-pack links are recorded individually in the inventory, including [Chemistry 2023](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/chemistry/2023), [Chemistry 2024](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/chemistry/2024), [Biology 2022](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2022) and [Biology 2024](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/hsc-exam-papers/biology/2024).
