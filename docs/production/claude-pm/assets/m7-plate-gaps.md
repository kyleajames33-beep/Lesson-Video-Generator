## Retired and appearance findings

- The 45 Module 7 PNGs inspected show isolated objects, organisms, cells, apparatus and products. None visibly shows a person or human hand. `m7-handwashing-sink-v1.png` shows the sink and soap only; its name does not indicate a hand in the image.
- Do not reuse `m6-hand-pollination-v1.png` or `m6-agricultural-liming-v1.png`; the [Module 6 plate audit](m6-plate-gaps.md) retires them because they show human hands.
- The existing `m7-polymer-objects-v1.png` is a mixed plastic-object grouping; it does not show nylon fibre, polyester fabric or polymer molecular structure. The latter is coded.
- The `m7-separating-funnel-cloudy` and `-clear` pair and `m7-agar-antibiotic-before` and `-after` pair are visibly aligned process candidates. Check their exact scientific use during design.
- The spirit burner calorimeter is the clearest Chemistry gap. The existing `m6-foam-cup-calorimeter-v1.png` depicts neutralisation, not open-flame combustion.
