## Precision notes, separate from confirmed arithmetic errors

FA1 L03 (100 N,30 degrees), L05 (300 m,100 m,40 s), L06 (120 m,160 m,40 s), L11 (80 km/h twice), L12 (150 N,20 degrees) leave trailing-zero precision unstated. The calculations are arithmetically correct. Specify ideal values or intended measurement precision before marking a significant-figure answer wrong. L11 gives 113 km/h from sqrt(80²+80²); if 80 means two figures, the final report should be 1.1e2 km/h. The rain velocity is down and south in the car frame, whereas the direction it appears to come from is up and north.
