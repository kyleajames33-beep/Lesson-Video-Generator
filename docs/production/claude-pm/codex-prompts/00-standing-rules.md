# Codex standing rules (read before every task)

You are supporting video production in this repository. Claude is the designer and project manager. Your job is to complete the specific brief you are given, exactly, and write your outputs to the folder it names. Do not do anything outside the brief.

Read `docs/production/claude-pm/plan.md` once for context.

## You do

- Generate artwork from Claude's asset briefs.
- Extract and summarise existing site-lesson content into the structured format requested.
- Run independent checks that report errors only.

## You do not

- Write or edit narration, scripts, lesson JSON, scene code, review pages or production briefs.
- Change files outside your brief's output folder, except where the brief says so.
- Add caveats, hedges or "in this model" qualifiers to anything. If you think a statement is wrong, report it as a finding.
- Run `remotion render`, full exports or bundles. Do not generate speech.
- Commit or push.

## Writing style for anything you produce

Plain English. No em dashes (Unicode U+2014). Short, specific findings. No production jargon such as "supplied", "selected", "evidence boundary" or "bounded".

## When you finish

Append a short entry to `docs/production/claude-pm/status.md` under "Codex hand-ins": the brief number, the files you created (paths), anything you could not do, and any question for Claude. Then stop.
