# Brief 43: C7 "Haber is a compromise" tug-of-war plates

Read `00-standing-rules.md` first. Follow the style rules of brief 27:
- transparent PNG with a real alpha channel;
- ink and watercolour textbook plate, matching the C7 plates in `public/assets/chemistry/` (the converter tower and the plant);
- no text, labels or numbers;
- at least 1600 px, 8% margin, reads clearly at 200 px.

C7 (`src/explainer/lessons/chem-m5-c07`, the "Haber is a compromise" scene) shows a tug-of-war, currently drawn as basic coded stick figures. A blue "yield (asks for cold)" side pulls left and an orange "speed (asks for hot)" side pulls right, with a flag at about 450 C in the middle. The owner wants a proper drawing.

Paint three plates that line up on one canvas, about 3000 x 1000:
1. `c7-tug-left-v1.png`
   - The left team, one figure: an adult or older student in a cool blue jumper, leaning back and pulling a thick rope that runs off the right edge of their canvas area.
   - A small ice-crystal or frost motif on their side, as a painted object, not text.
   - Friendly, simple, textbook style. Not cartoonish, no caricature, no brand logos.
2. `c7-tug-right-v1.png`
   - The right team, one figure in a warm orange jumper, leaning back the other way.
   - A small painted flame on their side.
3. `c7-tug-rope-flag-v1.png`
   - The rope alone, spanning the full width between the two figures' hands.
   - A small yellow flag tied at the centre.

Keep each figure on its own layer, in the same canvas position as the full scene, so the video can slide the figures a little and move the flag along the rope. Faces may be simple or seen in three-quarter view from behind. Keep them diverse and not stereotyped.

Write provenance to `docs/production/claude-pm/assets/brief43-provenance.json`. Put QA views in `docs/production/claude-pm/assets/brief43-qa/`: a checkerboard view, a 200 px view, and the three layers composited. Append a hand-in to `status.md`.
