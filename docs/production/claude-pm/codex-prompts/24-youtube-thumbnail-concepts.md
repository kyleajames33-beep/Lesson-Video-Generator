# Brief 24: YouTube thumbnail concepts (for the owner to choose; do not upload)

Read `00-standing-rules.md` first. The channel's videos currently use auto-picked frames. The owner wants to see custom thumbnail options before any are used. Make concepts only: nothing is uploaded or changed on YouTube in this brief.

## What to make

Three different thumbnail styles, each shown on the same two videos (so six thumbnails):
- C3, Le Chatelier's principle: concentration and temperature (https://youtu.be/w0GzgApQhQY; the cobalt tubes and iron thiocyanate tubes are in `public/assets/chemistry/c3-*.png`).
- B2c, how single-celled organisms reproduce (https://youtu.be/kG-KexGgCzc; plates in `public/assets/biology/b2c-*.png` and the B1 budding and fission plates).

Style ideas to try (one each): (A) a painted lesson plate on the warm paper background with a short hand-lettered question; (B) a bold split "before / after" or "A versus B" composition; (C) a clean subject-coloured band (Chemistry green, Biology blue) with the plate cut out and 2 to 4 large words. Every style: 1280 x 720 PNG, readable at 320 px wide, at most four words of text, no clickbait, no faces, no em dashes, the HSCScience name small in one corner. Use the existing painted plates where you can (you may generate new background art with GPT image in the same watercolour style).

Text must be spelled correctly and scientifically correct (for example "Le Chatelier", "binary fission", "budding").

## Output

Save to `docs/production/claude-pm/thumbnails/concepts/` as `c3-A.png`, `c3-B.png`, `c3-C.png`, `b2c-A.png`, `b2c-B.png`, `b2c-C.png`, plus `index.html` showing all six side by side at full size and at 320 px. Record prompts and sources in `thumbnails/concepts/provenance.json`. Append a hand-in to `status.md` with a one-line description of each style.
