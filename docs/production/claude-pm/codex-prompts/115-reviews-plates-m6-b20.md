## 2. Science check

Verify and date every fact. In particular:
- **Golden Rice:**
  - the 2000 and 2005 versions and their beta-carotene levels;
  - the food safety approvals from 2017 to 2018: which countries, and which agencies;
  - the 2021 Philippines approval for commercial growing;
  - the 2024 court order, and whether it still applies at the end of October 2026;
  - the licensing terms for humanitarian use.
- **Bt cotton:** adoption in Australia and other countries, and its effect on spraying.
- **Attempts:** the Bt eggplant facts and the drought-tolerant wheat facts. Wheat is HB4 wheat in Argentina and Australia; check its approvals.
- **Population dynamics:** the claims about how genetic change spreads, or fails to spread, through a population.

Check the dotpoint word for word against the 2017 syllabus. Write `checks/m6b20-claude-science-check.md`.

**Cultural:** the design flags the site's point about Indigenous perspectives for the owner's cultural review and leaves it unwritten. Check that nothing in the learner copy makes claims about specific Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander communities.
