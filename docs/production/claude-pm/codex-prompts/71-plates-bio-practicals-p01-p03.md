# Brief 71: plates for the Biology practicals P01, P02 and P03

Read `00-standing-rules.md` first. Style as in brief 64:
- transparent PNG with a real alpha channel;
- ink and watercolour, matching the existing `public/assets/biology/m5x-*` plates;
- no text, labels, numbers or arrows;
- **no people, hands or faces** (P03's mirror shows no reflection);
- about 2000 px on the long side, at least 8% clear margin;
- reads at 200 px.

Save each plate to `public/assets/biology/`. The exact framing for each one is in the "Codex asset brief" section of `docs/production/claude-pm/lessons/bio-m5-p01.md`, `bio-m5-p02.md` and `bio-m5-p03.md`. Follow those sections, because they are the final Revision 2 lists.
