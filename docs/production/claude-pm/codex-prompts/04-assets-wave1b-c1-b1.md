## B1 (Biology): the banana problem and three situations

Save to `public/assets/biology/`.

8. `b1-cavendish-bunch-v1.png`: one healthy bunch (hand) of ripe yellow Cavendish bananas.
9. `b1-banana-plantation-tr4-v1.png`: a row of banana plants in a plantation, wide framing. Several plants show Panama disease (TR4): yellowing, browning leaves collapsing and hanging down along the stem; one or two plants still look healthy. Realistic, calm, not gory. Include the ground strip so it reads as a field.
10. `b1-stable-pond-v1.png`: a small, calm, sunlit pond with clear water and reeds, peaceful and unchanging. (It represents a stable home for bacteria; no microbes need to be visible.)
11. `b1-lone-island-plant-v1.png`: a tiny sandy island in the sea with a single young flowering plant growing on it, no other plants.
12. `b1-diseased-crop-field-v1.png`: a field of one leafy crop (for example wheat or a leafy vegetable) where a new leaf disease is visibly spreading in patches. Wide framing.
