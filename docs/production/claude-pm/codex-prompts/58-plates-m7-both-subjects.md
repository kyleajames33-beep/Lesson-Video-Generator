# Brief 58: painted plate libraries for Year 12 Module 7 (organic chemistry; infectious disease)

Read `00-standing-rules.md` first. Style as in brief 27:
- transparent PNG with a real alpha channel;
- ink and watercolour textbook plates matching the existing chemistry and biology plates;
- no text, labels or numbers;
- at least 1600 px, 8% margin, reads at 200 px.

Rules from the owner:
- **No people, faces or body figures** (owner rule, 16 Oct). Show objects, apparatus, organisms, pathogens, cells and settings only.
- **Molecules and structural formulas are drawn in code;** do not paint them.

Use the Module 7 site extracts (`docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/chem-m7-*.md`, `bio-m7-*.md`) and, if brief 57 is done, the plate lists in its packs. Paint what the site uses. Save as `public/assets/chemistry/m7-<name>-v1.png` and `public/assets/biology/m7-<name>-v1.png`, with matched sets on one canvas.

**Chemistry Module 7 (organic):**
- Fractional distillation column for crude oil, plus a school distillation setup.
- Reflux apparatus with a condenser.
- Separating funnel with two layers.
- Bromine water test as a matched pair of tubes: orange, then colourless.
- Sources of hydrocarbons: an oil and gas rig, a crude oil barrel (plain).
- Polymer objects: a plastic bag, a PVC pipe, a polystyrene cup, a nylon rope, a PET bottle (all plain, no logos).
- Fuels: a camping gas burner and an ethanol fuel bottle (plain).
- Soap-making: soap bars, and a beaker of oil and lye (plain).
- Esters: fruit (banana, pineapple, pear) and a perfume bottle (plain).
- Process-clip pairs (aligned start and end):
  - bromine water decolourising;
  - layers separating in a separating funnel (shaken and cloudy, then two clear layers).

**Biology Module 7 (infectious disease):**
- Pathogens in textbook style:
  - a bacterium (rod and coccus);
  - a virus particle;
  - a fungal hyphae close-up;
  - a protozoan (Plasmodium stage in a red blood cell);
  - a prion as a stylised folded-protein blob;
  - a tapeworm or a macroparasite (only if the site uses it);
  - a tick or mosquito vector.
- Lab objects: a petri dish with colonies, an agar plate with antibiotic discs and clear zones (a matched before and after pair), a microscope, an inoculating loop.
- Settings: a handwashing sink (no person), a face mask, a vaccine vial and syringe (plain), a quarantine sign shape (no text).
- Plant disease: a healthy leaf and an infected leaf (a matched pair), and a banana plant with Panama disease (only if the site uses it).
- Immune system cells in textbook style: macrophage, neutrophil, B cell, T cell, an antibody as a stylised Y shape (no letters), a mast cell.
- Process-clip pairs:
  - a macrophage engulfing a bacterium;
  - antibiotic clear zones growing on an agar plate.

Write provenance to `docs/production/claude-pm/assets/brief58-provenance.json` and QA views to `docs/production/claude-pm/assets/brief58-qa/`. Hand in to `status.md` with one line per plate. If the site uses a different example, paint the site's and note the swap.
