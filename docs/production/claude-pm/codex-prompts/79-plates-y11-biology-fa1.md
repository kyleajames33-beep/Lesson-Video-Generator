# Brief 79: painted plate library for Year 11 Biology (new syllabus), focus area 1

Read `00-standing-rules.md` first. Use the same style as brief 58:
- transparent PNG with a real alpha channel;
- ink and watercolour;
- no text or labels;
- **no people, faces or hands**;
- about 2000 px, with an 8% margin;
- reads at 200 px.

Brief 61's packs list the plates each video needs (`docs/production/claude-pm/pre-design/y11-bio/fa1-*.md`, "plates needed").

1. Collect every plate the FA1 packs list.
2. Remove any that already exist in `public/assets/biology/`, checking by look as well as by name.
3. Paint the rest as `public/assets/biology/y11-<name>-v1.png`, with matched sets on one canvas.

Cells and organelles are textbook illustrations; microscope views are painted fields with no labels. Molecules and graphs stay coded, so don't paint them.

Write the provenance to `docs/production/claude-pm/assets/brief79-provenance.json` and QA views to `assets/brief79-qa/`. Hand in to `status.md` with one line per plate. If you finish FA1, continue to FA2 and say where you stopped.
