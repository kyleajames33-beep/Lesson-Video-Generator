## 20. integral-calculus/lesson09.sa.html: set-up-area-integrals

```text
The total profit generated between the break-even points is $\int_{10}^{50}[R(x) - C(x)]\,dx$
```

**Correction:** For total revenueR and total costC, profit at outputx isP(x)=R(x)-C(x). WithR=120x-x² andC=60x+500, P(x)=-x²+60x-500; its greatest value is400 atx=30. An integral over output is not total profit.
