### 8. how we know
> So how do we know all this? A light microscope can't separate two points closer than about two hundred nanometres. A nanometre is a thousandth of a micrometre. Electron microscopes show far finer detail, and they revealed that typical prokaryotes have no nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles. That's why scientists use a variety of sources: micrographs, which are microscope photos, labelled diagrams, and tables of measurements. Each can mislead on its own, so they're checked against each other.

### 11b. feedback, item one

### 13. key notes (quiet, about 60 seconds)
> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- Eukaryote: DNA inside a membrane-bound nucleus; has membrane-bound organelles (animals, plants, fungi, protists). Prokaryote: no nucleus; DNA free in the nucleoid; no membrane-bound organelles (bacteria).
