### 12. key notes (quiet, about 60 seconds)
> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- Prevention acts before disease or harm develops. Primary: stop the exposure (sun protection). Secondary: detect early (screening). Tertiary: limit damage in established disease (diabetes eye checks).
- Design a campaign from the mechanism: exposure, audience, action, measure. Knowledge alone rarely changes behaviour; changing conditions (shade, hat policies, prices, access) makes the safe choice easy.
- Process: what the campaign did. Impact: what people did (behaviour, exposure). Outcome: what the disease did (incidence, mortality). Long-latency diseases show outcomes only after decades.
- SunSmart (from 1981 and 1988): more hats, sunscreen and shade, less sunburn; melanoma falling in younger Australians, still rising in older people. Limits: no randomised control group, self-report, other changes, so it supports a package.
- Evaluate with secondary sources (who, when, real data, agreement). Improve a campaign by targeting the highest-risk groups where they are and measuring them separately.

Memory: Process, impact, outcome: what we did, what people did, what the disease did.
