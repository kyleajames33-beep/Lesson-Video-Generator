### 2. a test is a reaction you can see
> Each test borrows a reaction from Module 7. The trick is to pick a reaction that only one of these groups does quickly, with a change you can see: a colour that goes, a colour that changes, or a gas that fizzes. So before you test, predict. ## Teaching checks

- **Does it explain causes?** Why a test works: only one group does that reaction quickly, with a visible change (2). Why bromine fades: it adds across the C=C and is used up (3). Why an alkane stays orange, and why room light: no C=C to open; light lets it substitute slowly (3). Why an acid fizzes: it is a stronger acid than carbonic acid, which breaks up into water and CO₂ (4). Why an alcohol doesn't: far too weak an acid (4). Why green: the alcohol is oxidised and the dichromate reduced (5). Why a tertiary alcohol stays orange: no H on its C–OH carbon (5).
