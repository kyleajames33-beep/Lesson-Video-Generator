### 2. reflection is a method
> The syllabus asks you to reflect on your own health, and that word invites a diary entry. But **reflection** is a method: a structured review that ends in a conclusion and a change. If nothing you do next is different, you described, you didn't reflect. It has four steps. Describe what's actually happening, in specifics: not I sleep badly, but in bed by eleven, asleep near one, four nights in seven. Analyse why: is it a behaviour, a condition, or a coping strategy doing a job for you? Conclude something you didn't know at the start. Then plan a specific next action, with a date to review it.

### 3. not self-criticism

### 10d. feedback, item two
> Judge it with a boundary. For: it turns impressions into evidence, it's free, and it builds skills like self-efficacy.
