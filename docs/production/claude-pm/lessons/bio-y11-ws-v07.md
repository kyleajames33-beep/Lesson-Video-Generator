### 3. writing the argument
> Claim: the weed is more abundant in shade than in sun. Evidence: a mean of five point two per square metre in shade, against one point one in sun, with ranges that don't overlap. Reasoning: quadrats were placed randomly in each zone, so the difference isn't from where they were put. Then a limitation. This survey shows shade and the weed go together, not that shade causes it. Claim, evidence, reasoning: say it, show it, explain it.

## Practice question and feedback

1. (11a, construct, 4 marks; original.) Seagrass mean shoot length 32 cm (clear bay) and 14 cm (murky bay), six sites each.
   Model: claim (1): shoots are longer in the clear bay; evidence (1): 32 cm against 14 cm, six sites each; reasoning (1): clearer water lets more light reach the leaves, so more photosynthesis and growth; limitation (1): the bays may differ in other factors (nutrients, temperature, wave action), so the data suggest but do not prove that light is the cause; more sites or measured light levels would strengthen it. Classic mistake crossed: a conclusion with no data.
2. (11c, identify and correct, 3 marks; original.) "The Eucalyptus Regnans trees in this study grew 1.2 metres per year, which is proven because the paper was peer reviewed."
   Model: nomenclature: *Eucalyptus regnans*, italicised, species name lower case (1); overclaim: peer review does not prove a result (1); corrected sentence, e.g. "The *Eucalyptus regnans* trees in this study grew about 1.2 m per year." (1). Classic mistake crossed: "peer reviewed means it can't be wrong".

Feedback lengths about 28 s (11b) and 22 s (11d) by script.

## Numbers or facts to verify

- **Survey (2, 3).** Illustrative, tagged.
- **Peer review (5).** Standard description: editors send manuscripts to independent experts who assess methods, analysis and conclusions and recommend acceptance, revision or rejection. Correct.
- **Ulcer case (6).** Warren observed spiral bacteria in stomach biopsies (1979 to 1982); Warren and Marshall published in The Lancet (1983 letters; 1984 paper); initial scepticism; Marshall's self-ingestion experiment (1984; not mentioned); confirmation by others; Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2005 for the discovery of *Helicobacter pylori* and its role in gastritis and peptic ulcer disease. "a bacterium causes most stomach ulcers": most peptic ulcers are associated with *H. pylori* (others with anti-inflammatory drugs); science check to accept "most stomach ulcers" or change to "most ulcers of the stomach and duodenum".
- **Nomenclature (7).** *Eucalyptus regnans* is the mountain ash. Binomial convention correct.
- **Seagrass (11a).** Light limitation of seagrass growth in turbid water is well established. Correct (illustrative numbers).
