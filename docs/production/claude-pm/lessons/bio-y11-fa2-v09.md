### 8. pits, and why dead works
> So why can xylem be dead? Because the vessels don't power the flow. They only have to be a path, and the pull comes from somewhere else, which is a later video. Xylem is a pipe, not a **pump**. But it's a pipe with one more trick. The lignin has gaps called pits, thin patches of wall where water can pass sideways, into the next vessel or the living cells beside it. If an air bubble blocks one vessel, the water can detour around it, through the **pits**.

### 10. why several sources
> Notice how the sources fit together. The dye showed where the water travels, but not what the tubes look like. ## Memory aid check

- **"Xylem is a pipe, not a pump."** (Claude's, adapted from the site's l07 hook.) Assumptions: (1) movement of water through xylem is passive; the energy does not come from the xylem vessels (true; the pull and the small push of root pressure both originate outside the vessels; v12 explains the pull).
