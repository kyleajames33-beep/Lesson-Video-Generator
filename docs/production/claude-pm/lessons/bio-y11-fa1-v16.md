  1. L13 "why it matters": "your own aerobic respiration is why a breathalyser, or a CO₂ sensor, can detect that you are alive and active". A breathalyser measures ethanol in the breath, not carbon dioxide, and it detects drinking, not respiration. Proposed: "would release the energy as heat, with no way to capture it in ATP; small steps release amounts small enough to be transferred to ATP".
  3. L13 vocabulary and model answers: "release energy stored in its chemical bonds", "captured in the bonds of ATP", "transferred into the terminal bond of an ATP molecule". ### 4. formulas, and why we balance
