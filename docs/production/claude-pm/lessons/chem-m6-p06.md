### 3. make the standard: dry, weigh, rinse it all in
> Start with sodium carbonate dried in an oven and cooled in a **desiccator**, so none of its mass is water. Weigh it into a beaker and record the exact mass: **one point three six five six** grams. Dissolve it, pour it through a funnel into a two hundred and fifty millilitre volumetric flask, then **rinse** the beaker and funnel in, so every grain ends up inside.

## Teaching checks

- **Does it explain causes?** Why dry the carbonate: so none of its mass is water (3). Why rinse the beaker and funnel in: every weighed grain must reach the flask (3). Why invert: every aliquot the same (4). Why rinse the pipette with the solution: water would dilute it (5, A1). Why leave the last drop: the pipette is calibrated that way (5). ## Safety facts check (self-check, 21 Oct; science check to confirm)
