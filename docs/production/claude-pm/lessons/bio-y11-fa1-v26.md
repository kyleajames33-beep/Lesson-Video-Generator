> Eight, then sixteen. The number would double every generation: in humans, forty-six, then ninety-two, then a hundred and eighty-four. That doesn't happen. Every generation of humans has forty-six. So eggs and sperm must carry fewer chromosomes than body cells. But how many, and which ones?

### 4. chromosomes come in pairs

### 15. the comparison
> To compare them, take each feature in turn. Somatic cells are diploid, while gametes are haploid. Somatic cells are made by mitosis, gametes by meiosis.
