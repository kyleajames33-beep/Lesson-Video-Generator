### 2. the set-up, and why each part is there
> Here's the set-up. A spirit burner holds the alcohol: it's a small glass lamp with a wick. Above it, a metal can of water is clamped **close** to the flame. ## Safety facts check (self-check, 22 Oct; science check to confirm)

- **Methanol** (CLP Annex VI harmonised, index 603-001-00-X): Flam. - **Propan-1-ol** (index 603-003-00-0): Flam. Liq. 2, H225; Eye Dam. 1, H318 (causes serious eye damage); STOT SE 3, H336 (may cause drowsiness or dizziness). Flash point about 15 to 22 °C (sources differ). Learner copy: "highly flammable", "can seriously damage eyes".
