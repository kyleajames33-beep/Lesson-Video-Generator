### 2. time = BAC ÷ 0.015

### 3. worked example: hours, then minutes, rounded up

### 5. why any estimate is rough
> Now, how far can we trust these estimates? A limitation names what the model assumes, and why that fails. First, the formulas assume an average body for each weight, but real bodies differ, in build, health, food eaten, and medication. Second, they assume every drink was exactly one standard drink, but drinks poured at home are often bigger, so N is too small. Third, they assume the body clears alcohol at one steady rate, and real bodies **don't**. So the NSW Government says it plainly. It's impossible for you to estimate your own BAC, even if you think you know how many drinks you've had. Only a breath or blood test measures BAC, and on L or P plates, the only safe plan is no alcohol at all before **driving**.
