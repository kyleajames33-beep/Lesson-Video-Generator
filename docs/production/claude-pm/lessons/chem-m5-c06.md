The change row follows the **coefficients** in the equation. A substance used up gets a minus sign and a substance made gets a plus.

### 8. reversing the equation: K belongs to the equation as written
> K belongs to the equation as it is written. Take the same settled tube, 0.10, 0.10 and 0.70, and write the equation **backwards**: two H I making hydrogen plus iodine. K is now hydrogen times iodine over H I squared: 0.10 times 0.10, over 0.70 squared, is **0.010 over 0.49**, about 0.020, and that is **one over 49**. Flip the equation, flip K. Double every coefficient and every power doubles, so K is squared: 49 squared is 2401.

### 9. attempt (countdown, 10 seconds)

### 12. key notes (quiet, hold about 70 seconds)
> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- An ICE table has a column for each substance and three rows: Initial, Change and Equilibrium. Work in concentrations: concentration (mol/L) = moles divided by litres.
- The change row follows the coefficients of the balanced equation. A substance used up is minus and a substance made is plus. The initial and equilibrium rows need not follow the coefficients (the start is given and K sets the equilibrium row). Equilibrium = initial + change.
- To find K: write the expression, fill the table from the start and the one measured value, substitute the equilibrium values (never the initial ones), and check the subtraction.
- To find a concentration from K: call the change x, write the change row by the coefficients, substitute and solve. The square root shortcut works only when both sides are perfect squares, for example H2 and I2 starting equal. Other starts need a method from a later video. Keep the positive root, and check that no concentration comes out negative.
- K belongs to the equation as written, at one temperature. Reverse the equation: K becomes 1 / K. Multiply every coefficient by n: K goes to the power n (halving gives the square root).

Memory aid (large, on the notes screen): *Only the change row follows the coefficients.* Second line: *Flip the equation, flip K. Multiply the equation, power K.* Condition tag: "used up is minus, made is plus; same temperature, equation as written."
