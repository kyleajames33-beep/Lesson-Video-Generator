## Decisions

- **Learner question kept (route).** "How does changing the log base change its derivative?" The answer, built in order: every base-a log curve is the natural log curve scaled vertically by the factor 1/ln a (the change of base rule), and a vertical scale by a factor multiplies every slope at the same x by that factor. So d/dx(log_a x) = (1/ln a) × (1/x) = 1/(x ln a). The base sets one number, 1/ln a, and that number explains the steepness (a > e flatter than ln x, 1 < a < e steeper), the sign (0 < a < 1 falling) and the excluded base (a = 1 divides by zero).
