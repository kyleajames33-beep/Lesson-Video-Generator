## Practice question and feedback

1. (11a, identify and propose, 3 marks; original.) A results table with units in cells, "about 2.3", a crossed-out value with no note, a missing reading and no soaking time.
   Model (any three, 1 mark each, problem with fix): units in every cell (put units in the column heading); "about 2.3" (record the balance reading to 0.01 g); a value crossed out with no note (keep the value and note why); missing 0.6 M final mass (record every reading); soaking time not recorded (record the time for each beaker). 2. (11c, identify and propose, 3 marks; original.) Cutting in the air; scalpel at the bench edge; spill left on the floor.
