"Compensation point" is not named (author note).

The rule taught: levelling off means another factor has taken over; rising then falling means too much of that condition is starting to lower the rate. ### 7. what the bubbles really measure
> One more question: what did we actually measure? Bubbles vary in size, and in a fast stream they merge, so a count isn't a volume. Gas volume is better, though the gas isn't pure oxygen. Bubbles show only part of what's left over.
