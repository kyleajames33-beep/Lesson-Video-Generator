Bubbles are not net production: some net oxygen dissolves in the water (or stays in the plant's air spaces) and the bubbles also carry other gases, so bubbles show only part of the net oxygen, as a rough, indirect guide. Revision 1's "bubbles show net production" is withdrawn from narration, the net-gross bar, feedback, notes and memory line. In very dim light the plant can make oxygen and still release no bubbles. "Compensation point" is not named (author note).

The rise is v06's (enzymes faster); Revision 2: the fall is not deduced from the shape. Denaturing is offered as one possible reason, with heat slowing other steps (some reversible) as another; the shape alone cannot separate them. The rule taught: levelling off means another factor has taken over; rising then falling means too much of that condition is starting to lower the rate.
