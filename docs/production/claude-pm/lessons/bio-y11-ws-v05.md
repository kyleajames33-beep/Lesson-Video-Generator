### 5. standard deviation
> The range uses only two values. Standard deviation uses all of them. It's roughly the typical distance of the readings from the mean. Find each reading's distance from the mean, square it, add them up, divide by one less than the number of readings, then take the square root. Your calculator's statistics mode does this for you. Here, it's about thirty-three.
