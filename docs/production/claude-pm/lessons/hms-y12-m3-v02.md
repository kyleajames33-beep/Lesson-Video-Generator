### 4. how the determinants interact
> Second question: how do the determinants interact? In Year 11, the determinants ran in a chain, from broad features of society down to the body. **Interact** means one determinant changes the effect of another, so together they do more than add. Take crowded housing and low income. Crowding alone spreads infection. Low income alone delays care. Together, throat and skin infections spread, go untreated and keep coming back. Repeated attacks can trigger rheumatic fever, which scars the heart's valves, most often the mitral valve, on the heart's left side. That's rheumatic heart disease, lifelong. And don't leave out racism. It acts twice, through chronic stress, and by making people less likely to return to a service that treated them badly. So **cultural safety**, care that the person receiving it judges respectful of who they are, is clinical, not a courtesy.

### 7. where each action acts, and who decides
