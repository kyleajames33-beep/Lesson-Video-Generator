### 5. why delta U equals q V
> That gives the energy for any charge. If each coulomb's potential energy changes by V joules, then q coulombs change by q times V. Move a charge slowly, so it doesn't speed up, and all the outside push's work goes into potential energy, so that work is q V too. Last video's q V was the field's work on a released charge. Charge in coulombs, voltage in volts, and the energy comes out in **joules**.

### 6. one rule for W, delta U and delta K E
> But what if the charge doesn't move slowly? Here's the one rule that ties it all together. The work done by an outside force goes into two places: potential energy, and kinetic energy. Then the kinetic energy rises by exactly what the potential energy loses, just as you saw last **time**.
