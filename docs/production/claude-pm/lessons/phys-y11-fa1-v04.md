### 3. along one line: let the signs cancel

### 6. two directions: total each one separately
> Now journeys that turn corners. Here's the key idea. East and west legs can cancel each other. North and south legs can cancel each other. But an east leg can't cancel a north leg, because they're at right angles. That's last video's point: perpendicular parts act **independently**. So we total each direction on its own, with signs. Then we combine the two totals, which meet at a right **angle**.

## Teaching checks

- **Does it explain causes?** Why distance adds every leg: every metre of path counts (2). Why opposite legs cancel in displacement: only the end position matters (1, 3).
