### 6. why percentage, not grams
> Why a percentage, and not grams? Leaves from different species are rarely the same size. Say leaf A starts at four grams and loses zero point four grams, while leaf B starts at one gram and loses a quarter of a gram. In grams, leaf A lost more, so it looks worse. As a percentage, A lost ten percent and B lost twenty-five, so A held its water better. Percentage, not grams. A big leaf loses more of everything.

> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.
