### 1. hook (two answers, one point)
> This is a practice video. The questions mix the differentiation rules, and none of them tells you which rule to use. Here's the first. A skate bowl's side follows the curve y equals x squared, minus six times the natural log of x. A skateboard rests flat against it where the slope is four. - **"Last step picks the rule. The domain picks the answers."** with **"Point not given? Name it a."** Original.
