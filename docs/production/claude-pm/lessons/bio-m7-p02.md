### 2. why test, and what a school test can show
> Contaminated water can look clean, so the only way to find microbes is to grow them. Water authorities test for **E. coli**, a gut bacterium that signals faecal contamination, so other gut pathogens might be there too. A school test is **different**: it counts every microbe that grows on nutrient agar, so it compares samples, but it can't show water is safe.

## Safety facts check (self-check, 21 Oct; science check to confirm)
