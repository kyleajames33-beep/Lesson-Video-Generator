### 2. lifting stores energy
> The winch raises the box at a steady speed, so the forces balance. The winch pulls up with a force equal to the weight, m g. Over a height change delta h, the winch does work m g times delta h. Gravity does the same amount of negative **work**, so the net work is zero, and the kinetic energy doesn't **change**. So where did the winch's energy go? It's stored as gravitational potential energy, the energy an object has because of its height in Earth's gravity. Its change is delta U equals m g delta **h**. It works because near Earth's surface the field is uniform: g is the same at every height in the problem, so the weight stays the same all the way **up**.

### 3. only the change in height counts
> Now the puzzle. From the floor, the box rises two metres, so delta U is two times nine point eight times two, thirty-nine point two **joules**. From the table, it rises only one metre, so delta U is nineteen point six **joules**. Same shelf, half the increase, because only the change in height counts. You can measure height from the floor or from the table, and the change is the same. The path doesn't matter either, because gravity is conservative. And going down, delta h is negative, so delta U is **negative**.

### 5. why speed is squared
