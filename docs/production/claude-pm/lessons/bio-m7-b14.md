  2. L19 card 1 (and the intro and hook; see error 19): "Ragusa ordered arriving ships to anchor in the harbour for 30 days before anyone could disembark". The 1377 decree required people and goods from plague areas to spend 30 days at Mrkan (Mercana) island or Cavtat before entering the city, not to stay aboard in the harbour.   3.
