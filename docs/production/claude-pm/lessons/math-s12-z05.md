### 2. a probability is an area
> Here's the key idea. The whole area under the bell stands for all the loaves, a hundred percent, or a probability of one. So the chance that a loaf lands in some range is the share of the area over that range. The bell is symmetric, so half the area lies on each side of the mean. The chance a loaf weighs more than seven hundred and ten grams is a **half**. A z-score is a position on the axis, but a probability is an **area**.

### 3. under 700 grams: z first, then shade

Five percent lies outside, shared equally by two tails, so this tail is two point five percent, a probability of zero point zero two **five**. The bakery bakes four hundred loaves a day. Two point five percent of four hundred is ten, so we'd expect about ten loaves a day under the **label**.
