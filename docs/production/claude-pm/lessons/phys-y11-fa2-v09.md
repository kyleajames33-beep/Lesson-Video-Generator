### 2. what we observe
> Here's what happens. The ringing gets quieter and quieter, until we can barely hear it at all. But we can still see the hammer striking the bell, just as hard as before. Let the air back in, and the ringing comes straight **back**. The bell hangs on soft threads, so very little sound can escape through its supports. Most of it has to travel through the air in the jar. So the sound needs the **air**.

### 8. the medium sets the speed
> The stiff row wins. A stiff spring pushes back hard when it's squeezed even a little, so each particle gets its neighbour moving sooner.
