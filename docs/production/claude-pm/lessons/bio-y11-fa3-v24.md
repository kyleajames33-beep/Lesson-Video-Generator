### 6. stream and pool
> The stream: about eleven milligrams per litre, against the pool's six. Cold water holds more, and the tumbling flow keeps mixing air in. The still pool replaces its oxygen slowly. Never stretch; keep the native aspect ratio. The five ecosystem plates (items 1 to 5) share one style, one horizon height and one scale of detail so they read as a set; each is a self-contained vignette with a soft irregular edge (no rectangular frame).

1. `y11-coral-reef-v1.png` (about 2000 x 1200): a shallow Great Barrier Reef scene seen as a side cutaway: clear blue water with the surface line near the top, branching and plate corals and a few massive corals on a sandy bottom, two or three small reef fish. Bright, clear water.
2. `y11-desert-dune-v1.png` (same canvas): central Australian arid land: a low red sand dune with spinifex hummocks, a few desert oaks or mulga in the distance, cracked red ground in front, no water anywhere, intense pale sky band.
3. `y11-mountain-stream-v1.png` (same canvas): a cold, clear mountain stream tumbling over rounded stones in a shallow riffle (visible white water), ferns and tree ferns on the banks, the stream bed visible through the water at the front.
4. `y11-lowland-pool-v1.png` (same canvas): a warm, still lowland pool or billabong: flat, slightly green-brown water with floating leaves and a little algae scum, reeds at the edge, a river red gum leaning over, a muddy bank; no visible flow.
5. `y11-estuary-mangroves-v1.png` (same canvas): an estuary where a river meets the sea: brackish water in the centre with a soft tide line on the mud, grey mangroves with pneumatophores along one bank, a sandbar and open sea toward one side and the river channel toward the other; leave clear water at the left and right of the channel (the build places a fish at the left and a dashed oval at the right).
6. `y11-dissolved-oxygen-meter-v1.png` (about 1200 x 1200): a handheld dissolved oxygen meter with a blank screen and a probe on a coiled cable, lying flat. No brand marks.
7. `y11-mayfly-nymph-v1.png` (about 1400 x 900): a mayfly nymph (Ephemeroptera), top view, three tail filaments and the feathery gills along the abdomen clearly visible. No stone.
8. `y11-coral-branch-healthy-v1.png` and `y11-coral-branch-bleached-v1.png` (both about 1200 x 1200, identical canvas and pose): a single branching coral colony (Acropora). Healthy: golden-brown tissue. Bleached: the same colony bright white with faint pale tips. Only the colour differs.
9. `y11-lake-shore-grassland-v1.png` (about 2000 x 1000): a calm inland lake with a grassy shore and a few scattered eucalypts on the grassland, side view, summer light.
10. `y11-coastal-dune-v1.png` (about 2000 x 1000): a coastal sand dune running from the beach up to the back dune: pale sand, spinifex grass and pigface on the fore dune, coastal saltbush and banksia toward the back; sea at the far left edge.
