### 9. control kept, or control lost
> The fever. In a fever, the hypothalamus has raised its set point, so the loop is aiming at a higher target, and it still corrects changes around it. When the illness passes, the set point drops, and temperature comes back. In heatstroke, the loop has lost control. Heat is gained faster than it's lost, and the responses themselves are failing.
