### 4. physiological
> A physiological adaptation is a process inside the body. The kangaroo's kidneys reabsorb most of the water from the filtrate, so its urine is very concentrated, and it loses little water getting rid of waste. It also gets much of its water from the plants it eats. Koalas have their own: large amounts of liver enzymes break down the toxic chemicals in eucalypt leaves. And the mangrove's leaves have salt glands that push excess salt out, so you can see salt crystals on them.
