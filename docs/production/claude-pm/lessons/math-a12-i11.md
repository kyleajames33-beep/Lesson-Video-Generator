### 2. sideways strips: the integral of x, d y
> Cut the water into thin sideways strips, like layers. Each strip has thickness delta y, a small change in y. Its length is the x-value of the curve at that height. So each strip's area is about x times delta y. Add them up, make them thinner, and the area is the integral of x, d y, from y equals zero to y equals **one**. The limits are y-values, because the strips are stacked up the y-axis. To use it, write x in terms of y. If y is the natural log of x, then x is e to the y. So the area is the integral from zero to one, of e to the y, d y. That's e to the one, minus e to the zero, which is e minus **one**, about one point seven two square metres of water.
