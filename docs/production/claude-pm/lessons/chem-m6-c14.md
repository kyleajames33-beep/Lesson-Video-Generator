## Teaching checks

- **Does it explain causes?** Why the weak acid starts higher: it stays mostly un-ionised (2). Why the strong acid's pH stays low early: plenty of hydronium is still left (3). Why the weak acid levels off: base makes a buffer of the weak acid and its conjugate base (3). Why half-equivalence gives pKa: the partners are equal (4). Why both acids reach equivalence at the same volume: the titre counts moles, not strength (5). Why the equivalence pH differs: the salt (6, C13 callback).
