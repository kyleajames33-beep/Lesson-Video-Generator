### 6. random segregation, and the difference
> Then in anaphase one, the partners separate. That's segregation. In our model, each new cell gets exactly one partner from every pair, so one allele of each gene we're following: big A or small a, never both. Independent assortment is about the pairs together: one pair's choice doesn't affect another's.
