### 2. what counts as a species
> First, we need a test for a species. Biologists usually use this one: a species is a group that can interbreed in nature and produce fertile offspring. Fertile means the offspring can breed too. ### 3. gene flow holds a species together
> That's where gene flow comes in. Gene flow is alleles moving between populations when they interbreed. Picture finches spread across two nearby islands, flying back and forth and breeding together. If one island's birds start to differ, birds arriving from the other island mix their alleles back in, every generation. So gene flow keeps one shared set of alleles, one gene pool, and that keeps one species.
