### 2. one standard deviation each side: about 68%
> Fill in that band, from one standard deviation below the mean to one above, so z from minus one to one. Remember the thousand imagined loaves? Count the loaves **inside**. ### 3. two and three each side: about 95% and 99.7%
> Widen the band to two standard deviations each side, z from minus two to two. That's seven hundred to seven twenty grams, and it holds about ninety-five **percent**. Widen it to three each side, six ninety-five to seven twenty-five, and it holds about ninety-nine point seven percent, nearly every **loaf**. Each time, the band grows by the same five grams on each side, but it picks up less and less, because the tails are so thin. These three facts are called the empirical rule. Empirical means it comes from looking at real data. Sixty-eight, ninety-five, ninety-nine point seven, for one, two and three standard deviations **each side**.

### 5. halves, and the eight pieces
> The bell is symmetric, so every band splits evenly at the mean. Half of sixty-eight is thirty-four, so about thirty-four percent of loaves weigh between seven ten and seven **fifteen**. Next, the ring between one and two standard deviations. Ninety-five take away sixty-eight is twenty-seven, and that's both sides together, so each side gets thirteen point **five**. Between two and three, ninety-nine point seven take away ninety-five is four point seven, so each side gets two point three **five**. And beyond three standard deviations, a hundred take away ninety-nine point seven leaves zero point three, so each tail holds zero point one **five**. That's eight pieces, and together they make a hundred **percent**.
