## Memory aid check

- **"Together in time is a clue, not a cause."** (Claude's.) Assumption: two variables that change together, or in sequence, may share a cause, run in the opposite direction, or coincide; a time pattern is consistent with a cause but cannot establish it without an intervention or other evidence (true). Limit: time order does rule some explanations out (an effect cannot come before its cause); the aid does not say timing is useless. Said in scene 7 after the data are read.
- **"Change the hormone, watch the variable."** Assumption: an intervention that changes only the hormone, with other conditions controlled, tests whether it causes the change (true). Limit: in a whole body other hormones respond too (glucagon falls when insulin is given), so controlled experiments hold other things steady where they can (author note).
- **"A glucose graph can't see insulin."** Assumption: blood glucose readings do not measure insulin concentration (true). Limit: patterns of glucose can suggest how well insulin is working, which is why the data "support part of it" in 11d.
