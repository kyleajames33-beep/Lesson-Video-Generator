### 4c. two kinds of feedback (what the words mean)

### 5. the Explain answer (one stage at a time)
> Here is an Explain answer. First, the negative feedback. During pregnancy, the corpus luteum and then the placenta keep progesterone high. This is negative feedback, because high progesterone reduces the pituitary's release of FSH and LH, so no follicles mature and no egg is released. Second, the positive feedback. At birth, the baby's head stretches the cervix, which makes the pituitary release oxytocin. This is positive feedback, because oxytocin strengthens the contractions, which stretch the cervix more and release more oxytocin. It continues until the baby is born, when the stretch stops.

### 8a. feedback, item one

If you said FSH and LH would rise, check which way the brake pushes.

### 10. key notes (quiet, about 60 seconds)
> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- FSH (from the pituitary) makes a follicle grow, and a surge of LH makes it release its egg. Progesterone returning in the blood turns FSH and LH down. This is negative feedback: progesterone acts back on the pituitary and turns the process down. In pregnancy the corpus luteum (kept going by hCG) and then the placenta keep progesterone high, so no new follicle grows and no egg is released.
- Progesterone helps keep the uterus muscle calm. Late in pregnancy the placenta makes more oestrogen, which makes the muscle react more strongly to oxytocin.
- At birth, the baby's head stretches the cervix, nerve signals reach the brain, and the pituitary releases oxytocin, so the muscle contracts harder and stretches the cervix more. This is positive feedback: the stronger contractions add to the stretch that caused them. It ends when the baby is born and the stretch stops.
- Prolactin makes the breast produce milk, and oxytocin squeezes the milk out. Both are released when the baby suckles. High progesterone and oestrogen from the placenta hold milk production back in pregnancy.
- Negative feedback opposes a change (here it turns the process down). Positive feedback adds to a change (here it pushes the process further). Neither word means good or bad.
- Memory aid (large): Oxytocin squeezes (uterus and breast). Prolactin is pro-lactation. Stretch, oxytocin, squeeze, more stretch.
