### 5. wavelength: match to match
> No. On a smooth, regular wave like this one, that's only half a wavelength. The wavelength, called lambda, is the length of one complete wave. Measure it between two matching points, points at the same stage of their up and down motion, like a crest and the next **crest**. Careful: two points at the same height don't match if one is moving up and the other is moving down. On a ruler, three crests sit at the one point two oh, one point nine five and two point seven oh metre marks. One point nine five minus one point two oh is zero point seven five metres. The next gap is zero point seven five too, so the wavelength is zero point seven five **metres**.
