### 8. one signal, another resource
> Knowledge can link two organisms, too. A plant flowers at about the same time each year, because its flowering responds to seasonal cues like temperature and day length. If another resource is ready at that same time, the flowers become a signal. The plant doesn't cause the other event. Both respond to the same season. In the Torres Strait, knowing the winds, tides and seasons tells people when turtles and dugongs are easiest to find.

## Numbers or facts to verify

Self-check (23 Nov). The Codex science check must confirm each before voicing.
- **Cycads (3, 4).** *Macrozamia* (and *Cycas*) seeds are starch-rich and contain toxic glycosides (cycasin, macrozamin) as a chemical defence; the toxins are water-soluble; seeds were processed by prolonged leaching in running water (days to weeks), or by ageing and fermentation, then ground and cooked, in northern and eastern Australia (site; Beaton 1982). Spoken "Some Australian cycads"; "days or weeks". Correct.
- **Fish-stunning plants (5).** Several Australian plants were used to stun fish, including *Tephrosia* species (rotenoids, which block cellular respiration and are absorbed across fish gills) and saponin-containing plants; used in still pools or dammed sections of creek in northern Australia (site; Isaacs 1987; Low 1990). Spoken "In many parts of northern Australia". The chemicals' role as anti-herbivore defences is the standard interpretation. Correct at Year 11.
- **Bogong moth (6).** *Agrotis infusa* breeds over winter on inland plains of Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria; adults migrate up to about 1,000 km in spring to cool caves and crevices in the Australian Alps, aestivate through summer and return in autumn; high in fat; harvested in the high country in summer (site; Flood 1980; Warrant and colleagues 2016). Listed as Endangered on the IUCN Red List in 2021 (site). Spoken "the mountains of south-eastern Australia" (no named range). Correct.
- **Eels (7).** Short-finned eels (*Anguilla australis*) are catadromous: they grow in fresh water and migrate to the sea to spawn, in large numbers at predictable times (autumn, often with rain). Stone channels, weirs and holding ponds built to divert, trap and hold eels; parts dated to at least about 6,600 years ago (site; McNiven and colleagues 2012). Spoken "Parts of one system are more than six thousand years old" (place and People not named; owner rule). Correct.
- **Flowering and seasonal indicators (8).** Flowering time is cued by temperature, day length and rainfall, and is an adaptation that matches reproduction to pollinators and conditions (site b2). Using flowering to time another harvest relies on a shared seasonal cue, not causation (site b2). The wattle is a general example; no specific calendar pairing is claimed (the right frame says "a general example"). Torres Strait Islander Peoples' knowledge of winds, tides and seasons guides when green turtles and dugongs are found and hunted (widely published in general terms; the science check confirms the general statement; fallback: "knowledge of seasons tells people when turtles and dugongs are easiest to find").
- **Using well (10).** Consent, acknowledgement and benefit sharing are the standard principles for using Indigenous knowledge in research and commerce (for example the Nagoya Protocol's prior informed consent and benefit sharing; AIATSIS Code of Ethics). Not named on screen. Correct as principles.
- **Attempt 1 (11a).** Tea tree (*Melaleuca alternifolia*) leaf oil is antimicrobial (terpinen-4-ol), and crushed leaves were used on wounds and skin infections in north-eastern New South Wales; essential oils in leaves are generally interpreted as defences against herbivores and microbes. The stem names no species or place. Science check: confirm "protects the leaves from infection" is a fair statement of the oil's role (fallback: "which helps protect the plant").
- **Attempt 2 (11c).** Barramundi move to estuaries and river mouths to spawn at the start of the wet season (true in general; the stem says "a fish species"). Original scenario.
