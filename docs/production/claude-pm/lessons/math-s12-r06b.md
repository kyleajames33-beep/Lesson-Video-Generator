### 1. hook: how many tins?
> Here's a bedroom on a one to a hundred plan, three point eight centimetres by three point two, with a door and a window. The ceiling is two point four metres high. The walls need two coats of paint, and the paint comes in four litre tins. How many tins should you buy? Buy too few, and you're back at the shop halfway through. Buy too many, and you've wasted money. So how much paint do the walls need, and which paint is better **value**?

### 2. unfold the walls
> First, scale the sides. At one to a hundred, the room is three point eight metres by three point two **metres**. Now picture the four walls unfolded and laid flat, side by side. They make one long rectangle. Its length is the distance all the way around the room, called the perimeter. Three point eight plus three point two plus three point eight plus three point two is fourteen **metres**. Its height is the ceiling height, two point four metres. So the walls cover fourteen times two point four, thirty-three point six square **metres**.
