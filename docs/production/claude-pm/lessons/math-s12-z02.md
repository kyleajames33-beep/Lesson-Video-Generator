### 4. the formula
> What did we just do? We found the distance from the mean, then divided by one standard deviation. In symbols, z equals x minus mu, all over sigma. Here x is the value, mu is the mean, and sigma is the standard deviation. Always take the value minus the mean, in that order, and the sign comes out right by itself. Quick check. A test has a mean of eighty and a standard deviation of four. A mark of eighty-eight is eight above, and eight divided by four is a z-score of **two**.

## Teaching checks

- **Does it explain causes?** Why the spread is the right ruler: 8 g is nothing special if loaves vary by 20 g and huge if they vary by 1 g (1).
