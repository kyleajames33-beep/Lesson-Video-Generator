### 6. key-notes (reuse take: key-notes; quiet, about 60 seconds)
> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- Plan any strategy evaluation with four questions: which link does it break? What is the proof, and could something else explain the result? Who pays, and who decides? When does the verdict hold (the condition)?
