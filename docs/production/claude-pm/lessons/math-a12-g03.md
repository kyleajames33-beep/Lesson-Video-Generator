### 4. probability words and their models
> Probability questions work the same way. If the question lists the values with their probabilities, it's a discrete distribution, and the probabilities add to **one**. If it gives a density function, probabilities are areas under it, and the total area is one. If every value in an interval is equally likely, the density is flat, one over the width of the interval. And if it says normally distributed, with a mean and a standard deviation, the curve is a bell, and the empirical rule or z-scores give the **probabilities**. Here's a test.
