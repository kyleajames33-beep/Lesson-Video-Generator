### 2. four tests for choosing
> The syllabus lets you pick the third condition, so pick with four tests, in order. One, can you state a trend: rising, falling or flat? Two, can you name risk and protective factors with a mechanism, a reason each one changes the chance? Three, can you say where and for whom it's changing? And four, does it contrast with the other two? A condition that behaves just like heart disease gives you nothing to compare. They're the same kind of tests you used to choose a group. Choose for contrast, because comparison is what the long answers ask for.

### 7. three conditions, four axes
> Now compare all three on four axes, and each axis is a paragraph. Mortality against morbidity: heart disease and cancer kill, diabetes mostly disables. Prevention against treatment: heart disease is prevention, cancer is treatment, and diabetes sits between. Age: all three rise with age, but diabetes is moving younger.
