### 2. what an ecosystem is
> An ecosystem is all the living things in an area, plus their non-living surroundings, and the ways they affect each other. The living parts are biotic: plants, animals, fungi. The non-living parts are abiotic: light, temperature, soil moisture, pH. The conditions decide what can live here, and living things change the conditions too. These trees make the shade.

### 11. improving the investigation

> Here are the key notes. Pause the video if you need more time to copy them.

- Ecosystem: the living community (biotic) + the non-living surroundings (abiotic) + their effects on each other.
- Method: permission → two contrasting zones → random sampling points → at each point, abiotic readings (light meter, lux; soil moisture meter; thermometer; soil pH meter; three readings, averaged, same hour) and biotic records (1 m quadrat: plants present, percentage cover).
