## Numbers or facts to verify

- **Daphne Major (scenes 4, 5).** Daphne Major is a small island (about 0.34 km²) in the Galápagos; Peter and Rosemary Grant and colleagues measured and banded nearly all its medium ground finches (Geospiza fortis) from 1973. The G. fortis population fell from about 1,200 to about 180 (about 85 per cent; Boag and Grant 1981, Science 214: 82 to 85). Survivors had deeper beaks on average; offspring hatched in 1978 had deeper beaks on average (commonly cited as about 4 per cent).
