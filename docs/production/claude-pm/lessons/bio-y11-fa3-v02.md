## Numbers or facts to verify

Self-check (3 Nov). The Codex science check must confirm each before voicing.
- **Daphne Major (scenes 4, 5).** Daphne Major is a small island (about 0.34 km²) in the Galápagos; Peter and Rosemary Grant and colleagues measured and banded nearly all its medium ground finches (Geospiza fortis) from 1973. Beak depth is highly heritable (offspring-parent regression; Boag and Grant). In the 1977 drought, small soft seeds were depleted first; the large hard fruits of caltrop (Tribulus cistoides) remained, which only large-beaked birds crack efficiently. The G. fortis population fell from about 1,200 to about 180 (about 85 per cent; Boag and Grant 1981, Science 214: 82 to 85). Survivors had deeper beaks on average; offspring hatched in 1978 had deeper beaks on average (commonly cited as about 4 per cent). Spoken: "most of the island's finches died" and "the average beak got deeper"; the grey tags carry "about 85%" and "about 4%" and are removed if the check cannot confirm them (no narration change).
- **The reversal (scenes 7, 8).** The 1982 to 1983 El Niño brought very heavy rain; small-seeded plants increased and large seeds became relatively scarce. In the 1985 drought that followed, selection favoured smaller body and beak size in G. fortis (Gibbs and Grant 1987, Nature 327: 511 to 513). "A few years later" and "a very wet year" (spoken) avoid exact years; the screen chip says "1983: very wet". "Smaller-beaked finches handled them faster" is the proposed mechanism (small seeds are handled more efficiently by smaller beaks); science check to confirm the wording, fallback "smaller-beaked finches did better on small seeds".
- **Myxoma (scene 9).** Myxoma virus was released in Australia in 1950 (after earlier trials); spread by mosquitoes and, from the late 1960s, by introduced European rabbit fleas. Initial case fatality above 99 per cent (about 99.8 per cent). Genetic resistance in wild rabbits was detected within about seven years in field trials (Fenner and Ratcliffe 1965); Alves and colleagues (Science 2019) found parallel changes in immunity genes in Australian, British and French rabbits. "Fleas" is spoken alongside mosquitoes; the science check confirms that "mosquitoes and fleas carried it" is acceptable as a whole-history summary (fallback: "Mosquitoes carried it").
- **Virulence fell (scene 10).** Within a few years of release, moderately attenuated strains (grades III to IV) dominated in the field, because rabbits infected with them survived long enough to infect more mosquitoes (Fenner). Correct. "A drought can't evolve" is true.
- **Competition (scene 11).** Food competition among finches favouring efficient seed handlers is standard and consistent with the site's competition card. Correct.
