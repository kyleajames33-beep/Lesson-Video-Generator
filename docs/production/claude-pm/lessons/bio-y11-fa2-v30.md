### 9. cortisol: make new fuel, save glucose

That keeps glucose up after the glycogen runs **out**.

### 10. cortisol's costs, and its off switch
> Cortisol does more than raise glucose. It damps down inflammation, and slows jobs that can wait, like growth and repair. It's detected by the hypothalamus and the pituitary, which then release less CRH and ACTH.
