### 7. job two: stay upright
> Second job: hold itself up without a skeleton. This is where the cell wall and the vacuole work as a team. When the vacuole is full, it presses the cell outwards against the wall, and the wall pushes back, so the cell becomes firm instead of bursting. That outward push is called **turgor pressure**. Millions of firm cells pressing on each other hold a leaf up.

### 10. the mitochondria trap

One way to remember it: a plant cell runs a kitchen and a furnace, and only the kitchen closes at night.

### 11. how to write a contrast
> So what does an exam mean by contrast, based on the function of the cell? Contrast means differences, stated for both cells. And based on function means every difference needs its reason. Plant cells have a cellulose wall, animal cells don't, because the plant relies on turgor for support. Green plant cells have chloroplasts, animal cells don't, because the plant makes its own food. Plant cells have one large central vacuole, animal cells have small ones, because that vacuole stores sap and keeps turgor up.
