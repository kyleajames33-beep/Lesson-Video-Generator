### 5. what goes wrong
> In cystic fibrosis, both copies of the CFTR gene are altered. The most common change removes one amino acid, so the protein **folds** the wrong way, and the cell breaks it down before it reaches the membrane. With too few channels, less chloride leaves the cells, so less water follows. The mucus dries out and turns **thick** and sticky. The cilia can't clear it, bacteria stay trapped, and the lungs get repeated **infections**.

### 6. effect, not cause; inherited, not caught
