### 2. what advocacy is
> That's **advocacy**: speaking or acting to change the conditions, decisions or policies that affect health, your own or other people's. Every personal skill so far works on what a person does. Advocacy works on what they're doing it within, and it's how young people push for the regulation, services and environments you met earlier. So it isn't health education, which teaches someone something. It isn't awareness-raising, which makes an issue visible but asks nobody to act. And it isn't personal behaviour change, which aims at column one. It also runs two ways. **Self-advocacy** is acting for your own health, like asking a doctor for a referral. Advocacy for others acts for people who can't easily act for themselves.

### 4. various sources
> To examine a campaign, the syllabus says use various sources, and various means different kinds, not three news articles. Each kind fails in its own way.
