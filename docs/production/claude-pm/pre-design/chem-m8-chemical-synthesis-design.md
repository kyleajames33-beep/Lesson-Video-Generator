## 3. Number and science check

Arithmetic reproduces:918/1010=90.891%;895/987=90.679%. Aspirin masses agree to rounding with [CIAAW](https://ciaaw.org/atomic-weights.htm). General atom economy requires stoichiometric coefficients:ν(desired)M(desired)/Σν(products)M(products). Summing molar masses without coefficients fails for multiple soap units.

**Site error L16:** saponification is called base-catalysed while net reaction consumes 3NaOH. Hydroxide is stoichiometric reactant, not regenerated catalyst.

**Site error L16:** Pfizer story says palladium replaced titanium and reduced metal waste 85%. [EPA2002 sertraline award](https://www.epa.gov/greenchemistry/presidential-green-chemistry-challenge-2002-greener-synthetic-pathways-award) describes solubility/equilibrium-based TiCl₄ elimination and separate catalyst/solvent improvements. It does not support the page's one-step causal story or 85% metal-waste figure. Glycerol's market value does not itself change theoretical atom economy.
