## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

**L10 order:** Connect: predict which ethanol route wins; Explain: industrial hydration and equilibrium; Represent: substitute a haloalkane under reflux; Respond: balance fermentation and justify its conditions; Feedback: compare purity, rate and sustainability; Apply: build equations and evaluation responses.

**L10 key ideas:** 1. Brazil vs. Germany: Two Factories, One Molecule.
2. You met alkene hydration in L06 as a reaction type, now you examine it as an industrial process, which means thinking about equilibrium, yield, scale, and the economic context that determines whether this reaction is worth running at all.
3. Alkene Hydration, Industrial Context & Equilibrium.
4. Haloalkanes are the most versatile building blocks in organic synthesis, and one of their most important transformations is nucleophilic substitution with hydroxide ion, which converts a C-X bond directly into a C-OH bond without needing a double bond at all.
5. Substitution of Haloalkanes, Making Alcohols from Halides.
6. Fermentation is the oldest chemical process humans have used deliberately, and it works because yeast cells contain an enzyme (zymase) that catalyses the conversion of glucose to ethanol under conditions impossible to replicate in an industrial pressure reactor.
7. Fermentation, Biological Production of Ethanol.
8. Choosing between hydration, haloalkane substitution, and fermentation is not a chemistry question, it is an engineering, economic, and environmental question that chemistry informs but does not answer alone.

## 3. Number and science check

Fermentation C6H12O6 ->2C2H5OH +2CO2 conserves all atoms. Yeast enzymes catalyse it and are not consumed reactants. Aqueous NaOH/reflux substitutes halide with OH; ethanolic hydroxide promotes elimination. Site hydration conditions are approximately300 degrees C,65 atm and H3PO4, with approximately5% single-pass conversion. Recycling explains high overall conversion;5% is not overall yield. These operating figures are process examples, not physical constants.
