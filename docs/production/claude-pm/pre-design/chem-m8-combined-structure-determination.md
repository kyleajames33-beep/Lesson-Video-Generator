## 3. Number and science check

Nominal sums:ethanol 24+6+16=46;acid 24+4+32=60;pentane 60+12=72.46−15=31;72−15=57. C counts3/4 are correct.

**Site error L15:** answer says molecular mass fixes molecular formula. It constrains it: C₂H₆O and CH₂O₂ both have nominal 46. Other evidence is necessary. Worked A does combine methods correctly.

**Incomplete unknown C:** card gives alternative C counts without stating the unknown's measured count; no unique isomer follows. Branching alone does not justify a guaranteed relative 57 intensity. Check measured [NIST pentane](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C109660&Mask=200) and [2-methylbutane](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C78784&Mask=200) before adopting that comparison.
