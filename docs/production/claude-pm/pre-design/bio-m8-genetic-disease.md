## 3. Number and science check

For two heterozygous CFTR carriers, a Punnett square gives 1/4 affected, 1/2 carriers and 1/4 with two working alleles per pregnancy. These are probabilities, not guaranteed family totals. **Site correction:** L07 says PKU was "usually diagnosed at 2 to 4 years" before newborn screening, when intellectual disability became obvious. [US NICHD](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/pku/conditioninfo/symptoms) states that untreated children show symptoms by 3 to 6 months; a [National Academies review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/n/nap10839/pdf/) reports severe intellectual disability by about 1 year. Historical diagnosis ages varied. Remove the unsupported "usually 2 to 4 years" claim and explain that screening enables treatment before early damage. This finding is not yet in the [Module 8 correction log](../site-corrections-m8.md).

## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Trace CFTR variant to altered chloride transport, thick mucus and symptoms; contrast PKU enzyme deficiency. NESA feedback: 2019 Q23 requires mechanism and outcome rather than a disease label alone.
