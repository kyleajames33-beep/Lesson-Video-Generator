## 3. Number and science check

Propene gives [-CH2-CH(CH3)-]n; [-CH2-CH(CN)-]n recovers CH2=CHCN. PTFE [-CF2-CF2-]n recovers CF2=CF2. Site error: after restoring C=C, replacing continuation bonds with H overfills valence. Remove chain connections and restore the double bond without adding H. Site also calls PTFE surface devoid of polar groups; C-F bonds are polar. Low surface energy does not mean nonpolar C-F bonds. Site LDPE density0.91 to0.93g/cm3 and melting110 degrees C versus HDPE0.94 to0.97 and130; branch frequency about every50 carbons. Bond energies C-F485,C-H413,C-Cl339,C-O360kJ/mol and260-degree service/degradation statement need named reference checking before reuse. [Chemours PTFE data](https://www.chemours.com/en/-/media/files/teflon/teflon-ptfe-resins-product-info.pdf?rev=b94a4e250efa48adbac1be35ecd574c3) lists260 degrees C as continuous-use temperature and327 degrees C as melting peak. Correct the site implication that260 is a universal sudden decomposition onset. Grade-dependent ranges and operating limits are not fixed physical constants.
