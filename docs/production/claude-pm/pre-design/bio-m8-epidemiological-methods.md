## 3. Number and science check

Statin case: 100/5,000 = 2%, 150/5,000 = 3%, relative risk 0.667, absolute risk reduction 1 percentage point, relative reduction 33.3%, NNT 100 over five years. Tamoxifen: 89 versus 175 cases among 13,388 women; 49.1% relative case reduction. Equal group size is approximate, so the 1.3-point absolute reduction and NNT about 78 are approximate. ## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Cohort and case-control are observational; random allocation strengthens causal treatment claims. Judge sample, confounders and data, not labels alone. NESA feedback: 2021 Q31 and 2023 Q27 require judging the study design, not repeating definitions of validity and reliability. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
