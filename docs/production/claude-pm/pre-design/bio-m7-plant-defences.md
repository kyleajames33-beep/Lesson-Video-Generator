## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Physical barriers and antimicrobial chemicals; local hypersensitive cell death; whole-plant signalling; Banksia and Phytophthora cinnamomi. Cell death can restrict a pathogen that relies on living cells; it is not simply loss of the entire plant. Site contrast is structural defences versus induced responses. Practice identifies and explains a named plant response.

## 3. Number and science check

No worked arithmetic. P. cinnamomi is a water mould (oomycete), not a true fungus. The exact Banksia species must be named and a species-specific response sourced before claiming this meets the named-plant investigation. Generic plant defence mechanisms alone do not verify that this Banksia uses every listed response against P. cinnamomi.
