## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

ABA prompts K⁺ loss from guard cells, water leaves and the pore closes. Transpiration is the water-loss process, not the corrective response. NESA homeostasis questions such as 2019 Q29 require the internal variable and mechanism; adapt that check to guard cells and leaf water loss. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
