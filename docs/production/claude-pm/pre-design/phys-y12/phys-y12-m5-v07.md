## 6. Two original candidate questions and worked answers

### Question 1

At14.0 m/s, find both level-ground angles that give10.0 m range with g=9.8 m/s squared.

**Worked answer:** sin2theta=10 times 9.8/196=.5. Thus2theta=30 or150 degrees, so theta=15 or75 degrees.

### Question 2

Find the minimum speed needed to reach a target20.0 m away at the same height, ignoring drag and using g=9.8 m/s squared.

**Worked answer:** Maximum sin2theta=1 at 45 degrees, so umin=sqrt(Rg)=sqrt196=14.0 m/s. Lower speed would require sin2theta greater than 1.
