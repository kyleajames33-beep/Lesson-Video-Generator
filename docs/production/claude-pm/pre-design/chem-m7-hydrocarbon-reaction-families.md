## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

**L05 order:** Connect: classify the hydrocarbon reaction families; Explain: oxygen supply controls combustion products; Represent: saturated hydrocarbons substitute; Respond: unsaturated hydrocarbons add; Feedback: select the reaction from bonds and evidence; Apply: equations, observations and implications.

**L05 key ideas:** 1. Combustion is the most economically important hydrocarbon reaction, but it is also the one students answer too vaguely.
2. Alkanes are relatively unreactive because they contain only strong sigma bonds and no electron-rich multiple bond.
3. The double bond is the reactive feature that distinguishes alkenes from alkanes.
4. Use this sequence whenever a question gives you a structural formula and a reagent list.
5. A chemist carries out four experiments and records the observations below.

**Worked problems:** Problem: Ethene is bubbled through bromine water. Explain the observation and write the equation. 1 Problem: Draw or describe the repeating unit formed when propene polymerises. 1 Problem: Write the complete combustion equation for butane, C 4 H 10 . 1

**L05 practice:** Question types include the page’s retrieval, application and practice tasks. Representative questions: “Before reading on, predict what kind of hydrocarbons could produce each observation. What does each test tell you about the bonding inside the molecule?” “What are the products of complete combustion of a hydrocarbon?”

**Memory aids:** No named mnemonic was found in the extract. The site uses comparisons, reaction maps and retrieval prompts. Do not invent a new memory aid from these notes.

## 3. Number and science check

Butane combustion: 2C4H10 + 13O2 -> 8CO2 + 10H2O, with C8 H20 O26 on both sides. Ethene + Br2 -> C2H4Br2 conserves atoms. Propene gives [-CH2-CH(CH3)-]n, retaining methyl substituents. Methane chlorination gives CH3Cl and HCl under UV. No numerical worked calculation. L08 adds reaction-condition diagnosis.
