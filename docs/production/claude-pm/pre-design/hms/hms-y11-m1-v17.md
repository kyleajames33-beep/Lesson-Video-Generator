## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v16: Ottawa Charter and the socio-ecological approach.
- **Opens on:** the five-row Ottawa grid from v16 zooms out to a ring of seventeen goals (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v16).
- **Start:** Start from what the SDGs are (2015, seventeen goals, 2030) and what changed from the Millennium Development Goals.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can describe the goals, explain the health lens (health as a cause and a result of the other goals, with mechanisms), describe how the WHO uses the goals, and evaluate how Australia uses them (reporting against versus acting because of). Local application belongs to v18.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v18: opens on the SDG ring from v17 shrinks onto a local council map.
- **Later homes:** Local application: hms-y11-m1-v18. Year 12 SDGs 3, 4, 10 and 11 in depth: hms-y12-m3-v19 and v20 (reuse the ring).
- **Recurring models:** intro:SDG ring (with mechanism links)
- **Cross-year overlap:** Year 12 recalls the ring in a sentence and develops four goals.
