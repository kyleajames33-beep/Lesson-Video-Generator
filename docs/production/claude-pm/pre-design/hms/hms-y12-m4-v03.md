Its mechanism is the stretch-shortening cycle : a muscle that is rapidly lengthened under load and then immediately shortened produces more force than the shortening action alone. The exercise chosen matters far less than the loading variables, and the same back squat can be a strength session, a hypertrophy session or a power session depending only on how it is prescribed.
