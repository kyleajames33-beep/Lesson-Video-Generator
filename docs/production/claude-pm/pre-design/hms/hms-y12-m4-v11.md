## 2. Site order, ideas, examples and practice

- **The three phases, and what each is actually for:** A yearly program exists because an athlete cannot be at their best all year, and pretending otherwise produces an athlete who is neither well prepared in March nor still standing in September. ## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- The mistake that undoes a taper Reducing intensity along with volume is the most common error, and it produces an athlete who is rested and slow. A taper drops the amount of work, not the quality of it. The second error is tapering for too long: at some point fatigue has cleared and further reduction only starts to remove the fitness the training built.
