## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** Stage 5 PDHPE (assumed, not re-taught): Health has physical, mental and social parts.; Stage 5 PDHPE (assumed, not re-taught): Personal health choices and influences on health (Stage 5 PDHPE).
- **Opens on:** a person living with a well-managed chronic condition who calls themselves healthy: are they?.
- **Start:** Open on one case the definitions disagree about (a fit, employed person with a lifelong condition). Define health as contested, then quote the WHO definition.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can state the WHO definition, give its main criticism (the word 'complete') and its defence (an aim for systems, not a test of individuals), place the biomedical, functional, WHO and holistic meanings on one narrow-to-wide line, explain five influences on a person's meaning, and compare two meanings on counts / leaves out / useful for. The dimensions are named only as what a definition counts; how they interact belongs to v02.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v02: opens on the meanings ladder from v01 with its WHO rung lit and the three dimension words (physical, mental, social) glowing, about to lift off as linked boxes.
- **Later homes:** Interacting dimensions and the continuum: hms-y11-m1-v02. Social and emotional wellbeing as an Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander concept returns in hms-y11-m1-v08 and hms-y12-m3-v02; this video introduces the term only, in general terms.
- **Recurring models:** intro:narrow-to-wide meanings line, intro:compare method (counts / leaves out / useful for)
- **Cross-year overlap:** None in Year 12 directly; the compare method recurs in every compare question.
