## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v02: Dimensions and circumstances.; hms-y11-m1-v11: Protective skills.
- **Opens on:** the nine skills from v10 and v11 turn inward: an audit sheet with your own name space (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v11).
- **Start:** Start from reflection as a method that ends in a change, not a diary entry.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can audit their health across the dimensions with evidence, sort what is in their control from the conditions around it (the agency boundary), write a course of action with five usable properties, and design for the intention-behaviour gap with if-then plans. The full self-regulation model belongs to hms-y11-m2-v21.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v13: opens on the agency boundary from v12: the third column (conditions) is where advocacy aims.
- **Later homes:** Self-regulation and implementation intentions in depth: hms-y11-m2-v21 (this video names the gap and one if-then plan only).
- **Recurring models:** intro:agency boundary (three columns), intro:intention-behaviour gap (named)
- **Cross-year overlap:** hms-y11-m2-v21 develops self-regulation; this video stops at naming the gap.
