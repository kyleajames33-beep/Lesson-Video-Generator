## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v17: The SDGs and their health lens.; hms-y11-m1-v16: Ottawa action areas.
- **Opens on:** the SDG ring from v17 shrinks onto a local council map (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v17).
- **Start:** Start from the painted school wall case (awareness) and the idea of localisation through local government.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can explain localisation and why councils matter, run the six-step method from a local need to a proposal (assets before deficits), work one local case, judge a proposal with five questions, and recognise SDG-washing.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m2-v01: opens on a knee joint at rest, then the same knee bending in a squat: what makes this possible?.
- **Later homes:** Year 12 application to a community: hms-y12-m3-v21 and v22.
- **Recurring models:** intro:six-step localisation method, reuse:SDG ring, reuse:Ottawa five action areas grid
- **Cross-year overlap:** Year 12 hms-y12-m3-v21 and v22 apply the four named goals to a community; they recall this method in one sentence.
