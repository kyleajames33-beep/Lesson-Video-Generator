## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- The most common error on this topic Writing that anxiety is always harmful and must be eliminated. It cannot be eliminated, and an athlete with no activation at all performs poorly. The goal is management: bring arousal towards the athlete's optimum for the task, and reinterpret the physical signs as readiness rather than as evidence that something is wrong.
- No NESA marking-trend claim is made.
