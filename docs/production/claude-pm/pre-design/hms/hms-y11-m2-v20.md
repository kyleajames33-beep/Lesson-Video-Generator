## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** Stage 5 PDHPE (assumed, not re-taught): Benefits of physical activity and reasons people participate.
- **Opens on:** the evidence ladder from v19 gives way to a new question with no ladder: why do people keep turning up? (the final frame of hms-y11-m2-v19).
- **Start:** Start from personal identity and what people turn up to.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can explain how identity affects participation and performance (including the risk of a strong athletic identity), intrinsic and extrinsic, positive and negative motivation (and the overjustification effect), and competence, autonomy and relatedness as the conditions for intrinsic motivation.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m2-v21: opens on the motivation grid from v20 is full, but a gap opens between intending and doing.
- **Later homes:** Self-regulation and behaviour change: hms-y11-m2-v21.
- **Recurring models:** intro:motivation two axes (intrinsic-extrinsic, positive-negative), intro:competence, autonomy, relatedness
- **Cross-year overlap:** None.
