## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- Re-injury risk is highest immediately after returning The period just after a return is when a further injury to the same site is most likely, which is why a graded return, reduced minutes, restricted role, no contact initially, is often better than a binary in-or-out decision. Returning is a process, not a switch.
