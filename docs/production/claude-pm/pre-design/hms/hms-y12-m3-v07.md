It is the process of developing and maintaining the ability to do the things a person has reason to value: to move around, to meet their own needs, to learn and decide, to build and keep relationships, and to contribute. - **Opportunities first, then challenges:** The challenges are real, and almost every one is a challenge of design rather than of demography. The number of older people is not the problem; a system, a housing stock and a workforce built for a different age structure is.
