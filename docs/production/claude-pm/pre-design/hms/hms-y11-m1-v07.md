## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v06: Generational claims need evidence.
- **Opens on:** the then-and-now board from v06 ends on a question mark: how would we find out what young people think, rather than guess? (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v06).
- **Start:** Start from one weak investigation (a student surveys her own team) and repair it step by step.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can write an answerable research question, choose between survey, interview and focus group by fit, name the ethical requirements (consent, withdrawal including data, confidentiality, harm), separate validity, reliability and credibility, and separate findings from conclusions and propose a further question. Physiological measurement is hms-y11-m2-v15.
