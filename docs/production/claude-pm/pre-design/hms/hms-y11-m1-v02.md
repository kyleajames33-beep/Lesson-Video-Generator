- **The health continuum, and what a position on it means:** The health continuum models health as a line rather than a switch. At one end sits optimal health; at the other, death. Everyone is somewhere on it, and nobody stays still. What the model buys you is the ability to describe direction and rate, not just position. - **How health changes over time, three timescales:** “Health changes over time” is true and, said on its own, worth nothing in an exam. What earns marks is naming which timescale, because the causes are different on each. Within a day or a week. Sleep, food, stress, a passing infection. Read it again: how an individual's circumstances affect their health . Circumstances are the conditions of a life, and most of them are not decisions. Income sets what housing, food and care are reachable. Where a person lives sets how far the nearest service is and whether getting there costs...

## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v01: The WHO definition and its dimensions (physical, mental, social); meanings of health are boundaries drawn for a purpose.
- **Opens on:** the meanings ladder from v01 with its WHO rung lit and the three dimension words (physical, mental, social) glowing, about to lift off as linked boxes (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v01).
- **Start:** Start from the dimensions named in v01 and show one change travelling between them (the hamstring loop).
- **Stop:** Stop when students can trace an interaction loop through the dimensions with a mechanism on every arrow, explain why 'good health' is relative to circumstances, expectations and life stage, place people on the health continuum with direction and rate, name the three timescales of change, and separate direct effects of circumstances from effects on the range of choices. Determinants as a named framework belong to v05.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v03: opens on the continuum from v02 multiplies into a crowd of markers: one person's health is a point, a population's is a pattern.
- **Later homes:** Population measures of health status: hms-y11-m1-v03. Determinants as five ordered groups: hms-y11-m1-v05.
- **Recurring models:** intro:dimension interaction loop, intro:health continuum with direction arrows, reuse:narrow-to-wide meanings line
- **Cross-year overlap:** None.
