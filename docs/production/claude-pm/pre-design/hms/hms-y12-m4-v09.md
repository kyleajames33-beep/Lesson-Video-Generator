## 2. Site order, ideas, examples and practice

- **The aim comes first, and it must be goal specific:** The syllabus lists the overview or aim of the session as goal specific, and that phrase is doing real work. A session aim is not a description of what the athletes will do. It is a statement of what the session is meant to change, expressed so that you can tell afterwards whether it worked. - **Health and safety considerations, and duty of care:** Health and safety is the sub-point students most often answer as a list of obvious precautions. It earns more marks when it is answered as a process : identify the hazards, judge each one, control it, and check the control is still there on the day. - **The warm-up, and what it is actually for:** A warm-up has two halves and they do different jobs. The general warm-up is low-intensity whole-body work that raises muscle and core temperature, heart rate and blood flow to working muscle. Warmer muscle contracts and relaxes faster and is more compliant, nerve conduction speeds up, and oxygen is released more readily to the tissue. Its clearest physiological justification is circulatory.
