## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- The claim to be careful with "Protein builds muscle" is true only with training and only up to adequacy. An answer stating that a higher protein intake produces greater muscle gain, independently of the training stimulus and above an adequate intake, is describing something the evidence does not support and is repeating marketing.
- No NESA marking-trend claim is made.
