## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y12-m3-v01: Reading national data and where inequities exist.; Year 11 HMS (recalled in one sentence; do not assume our Year 11 video was watched): Determinants interact.
- **Opens on:** the inequity rows from v01 enlarge: the first group (the final frame of hms-y12-m3-v01).
- **Start:** Start from the data in trend terms: gaps, some narrowing, some not.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can describe the data in trend terms, explain how determinants interact, explain colonisation and its continuing impacts as causes underpinning the inequity (in general terms, no named communities or people), and evaluate actions by principle (community control, cultural safety, self-determination, addressing determinants).
