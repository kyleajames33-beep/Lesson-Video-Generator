## 3. Number and science check

Figures the page states (to be independently checked in the design; current statistics must come from a dated report):
- A 100 metre sprint Maximal power for about ten seconds Muscular, by fuel.
- The stored phosphocreatine that powers maximal effort is depleted within roughly ten seconds, which is why the last twenty metres of a 100 metre race are run slower than the middle, by everyone.
- A 400 metre run Near-maximal effort for 45 to 60 seconds Muscular and circulatory together.
- Ice for around 20 minutes every two hours in the first 48 to 72 hours, wrapped rather than applied directly to skin.

- How to justify a limiting system, rather than guess one Do not choose the system that sounds most impressive. Choose it with evidence from the movement itself, in three steps. - **Stop:** Stop when students can follow a movement through all seven systems, identify the limiting system for different movements, analyse a movement in phases, and discuss the role and limits of first aid for inefficient movement, dehydration and undue stress on the body.
