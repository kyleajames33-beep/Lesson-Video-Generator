## 3. Number and science check

Figures the page states (to be independently checked in the design; current statistics must come from a dated report):
- Alveoli, roughly 300 million microscopic air sacs, where all gas exchange happens.
- Four features make them extraordinarily good at exchanging gas, and each one is a direct application of what makes diffusion fast: Enormous surface area, around 70 square metres in total, roughly the floor of a classroom, folded into your chest.
- At rest that is roughly 500 mL per breath at about 12 breaths per minute, or about 6 litres per minute.
- During maximal exercise a trained athlete can reach 150 litres per minute or more, a twenty-five-fold increase.
