## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v13: Advocacy, ask and audience, participation ladder.
- **Opens on:** the participation ladder from v13 stands beside three buildings: government, NGO, community (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v13).
- **Start:** Start from the three levels of government and what only government can do.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can explain the distinct powers and limits of government, non-government organisations and communities, judge impact as outcomes (not activity), and explain why all three are needed and what fails when one is missing ('for' versus 'by').
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v15: opens on the three-sector board from v14 lights one shared job: health promotion.
- **Later homes:** Health promotion approaches: hms-y11-m1-v15. Shared responsibility in the healthcare system: hms-y12-m3-v09.
- **Recurring models:** intro:three-sector powers and limits, intro:activity vs impact
- **Cross-year overlap:** Year 12 hms-y12-m3-v09 recalls the sectors for the health system.
