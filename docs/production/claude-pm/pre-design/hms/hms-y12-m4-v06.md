## 3. Number and science check

Figures the page states (to be independently checked in the design; current statistics must come from a dated report):
- That single sentence is the answer to card 01: the first two months worked because the load was new, and the next four failed because it was not.
- Below the aerobic training threshold, around 70 per cent of maximum heart rate, the stimulus is too small.
- Loads below roughly 65 per cent of maximum develop muscular endurance rather than maximal strength; loads at 85 per cent and above are the band in which maximal strength is developed.
- The same 8 km jog is above the threshold for a beginner and below it for a trained runner, which is why the same session is training for one person and recovery for the other.
