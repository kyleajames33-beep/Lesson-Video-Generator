## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- The two words are not interchangeable Marks are lost every year for using strategy and tactic as synonyms. Strategy is the plan for the contest; tactics are the moves that serve it. If a response says "the team's tactic was to play a possession game all match", it has described a strategy and called it a tactic. Fix it by asking: is this the whole approach, ...
