## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m2-v06: Endocrine control is slow and chemical.
- **Opens on:** the hormone timeline from v06 is slow; a much faster control line appears: the nervous system (the final frame of hms-y11-m2-v06).
- **Start:** Start from the nervous system as a branching tree (central and peripheral, then somatic and autonomic).
- **Stop:** Stop when students can trace a voluntary movement from motor cortex to muscle, explain how force is graded (motor units), explain feedback from proprioceptors and the reflex loop, explain the autonomic system's role in exercise, and name what makes neural control better or worse (myelin, fatigue, training).
