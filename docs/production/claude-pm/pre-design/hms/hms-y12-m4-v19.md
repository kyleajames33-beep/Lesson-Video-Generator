## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes (from the page)

- Keep the examples current This is a fast-moving area and any specific product named in a textbook dates quickly. What does not date is the framework: which of the three categories the technology belongs to, what mechanism it acts through, and what its limitations are. Learn the framework and you can apply it to a device that does not exist yet.
- No NESA marking-trend claim is made.
