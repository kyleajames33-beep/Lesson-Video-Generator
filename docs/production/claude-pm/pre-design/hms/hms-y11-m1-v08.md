## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v03: Health measures and trends.; hms-y11-m1-v05: Determinants and inequity.
- **Opens on:** the research cycle from v07 closes on its conclusion box, which becomes the first finding: young people are the healthiest age group (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v07).
- **Start:** Start from the paradox: low mortality and chronic disease, yet injury, mental health and risk behaviours dominate.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can read trends (direction, not level), separate risk and protective factors (not mirror images), use the determinants to explain behaviours, and explain inequities experienced by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander young people with historical and structural causes and protective factors, in general terms only.
