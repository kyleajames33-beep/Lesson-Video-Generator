## 3. Number and science check

Figures the page states (to be independently checked in the design; current statistics must come from a dated report):
- Each one degrades the others, so a problem in one place shows up somewhere else entirely, which is exactly why the athlete in card 01 has three different specialists each seeing a different symptom.
- A routine is a sequence that runs the same way every time, so the behaviour does not depend on how motivated the athlete feels at 9 pm after a hard session.
- The athlete in card 01 does not have a knowledge problem, they almost certainly know they should eat better and sleep more.
- A rising resting heart rate, worsening wellness scores and reported poor sleep all appearing together is a much stronger signal than any one of them alone, and it is also exactly the pattern the athlete in card 01 would produce.
