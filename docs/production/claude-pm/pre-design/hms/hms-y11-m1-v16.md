## 2. Site order, ideas, examples and practice

- **The five action areas, and what each one actually changes:** The Ottawa Charter was adopted in 1986 and remains the framework Australian health promotion is written around. Its five action areas are not five kinds of campaign. They are five different levels at which you can intervene, and the reason the framework endures is that a strategy using only one of them is usually the one that fails. ## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v15: The socio-ecological approach and the WHO definition of health promotion.
- **Opens on:** the socio-ecological approach from v15 expands into five action areas (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v15).
- **Start:** Start from the failed outdoor gym case and diagnose it with one action area used out of five.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can explain each of the five action areas by what it changes, distinguish them from the three strategies (enable, mediate, advocate), explain why one action area is never enough, describe partnerships with communities by who holds power, and evaluate an initiative by naming empty action areas.
