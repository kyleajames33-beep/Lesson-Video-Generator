## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** Stage 5 PDHPE (assumed, not re-taught): Practising and improving movement skills.
- **Opens on:** the debate scale from v15 tips towards one question: how do people actually learn movement skills? (the final frame of hms-y11-m2-v15).
- **Start:** Start from the characteristics of learners as filters on instruction.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can describe six learner characteristics, describe the cognitive, associative and autonomous stages by what the learner is doing, identify a stage from evidence (the error-detection test), explain why coaching reverses between stages, and apply this to recreational and elite athletes.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m2-v17: opens on the three-stage track from v16 gains four sliding scales beneath it.
- **Later homes:** Classifying skills: hms-y11-m2-v17. Skill instruction inside a session: hms-y12-m4-v10.
- **Recurring models:** intro:three stages of learning (with error-detection test)
- **Cross-year overlap:** Year 12 recalls the stages in one sentence.
