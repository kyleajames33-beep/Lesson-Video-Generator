## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m2-v01: Bones, synovial joints and joint actions.
- **Opens on:** the coded leg linkage from v01 holds at the bottom of the squat: something must be pulling (the final frame of hms-y11-m2-v01).
- **Start:** Start from the three muscle tissues, then the fact that skeletal muscle can only pull.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can name major muscles with their joint action, compare slow and fast twitch fibres, explain isotonic concentric, isotonic eccentric and isometric contractions (including lowering a bag slowly), and assign agonist, antagonist and stabiliser roles in one movement.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m2-v03: opens on the arm plate from v02: the forearm is a lever, and the elbow is its pivot.
- **Later homes:** Biomechanics: hms-y11-m2-v03. Fibre-type adaptations to training: hms-y12-m4-v08.
- **Recurring models:** intro:agonist / antagonist / stabiliser, intro:fibre-type comparison
- **Cross-year overlap:** Year 12 hms-y12-m4-v08 recalls fibre types in one sentence.
