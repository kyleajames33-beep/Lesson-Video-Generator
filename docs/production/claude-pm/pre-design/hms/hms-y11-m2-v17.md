- Here is the answer to Card 01, and it is not nerves. Shooting 45 from 50 unopposed from one spot is a closed practice. Shooting in a match is an open skill: a defender is closing, the pass arrived early, her feet are not where she chose. - The rule to take away. Classifying a skill is not the end of the task, it is the beginning. The classification is only worth making if you then say what it changes. In an exam, a classification stated without its practice implication earns roughly half of what the same classification earns with one.
