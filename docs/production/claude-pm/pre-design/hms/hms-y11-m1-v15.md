## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v05: Determinants and why behaviour-only campaigns widen gaps.; hms-y11-m1-v14: Sectors and impact.
- **Opens on:** the three-sector board from v14 lights one shared job: health promotion (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v14).
- **Start:** Start from the WHO definition of health promotion (enabling people to increase control over and improve their health) and the word 'control'.
- **Stop:** Stop when students can distinguish health promotion from health education and health care, name the biomedical, behavioural and socio-ecological approaches by the question each asks first, explain the evidence that forced each shift, name the global policies behind Australian practice (WHO, UNESCO examples), and describe the nature of health promotion as a system, using smoking as the worked case. The Ottawa Charter action areas belong to v16.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v16: opens on the socio-ecological approach from v15 expands into five action areas.
- **Later homes:** Ottawa Charter and partnerships: hms-y11-m1-v16.
- **Recurring models:** intro:three approaches (biomedical, behavioural, socio-ecological)
- **Cross-year overlap:** None.
