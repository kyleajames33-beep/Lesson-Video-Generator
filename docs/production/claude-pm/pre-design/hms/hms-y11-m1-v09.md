## 6. Course coherence

- **Entry knowledge:** hms-y11-m1-v08: Trends, risk and protective factors for young people.
- **Opens on:** one trend line from v08 is picked out and enlarged: insufficient sleep on school nights (the final frame of hms-y11-m1-v08).
- **Start:** Start by narrowing a field (mental health) to a researchable issue (psychological distress in 15 to 19 year olds).
- **Stop:** Stop when students can run the seven syllabus questions on one issue (nature, data, why it is an issue on three grounds, protective factors, current strategies, a new strategy passing five tests, further questions), using insufficient sleep on school nights as the worked issue.
- **Next handoff:** hms-y11-m1-v10: opens on the seven-question frame from v09 shows its protective-factors row, which opens into four skills.
- **Later homes:** Skills that protect the individual, applied to the researched issue: hms-y11-m1-v10 and v11.
