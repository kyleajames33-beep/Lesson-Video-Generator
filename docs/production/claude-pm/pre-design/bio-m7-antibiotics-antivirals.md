## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Antibiotic bacterial targets; antivirals target viral enzymes/entry; selection of resistant variants; compare practical effectiveness and resistance time-series. Site cases: penicillin, aciclovir/remdesivir/tenofovir, oseltamivir, protease inhibitors, enfuvirtide/maraviroc and AZT/efavirenz. Practice explains mutation, selection and reproduction rather than purposeful adaptation.

**L16 page sequence:** When Howard Florey and Ernst Chain at Oxford treated Albert Alexander with penicillin in 1941, the drug worked by attacking the peptidoglycan cell wall of his infecting bacteria.; Annotated Diagram, Antibiotic Targets in a Bacterial Cell.; We just saw that every antibiotic targets a structure unique to bacteria.; We just saw that antibiotics can't touch viruses because viruses have no bacterial targets.; We just saw that both bacteria and viruses can develop drug resistance.; Mechanisms of Resistance.; The table shows the percentage of Staphylococcus aureus isolates resistant to key antibiotics in Australian hospitals over time.; Structured Data Analysis, Antibiotic Resistance Over Time.
