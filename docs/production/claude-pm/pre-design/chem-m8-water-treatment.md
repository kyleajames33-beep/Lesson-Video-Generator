## 3. Number and science check

Hydrolysis balances atoms/charge. Chlorine hydrolysis:Cl₂+H₂O⇌HOCl+H⁺+Cl⁻. Higher pH favours OCl⁻ relative to more active HOCl. No numeric pKa calculation is present. Chloramines may reduce THMs and provide residual protection, but fewer THMs does not mean fewer of every by-product. [ADWG](https://guidelines.nhmrc.gov.au/australian-drinking-water-guidelines) is the primary treatment source. ## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes
