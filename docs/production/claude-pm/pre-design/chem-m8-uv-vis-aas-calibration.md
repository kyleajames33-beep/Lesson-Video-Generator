## 3. Number and science check

AAS arithmetic:0.113/0.162×10=6.97531. Practice interpolation1+(0.201−0.161)/0.081×0.5=1.24691; ×5=6.23457, consistent with approximate graph readings. ε is exercise data, not a universal material constant.

**Site error L04:** ADWG lead is 0.005 mg L⁻¹, endorsed 2025, not 0.01. Correct comparison about 1,400-fold. [NHMRC v4](https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/attachments/publications/Australian-drinking-water-guidelines-6-Version%204.pdf). A discharge is not legally assessed by drinking-water limits.

**Site errors L08:** water/tissue ratios retain L kg⁻¹, so final 10,000 is not dimensionless. Field tissue/water data do not isolate water-only uptake required for BCF. [EPA definitions](https://www.epa.gov/expobox/exposure-assessment-tools-media-food) distinguish uptake routes. Methylmercury alone cannot establish compliance with a total-mercury limit, and one analyte cannot establish water is safe to drink. [NHMRC mercury](https://guidelines.nhmrc.gov.au/australian-drinking-water-guidelines/part-5/physical-chemical-characteristics/mercury) confirms total mercury 0.001 mg L⁻¹. Shark 2.0 exceeds the page comparator, but formal food compliance requires Schedule 19 sampling, not one fish.

## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Treating absorbance as concentration; axes reversed; intercept/blank or path length omitted; extrapolation; lost dilution factor; hydrated ions said to absorb in AAS; accumulation confused with trophic magnification. Research bank section 3,2023 Q31/2024 Q25.
