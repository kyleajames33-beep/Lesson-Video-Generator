## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Compare spontaneous generation and germ theory. Pasteur boiled broth, allowed air through a curved neck and prevented dust reaching broth; breaking or tilting the neck permitted contamination. Koch associated a specific organism with disease, isolated it, introduced it to a healthy susceptible host and re-isolated it. TB and guinea pigs are the site example. Practice applies the postulates and identifies their limits.

**L03 page sequence:** See what people believed before.; Follow Pasteur's swan-neck experiment.; Learn Koch's four postulates.; the postulates, then find their limits.; Consolidate the germ theory story.
