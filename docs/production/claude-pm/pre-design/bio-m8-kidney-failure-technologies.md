## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Healthy kidneys filter blood and regulate water and salts. Dialysis moves wastes across a membrane down a concentration gradient and removes excess water. NESA assistive-technology questions such as 2024 Q35 require the lost organ function and how the device compensates. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
