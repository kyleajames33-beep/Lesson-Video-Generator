PROBLEM 3 · EXPANDING BRACKETS Expand and simplify $(\sqrt{5} + 2)(\sqrt{5} - 3)$. 1 $= \sqrt{5}\sqrt{5} - 3\sqrt{5} + 2\sqrt{5} - 6$ Multiply every term by every term, as with any binomial product. 2 $\sqrt{5}\sqrt{5} = 5$ A surd times itself gives the number under the root. 3 $= 5 - \sqrt{5} - 6 = -1 - \sqrt{5}$ Collect the like surds and the rational terms separately.

PROBLEM 1 · SINGLE SURD DENOMINATOR Rationalise the denominator of $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3}}$. 1 $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{3}} \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}$ Multiply by 1, written as the surd over itself. 2 $= \dfrac{6\sqrt{3}}{3}$ The denominator becomes $\sqrt{3}\sqrt{3} = 3$. 3 $= 2\sqrt{3}$ Cancel 6 over 3. Always finish by simplifying.

### Source algebraic-techniques-and-inequalities/lesson08.sa.html, `we-2`

PROBLEM 2 · USING THE CONJUGATE Rationalise the denominator of $\dfrac{4}{2 + \sqrt{3}}$. 1 Conjugate of $2 + \sqrt{3}$ is $2 - \sqrt{3}$ Reverse the middle sign only. 2 Denominator: $(2+\sqrt{3})(2-\sqrt{3}) = 4 - 3 = 1$ Use $a^2 - b$ directly. 3 Numerator: $4(2-\sqrt{3}) = 8 - 4\sqrt{3}$, so the answer is $8 - 4\sqrt{3}$ Expand the numerator; the denominator of 1 disappears.

### Source algebraic-techniques-and-inequalities/lesson08.sa.html, `we-3`

PROBLEM 3 · SURD OVER SURD Rationalise the denominator of $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}}$. 1 Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2}$ The conjugate of $\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}$. 2 Denominator: $\left(\sqrt{5}\right)^2 - \left(\sqrt{2}\right)^2 = 5 - 2 = 3$ Difference of two squares, both surds squared away. 3 Numerator: $\sqrt{2}\left(\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{2}\right) = \sqrt{10} + 2$, so the answer is $\dfrac{\sqrt{10} + 2}{3}$ Expand, using $\sqrt{2}\sqrt{2} = 2$.

## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Adding unlike surds (sqrt2 + sqrt3 is not sqrt5).
- Not taking the largest square factor (sqrt72 = 2 sqrt18 left unfinished).
- Using a + sqrt b as its own conjugate, or changing both signs.
- Multiplying only the denominator by the conjugate.
- Forgetting to simplify at the end (6 sqrt3 / 3 left as is).

These follow from the functions and official content, not claims about how often markers see them.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

### Question 1

Simplify sqrt48 - sqrt27 + sqrt12.

**Worked answer:** 4 sqrt3 - 3 sqrt3 + 2 sqrt3 = 3 sqrt3.

### Question 2

Rationalise the denominator of (sqrt6 + sqrt2)/(sqrt6 - sqrt2).

**Worked answer:** Multiply by (sqrt6 + sqrt2)/(sqrt6 + sqrt2): numerator 8 + 2 sqrt12 = 8 + 4 sqrt3, denominator 6 - 2 = 4. Answer 2 + sqrt3.
