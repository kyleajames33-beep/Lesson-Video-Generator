## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Reading f(x) as f times x.
- Squaring before applying the sign: (-1)^2 versus -1^2 without brackets.
- f(a + 1) written as f(a) + 1.
- Mixing up which variable is independent in context.

These follow from the functions and official content, not claims about how often markers see them.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

### Question 1

If g(x) = 3 - 2x^2, find g(-2) and g(a + 1).

**Worked answer:** g(-2) = 3 - 2(4) = -5. g(a + 1) = 3 - 2(a + 1)^2 = 1 - 4a - 2a^2.

### Question 2

A phone plan charges C(g) = 15 + 6g dollars a month, where g is extra data in GB. Find C(4), name the independent variable and say what C(0) means.

**Worked answer:** C(4) = 15 + 24 = 39 dollars. g is independent, C is dependent. C(0) = 15 is the monthly charge with no extra data.
