## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Order: bromine water for C=C in darkness; esterification/oxidation for alcohols; carbonate and limewater for carboxylic acids; overlapping tests; decision tree and controls.

Worked unknown: no carbonate fizz, bromine rapidly decolourises, dichromate orange→green. Careful worked card leaves unsaturated alcohol/aldehyde candidates. Practice X has no fizz, no bromine change, dichromate orange→green and is labelled primary/secondary alcohol. Y fizzes with hydrogencarbonate. Prediction combines fizz and bromine loss as acid plus C=C.

Equations:CH₂=CH₂+Br₂→CH₂BrCH₂Br;2CH₃COOH+Na₂CO₃→2CH₃COONa+H₂O+CO₂;CO₂+Ca(OH)₂→CaCO₃+H₂O; ethanoic acid+ethanol⇌ethyl ethanoate+water. No numeric calculation. Aid: test each group independently; one molecule may have several.

## 3. Number and science check

Equations balance. **Site errors L11:** acidified permanganate is paired with brown MnO₂. Distinguish acidic reduction to Mn²⁺ from neutral/alkaline MnO₂ conditions. [RSC practical](https://edu.rsc.org/experiments/unsaturation-test-with-potassium-manganatevii/550.article) describes its actual reagent and brown Mn(IV) observation.

The careful worked card recognises aldehyde interference, but Activity2/model answers again call dichromate confirmation of alcohol without excluding aldehydes. Dichromate reduction alone cannot prove OH. Rapid bromine-water loss in the dark is also not specific to C=C when aldehydes are candidates. Restrict candidates or correct the inference. No numeric constant needs validation.
