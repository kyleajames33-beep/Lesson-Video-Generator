## 3. Number and science check

If a town doubles from 10,000 to 20,000 and case count doubles, the rate is unchanged: cases/population has the same value. The page's table gives 640,000/19.2 million = 3.33% in 2000, 970,000/22.3 million = 4.35% in 2010 and 1.3 million/25.9 million = 5.02% in 2022. Its rounded crude prevalence values of 3.3%, 4.4% and 5.0% are consistent. The separate age-standardised figures are 4.1%, 4.3% and 4.2%; do not treat them as crude rates. [Module 8 correction log](../site-corrections-m8.md) records no verified correction.

## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Incidence counts new cases in a period; prevalence counts existing cases; mortality records deaths. Check population and time denominator. NESA feedback: 2024 Q29 requires graph scales and extrapolation to match the supplied data. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
