## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Humoral versus cell-mediated response; error spotting; CD4 helper versus CD8 cytotoxic cells; targeted apoptosis; HIV loss of helper-cell coordination. Helper cells release signals that coordinate other cells. Cytotoxic cells kill infected host cells rather than secreting antibodies or directly engulfing every pathogen.

**L12 page sequence:** Two arms, one system.; Error Spotting, Cell-Mediated Immunity.; CD4 versus CD8.; Killing with precision.; Losing the officers.

## 3. Number and science check

No worked arithmetic. CD4/CD8 are surface-marker names, not quantities to calculate. Site activation table associates CD8 activation with antigen on any nucleated cell plus helper signal. Finding: distinguish naive T-cell activation by professional antigen-presenting cells from recognition and killing of infected tissue cells by already activated cytotoxic cells.

Mechanism reference: [Immunobiology: production of armed effector T cells](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27118/) explains co-stimulation and professional antigen-presenting cells.
