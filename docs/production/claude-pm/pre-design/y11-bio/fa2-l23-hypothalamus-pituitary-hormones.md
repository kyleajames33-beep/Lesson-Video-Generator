## 2. Site lesson material

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year11/fa2-cells-to-systems/lesson23.sa.html` and `docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/bio-y11-fa2-l23.md`. Site section order: 1. The Hypothalamus, Brain Meets Hormones; 2. The Pituitary, The Master Gland; 3. Feedback Regulates the Whole Chain; 4. Why doctors test pituitary hormones to diagnose thyroid disease. Relevant ideas:

- The Hypothalamus, Brain Meets Hormones: The hypothalamus is the place where the brain's electrical signals are turned into hormonal commands, making it the bridge between the nervous and endocrine systems.
- The Pituitary, The Master Gland: The pituitary takes the hypothalamus's instructions and relays them to glands all over the body by secreting its own hormones into the blood.
- Feedback Regulates the Whole Chain: The hypothalamus and pituitary do not just switch hormone production on, they are switched off again by the very hormones they cause to be released, through negative feedback.
- Why doctors test pituitary hormones to diagnose thyroid disease: Because the pituitary and target glands are linked by feedback, doctors can read the whole chain from a blood test.

Practice demand: Site review sample: Which structure links the nervous and endocrine systems?

A blood test shows a patient has a high level of TSH together with a low level of thyroxine. A second patient has a low TSH together with a low thyroxine. Analyse what each result indicates about where the fault lies, and explain how the hypothalamus-pituitary-thyroid feedback loop makes this interpretation possible.
