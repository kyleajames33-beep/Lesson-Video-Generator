## 5. Misconceptions and traps

'A hypothesis is a guess' (it is a prediction with a reason). 'A true statement is a good hypothesis' ('catalase is an enzyme' predicts nothing). 'A result that matches proves the hypothesis' (it supports it). 'Contradicting evidence means the science is wrong' (it means the hypothesis or question needs changing). 'Heavier potato pieces foam more, so mass is the cause' (two things changed).
