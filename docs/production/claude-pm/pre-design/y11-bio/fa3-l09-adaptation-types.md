## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: Structural Adaptations, the Body's Build; Physiological & Behavioural Adaptations; Adaptations Are Shaped by Evolution.

Selected material:
- Structural Adaptations, the Body's Build: A structural adaptation is a physical part of an organism's body, anything about its shape, size, covering or internal parts that you could point to in a diagram.
- Physiological & Behavioural Adaptations: Not every survival feature is visible, some are internal chemical processes, and some are things the organism does rather than is.
- Adaptations Are Shaped by Evolution: An adaptation is not a lucky accident an individual makes, it is the end result of natural selection acting on inherited variation over many generations.

## 4. Misconceptions and traps

Site misconception: - Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: An organism "develops" an adaptation during its lifetime because it needs one. ✓ Right: Adaptations are inherited features that already vary within a population.

Source-backed response trap: A feature earns marks only when the type and survival effect are both explained. Kidney function is physiological; burrowing is behavioural. Source: site L09, misconception, activity and short-answer task. This is a page-based trap, not a claim about NESA marker trends.
