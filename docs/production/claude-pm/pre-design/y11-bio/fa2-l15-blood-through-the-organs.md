## 2. Site lesson material

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year11/fa2-cells-to-systems/lesson15.sa.html` and `docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/bio-y11-fa2-l15.md`. Site section order: 1. One Rule Explains Every Change; 2. Organ by Organ; 3. The Liver; 4. Why blood tests reveal organ problems. Relevant ideas:

- One Rule Explains Every Change: Every change in blood composition follows one simple logic: an organ removes from the blood whatever it consumes, and adds to the blood whatever it produces or absorbs.
- Organ by Organ: Applying the rule to each organ in turn gives a complete map of how blood content rises and falls around the body.
- The Liver: The Blood's Manager: After a meal, nutrient levels could swing wildly, the liver exists to buffer these swings so that the rest of the body receives a steady supply.
- Why blood tests reveal organ problems: Because each organ changes specific substances in the blood, a simple blood test can flag where something is wrong.

Practice demand: Site review sample: How does blood change as it passes through the lungs?
