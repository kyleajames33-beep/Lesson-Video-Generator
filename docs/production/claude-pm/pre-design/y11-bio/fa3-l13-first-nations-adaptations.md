## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: Knowledge of Adaptations on Country; Using Plant Adaptations; Using Animal Adaptations & Behaviour.

Selected material:
- Knowledge of Adaptations on Country: To use an organism's adaptation, you first have to understand that adaptation in detail, what the feature does for the organism and how it works.
- Using Plant Adaptations: Many plants produce defensive chemicals, a physiological adaptation against herbivores, and these same chemicals can serve human purposes such as fishing, medicine or food preparation.
- Using Animal Adaptations & Behaviour: Knowing an animal's behavioural adaptation, its migration, breeding time or daily movement, tells you exactly when and where it can be reliably and sustainably harvested.

Practice: Activity 1 Understand Band 4 Adaptation → Purpose Map Pattern A, Link cause to use For each use, name the underlying adaptation and classify it as structural, physiological or behavioural: (a) gathering Bogong moths in the alpine summer, where the moths migrate to cool mountain caves to escape lowland heat; (b) crushing Tephrosia into a still pool to stun fish; (c) leaching Macrozamia cycad seeds before eating them; (d) cutting channels and holding ponds at Budj Bim Site short-answer demand: Using one plant example and one animal example, explain how Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples use the adaptations of local species for specific purposes. In each case, identify the adaptation and the purpose it serves.

## 4. Misconceptions and traps

Site misconception: - Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander use of plants and animals was simple trial-and-error "folklore". ✓ Right: It is a sophisticated, empirical knowledge system built from careful, repeated observation over tens of thousands of years, tested across countless generations and specific to each Country.

Source-backed response trap: Do not generalise one named Country practice to all First Nations Peoples. Name the plant or animal adaptation, its use and its published source.
