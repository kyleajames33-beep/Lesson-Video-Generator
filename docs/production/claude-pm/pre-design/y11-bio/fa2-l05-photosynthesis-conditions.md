## 2. Site lesson material

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year11/fa2-cells-to-systems/lesson05.sa.html` and `docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/bio-y11-fa2-l05.md`. Site section order: 1. The Required Conditions; 2. Limiting Factors; 3. Temperature, A Condition with an Optimum; 4. Why greenhouses pump in carbon dioxide. Relevant ideas:

- The Required Conditions: Photosynthesis can only proceed when light energy, raw materials, a working enzyme system and the light-absorbing pigment are all present together.
- Temperature, A Condition with an Optimum: Because photosynthesis is run by enzymes, temperature speeds the reaction up to an optimum and then sharply slows it as the enzymes are destroyed.

Practice demand: Site review sample: Which list gives conditions required for photosynthesis?

Site short-answer questions (3): 1. Discuss the conditions required for photosynthesis. In your answer, explain how light, carbon dioxide and temperature can each limit the rate, and how temperature differs from the other two. 2. A graph plots the rate of photosynthesis against light intensity for a plant at 20 °C and again at 30 °C. Both curves rise and then flatten, but the 30 °C curve flattens at a higher rate. Analyse what is limiting the rate on the steep part of each curve and on each plateau, and explain what the difference between the two plateaus shows. 3. A greenhouse grower already runs lights at high intensity and keeps the air enriched with carbon dioxide. They propose raising the greenhouse temperature from 28 °C to 42 °C to increase yield further. Discuss whether this proposal will achieve what they expect, referring to the conditions required for photosynthesis.
