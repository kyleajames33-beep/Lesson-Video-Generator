## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: The Three Ingredients; From Variation to Evolution; When Natural Selection Cannot Act.

Selected material:
- The Three Ingredients: Natural selection is not a single force but a result that emerges when three ordinary biological facts come together in a population.
- From Variation to Evolution: Natural selection always runs through the same four steps: variation exists, a pressure acts, survivors reproduce, and the population shifts.
- When Natural Selection Cannot Act: The three ingredients are each necessary: take any one away and the population cannot evolve by natural selection, no matter how harsh the environment.
