## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

- **Meiosis and Cytokinesis: Variation and Completing Division:** The processes behind sexual reproduction and the final split Meiosis and cytokinesis matter because one generates the variation life depends on, and the other physically completes the production of new cells. Meiosis is important because it produces haploid gametes and, through crossing over, independent assortment and random segregation, generates the genetic variation that makes sexual reproduction possible and provides the raw material for evolution by natural selection . - **Reaching a Judgement:** How the four processes depend on each other, and a justified conclusion A good evaluation does not just list importance, it recognises how the processes depend on one another and concludes accordingly. The four processes are interlocked: DNA replication must happen first or neither mitosis nor meiosis could pass on a complete genome; cytokinesis completes both kinds of division; mitosis maintains the organism while meiosis renews the species . Then write a short concluding paragraph that evaluates their importance, argue whether they are equally essential or whether one is more fundamental, and justify your position with evidence from this lesson.

**Page misconception:** ✗ Wrong: To "evaluate" the importance of these processes, you just list what each one does. ✓ Right: Evaluating means weighing up their significance and reaching a justified conclusion, e.g. arguing that all four are essential because each supports a different life function, and that DNA replication and cytokinesis underpin the others. A list of functions describes; an evaluation judges and concludes.
