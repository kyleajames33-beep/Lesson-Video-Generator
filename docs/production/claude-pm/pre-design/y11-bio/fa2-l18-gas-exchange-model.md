## 4. Mistakes and traps

The site names this misconception: Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: The lungs actively pump oxygen into the blood. ✓ Right: Gas exchange in the alveoli is passive diffusion, gases move down their own concentration gradients with no energy used by the lung. This is a page-backed teaching trap, not a claim about NESA marking trends.
