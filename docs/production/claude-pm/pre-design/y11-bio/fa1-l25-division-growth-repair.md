## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

- **Replication and Mitosis: Growth, Repair and Continuity:** The processes that keep an organism alive and identical-cell-based DNA replication and mitosis matter because together they let an organism grow, repair itself and reproduce asexually without losing genetic information. DNA replication is important because it produces an exact copy of the genetic information before division, so every new cell receives a complete and accurate set of instructions, without it, daughter cells would lack the information needed to function . Mitosis is important because it produces genetically identical cells for growth (increasing cell number), repair and replacement of damaged cells, and asexual reproduction in some organisms . **Page misconception:** ✗ Wrong: To "evaluate" the importance of these processes, you just list what each one does. ✓ Right: Evaluating means weighing up their significance and reaching a justified conclusion, e.g. arguing that all four are essential because each supports a different life function, and that DNA replication and cytokinesis underpin the others.
