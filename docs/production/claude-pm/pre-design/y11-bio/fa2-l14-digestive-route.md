## 2. Site lesson material

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year11/fa2-cells-to-systems/lesson14.sa.html` and `docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/bio-y11-fa2-l14.md`. Site section order: 1. Why Food Must Be Broken Down; 2. The Journey; 3. How Digestion Serves the Cell; 4. When absorption fails. Relevant ideas:

- Why Food Must Be Broken Down: Your cells run on small, soluble molecules, glucose, amino acids, fatty acids, but food arrives as huge insoluble structures, so the digestive system exists to convert one into the other.
- The Journey: Mouth to Anus: The digestive tract is one long tube, and food is processed in a fixed order as it moves through, each region adding its own contribution before passing food on.

Practice demand: Site review sample: Which is an example of mechanical rather than chemical digestion?
