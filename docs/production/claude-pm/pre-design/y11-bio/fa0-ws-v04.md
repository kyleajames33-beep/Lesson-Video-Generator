## 3. Biology context and stop boundary

Potometer readings converted from mm/min to µL/min with the tube's bore (πr² × distance); choosing a table, line graph or bar chart; a dichotomous key as a representation; referencing a source in one accepted style; how observations by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples contribute to scientific knowledge, with a general example (knowledge holders' observations of where and when animals occur helping surveys find threatened species), no names (owner rule). Seasonal indicators and megafauna evidence are left to fa3-v16 and fa3-v05.

Stop: Selecting the information a question needs from a table, graph or text; unit conversions with working (mm to µL, mm to µm, per minute to per hour); choosing a representation by data type (continuous: line graph; categories: bar chart; identification: a key); acknowledging every source in one accepted style (author, year, title, publisher or URL, date accessed); Cultural Knowledges described in general terms with their contribution (long-term observation, seasonal records) and the attribution principle. No named communities (owner rule, 18 Nov 2026).

## 4. Numbers and facts

Potometer: 3 mm/min (fa2-v14) through a capillary of internal radius 0.5 mm: cross-section π × 0.5² ≈ 0.785 mm², so 3 mm/min × 0.785 mm² ≈ 2.4 mm³/min = 2.4 µL/min (1 mm³ = 1 µL); per hour × 60 ≈ 141 µL/h. Cell size 0.02 mm = 20 µm (fa1-v06 recall).

## 5. Misconceptions and traps

Converting length to volume without the area; µL and mL confused; a line graph for categories; a reference with no date or access date; treating knowledge holders' observations as anecdote rather than data.
