## 4. Misconceptions and traps

Site misconception: - Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: A transitional fossil is a "half-and-half" animal that was a direct ancestor of a living species, and gaps in the fossil record disprove evolution. ✓ Right: A transitional fossil shows a mix of ancestral and derived features and represents the kind of intermediate form predicted by evolution, not necessarily a direct ancestor.

Source-backed response trap: In undisturbed strata the base is oldest. A fossil assemblage helps compare periods; a transitional fossil is not necessarily a direct ancestor. Source: site L16, misconception, activity and short-answer task. This is a page-based trap, not a claim about NESA marker trends.
