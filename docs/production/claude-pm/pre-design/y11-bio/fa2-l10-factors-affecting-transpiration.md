## 2. Site lesson material

Source: `C:/Users/kygs/hscscience/subjects/biology/year11/fa2-cells-to-systems/lesson10.sa.html` and `docs/production/claude-pm/site-extracts/bio-y11-fa2-l10.md`. Site section order: 1. Light and Temperature; 2. Wind and Humidity; 3. Putting the Four Factors Together; 4. Why farmers watch the weather to schedule irrigation. Relevant ideas:

- Light and Temperature: Transpiration is fastest in bright, warm conditions because light opens the doorways out of the leaf and heat gives the water molecules more energy to escape.
- Wind and Humidity: Whether water vapour keeps diffusing out depends on how dry the air just outside the leaf is, wind keeps it dry, humidity makes it moist.
- Putting the Four Factors Together: All four environmental factors change the transpiration rate through just two mechanisms: how wide the stomata are open, and how steep the water-vapour gradient is out of the leaf.
- Why farmers watch the weather to schedule irrigation: Australian farmers and horticulturalists use exactly these four factors to decide when and how much to water.
