## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: The Osmotic Problem; Freshwater vs Saltwater Solutions; Why These Are Physiological Adaptations.

Selected material:
- The Osmotic Problem: An aquatic animal's body fluids rarely match the salt concentration of the water around it, so osmosis constantly pushes water in or pulls it out.
- Freshwater vs Saltwater Solutions: Each animal's adaptations exactly reverse the direction of its osmotic threat, gaining what it loses and shedding what floods in.
- Why These Are Physiological Adaptations: Each adaptation is an internal process that the body carries out continuously, often using active transport, rather than a body part you draw or an action the animal chooses.
