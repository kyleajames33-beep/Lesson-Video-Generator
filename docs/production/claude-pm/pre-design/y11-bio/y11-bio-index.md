# Year 11 Biology (2025 syllabus): pre-design pack index

105 suggested video-sized topics draw on 75 content-bearing site lessons: 34 in Focus Area 1, 35 in Focus Area 2 and 36 in Focus Area 3. This is 105 ÷ 75 = 1.4 videos per site lesson. The order below follows the site lesson sequence, with separate investigations, calculations and mechanisms where one page contains more than one learner question. These are design inputs, not recording or publication approval.

All packs use the exact 2025 NSW Biology Year 11 content-point wording from the [local ledger](../../../programme/syllabus/bio-new-y11.json), paired with the [Year 11 scoping inventory](../../../programme/year11-scoping.md), [site extracts](../../site-extracts/) and read-only site pages. The [site corrections log](../../../programme/site-corrections-y11.md) was a metadata check only. Source science findings from this pass are noted below and in the affected packs.

## Source findings to resolve before design

- FA1 L05 emphasises image size divided by magnification, while the syllabus also asks for a field-of-view method. Include field-of-view diameter divided by the number of cells across.
- FA1 L24 uses "random segregation" for variation. Explain the contribution of independent assortment separately.
- FA2 L24 generalises that a regulatory hormone rises whenever the variable moves away from its set point. Describe the named insulin and glucagon responses instead.
- FA2 L25 treats approximate body temperature examples as fixed failure limits and calls denaturation permanent. The affected pack gives the correction and sources.
- FA3 L11 applies a 36 to 38 °C mammal range to birds; the graph needs a named organism.
- FA3 L15 labels an illustrative resistance series as a published dataset in its short-answer task.
- FA3 L17 asks for a full non-human branching order from distances to humans alone. More pairwise evidence is required.

The source pages remain read-only. The numerical checks and any source-dependent cautions are recorded in pack section 3. Claude should choose the final lesson boundaries and inspect any painted plate candidate before reuse.
