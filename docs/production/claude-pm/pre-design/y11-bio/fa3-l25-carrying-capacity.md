## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: Carrying Capacity & the Growth Curve; What Changes the Carrying Capacity; Evaluating Validity, Reliability & Accuracy.

Selected material:
- Carrying Capacity & the Growth Curve: A population grows quickly while resources are plentiful, but as it becomes crowded, limited resources slow the growth until it levels off at the carrying capacity.
- What Changes the Carrying Capacity: Carrying capacity is not fixed, it rises when limiting resources increase and falls when they decrease, whether the factor is living or non-living.

Practice: Explain why drought lowers the carrying capacity of island rabbits, even without a new predator. Site short-answer demand: explain how one biotic and one abiotic limit affect K, with direction.

## 4. Misconceptions and traps

Site misconception: - Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: Carrying capacity is a fixed number for a species, and "reliable" data is the same thing as "accurate" data. ✓ Right: Carrying capacity changes whenever the limiting factors change (e.g. a drought lowers it; more rain raises it).

Source-backed response trap: K can move when limiting resources change. Reliable means repeatable; accurate means close to truth; valid means the sample answers the question. Source: site L25, misconception, activity and short-answer task. This is a page-based trap, not a claim about NESA marker trends.
