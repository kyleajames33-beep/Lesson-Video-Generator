## 2. Site lesson content

Whole-page section order: Three Kinds of Mammal; The Role of Isolation; The Role of Adaptation.

Selected material:
- Three Kinds of Mammal: Mammals fall into three groups based on how they reproduce, and Australia is unusual in being dominated by the first two.
- The Role of Isolation: Australia broke away from the supercontinent Gondwana and drifted alone for tens of millions of years, cut off from the placental mammals that came to dominate elsewhere.
- The Role of Adaptation: Isolation gave these mammals the opportunity, but it was adaptation by natural selection that actually fitted each species to a particular Australian environment.

Practice: Activity 1 Evaluate Band 6 Isolation or Adaptation, Which Mattered More? Pattern D, Take and justify a position A student claims: "Australia's unique mammals are entirely due to isolation, adaptation played no real part." Evaluate this claim. Explain what isolation contributed and what adaptation contributed, then judge whether either could have produced the outcome alone. Use the platypus or kangaroo as supporting evidence. ## 4. Misconceptions and traps

Site misconception: - Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: Monotremes and marsupials are "primitive" or "failed" mammals that placental mammals would replace if given the chance. ✓ Right: These groups are highly successful and well adapted to Australian conditions.

Source-backed response trap: Isolation limits gene flow; it does not by itself explain which traits are favoured. The answer needs both isolation and selection. Source: site L06, misconception, activity and short-answer task. This is a page-based trap, not a claim about NESA marker trends.
