### Exact site example `we-1`

WORKED EXAMPLE · BUILDING A v–t GRAPH A car's velocity is recorded as: $0\ \text{s} \to 0\ \text{m s}^{-1}$, $2\ \text{s} \to 4\ \text{m s}^{-1}$, $4\ \text{s} \to 8\ \text{m s}^{-1}$, $6\ \text{s} \to 12\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. Describe the v–t graph and state the acceleration. 1 GIVEN: velocity rises by $4\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ every $2\ \text{s}$. FIND: shape of the v–t graph and the acceleration. Equal velocity steps in equal time steps means a straight line. 2 METHOD: plot the points and find the gradient. $$a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{4}{2}$$ The gradient of a v–t graph is the acceleration. 3 ANSWER: a straight line rising from the origin, gradient $$a = 2.0\ \text{m s}^{-2}$$ Constant slope → constant acceleration of $2.0\ \text{m s}^{-2}$. ✓ 1 of 3 Reveal next step →

## 3. Recalculation, units and precision

(t,v) = (0,0),(2,4),(4,8),(6,12) in s and m/s: all adjacent gradients = 4/2 = 2 m/s². A straight-line interpolation has v=2t, a=2 m/s² and, if s(0)=0, s=t² m with t in seconds. Acceleration 2.0 m/s² is the stated teaching precision. Discrete samples alone cannot prove constant acceleration between observations.

## 4. Misconceptions and causal teaching

Misconceptions To Fix watch out ✗ Wrong: A horizontal line always means the object is not moving. ✓ Right: It depends on the graph. A flat d–t line means at rest, but a flat v–t line means constant velocity (still moving), and a flat a–t line means constant acceleration. ✗ Wrong: A steeper line always means a bigger displacement. ✓ Right: A steeper line means a bigger gradient, a higher velocity on a d–t graph, or a higher acceleration on a v–t graph, not a bigger displacement. 

Explain the definition or physical cause before relying on the formula. A direction convention belongs on the task, not only in the answer. A graph is a relation between labelled quantities, not a picture of the road.

## 7. Candidate practice and questions for Claude

### Candidate 1
Given t=0,1,2 s and v=2,5,8 m/s, sketch a straight-line interpolation and find acceleration.

Worked answer: Plot the labelled points, join linearly: v=2+3t. Gradient=(8-2)/2=3 m/s². State that constant acceleration between samples is the interpolation assumption.

### Candidate 2
A v-t graph is horizontal at -3.0 m/s for 4.0 s. What does the a-t graph show?

Worked answer: Zero acceleration throughout. Signed displacement=(-3.0)(4.0)=-12 m; a horizontal v-t line need not mean rest.
