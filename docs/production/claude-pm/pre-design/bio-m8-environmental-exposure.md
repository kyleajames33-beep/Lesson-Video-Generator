## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

**Page sections in order:** Warm up first; Predict first: UV exposure; Environmental vocabulary, translated; Three principles: exposure, dose and latency; Trace one causal pathway; Tobacco smoke: one exposure, three diseases; Same pattern, different exposures; UV radiation: a physical mutagen; Asbestos: cancer from shape, not chemistry; Epigenetics: silencing genes without mutating them; Why some heavy smokers never get cancer; Choose your route; Exit check; Multiple Choice; Short Answer, 15 marks; Check what actually stuck; Take the full module quiz; Blast the Correct Answer; Race Through Environmental Diseases!; How did your thinking change?.

**Teaching points in order:** 1. Exposure, damage, disease. 2. Repeated ultraviolet (UV) radiation is absorbed by DNA in skin cells. 3. Chemicals and particles damage airway tissue and DNA and can also damage blood vessels. 4. If smoking causes lung cancer, why do many lifelong heavy smokers never develop it, while about 15% of lung cancers occur in people who never smoked? The answer is that environmental disease is multifactorial. 5. Explain how tobacco smoke causes lung cancer, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and cardiovascular disease.

**Site examples and practice:**  “Which statement best explains how repeated UV exposure can contribute to skin cancer?” “How do tobacco carcinogens initiate lung cancer at the molecular level?”

## 3. Number and science check

The site says about 15% of lung cancers occur in never-smokers. This lies within the [US CDC estimate of 10% to 20%](https://www.cdc.gov/lung-cancer/nonsmokers/index.html), but that estimate is for the United States and does not establish an Australian percentage. No worked calculation is in the extract. [Module 8 correction log](../site-corrections-m8.md) records no verified correction.

## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

UV can damage skin-cell DNA; tobacco particles and chemicals damage airways and DNA. Exposure raises risk without guaranteeing disease. NESA feedback: 2024 Q24 requires a prevention action specific to the named exposure and disorder.
