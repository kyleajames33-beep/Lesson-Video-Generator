## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Screening detects risk or disease; embryo testing selects; engineering changes genetic material. Judge what each can prevent and its limits. NESA feedback: 2024 Q24 requires a precise prevention mechanism and realistic effect. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
