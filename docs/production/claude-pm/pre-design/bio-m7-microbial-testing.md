## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Purpose of water/food testing; serial dilution and plating; valid investigation design; flawed-design error spotting; safe handling. IV is water source, DV CFU/mL; control volume, agar, dilution and incubation. Negative sterile-water control tests contamination; positive culture tests growth conditions; three or more replicate plates assess consistency. ## 3. Number and science check

1 mL + 9 mL gives 10⁻¹; repeats give 10⁻², 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴. Worked plate: 156 colonies, 0.1 mL of 10⁻³ dilution. 156/(0.1 × 0.001) = 1,560,000 CFU/mL, correct. Report colony-forming units, not an exact bacterial cell count. Error: answer calls 4 CFU/100 mL four times the maximum. Guideline is no E. coli detected in 100 mL, so no fourfold ratio exists. Source: https://guidelines.nhmrc.gov.au/australian-drinking-water-guidelines/part-5/microorganisms/microbial-indicators/escherichia-coli . Site 30 to 300 count window is a classroom convention, not a universal method specification. Safety timings (121°C/15 min, bleach 10%/30 min, spill 20 min) need school-approved procedure verification before use.

Bank calculations:85/(0.1 ×10⁻³)=850,000 CFU/mL;54/(0.1 ×10⁻³)=540,000 CFU/mL. Correct.
