- Chemistry C6-IQ3-02 asks students to investigate titration curves and conductivity graphs to analyse data. C14 teaches the graph analysis, and P06 may collect a probe curve. The wording does not demand an eighth wet practical.
- Biology B6-IQ2-01, B6-IQ3-01 and B6-IQ3-03 have a combined `depthStudyOrPractical` flag in the local ledger. Site L08 to L16 use secondary-source inquiry for biotechnology and cloning, with no school-lab procedure specified. The core videos and G02 own those inquiries. Claude should review this decision before locking the P list.

## Practical presentation rule

Each plan lists existing and needed painted apparatus or organism plates. Planned visuals have no people or hands. Values are worked examples for design, not observed student results. Each method requires a school-specific risk assessment and the actual product safety data sheets before a class conducts it.
