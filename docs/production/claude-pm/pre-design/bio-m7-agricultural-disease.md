## 3. Number and science check

UK FMD site values: £2.7 billion direct agriculture; £8 billion total. Difference £5.3 billion; total/direct = 2.96. Australia estimate is $52 billion over 10 years (2013 model), distinct from annual $30 billion exports. Do not present old economic estimates as current prices. Site also lists 6.5 million or over 6 million culled animals; these are different descriptions, not a recomputed result. Unverified claims needing sources: $300 million annual myrtle-rust loss, $80 billion nematode losses, $100+ million Australian hydatid losses, 350+ affected Myrtaceae, 14 EPBC species and 10 to 16% crop loss. Error in causal wording: no co-evolution does not prove complete absence of resistance in every host species or individual.

Historical amounts and teaching datasets are kept distinct from verified arithmetic. No chemistry constants are used. Unverified claims above are questions for sourcing, not approved factual statements.
