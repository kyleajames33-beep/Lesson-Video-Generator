## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

**L07 key ideas:** 1. A triple bond reacts twice, controlling which step you stop at controls the product.
2. Hydrogenation, Full and Partial.
3. Halogenation, Two Steps.
4. Hydrohalogenation, Two Steps (Markovnikov applies each time).
5. The only addition in this module where the expected product does not form.
6. Enol Tautomerism Explained.
7. UV light is the energy source, not a catalyst, and this distinction is heavily tested.
8. Balancing Combustion Equations: C → H → O.

## 3. Number and science check

But-2-yne consumes one Br2 to give 2,3-dibromobut-2-ene, then a second to give 2,2,3,3-tetrabromobutane. Propyne + water -> propanone via CH3C(OH)=CH2; site H2SO4/Hg2+, about 60 degrees C. Full hydrogenation consumes two H2 equivalents. Site error: unchanged enol would remain C3H6O, not become propan-2-ol C3H8O as Worked Example2 claims. In the W/X/Y/Z example, X C4H8 is not proved unbranched; 2-methylpropene hydrogenates to 2-methylpropane, not butane. C4H6 consuming two Br2 can also be a diene, so the alkyne assignment needs a topic restriction or more evidence.
