## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

Traditional-knowledge drug discovery; documented bush medicine plants; annotate one Australian plant research task; intellectual-property limitations; Nagoya Protocol; Western Australian smoke bush case. Compare ethical bioprospecting and biopiracy through consent, involvement, attribution, control over knowledge and agreed benefit sharing. Artemisia/Youyou provides an international comparison, not the required Aboriginal case.
