## 3. Number and science check

All calculations reproduce using M(O₂)32.00 g mol⁻¹. Ratio:1 O₂:2 I₂:4 S₂O₃²⁻. Simple BOD subtraction applies to undiluted, unseeded examples. Dilution and seed require corrections. [USGS BOD method](https://pubs.usgs.gov/twri/twri9a7/twri9a7_NFMChap7.pdf) specifies incubation/control requirements. Site categories below 2,2 to 8, above 8 are teaching classifications, not universal legal limits. DO/BOD supports the broad environmental-monitoring point; it is not a separately named 2017 dotpoint.
