## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Multiplying when going to a bigger unit (1850 mm × 1000).
- Calculating with mismatched units (25 m ÷ 40 cm).
- Using × 100 for every step (mL to L).
- Reading "kilo" as 100.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

**Question 1.** A roll of ribbon holds 25 m. How many 40 cm pieces can be cut from it?
Worked answer: 25 m = 2500 cm; 2500 ÷ 40 = 62.5, so 62 whole pieces. Slip: "25 ÷ 40 = 0.625" (units not matched).

**Question 2.** A café uses 180 mL of milk in each latte. (a) How many full lattes can be made from a 2 L bottle? (b) How many litres of milk do 45 lattes need?
Worked answer: (a) 2000 ÷ 180 = 11.1, so 11 lattes. (b) 45 × 180 = 8100 mL = 8.1 L. Slip: "8100 mL = 81 L" (÷ 100 instead of ÷ 1000).
