**Start:** A phone on its charger and a winter heater plugged into the same power strip. The power bill charges for "kWh". What is a kWh, and which of these two actually costs money?

**Stop:** Power in watts is how fast an appliance uses energy while it is on; 1000 W = 1 kW (divide by 1000); energy in kilowatt hours = power in kW × time in hours (a kWh is the energy a 1 kW appliance uses in one hour), so convert minutes to hours first; cost = kWh × price per kWh; hours per day × number of days; adding several appliances' power before converting; comparing appliances: a lower-power appliance switched on for longer can cost more; reading the usage price on a bill. ### Source lesson05, key terms and card 05

"Power, in watts: How fast an appliance uses energy, at every instant it is switched on. Like this: a $2400\text{ W}$ heater draws energy twice as fast as a $1200\text{ W}$ one". ## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Multiplying watts by hours and calling it kWh (a thousand times too big).
- Using minutes as hours (3 minutes as 3 h).
- Confusing power (kW, a rate) with energy (kWh, an amount); "kW per hour".
- Thinking the higher-rated appliance always costs more.
- Leaving the price in cents and the answer in dollars, or the reverse.

These follow from the content, not claims about how often markers see them.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

### Question 1

A gaming console uses 160 W while it is on. Ella plays for 3.5 hours a day. Electricity costs 33 c per kWh. (a) Convert the console's power to kilowatts. (b) How many kilowatt hours does it use in a day? (c) Find the cost of running it for a week and for a year (365 days), to the nearest cent.

**Worked answer:** (a) 160 ÷ 1000 = 0.16 kW. (b) 0.16 × 3.5 = 0.56 kWh. (c) Week: 0.56 × 7 = 3.92 kWh, × $0.33 = $1.29. Year: 0.56 × 365 = 204.4 kWh, × $0.33 = $67.45.

### Question 2

In winter, a family heats their lounge room for 5 hours every evening. A portable heater draws 2.4 kW. A reverse-cycle air conditioner heating the same room draws 0.9 kW. Electricity costs 33 c per kWh. (a) Find the energy each uses over 30 days. (b) How much would the family save in 30 days by using the air conditioner instead of the heater?

**Worked answer:** (a) Heater: 2.4 × 5 × 30 = 360 kWh. Air conditioner: 0.9 × 5 × 30 = 135 kWh. (b) Heater $118.80; air conditioner $44.55; saving $74.25.

Only the question goes on the card. Hide the answer until feedback. "Pause here to answer." then a 10-second countdown. Feedback stays brief: answer, key reason, one crossed-out mistake (for Question 1 (b): "160 × 3.5 = 560 kWh", crossed, "convert to kW first"). Answers were checked with SymPy. The 0.9 kW figure is an example of the electrical power a reverse-cycle unit draws, not a product rating; label it as an example.
