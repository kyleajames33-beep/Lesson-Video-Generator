## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- The triangle's height confused with the prism's length.
- The diameter used as the radius.
- Mixed units before multiplying.
- Leaving a volume in m³ when the question asks for litres or kilolitres.
- Volume and surface area mixed up (cm² against cm³).

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

**Question 1.** A concrete retaining wall has a trapezium-shaped cross-section with parallel sides 0.5 m and 0.8 m and a perpendicular height of 1.2 m. The wall is 15 m long. (a) Find the volume of concrete. (b) Concrete costs $220 per cubic metre (example price). Find the cost.
Worked answer: (a) ½ × (0.5 + 0.8) × 1.2 = 0.78 m²; 0.78 × 15 = 11.7 m³. (b) 11.7 × 220 = $2574. Slip: "(0.5 + 0.8) × 1.2 × 15 = 23.4 m³" (the ½ left out).

**Question 2.** A cylindrical rainwater tank has an internal diameter of 2.4 m and a height of 3 m. Find its capacity in kilolitres, correct to 2 decimal places.
Worked answer: r = 1.2; π × 1.44 × 3 = 4.32π = 13.57 m³ = 13.57 kL. Slip: "r = 2.4: 17.28π = 54.29 kL".
