## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Swapping the fixed charge and the rate (6t + 18 = 78).
- Putting the total on the wrong side, or writing an expression with no equals sign.
- Dividing before subtracting (3x + 5 = 26 → x + 5/3 = 26/3).
- Doing an operation to one side only.
- Stopping at "n = 140" with no sentence, or giving 43.67 cars.
- Rounding the wrong way: a target needs rounding up; a budget needs rounding down.

These follow from the content, not claims about how often markers see them.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

### Question 1

A swim school charges a $45 enrolment fee plus $22 per lesson. A family paid $309 for one child's lessons this term. Write and solve an equation to find the number of lessons.

**Worked answer:** Let L be the number of lessons. 45 + 22L = 309, so 22L = 264 and L = 12. Check: 45 + 22 × 12 = 309. The child had 12 lessons.

### Question 2

Mia has $1,250 in her account and spends $35 each week on transport. Write an equation to find when her balance reaches $500, solve it, and state after how many whole weeks her balance first falls below $500.

**Worked answer:** Let w be the number of weeks. 1250 − 35w = 500, so 35w = 750 and w ≈ 21.43. After 21 weeks she has $515; after 22 weeks, $480. Her balance first falls below $500 after 22 weeks.

Only the question goes on the card. Hide the answers until feedback. "Pause here to answer." then a 10-second countdown. Feedback stays brief: answer, key reason, one crossed-out mistake (Question 2: "21 weeks", crossed, "after 21 weeks she still has $515"). Answers were checked with SymPy.
