## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- 1 m³ = 1 L or 1 m³ = 1000 mL.
- cm³ to L by dividing by 100.
- Forgetting to convert dimensions to one unit before multiplying.
- Treating volume and capacity as different amounts rather than different units.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

**Question 1.** A box is 50 cm long, 20 cm wide and 15 cm high. How many litres does it hold?
Worked answer: 50 × 20 × 15 = 15 000 cm³ = 15 000 mL = 15 L. Slip: "15 000 cm³ = 15 000 L".

**Question 2.** A rectangular water tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1.2 m high. (a) Find its capacity in kilolitres and in litres. (b) A hose fills it at 20 L per minute. How many hours does it take to fill?
Worked answer: (a) 2 × 1.5 × 1.2 = 3.6 m³ = 3.6 kL = 3600 L. (b) 3600 ÷ 20 = 180 minutes = 3 hours. Slip: "3.6 m³ = 3.6 L".
