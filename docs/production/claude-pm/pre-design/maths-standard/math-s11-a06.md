## 4. Misconceptions and HSC-style traps

- Adding the squares when the hypotenuse is known (a shorter side comes out longer than the hypotenuse).
- Labelling the wrong side c in a turned triangle.
- Adding the sides, not their squares.
- Rounding the square early.
- Using Pythagoras on a triangle with no right angle.

## 6. Two original candidate practice questions

**Question 1.** A gate is 2.4 m wide and 1.8 m tall. A diagonal brace runs from one corner to the opposite corner. How long is the brace?
Worked answer: c² = 2.4² + 1.8² = 5.76 + 3.24 = 9; c = 3 m. Slip: "2.4 + 1.8 = 4.2 m".

**Question 2.** A wheelchair ramp is 6.5 m long and rises 0.6 m. How far does it reach horizontally? Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.
Worked answer: d² = 6.5² − 0.6² = 42.25 − 0.36 = 41.89; d = 6.47 m. Slip: "√(42.25 + 0.36) = 6.53 m" (longer than the ramp).
