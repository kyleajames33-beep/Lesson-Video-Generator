## Site sequence and material

Order: The ribosome reads in threes; The adaptor molecule; Codon meets anticodon; Start, elongate, stop; Put it together and apply; Decode and Match; Insulin Translation Reasoning; independent practice; Retrieve and reflect. The page begins with orientation and worksheet links.

- Cytoplasmic ribosomes read mRNA three bases at a time. A ribosome contains protein and rRNA, and rRNA catalyses peptide bonds. tRNA is the adaptor with an anticodon at one end and its amino acid at the other. Codon and anticodon pair antiparallel. AUG starts and codes for methionine; UAA, UAG and UGA are stops, with no tRNA-delivered amino acid. Repeated tRNA arrival and peptide-bond formation build a polypeptide.
- Worked pair: mRNA `5′-AUG GAA-3′` pairs with anticodons `3′-UAC CUU-5′`. Reversed into each tRNA's own 5′ to 3′ direction, they are `CAU` and `UUC`. Page prints `UAC CUU` without direction in its answer; that is correct only if read 3′ to 5′ against the codons. Its separate example `5′-GCA-3′` / `3′-CGU-5′` correctly shows antiparallel pairing. AUG codes methionine; GAA codes glutamate on the standard code. The page asks which molecule carries amino acids: tRNA.
- Insulin reasoning: an incorrect amino acid sequence may change folding or function; no specific insulin sequence or quantity is given. Streptomycin is historical context: page says 1943 isolation, bacterial 30S target, bacterial 70S = 30S + 50S and eukaryotic 80S = 40S + 60S. These are sedimentation labels, not additive arithmetic: 30 + 50 is not meant to equal 70. The page's `50+ antibiotics` line has no cited count and is not needed for the core mechanism.
- Misconceptions: translation in the nucleus; mRNA carries amino acids; one kind of tRNA; start codon AUG codes tryptophan; treating codon and anticodon as the same sequence. Questions: decode `AUG GAA`; reason about insulin; analyse polypeptide synthesis and characteristics (7 marks); explain tRNA and ribosome roles (5 marks); MC, true/false and three short answers (12 marks). Retrieval cues: ribosome, tRNA, peptide bond, exam trap. No named memory aid; the adaptor idea is the page's useful analogy.
