## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

ADH changes water permeability in collecting ducts; aldosterone affects sodium handling. Do not swap their targets. NESA feedback on homeostasis in 2019 Q29 requires the regulated internal variable and reversing response. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
