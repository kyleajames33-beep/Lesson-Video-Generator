## 3. Number and science check

Comparisons: B/A nitrate×4, phosphate×9, DO falls 2.3 mg L⁻¹; C/A nitrate×5, phosphate×12.5, DO falls 4.5. No universal ecological threshold follows. Ion mass and elemental mass differ: mg nitrate/L must not be compared directly with mg N/L.

**Site error L06:** total coliforms are described as faecal-contamination indicators; total coliforms can be environmental, while E. coli is the more specific routine faecal indicator. Conductivity is not universally proportional to total ion concentration across different mixtures/temperatures. Claiming pure distilled water inherently harmful is not justified by this parameter comparison. [NHMRC E. coli indicator guidance](https://guidelines.nhmrc.gov.au/australian-drinking-water-guidelines/part-5/microorganisms/microbial-indicators/escherichia-coli) supplies the parameter framework. No fixed guideline values are needed for this dataset.
