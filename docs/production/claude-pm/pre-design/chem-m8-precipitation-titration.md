## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

## 3. Number and science check

All calculations reproduce. Tablet calculation:(0.0100−0.00225)/2 ×100.1=0.3878875 g. Site AgCl Ksp 1.8×10⁻¹⁰ gives s1.34×10⁻⁵ M; Ag₂CrO₄ Ksp 1.1×10⁻¹² gives s=(Ksp/4)^(1/3)=6.50×10⁻⁵ M, matching its approximate solubilities.

**Site error L01:** comparing pure-water molar solubilities does not determine precipitation order in the mixture. Thresholds depend on actual concentrations: [Ag⁺]=Ksp(AgCl)/[Cl⁻] and [Ag⁺]=√(Ksp(Ag₂CrO₄)/[CrO₄²⁻]). [University Mohr manual](https://www.gju.edu.jo/sites/default/files/analytical_manual_2.2020_3.pdf) explains indicator-concentration control. Saying every added Ag⁺ is removed until chloride runs out also overstates finite-solubility equilibrium.
