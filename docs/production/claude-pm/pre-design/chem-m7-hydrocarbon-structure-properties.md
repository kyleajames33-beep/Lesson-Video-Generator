## 2. Site order, ideas, examples, practice and memory aids

**L03 order:** Connect: retrieve the homologous-series pattern; Explain: construct the alkane series; Represent: chain length and boiling point; Respond: branching changes intermolecular contact; Feedback: compare hydrocarbon structures; Apply: predict solubility and trends.

**L04 order:** Connect: three molecules, three shapes; Explain: a single bond gives tetrahedral carbon; Represent: a double bond fixes a planar arrangement; Respond: a triple bond makes carbon linear; Feedback: compare bond, shape and angle; Apply: reason from a structural formula.

**L03 key ideas:** 1. Alkanes are the structural baseline for the entire module.
2. A strong answer does not stop at “larger molecules have higher boiling points.” It explains why larger hydrocarbon molecules create stronger dispersion forces at the electron-cloud level and why that means more energy is needed to separate them.
3. The syllabus asks you to investigate the trend, not only to explain it.
4. Two molecules can have the same formula and the same molecular mass, yet different boiling points.
5. Hydrocarbon series differ not only in formula but also in local geometry.
6. “Like dissolves like” is only the shortcut.
7. Activity 1, Predict, Rank, Explain.

**L04 key ideas:** 1. Each carbon in ethane makes four σ bonds: three C-H bonds and one C-C bond.
2. At each carbon in ethene there are three bonding regions: two C-H directions and the C=C direction.
3. Each carbon in ethyne has only two bonding directions: one towards hydrogen and one towards the other carbon.
4. compare bond, shape and angle.
5. Mark the bond type at each carbon.
6. Model and explain.

## 3. Number and science check

Methane < propane < butane < heptane is the increasing boiling-point order. Pentane and 2,2-dimethylpropane are C5H12 isomers; branching reduces contact between molecules. Hexane mixes with heptane and separates from water. Ideal local bond angles are 109.5, 120 and 180 degrees for tetrahedral, trigonal planar and linear carbon. Local planar geometry does not make an entire longer molecule planar. No numerical worked calculation.
