## 1. Exact 2017 syllabus content

> investigate the various mechanisms used by organisms to maintain their internal environment within tolerance limits, including:
> - trends and patterns in behavioural, structural and physiological adaptations in endotherms that assist in maintaining homeostasis (ACSBL099, ACSBL114)
> - internal coordination systems that allow homeostasis to be maintained, including hormones and neural pathways (ACSBL112, ACSBL113, ACSBL114)
> - mechanisms in plants that allow water balance to be maintained (ACSBL115)

## 3. Number and science check

The [Bureau of Meteorology](https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/current/statements/scs67.pdf) reports 42.6 °C at Cairns on 27 November and exceptional heat across multiple days. ## 4. Exam traps and student mistakes

Do not identify the external air temperature as the regulated internal variable. Link each effector to heat gain or loss. NESA feedback: 2021 Q29 and 2025 Q24 require the causal path from response to temperature change. See [exam research](../research/question-types-bio-m8.md) for the official paper links and marker context.
