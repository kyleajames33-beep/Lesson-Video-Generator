## Method notes, not claimed factual errors

Chemistry L02 omits dilute HCl before BaCl₂ and protection of AgCl from strong light. It does not explicitly assert these are unnecessary. [USGS sulfate method](https://pubs.usgs.gov/wsp/1454/report.pdf) and [EPA 375.3](https://www.nemi.gov/methods/method_summary/4703/) support acidification to suppress carbonate interference.

Biology L01's action-potential example is scientifically defensible: sodium-channel activation during the rising phase provides positive feedback. Its lack of explanation is a teaching issue, not logged as a factual error.
