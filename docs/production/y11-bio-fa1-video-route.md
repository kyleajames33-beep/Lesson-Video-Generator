## Merges, splits and reorders

- Merge fa1-l06-light-microscopy and fa1-l06-electron-microscopy-impact into bio-y11-fa1-v07. Both own B25-FA1-G1-07, the light microscopy pack has one section, and the assess demand needs the light microscope limit and the electron microscope gains in one argument.
- Re-split L08: bio-y11-fa1-v09 merges fa1-l08-facilitated-diffusion (one section) into the diffusion and osmosis explanation, so all three passive processes are explained together for B25-FA1-G2-02. The demonstration section of fa1-l08-diffusion-osmosis becomes its own practical video, bio-y11-fa1-v10, for B25-FA1-G2-03.
- Keep fa1-l10-active-transport (bio-y11-fa1-v12) and fa1-l10-bulk-transport (bio-y11-fa1-v13) separate. Pumps and vesicles are different mechanisms, and together they would rush. B25-FA1-G2-05 is jointly owned; bio-y11-fa1-v13 closes with the passive versus active comparison.
- Merge fa1-l17-enzyme-factors (one section) into bio-y11-fa1-v20 with fa1-l18-enzyme-graphs, because the factor explanations are the causes behind the graph shapes; standing alone they would double-teach. Reorder so the graph video (bio-y11-fa1-v20, G3-07) comes before the fair-test practical (bio-y11-fa1-v21, G3-06), so students plan with a prediction. This departs from site order (L17 before L18).
- Keep the two L05 practical packs separate (bio-y11-fa1-v05 wet mount, bio-y11-fa1-v06 cell size): each carries its own syllabus investigation. bio-y11-fa1-v06 leads with the syllabus field-of-view equation.
- Keep fa1-l20-dna-replication (bio-y11-fa1-v23, practical) and fa1-l20-dna-replication-models (bio-y11-fa1-v24) separate. The fork, two strands and four enzymes fill a video; the model assessment is a distinct evaluative question. bio-y11-fa1-v24 is the thinnest video and the first merge candidate if design finds it under 5 minutes.
- Keep fa1-l23-cell-cycle and fa1-l23-mitosis-model separate, and fa1-l24-meiosis-model and fa1-l24-meiosis-variation separate. The mitosis and meiosis models are syllabus investigations; the cycle and variation points are distinct questions. The meiosis model comes before variation so the variation events appear on a model students already know.
- Merge fa1-l25-division-growth-repair and fa1-l25-division-reproduction into bio-y11-fa1-v31. Both own B25-FA1-G4-10, and the evaluate demand needs all four processes weighed in one judgement.
- Otherwise follow site lesson order L01 to L25. Result: 34 packs become 31 videos.
- All cell division videos use the 2n = 4 model with painted chromosomes at true scale, following the cell division visual rules.
