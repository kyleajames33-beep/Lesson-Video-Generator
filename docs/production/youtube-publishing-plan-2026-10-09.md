## Search and upload metadata

Lead titles with the topic a student is likely to search for, then the year
and subject. Keep syllabus module numbers and content IDs in the description
and playlist context. These query choices are hypotheses, not measured search
volume, ranking predictions or guarantees.

| Video | Proposed title | Main query hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Molar mass | Molar Mass: Grams to Moles and Formula Calculations \| Year 11 Chemistry | molar mass grams to moles |
| Limiting reagents | Limiting Reagents: Theoretical Yield and Excess Reactant \| Year 11 Chemistry | limiting reagent theoretical yield |
| Enzyme models | Lock and Key vs Induced Fit: Enzyme Models Explained \| Year 11 Biology | lock and key vs induced fit |

Each prepared description starts with the learning task, follows with the
worked examples, then paraphrased current/new syllabus coverage and official
links. It includes measured chapters and three relevant hashtags. Tags are
short and specific. No bulk keyword list is added to the description.

This follows YouTube's guidance on accurate titles and thumbnails, unique
descriptions and the limited value of tags. Search also uses content relevance,
engagement and quality, so syllabus wording alone cannot guarantee discovery.
[Search guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en),
[description guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en),
[tag guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en).

Prepared packages: `out/prototypes/continuity-batch-01/youtube/` contains exact
titles, descriptions, tags, asset paths, curriculum IDs and review status.
Chapter validation checks a `00:00` start, at least three chapters and at least
ten seconds between chapter starts. These chapters derive from the measured
lesson timeline, with brief title scenes merged into the preceding chapter.
[Chapter requirements](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en-GB).

Use the supplied aligned SRT and 1280 by 720 covers. Covers preserve the
approved artwork. They are initial accurate covers, not a completed thumbnail
experiment. Inspect phone readability and compare alternatives after actual
audience data exists. Measure impressions, click-through rate, query traffic
and retention at the opening/prediction holds once sufficient data exists.
