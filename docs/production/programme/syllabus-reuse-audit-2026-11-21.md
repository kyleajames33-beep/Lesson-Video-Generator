# Syllabus reuse audit: 2017 and 2025 NSW science courses

Prepared 21 November 2026 by a Claude research agent for root (production manager). Read-only research: nothing was voiced, built, rendered or committed. Machine-readable rows: `syllabus-reuse-audit-2026-11-21.json` (554 rows, sections Q1 to Q4).

## Summary

**The big finding.** The Physics 11-12 (2025) Year 12 course is close to a copy of the 2017 Year 12 course (the same four areas: advanced mechanics, electromagnetism, light, atom and cosmos). 69 of the 81 unvoiced Year 12 Physics designs teach 2025 Year 12 content as they stand or with small edits. They are long-life work (the 2027 HSC cohort, then every 2025 cohort from Term 4 2027), not one-cohort work. No 2025 Year 12 Physics redesign is needed: plan about 12 gap videos instead of the 139 to 153 videos the master plan estimated.

### Top savings

1. **Year 12 Physics 2025 needs gap videos, not a new course.** 69 designs serve both syllabuses; 7 more give partial 2025 value. The 2025-only points with no design are listed under "Gaps" below (about 12 short videos). Confidence: high on content, but the 2025 Year 12 IDs are provisional (no official ledger).
2. **Year 11 Chemistry 2017 is mostly long-life.** 47 unbuilt designs (and 17 built videos) teach 2025 Year 11 points, and 2 (galvanic cells, `m3-v11`, `m3-v12`) teach 2025 Year 12 FA4. Only 8 designs (`m4-v07` to `m4-v14`: Hess's law, formation enthalpies, entropy, Gibbs) are 2027-only; Hess's law, entropy and Gibbs appear in neither 2025 Chemistry course. When 2025 Year 11 Chemistry starts (Term 1 2028), about 15 gap points need videos, not 105 to 116 new videos.
3. **The Year 12 Biology and Chemistry 2025 transitions are relabel-plus-gaps.** 44 Biology and 73 Chemistry Year 12 designs are likely to carry over to 2025 Year 12 after a content check; do not redesign them. 13 Biology designs (DNA replication, mitosis, meiosis, DNA forms, homeostasis, glucose, plant water, potometer, dialysis) belong to 2025 **Year 11**, which gives them a long life if relinked.
4. **Year 11 Biology could save up to about 10 Google voicings** by using published Year 12 videos as stand-ins or re-cuts (`bio-m5-b06a`, `bio-m8-b01`, `bio-m8-b03`, `bio-m8-p02`, `bio-m6-b07` or `bio-m5-b08`, `bio-m5-p01`, `bio-m5-b05b` with `bio-m5-p02`, `bio-m8-b05`, `bio-m8-b02`), and one more by merging `bio-m8-b17` (unvoiced, ready to voice) with `bio-y11-fa2-v25` (dialysis). This is an owner call: the stand-ins are ElevenLabs-voiced, carry Year 12 metadata and were written for a Year 12 entry point. Confidence: medium.
5. **Shared art and sims, not shared audio, between Year 11 and Year 12 Physics.** `phys-y12-m6-v01` and `v03` and `phys-y11-fa3-v07` to `v10` teach the same physics (fields between plates, W = qV). 2025 teaches it in both years, so both sets of videos are needed, but build the scenes and the field-motion setup once. The 2017-only designs `m5-v14`, `m5-v15`, `m6-v08` and `m6-v15` get a second life as art for `phys-y11-fa1-v21`, `fa1-v26`, `fa3-v24`/`v25` and `fa3-v17`.

### Recommended voicing order changes

1. **Year 12 Physics in module order (the 2027 cohort is on Module 5 now), long-life designs first within each module.** Voice the 54 as-is and 15 minor-edit designs first. Put the 2017-only and low-value partial designs last in each module wave: `m5-v10`, `m5-v15`, `m6-v08`, `m6-v15`, `m7-v02`, `m8-v01`. They serve one cohort (HSC 2027) but are mandatory 2017 points, so voice them before that module is taught. `m5-v14` (banked tracks) is only a "for example" case in 2017 P5-IQ2-02 and is absent from 2025: lowest priority; consider not voicing it.
2. **Remove course labels from narration before voicing, so one take serves both syllabuses.** Physics: `m5-v09`, `m6-v05`, `m6-v07`, `m6-v09`, `m6-v19` ("the syllabus calls/asks/writes"), `m8-v07` ("Module 6"), `m8-v10` ("Module 7"). Chemistry Year 11: `m2-v20`, `m3-v01`, `m3-v13`, `m3-v15` ("Module 2/3"). Settle the torque angle convention in `phys-y12-m6-v16` first (the design uses tau = nIAB sin theta; 2025 writes tau = nBIA cos theta with a different angle).
3. **Year 11 Chemistry: voice Modules 1 to 3 and `m4-v01` to `m4-v06` before `m4-v07` to `m4-v14`.** The eight 2027-only designs are taught in Terms 3 and 4 of 2027, so they can sit at the end of the queue.
4. **Bring Year 12 Physics forward relative to the 2017-only Chemistry Year 11 work** where the two compete for nightly voice slots: almost every Physics design has a life of 2027 plus 2028 onward, while the eight Chemistry Module 4 designs serve 2027 only.
5. **Do not voice both `bio-m8-b17` and `bio-y11-fa2-v25`.** Write one dialysis script that satisfies 2017 B8-IQ5-02 and 2025 B25-FA2-G3-09.

### Recommended design order changes

1. Before designing any 2025 Year 12 Physics video, have Codex build an official `phys-new-y12.json` ledger (mirroring `phys-new-y11.json`), then relabel the 69 dual-use designs with both 2017 and 2025 point IDs in their metadata. Dual-labelled designs satisfy the owner's 1 November rule that Physics uses the new syllabus.
2. Design only the 2025 Year 12 Physics gap videos (below). Do not re-route the course from the site's 99 lessons.
3. For the Biology Year 12 2025 transition (before Term 4 2027), start from the Q4 rows: relabel likely-carry videos, check partial ones, and design only the gaps (introns and alternative splicing, regulatory RNA, epigenetics, SNP limitations, epidemiology study types and causation, Panama disease, invasive and keystone species, conservation and bioinformatics). The Chemistry Year 12 2025 transition follows a year later (Term 4 2028): its gaps are petroleum and fractional distillation, base metal ores, AES, the FA4 polymers, and galvanic cells (already designed as `chem-y11-m3-v11` and `v12`).

### Class meanings

- Q1: **serves-2025-as-is** (teaches 2025 points with only metadata changes); **serves-2025-minor-edit** (a label line, an opening frame, a formula convention or one added scene); **partial-2025** (some 2025 value; re-cut or companion); **2017-only** (one cohort, HSC 2027).
- Q2: **maps-2025-y11** and **maps-2025-y12** (long life from 2028); **partial-2025**; **2017-only** (taught in 2027 only); **not-designed**.
- Q3: **reuse-whole-video-candidate** (a published video could stand in); **re-cut-candidate** (a subset of scenes plus a new intro or outro); **merge-with-2017-design** (one script for both courses); **reuse-art-sims-new-narration** (plates, coded parts or sims, with a new voice); **new**; **not-designed**.
- Q4: **likely-carry** (relabel after a content check); **partial-2025**; **moves-to-2025-y11**; **2017-only**; **superseded** (design replaced by split versions).

### ID conventions

`P5-`, `C1-`, `B5-` and similar are 2017 ledger IDs. `P25-`, `C25-` and `B25-` are 2025 Year 11 ledger IDs. `C25Y12-` and `B25Y12-` are 2025 Year 12 ledger IDs (prefix added here). `P25Y12-` are provisional 2025 Year 12 Physics handles (see above).
