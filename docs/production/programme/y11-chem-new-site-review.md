## Syllabus fit and completeness

- The 2025 ledger has 46 FA1, 27 FA2 and 26 FA3 content points. Every point has a topical lesson in this draft set, so there is no whole content point with no lesson. This is topic matching, not full completion.
- Practical points require students to conduct investigations. FA1 L02, L12 and L24; FA2 L02, L03, L06 and L12; and FA3 L02, L04, L05, L08, L14, L16, L19 and L22 provide preparatory text or example data. They do not by themselves record conducted practical work. The affected point IDs are in the table and correction list.
- FA1 L14 does not complete its four-property data and graph investigation. FA1 L19/L26 and FA3 L11/L23 need traceable secondary sources and evaluation. No complete lesson was found to be wholly outside the new syllabus. FA2 L07 molecular-formula work, L09 percentage yield, and L21 molar-volume derivation are extensions; L09 currently assesses percentage yield without teaching it.
- All 75 pages have substantial Learn, Practice and Review content, and no visible production TODO/coming-soon placeholder text was found. All 75 are marked `noindex`. The draft folder and a comment in every page still say 2027, while NESA starts Year 11 Chemistry in Term 1 2028.

## Site behavior and visual standard

- The shared footer `../../index.html` points to a missing Chemistry course index on all 75 pages. The structured breadcrumbs also name missing course and year index pages. Resolve these destinations before promotion.
- A static audit found 75 lesson-to-bank matches, 558 parseable bank questions, 279 microtasks, no duplicate HTML IDs, and no missing local script/style files. In a local browser all 75 pages returned HTTP 200 and mounted Quick Check without a page error. This load test does not validate every answer or every interaction path.
- Excluding shared icons and the decorative hook graphic, subject SVGs occur in 10 of 27 FA1 pages, 0 of 24 FA2 pages and 2 of 24 FA3 pages. There are no subject images or canvases in these lesson pages. Thus 63 pages lack a purpose-built subject visual. FA2 quantitative pages especially miss the required formula-flow or worked visual support. Existing useful FA1 diagrams should be retained.

## Decision needed

Correct the wrong science and answer keys first, then repair incomplete syllabus tasks and the shared navigation. Review new subject visuals by teaching purpose. Recheck the exact revised pages and question banks before any of these drafts become video sources or are promoted to the live site.
