# Exam skills series: plan (owner request, 19 Oct 2026)

**Owner's brief:** "Students know the content but don't know how to write the answer." Make occasional lessons on the HSC key verbs and how to answer properly: tips, tricks and approaches, and real training of the skill of reading, understanding and then answering a question.

## Format

- **Ids:** `skills-sNN` for videos shared by Chemistry and Biology, `chem-skills-sNN` and `bio-skills-sNN` for subject-specific ones. Each video sits in the subject playlist near the module where its skill first matters, and in its own "HSC exam skills" playlist.
- **Teaching method:**
  1. Show a real-style question.
  2. Pull it apart on screen: underline the verb, circle the marks, box the givens, and mark the "in terms of", "with reference to" and "using" constraints.
  3. Build the answer step by step.
  4. Show a weak answer beside a strong one, with the marks the weak one loses and why.
  5. Give one training drill: "Pause here to answer" with the 10 s countdown, worked feedback, and one crossed-out habit.
- **Questions:** written for HSCScience in the HSC style, not copied from NESA papers. NESA's glossary wording and the general patterns in its marking guidelines are quoted or paraphrased with attribution.
- **Visual system:** the existing explainer kit. The question card gets an annotation layer: a highlighter for the verb, a marks badge, givens underlined (the practice-video rule), and a mark counter that ticks as each mark-earning point appears.
- **Length:** the usual 5,500 to 6,800 characters.

## Planned videos (first pass)

1. **s01: Read the question before you answer it.**
   - The verb, the marks and the content words.
   - Constraint phrases such as "in terms of" and "with reference to".
   - The "one mark, one idea" rule of thumb, and why it is only a rule of thumb.
   - A 30-second reading routine.
2. **s02: Identify, describe, explain.**
   - What each verb asks for.
   - Explain means a cause and an effect, linked with "because" or "so".
   - The classic mistake: describing when asked to explain.
3. **s03: Compare, contrast, distinguish.**
   - Similarities and differences stated *as pairs*.
   - Comparison words, and the table-to-sentence trick.
4. **s04: Analyse, assess, evaluate, justify.**
   - The judgement verbs.
   - A judgement plus criteria plus evidence.
   - How "assess" differs from "evaluate".
   - Why a judgement with no reasons earns little.
5. **s05: Calculation answers that get full marks.**
   - The equation, values with units, substitution, the answer with units and sensible significant figures.
   - Showing working when the answer is wrong (consequential marks).
   - Chemistry examples: pH, Ksp, moles. Biology examples: frequencies, magnification.
6. **s06: Graphs and data questions.**
   - Describe a trend with numbers.
   - Read values with units.
   - Draw a graph well (axes, scale, line of best fit).
   - Interpolate against extrapolate.
7. **s07: Practical and investigation questions.**
   - Validity, reliability and accuracy, defined in the NESA sense.
   - Variables, and improvements that actually fix the stated problem.
   - The classic "repeat it" answer and when it earns nothing.
8. **s08: Extended responses (6 to 9 marks).**
   - Plan in 60 seconds.
   - One idea per paragraph, linked to the question.
   - Use the stimulus.
   - Finish with the judgement when the verb asks for one.
9. **Subject-specific follow-ups:**
   - chem-skills: writing equations with states; naming and structural formulae.
   - bio-skills: interpreting pedigrees and Punnett answers; "evaluate a model" questions.

## Pipeline

- **Research:** Codex brief 83 collects:
  - the NESA glossary of key words, word for word;
  - the patterns in recent public NESA marking guidelines for Chemistry and Biology;
  - marker feedback on common answer-writing faults (HSC "Notes from the Marking Centre");
  - which verbs appear most, and at what mark values.
- **Then the usual pipeline:** Claude designs s01 and s02 first, then learning review and science check, then a Codex error check, then the owner's voicing go-ahead.
- **Placement:** s01 and s02 open the next uploads for both subjects. The rest follow every few videos.

## Owner decisions (19 Oct 2026)

- **Placement:** interleave the series through the Chemistry and Biology module playlists, and also keep its own "HSC exam skills" playlist.
- **Questions:** don't copy real HSC questions. Write new ones in a closely similar style: the same stem structure, stimulus types, verbs, mark values and level of demand as recent NESA papers.
