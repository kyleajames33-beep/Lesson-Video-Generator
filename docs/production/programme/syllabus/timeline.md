# NSW Stage 6 syllabus transition, checked 16 October 2026

| Course | New Year 11 teaching starts | New Year 12 teaching starts | First new HSC | Last regular HSC on 2017 syllabus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Biology 11–12 (2025) | Term 1, 2027 | Term 4, 2027 | 2028 | 2027 |
| Chemistry 11–12 (2025) | Term 1, 2028 | Term 4, 2028 | 2029 | 2028 |
| Physics 11–12 (2025) | Term 1, 2027 | Term 4, 2027 | 2028 | 2027 |
| Mathematics Advanced and Standard 11–12 (2024) | Term 1, 2026 | Term 4, 2026 | 2027 | 2026 |

NESA explicitly directs schools to continue the 2017 Year 12 Biology course during 2027 and the 2017 Year 12 Chemistry course during 2028. Therefore the 2017 Year 12 syllabus is examined for both subjects in 2026 and 2027; Chemistry remains in use for the 2028 HSC. The last regular HSC year in the table follows from NESA's first-new-HSC dates. It does not address exceptional repeat candidates or special examination arrangements.

NESA's Biology transition instruction includes: “Continue to teach the Biology Stage 6 Syllabus (2017) for Year 12” in 2027. Its Chemistry instruction gives the same direction for Chemistry in 2028. Both new syllabuses are already published, including Year 11 and Year 12 content.

## Planning consequence

The current Year 12 Biology Module 5–8 videos serve the 2026 and 2027 HSC cohorts. Current Year 12 Chemistry Module 5–8 videos also serve the 2028 HSC cohort. Year 11 Biology should use the 2025 focus areas from 2027; Year 11 Chemistry should use them from 2028. A 2017 Year 12 lesson must not be labelled as covering a 2025 focus area without a separate content check.
