# Physics and mathematics syllabus scope, checked 16 October 2026

Content-point counts below count NESA's main content bullets in each focus area or module. They exclude nested examples. The 2025 Physics totals list the seven Working scientifically groups separately because schools integrate them across all focus areas. These counts come from the current NESA content pages and the 2017 Physics Word syllabus, not site lesson counts.

## Physics

NESA starts the [Physics 11–12 Syllabus (2025)](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/physics-11-12-2025/overview/course) in Year 11 in Term 1 2027 and Year 12 in Term 4 2027. Its first HSC is 2028. The [2017 course](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/science/physics-stage-6-2017) remains the Year 12 course during 2027.

| Year | 2017 modules and main point counts | 2025 focus areas and main point counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | M1 Kinematics 10; M2 Dynamics 13; M3 Waves and Thermodynamics 27; M4 Electricity and Magnetism 33 | Fundamentals of mechanics 49; Waves 38; Electricity and magnetism 47 |
| 12 | M5 Advanced Mechanics 14; M6 Electromagnetism 15; M7 The Nature of Light 17; M8 From the Universe to the Atom 23 | Advanced mechanics 37; Electromagnetism 44; Nature of light 42; Matter, energy and the cosmos 72 |

The 2025 syllabus also has 34 Year 11 and 34 Year 12 Working scientifically points. These are cross-course content and are not added to each focus area. Counts were read from NESA's [2025 content](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/science/physics-11-12-2025/content) and [2017 syllabus DOCX](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2025-03/physics-stage-6-syllabus-2017.docx).

## Mathematics Standard

The [Mathematics Standard 11–12 Syllabus (2024)](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/mathematics/mathematics-standard-11-12-2024/overview/course) is the current course. New Year 12 teaching starts in Term 4 2026; the first HSC is 2027. The [2017 course](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/curriculum/mathematics/mathematics-standard-stage-6-2017) is the outgoing HSC course in 2026. Year 12 has separate Standard 1 and Standard 2 pathways.

| --- | --- | --- |

| 12 Standard 1 | Algebra; Measurement; Financial Mathematics; Statistical Analysis; Networks. 34 main points. | Algebraic relationships 8; Investment 8; Depreciation and loans 15; Right-angled triangles 5; Ratios and rates 16; Bivariate data analysis 9; Relative frequency and probability 10. Total 71. |
| 12 Standard 2 | Algebra; Measurement; Financial Mathematics; Statistical Analysis; Networks. 43 main points. | Algebraic relationships 25; Investment and loans 29; Annuities 7; Trigonometry 10; Ratios and rates 21; Network flow 6; Critical path analysis 9; Bivariate data analysis 13; Relative frequency and probability 12; The normal distribution 11. Total 143. |

The counts use NESA's [2024 content pages](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/mathematics/mathematics-standard-11-12-2024/content) and [2017 syllabus DOCX](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/noindex/2026-09/mathematics-standard-stage-6-2017-updated-implementation-info.DOCX). The Standard 1 and Standard 2 Year 12 counts are separate pathways and must not be added together as one course.
