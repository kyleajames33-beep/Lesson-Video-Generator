# Year 11 Advanced: proposed whole-course outline

Brief 243 research, 16 November 2026. All 201 official Year 11 points (2024 syllabus, read from the NSW Curriculum site on 16 November 2026) have an owner. 90 content videos plus 3 optional practice videos. Assignment is a proposed teaching home, not approval, and not evidence that a site page fully teaches the point. No unowned points. Exact official HTML and the point-to-video ledger are in the companion JSON and the crosswalk.

**Id prefixes.** w = Working with functions; t = both trigonometry focus areas (Trigonometry and measure of angles, then Trigonometric identities and equations); c = Introduction to differentiation; e = Exponential and logarithmic functions; g = Graph transformations; s = Probability and data; p = optional practice (no ownership).

**Recommended order:** functions, differentiation, exponentials and logs, trigonometry, graph transformations, then probability (which can run in parallel at any time). Functions underpin everything. Differentiation and exponentials come early because the already-built Year 12 videos assume them (math-a12-d01 relies on e01, e02, c07 and c10). Exponentials follow differentiation because the syllabus defines e by a tangent gradient. Transformations need the graphs they act on. The syllabus listing order is not a mandated teaching order.
