## Source and science decisions

The source scaling claim that flight time, height and range all grow with launch-speed squared is wrong: for fixed angle and equal levels, time is proportional to speed, height/range to speed squared. Wall clearance must retain precision to 10.4 m at three significant figures. Exact corrections and the source torque-angle issue are in claude-pm/site-corrections-phys-y12.md. The 2017 parallel-wire wording reflects the historical ampere definition; current SI fixes elementary charge, as [BIPM specifies](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/ampere). Keep both contexts clear without altering the official quote.
