# Year 11 Chemistry (2017): site crosswalk and route inputs

Fresh official DOCX read 5 November 2026. The 2017 syllabus applies to this course; the 2025 Year 11 course begins in Term 1 2028. The ledger records all 52 parent content points and their nested requirements, all 7 Working Scientifically outcomes with 19 content actions, and the depth-study/practical requirements.

The 66 read-only site lessons provide source teaching for 43 covered and 9 partial parent actions. No parent action lacks all source material, but partial teaching is not complete coverage. The cultural action stays held. The source flags are planning judgements based on the named teaching sections, not an independent full science review of every page.

The route proposes 83 videos: 76 core/practical entries and 7 WS support entries integrated at the named teaching positions. The first ten packs follow the first ten core entries. Atomic structure and periodicity precede detailed bonding; distillation, chromatography, measured mixture percentages and formula percentages each have a focused question. Mole-based gravimetry is moved out of the opening separation sequence.

## Remaining source work

- C1-IQ2-04: The site has a flame-test method and Bohr evidence, but explicit Schrodinger model teaching is thin; practical safety needs fresh review.
- C1-IQ3-01: State, configuration, radius and electronegativity are present; add an explicit first-ionisation-energy explanation and checked water-reaction evidence.
- C1-IQ4-02: Nomenclature, Lewis and polarity are present; systematic molecular-shape construction needs expansion.
- C2-IQ2-01: The compound/volatile-liquid method is present; an element molar-mass investigation is not developed.
- C2-IQ4-01: All four laws and PV=nRT are explained; methods are developed for Boyle and Charles, with less investigation support for the other laws.
- C3-IQ1-04: Site chemistry exists, but attributed community-specific provenance/permission is unresolved; cultural hold.
- C3-IQ2-01: Reaction patterns and series are present; a complete safe primary-data sequence across every listed reagent needs preparation.
- C3-IQ2-05: Cell voltage is explained; comparison against a reference needs an explicit investigation and uncertainty treatment.
- C3-IQ3-01: Factor mechanisms are present; complete digital data-collection/analysis/reporting plans need preparation.

No video viewing substitutes for the minimum 35 hours of practical investigations or 15 hours of depth studies within the 120-hour course. At least one depth study, Questioning and Predicting and Communicating, two further WS outcomes and one Knowledge and Understanding outcome are retained in the requirements record. The site was not edited.
