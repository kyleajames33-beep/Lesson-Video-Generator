## Review notes

- Chemistry has seven proposed practical videos: indicators, neutralisation enthalpy, measuring a pH range, comparing strength through pH, buffer preparation, titration and household-substance analysis. Student conduct and results need separate evidence.
- Biology has investigation and source-evaluation actions but no explicit hands-on practical in the local Module 6 content-point wording. The ledger's `depthStudyOrPractical` flag is automated and is not an official practical classification.
- Chemistry site L12 and its pre-design pack quote a Ka/pKa dotpoint absent from the local 2017 ledger. C10 has no invented syllabus ID. Verify the official source before its recording brief.
- Chemistry L06, L11 and L17 mainly supply practice and consolidation examples. Biology L19 supplies gene-pool examples near B08 and population-context application in B20.
- Pre-design site corrections remain open for individual designs, especially Chemistry L09 and L17 and Biology L01, L11, L16 and L19. The [Module 6 memory-aid bank](module6-memory-aids-2026-10-11.md) is a draft and each selected aid needs a science check.
- Durations are planning estimates. Split any script that needs more than about 6,800 narration characters. No narration, visual, listening or release approval is claimed.
