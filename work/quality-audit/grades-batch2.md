3. **Ghosted or half-faded frames.** Crossfades leave two scenes overlaid or a whole scene at low opacity in chem-m6-c02, c07, c09, c11 and c12, and in bio-m7-b09. Check these at playback speed. If the fade holds while narration runs, shorten it.
4. **Empty exam-card bodies and empty tables.** This carries over from batch 1. Biology Module 7 exam cards open with one line of stimulus in a full-height card. The practical risk tables (bio-m7-p01, p05; chem-m5-p01, p03) appear with one row filled.
5. **Key-notes walls in Biology.** Biology Module 7 still ends on dense bullets. Biology Module 8 and Chemistry use a framed memory card, which is better, but bio-m8-b03 and b04 hold the key notes twice.
6. **Equation-only stretches in Chemistry Module 6.** c06, c07 and c10 run several frames with a bare equation or working in a mostly empty stage. The Module 5 chemistry run keeps the tube, syringe or particle view beside the working. That is the bar.

Quality is highest in chem-m5 (c03 to g03d, mostly A) and bio-m8 (b01, b05). It drops in the chem-m6 run, which holds most of the missing titles and three of the five C grades.
