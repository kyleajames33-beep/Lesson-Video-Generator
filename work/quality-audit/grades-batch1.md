## Summary

Counts (50 videos): A 8, B 32, C 10, ? 0.

- A: bio-m5-b02a, b02c, b06a, b13, g01, g02; bio-m6-g01; bio-m7-b01.
- C: bio-m5-g03; bio-m6-b04, b05, b07, b10, b11, b12, b16, b19; bio-m7-b06.

Recurring problems:

1. **Empty or title-only frames while narration runs.** This happens in almost every B and C video. Often the visual arrives seconds after the title, or the closing "how to answer" card holds a single chip.
2. **Exam-question cards with a large empty body.** These are common in M6 and M7: a two-line stimulus in a full-height card, no plate, and an empty answer box. In bio-m7-b04 (frame 11) and bio-m7-b06 (frame 13) the card is blank, so the question text appears to be missing.
3. **Tiny text.** Comparison tables, criteria cards and practice answer panels sit well below 26 px (m6-b09, b12, b13, b15, b16, b19, m6-g02, m7-b02).
4. **Key-notes walls.** Many videos end on 6 to 13 long, dense bullets. Item 8 asks for short bullets plus the memory card. In the M5 b06b to b13 run the memory aid is also a loose line of text, not a framed card.
5. **Coded stand-ins where a plate should carry the idea.** Examples are the coded oval cells in b05a and b05b, the bare DNA lines in m6-b05 and the CRISPR scene in m6-b11, and the letter-bar chromosomes in m6-b04.
6. **Asset failures.** bio-m7-b06 shipped with broken-image placeholders, and bio-m5-b06b ends on a black frame.

Quality drops from the M5 b02 to b06a run, which meets the bar, to the M6 b04 to b19 run, where most of the C grades sit.
